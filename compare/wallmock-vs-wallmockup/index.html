WallMock vs WallMockup: similar names, different tools.

Short answer: WallMock (wallmock.com) and WallMockup (wallmockup.net) are different products from different companies. WallMockup is a true-scale wall art mockup editor with 720 room scenes, a 7-day trial and a $4.99 monthly plan. WallMock places your exact artwork in 500+ curated rooms as framed canvas, framed prints or posters, for a whole collection at once, with open sign-up. Why the names get mixed up

"WallMock" sounds like "wall mockup", the everyday phrase for a picture of art on a wall, and it is only two letters shorter than WallMockup. A search for "wallmock" can return pages about wall mockups in general, or about WallMockup. They are not the same: WallMock is the product at wallmock.com, made by the WallMock team; WallMockup is a separate product at wallmockup.net, run by a different company. Neither is a version of the other.

If you are not sure which one you are using, look at the address bar: WallMock's app is at app.wallmock.com.

Where WallMockup gives more

  • True-scale sizing. WallMockup says every scene is measured, so the print shows at the size you sell, in inches and centimetres. WallMock shows a sensible starting size, but it is not a measuring tool.
  • A lower price for heavy use. Pro Monthly is $4.99 a month with no export cap stated, against WallMock's $5 for 200 clean images. It also sells a $5.99 one-time pack of 100 credits.
  • More free tools. 21 on its tools page, from Etsy tag and title generators to an image cropper for print.
  • Custom rooms on demand. Its credits pay for AI-made rooms built around your art; WallMock uses curated rooms only.

Where WallMock is different

Your exact art, never redrawn

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Text, signatures, fine lines and colours stay as you made them, so the photo matches the print your buyer receives.

Curated rooms

Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock. They are chosen for showing wall art, from living rooms and bedrooms to offices and nurseries.

Favourite rooms, whole collections

Set up the rooms and frame you like once, add a whole collection, and WallMock makes every artwork in every chosen room, so a whole shop looks consistent. Pairs, trios and sets of four go on one wall.

WallMock also shows the products print-on-demand shops sell: a floating frame with its gap around the canvas, stretched canvas with no frame, framed prints with or without a mat, and posters.

Which one to choose

  • Choose WallMockup if buyers keep asking about size, you want the lowest monthly price, or you want to start today with open sign-up.
  • Choose WallMock if you sell framed canvas, framed prints or posters through print-on-demand, list many designs, and want your exact file shown in the same curated rooms across your shop.
  • Try both. WallMock's free previews need no sign-up, and WallMockup's trial needs no card.

Questions

Is WallMock the same company as WallMockup?

No. WallMock (wallmock.com) is not WallMockup (wallmockup.net). They are different products from different companies. WallMock's website is wallmock.com and its app is app.wallmock.com.

Which is cheaper, WallMock or WallMockup?

On 2026-10-28 WallMockup's Pro Monthly plan was $4.99 a month with no export cap stated, plus a $5.99 one-time pack of 100 credits. WallMock's plans start at $5 a month for 200 clean images. For a seller who exports a lot, WallMockup's plan costs less.

Does WallMockup show art at true scale?

Yes, according to its own home page: every scene is measured, so the print shows at the size you sell, in inches and centimetres. WallMock shows a sensible starting size in each room but is not a measuring tool.

Can both make mockups for many designs at once?

Yes. WallMockup's batch page says one set holds up to 60 room-and-frame combinations that you can reuse. WallMock saves your favourite rooms and frame looks once and makes every artwork in every chosen room.

Does either tool redraw my artwork?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. WallMockup's credits also pay for custom rooms made with AI around your art; we did not find a statement on its pages about whether the artwork itself is changed.

Can I try both for free?

WallMockup offers a 7-day trial with no credit card. Try WallMock free with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews made on your device.

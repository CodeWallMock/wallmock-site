WallMock vs WallMockup: similar names, different tools.

Short answer: WallMock (wallmock.com) and WallMockup (wallmockup.net) are different products from different companies. WallMockup is a true-scale mockup editor with 720 room scenes, 54 frame styles, AI-made rooms and a $4.99 monthly plan. WallMock makes Photoshop-quality framed canvas and print mockups of your exact art in 500+ curated rooms, for a whole collection at once, with 7 finishes plus any frame colour you design, and unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images on Pro. Why the names get mixed up

"WallMock" sounds like "wall mockup", the everyday phrase for a picture of art on a wall, and it is only two letters shorter than WallMockup. A search for "wallmock" can return pages about wall mockups in general, or about WallMockup. They are not the same: WallMock is the product at wallmock.com, made by the WallMock team; WallMockup is a separate product at wallmockup.net, run by a different company. Neither is a version of the other.

If you are not sure which one you are using, look at the address bar: WallMock's app is at app.wallmock.com.

Where WallMockup gives more

  • Measured scenes. WallMockup says every scene is measured, so the print shows at the size you sell, in inches and centimetres. WallMock also takes your real size, but its wall scale is estimated from the room photo in 332 rooms, and the other rooms use a reviewed size.
  • More rooms and frame styles listed. 720 room scenes and 54 frame styles on its home page, against WallMock's 500+ curated rooms and 7 finishes plus any colour you design.
  • A lower monthly price. Pro Monthly is $4.99 a month with exports not capped, against WallMock's $12 for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images (or $5 for 200). It also sells a $5.99 one-time pack of 100 credits. Its pages don't state an export size in pixels.
  • More free tools. 21 on its tools page, from Etsy tag and title generators to an image cropper for print.
  • Custom rooms on demand. Its credits pay for AI-made rooms built around your art; WallMock uses curated rooms only.

Where WallMock is different

Your exact art, never redrawn

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Text, signatures, fine lines and colours stay as you made them, so the photo matches the print your buyer receives.

Curated rooms

Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock. They are chosen for showing wall art, from living rooms and bedrooms to offices and nurseries.

Favourite rooms, whole collections

Set up the rooms and frame you like once, add a whole collection, and WallMock makes every artwork in every chosen room, so a whole shop looks consistent. Pairs, trios and sets of four go on one wall.

Photoshop-quality framing

Clean 4K (4096 px) images with the gap around floating-frame canvas, a natural shine on the frame and a soft wall shadow. Every example opens at full size on the examples page.

Any frame colour

Design your own frame: pick wood grain, metallic or painted, then any colour with the colour picker or one of 21 one-click colours (5 woods, 6 metals and 10 paints). Some rooms keep only the frame looks we reviewed for them; custom frames work in all the others. Colours are close approximations, not a supplier's finish.

Fast, in your browser

In our tests, a full-size 4K (4096 px) image takes about 7 to 12 seconds in the browser, and a laptop makes up to 4 images at once.

WallMock also shows the products print-on-demand shops sell: a floating frame with its gap around the canvas, stretched canvas with no frame, metal prints on a frameless aluminium panel, and framed prints in wood frames and aluminum framed prints, with or without a mat.

Which one to choose

Choose WallMock if

  • you sell framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed prints and want Photoshop-quality framing: the floating-frame gap, frame shine and a soft wall shadow;
  • your exact file must appear unchanged, with text, signatures and fine lines as you made them;
  • you list many designs and want every artwork in the same saved rooms in one run, sets of 2, 3 or 4 included;
  • you need a frame colour your supplier sells that is not a standard finish: design it in any colour;
  • you want to try it with no sign-up, then pay $5 a month for 200 clean 4K images, or $12 for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images.

Choose WallMockup if

  • buyers keep asking about size and you want every scene measured for scale, not estimated;
  • you want more rooms to pick from (720 listed) or rooms made by AI around your art;
  • you export a lot and want the lowest monthly price, with exports not capped for $4.99.

Or try both: WallMock's free previews need no sign-up, and WallMockup's trial needs no card.

Questions

Is WallMock the same company as WallMockup?

No. WallMock (wallmock.com) is not WallMockup (wallmockup.net). They are different products from different companies. WallMock's website is wallmock.com and its app is app.wallmock.com.

Which is cheaper, WallMock or WallMockup?

On 2026-10-29 WallMockup's Pro Monthly plan was $4.99 a month with exports not capped, plus 10 credits a month, and a $5.99 one-time pack of 100 credits. Its 10 credits a month buy 2 preview 1K AI rooms or 1 listing-ready 2K AI room, and no page we read states its export size in pixels. WallMock's Lite is $5 a month for 200 clean 4K images, and Pro, at $12, includes unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server (600 on Studio). WallMockup costs less a month; every WallMock image is a full-size 4K file.

Which has better frame quality?

Judge it by eye. WallMock's wall art mockup examples open every frame at full size, so you can check the corners, the floating-frame gap, the shine and the wall shadow; its frames come in 7 finishes plus any colour you design, and framed prints in 20 mm wood, 31.75 mm gallery wood or slim aluminum, with or without a mat. WallMockup lists 54 frame styles with adjustable thickness and an optional mat.

Which is faster?

In our tests, a full-size 4K (4096 px) image takes about 7 to 12 seconds in the browser, and a laptop makes up to 4 images at once. WallMockup's home page says a mockup takes under 60 seconds. WallMock also makes every artwork in every saved room in one run.

Does WallMockup show art at true scale?

Yes, according to its own home page: every scene is measured, so the print shows at the size you sell, in inches and centimetres. WallMock also takes your real size: Enter your artwork's real size in centimetres or inches (long side 4 to 60 in). In the 332 library rooms with a published estimated wall scale, the artwork can be drawn at that size against the room's scale, and size matches show the estimate's range; other rooms use a reviewed placement size. The scale is estimated from the room photo, not measured on a wall, so check sizes against your product.

Can both make mockups for many designs at once?

Yes. WallMockup's batch page says one set holds up to 60 room-and-frame combinations that you can reuse. WallMock saves your favourite rooms and frame looks once and makes every artwork in every chosen room.

Does either tool redraw my artwork?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. WallMockup's credits also pay for custom rooms made with AI around your art; we did not find a statement on its pages about whether the artwork itself is changed.

Can I try both for free?

WallMockup offers a 7-day trial with no credit card. Try WallMock free with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews made on your device.

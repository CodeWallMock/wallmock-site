Where Smartist is better

  • A polished native app. On an iPhone or iPad, a touch-first app with layouts, angle views and movable objects is pleasant to use, even in a client's home.
  • Your own room and augmented reality. Smartist lets you upload a photo of your own space, and lists augmented reality among its features. WallMock has neither.
  • True scale. You enter your artwork's dimensions in Smartist. WallMock shows a sensible starting size in each room, but it is not a measuring tool; for sizes, use our free art size over a sofa calculator.
  • A large ratings base. On 2026-10-29 the US App Store showed 4.8 from about 8.5K ratings. WallMock is a newer web tool with no app store ratings to show.
  • More interiors and bigger walls. Smartist claims 3000+ interiors on Pro and up to 20 artworks on one wall; WallMock has 500+ rooms and sets of 2, 3 or 4.
  • Edge colour on unframed art. Smartist offers a side colour for unframed artwork. WallMock shows stretched canvas front-on and has no edge-colour choice.

Where WallMock is different

It runs on whatever you already own. WallMock opens in a web browser on a Windows laptop, a Mac, an Android phone or an iPad, with no app to install. Previews and images are made in your browser, on your device.

It makes whole collections, not one mockup at a time. Heart the rooms you like, save the frame look once, then add a whole collection: every artwork is made in every chosen room. Worked example: 30 new designs in 6 saved rooms is 180 images, inside the Lite plan's 200 images a month ($5). Pairs, trios and sets of four go on one wall.

It shows the products print-on-demand shops ship. Framed canvas sits in a floating frame with the narrow gap drawn between canvas and frame, in black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver. Framed prints come in a wood frame or a slim aluminium frame, each with or without a mat. See the gap up close on the framed canvas mockup generator page.

Your art is never redrawn. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Lettering, signatures and fine lines stay as you made them. More in our guide to AI mockup generators: exact art or redrawn art.

Bigger files. Clean images go up to 4096 px, against Smartist's 3840 × 2400 px maximum. The extra pixels help when you crop a square thumbnail from a wide room.

How to switch from Smartist on Windows, Android or Mac

  1. Open the guest studio at app.wallmock.com/try in the browser you already use. Try WallMock free with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews made on your device.
  2. Add your full-size art files, the ones you send to your printer, not exported mockups. Each keeps its own shape, from square to 3:1 panoramas.
  3. Pick the product your buyer receives: stretched canvas, a floating frame in your provider's colour, or a wood or metal-framed print. Our floater frame guide explains the difference from a standard frame.
  4. Create a free account with Google or an email code, save your favourite rooms, add the collection, and take a plan when you want clean images. Keep Smartist if you still need client rooms, true scale or augmented reality.

Questions

Is there a Smartist alternative for Windows?

Yes. Smartist's help centre says it is only available on Apple devices (checked 2026-10-29), so there is no Windows version. WallMock runs in a web browser, so it works on a Windows PC with nothing to install.

Is Smartist available on Android?

Not yet. On 2026-10-29 Smartist's help centre answered "Not yet" and said an Android version is being worked on. WallMock works in the browser on an Android phone or tablet today.

Is there an app like Smartist that works in a browser?

WallMock is a browser-based alternative: open it at app.wallmock.com, with no download. Try WallMock free with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews made on your device.

How much does Smartist cost compared with WallMock?

On 2026-10-29 Smartist's site showed Pro at $19.99 a month, or $7.49 a month on the yearly plan, and its help centre says the price depends on your region. WallMock plans are Lite $5, Pro $12, Studio $24 a month, or $39, $89 and $179 a year.

Can I use a photo of my own room in WallMock, as in Smartist?

No. WallMock uses its own 500+ curated rooms and has no upload for your own room photo. If you need your own space or augmented reality, Smartist does both.

What image size do Smartist and WallMock export?

Smartist's help centre gives a maximum of 3840 by 2400 pixels (checked 2026-10-29). WallMock's clean images go up to 4096 px.

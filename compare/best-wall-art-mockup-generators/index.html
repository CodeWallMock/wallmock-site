How to choose

  • You sell framed canvas through print on demand: pick a tool that shows the floating-frame gap and canvas edge your provider ships. WallMock shows both in 7 finishes; ArtPlacer lets you set the gap width and the edge. Our print on demand canvas mockups page lists the gaps providers state.
  • You list a whole collection: use a batch tool. WallMock makes every design in every curated room you save; Scaylr fills PSD templates for many products. See our bulk mockup generator.
  • Buyers ask "how big is it?": a true-scale tool such as WallMockup, Situ.art, ArtPlacer or Smartist, or a size image made with our free art size calculator.
  • You sell to collectors or galleries, or want art in a client's room: ArtPlacer's collector tools and Personal Spaces, Smartist, or Situ.art's Pro plan.
  • You sell more than wall art: Scaylr, Mockey, Canva or Placeit, with many product types in one place.
  • You print through Printify, Printful or Gelato: start with their own mockups, then add room photos that match the frame they ship.

WallMock

Best for: print-on-demand and wall art sellers on Etsy and Shopify who list many designs and want every one shown in the same favourite rooms.

WallMock is a wall art mockup generator for print-on-demand sellers on Etsy and Shopify: your exact art in styled rooms as framed or stretched canvas, or framed prints. It shows framed canvas in a floating frame with its gap, in black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver; stretched canvas (gallery wrap); metal prints, art on a frameless aluminium panel; and framed prints in wood frames and aluminum framed prints, with or without a mat. Sets of 2, 3 or 4 go on one wall. You save favourite rooms and frame looks once, add a whole collection, and every artwork is made in every chosen room, as 4096 px images. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Try WallMock free with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews made on your device. Plans: Lite $5, Pro $12 and Studio $24 a month.

Where others are better: WallMock's wall scale is estimated from the room photo (in 332 rooms) rather than measured, it has no uploads of your own room photo, no unframed posters and no video on Lite, and it has fewer rooms (500+) than ArtPlacer, Smartist Pro or Situ.art list. To check sizes, use our free art size calculator.

ArtPlacer

Best for: artists, galleries and art advisors who sell to collectors, and anyone who wants fine control over frames.

ArtPlacer is a platform for artists, galleries and art advisors, with room mockups, presentations, Personal Spaces for clients' rooms and virtual exhibitions. On 2026-10-29 its pricing page showed a 14-day trial and Artist plans from $9 a month (Basic: up to 50 artworks, 2900+ room mockups, 30 design downloads a day), or $8 a month billed yearly. Its help centre says frames can float with an adjustable gap width, canvas sides can be a white, black or mirror edge, and mats can be set per side. Its Room Mockups page says art is placed on the wall to scale, and its Instant Mockups take up to three artworks at a time and return 12 room mockups. Personal Spaces, from the Standard plan ($16 a month), let you upload a photo of a client's room. A free AR app is on the App Store and Google Play. The pages we checked don't say whether artwork is ever changed by AI.

Where it is better than WallMock: an adjustable floating-frame gap, a choice of canvas edges, true scale, your clients' own rooms, more rooms (2900+) and tools for galleries and collectors. WallMock is built for listing many designs: a whole collection per run, with plans priced by images made rather than artworks stored. Our ArtPlacer alternative page has the details.

Smartist

Best for: artists who work on iPhone, iPad or Mac and want to use their own interiors.

Smartist is an app for showing art in styled interiors. On 2026-10-29 its site showed a free plan with 5 interiors whose exports carry a small watermark, and Pro at $19.99 a month, or $7.49 a month on the yearly plan, with 3000+ interiors and a 7-day trial; its help centre says the price depends on your region. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and its help centre says there is no Android version yet. Its feature list includes uploading your own space, custom frames and mats, several pieces on one wall, angle views, video mockups and augmented reality. You enter your artwork's dimensions and it shows a to-scale interior. Its help centre lists a Float frame type and a side colour for unframed art, but doesn't describe the floating gap or canvas depth, and the pages we checked don't say whether artwork is ever changed.

Where it is better than WallMock: a native Apple app, more interiors on Pro (3000+), your own spaces and sizing from the dimensions you enter. Our Smartist alternative page has the details.

WallMockup

Best for: sellers who want previews at true scale and a low monthly price.

WallMockup (wallmockup.net, a different company from WallMock) calls itself a wall mockup editor for artists, photographers and print sellers. On 2026-10-29 its home page listed 720 room scenes, 54 frame styles with adjustable thickness and an optional mat, true-scale previews in inches and centimetres, and gallery walls of up to 24 pieces in one room. It offered a 7-day trial with no credit card and up to 10 exports in any rolling 24 hours, Pro Monthly at $4.99 a month with no export cap and 10 credits a month, and a $5.99 one-time Creator pack of 100 credits that don't expire. Credits pay for AI-made rooms built around your art and HD exports; it says your original artwork stays a separate layer and is never sent to the AI model. Its bulk generator pairs one artwork with every room and frame look you pick, up to 60 results. Its canvas page says the current editor does not render gallery-wrap side edges or a dedicated floating-frame construction.

Where it is better than WallMock: true-scale sizing, the lowest monthly price listed here, large gallery walls and custom AI rooms around your art. See our WallMock vs WallMockup comparison.

Situ.art

Best for: painters, printmakers and galleries who want one work, or a small set, shown at true scale in many rooms.

On 2026-10-29 Situ.art's home page said it shows your art in 730+ rooms at once (its pricing page says 700+), with slim, box or floating frames in wood, paint or metal, an optional mount, and sets of a pair, a triptych or a small series. You enter the artwork's physical dimensions, and every plan lists scale-accurate placement. Its tool page says your work is drawn from your own image in every room, never redrawn or repainted, and AI is used only when you ask, to change the scene. Prices are in pounds: a free plan (5 downloads, 10 artwork uploads, no watermark), Artist at £9 a month (up to 30 artworks, a 7-day trial) and Pro at £29 a month, which adds uploads of your own background photos and AI scene tools; paying yearly saves 17%. It works on its website; the pages we checked mention no app, no AR and no canvas side edges, and don't state the floating-frame gap or a batch of many different artworks.

Where it is better than WallMock: true scale, more rooms, free downloads without a watermark, your own room photos on Pro and optional AI scenes.

Scaylr

Best for: sellers who batch many designs into mockup templates for many products, from prints to mugs and tees.

On 2026-10-29 Scaylr's site said it runs in your browser, opens PSD templates without Photoshop, and puts every design into every template you pick in one batch, downloadable as a ZIP sorted by design name. Its free library has 102 poster and frame scenes (black, white, oak, dark wood and silver metal frames, with or without a mat, plus unframed posters and gallery walls of 3 to 6 frames) and canvas templates including gallery wrap with a 1.25-inch mirror edge; you can also upload your own PSDs. Each design goes into the template's smart-object layer with fit, fill or stretch. The free plan allows 30 mockups a day with a watermark, with no account needed to try, and 50 without a watermark in your first 7 days. Starter is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year (1,000 mockups a month); Pro is $17.99 a month or $179.99 a year, with no monthly mockup cap and a Shopify Publisher that writes listings with AI. One-time credits start at $4.99. Its pages don't state floating frames or true scale.

Where it is better than WallMock: many product types, your own PSD templates, unframed posters, a free daily allowance and Shopify publishing on Pro.

Mockup Studio is part of the Gelato+ and Gelato+ Gold plans; you can create your first template for free, and a free Gelato account includes mockups for all products in natural-looking settings. Gelato's help centre lists Gelato+ at $29.99 a month, or $239.88 a year ($19.99 a month). Its Magic Mockups use AI to create scenes. The pages we checked don't state floating-frame gaps, canvas edges, mats, true scale or whether your artwork is changed.

Where it is better than WallMock: it sits inside Gelato's ordering and store connections, shows Gelato's own products, and needs no extra tool if you already print with Gelato. Our print on demand canvas mockups page covers Gelato's framed canvas gap.

Questions

What is the best wall art mockup generator?

It depends on what you sell and how many designs you list. ArtPlacer suits artists and galleries, Smartist suits Apple users, WallMockup and Situ.art suit sellers who need true scale, Scaylr suits batches across many product types, and Canva, Placeit and Mockey cover many products. WallMock suits print-on-demand sellers who want their exact art as framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed prints in curated rooms, a whole collection at once.

What is the best tool for framed canvas mockups for a print-on-demand shop?

Pick one that shows the canvas your provider ships: a floating frame with its gap, or a stretched canvas with its edge. WallMock is built for this: framed canvas in 7 floating-frame finishes and stretched canvas, for a whole collection at once, with your file placed as it is. ArtPlacer also shows floating frames with an adjustable gap and white, black or mirror canvas edges, at true scale, but its Instant Mockups take up to three artworks at a time.

What is the best floating frame canvas mockup generator?

One that draws the gap between the canvas and the frame. On 2026-10-29, ArtPlacer let you adjust the gap width, Smartist and Situ.art listed floating frames without stating the gap, and WallMockup said its current editor doesn't render a floating-frame construction. WallMock shows the gap in black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver.

What is the best mockup generator for Printify or Gelato canvas wall art?

Use your provider's own mockups for the product details, then add room photos that match the frame your provider ships. Gelato's Mockup Studio is designed for wall art and is part of Gelato+; Printify's mockups are free for its products; WallMock adds curated rooms for framed canvas and stretched canvas across a whole collection. Check your provider's frame colour and gap before you pick a finish.

How can I make mockups for a whole collection of wall art at once?

Use a batch tool that puts every design into every scene you choose. Scaylr does this with PSD templates for many products, with 30 watermarked mockups a day free; WallMock does it in curated wall art rooms, with favourite rooms and frame looks saved once. ArtPlacer's Instant Mockups take up to three artworks per run, and WallMockup's bulk generator pairs one artwork with up to 60 room-and-frame looks.

Is there a free wall art mockup generator?

Several have a free option: WallMock gives free watermarked previews with no sign-up, and a free account adds 10 watermark-free downloads (1024 px) every 30 days; Situ.art (5 downloads, no watermark), Smartist, Mockey and Canva have free plans; Scaylr allows 30 watermarked mockups a day; WallMockup has a 7-day trial with no card; and Printify, Printful and Gelato make mockups of their own products with a free account.

Which mockup generators show true scale?

On their own pages: WallMockup (inches and centimetres), Situ.art (scale-accurate placement from the dimensions you enter), ArtPlacer (art placed to scale) and Smartist (to-scale interiors from your dimensions). WallMock takes your real artwork size in centimetres or inches and draws it against an estimated wall scale in 332 rooms, with the range shown; the scale is estimated from the room photo, not measured, and other rooms use a reviewed size.

Which tools let me use my own room photo?

ArtPlacer's Personal Spaces (from its Standard plan), Smartist, and Situ.art's Pro plan let you upload a photo of a room. WallMock uses its own 500+ curated rooms.

Which mockup tools keep my artwork exactly as it is?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Situ.art says your work is never redrawn or repainted, and WallMockup says your artwork stays a separate layer that is never sent to its AI model; the other pages we checked don't say.

Is WallMock the same as WallMockup?

No. WallMock (wallmock.com) is not WallMockup (wallmockup.net). They are different products from different companies.

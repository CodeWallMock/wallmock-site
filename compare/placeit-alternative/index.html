Who each tool suits

Placeit suits

Shops and marketers who need many kinds of images from one subscription: mockups for apparel, digital devices, print and promotional products, plus logos, design templates and videos.

WallMock suits

Sellers of framed canvas, stretched canvas and framed prints on Etsy or Shopify, including print-on-demand shops, who list many designs and want every one shown in the same favourite rooms.

Both can work

If wall art is one line among many products, you might keep Placeit for merch and use WallMock for the wall art listings.

Where WallMock is different

Your exact art, never redrawn

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Text, signatures, fine lines and colours stay as you made them, so the photo matches the print your buyer receives.

Curated rooms

Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock. They are chosen for showing wall art, from living rooms and bedrooms to offices and nurseries.

Favourite rooms, whole collections

Set up the rooms and frame you like once, add a whole collection, and WallMock makes every artwork in every chosen room, so a whole shop looks consistent. Pairs, trios and sets of four go on one wall.

Why exact art matters: if a mockup tool repaints or "improves" your file, the listing can show colours or lettering your buyer will not receive. Placing the file as it is avoids that.

When Placeit is the better choice

  • You sell more than wall art. Placeit has mockups for apparel, digital devices, print and promotional products in one place; WallMock only shows art on walls.
  • You need logos, videos or design templates too. Placeit offers design templates, logos and videos; WallMock does not.
  • You already publish through Printify. Printify's help centre points to Placeit for extra mockups.
  • You make a few mockups now and then. If you list one design a month, a general tool you already pay for may be enough.

How to switch from Placeit

  1. Gather your original art files. Use the full-size files you send to your printer or print-on-demand provider, not old mockup downloads. Downloads you made in Placeit stay yours after you cancel.
  2. Create a free account at app.wallmock.com with Google or an email code.
  3. Pick favourite rooms and a frame once: the product you sell, such as a framed canvas or a framed print with a mat.
  4. Add a collection. Check the free watermarked previews, then download clean listing images with a plan.
  5. Cancel what you no longer use. Check Placeit's terms first: its FAQ notes a one-month minimum once you have downloaded a template.

Questions

What is a good Placeit alternative for wall art?

If you sell framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed prints and want your exact artwork in curated rooms for a whole collection, WallMock is built for that one job. If you also need logos, videos and mockups for apparel or devices, Placeit's broad library may still suit you better.

Is there a free alternative to Placeit?

WallMock gives free watermarked previews in every room; clean images need a plan, from $5 a month. Placeit also has some free templates; see its site for what they include.

How much does Placeit cost?

Placeit sells one subscription with no download limit. On 2026-10-26 its pricing page showed it from $7.47 a month, billed yearly in US dollars. Check Placeit's pricing page for today's price before you decide.

Does WallMock change my artwork?

No. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. That matters for art with lettering, signatures or exact brand colours.

Can I make mockups for many designs at once?

Yes, in WallMock. Save your favourite rooms and frame once, add a whole collection, and each artwork is made in every chosen room.

Can I use WallMock today?

Anyone can try free watermarked previews with no sign-up, then create a free account with Google or an email code.

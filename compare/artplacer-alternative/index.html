Who each tool suits

ArtPlacer suits

Artists, galleries and art advisors who sell original work to collectors and want exhibitions, art fair planning, website widgets and contact management alongside room mockups.

Where WallMock is different

Your exact art, never redrawn

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Text, signatures, fine lines and colours stay as you made them, so the photo matches the print your buyer receives.

Curated rooms

Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock. They are chosen for showing wall art, from living rooms and bedrooms to offices and nurseries.

Favourite rooms, whole collections

Set up the rooms and frame you like once, add a whole collection, and WallMock makes every artwork in every chosen room, so a whole shop looks consistent. Pairs, trios and sets of four go on one wall.

Why it matters at volume: a shop with 300 designs needs the same rooms for every listing. In WallMock you set the rooms once and add the collection; plans count images made, not artworks stored.

When ArtPlacer is the better choice

  • You sell originals to collectors. ArtPlacer's Personal Spaces places art in a collector's own home at real scale, and its virtual exhibitions show a body of work.
  • You run a gallery or advise clients. Gallery plans, presentations, contact management and an art show planner are built for that work.
  • You want mockups on your own website. ArtPlacer offers widgets for websites and online stores.

How to switch from ArtPlacer

  1. Export your art files. Use your full-size originals, the same files you send to your printer or print-on-demand provider.
  2. Create a free account at app.wallmock.com with Google or an email code.
  3. Choose the product you sell (framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed print) and save your favourite rooms once.
  4. Add the whole collection. Review the free watermarked previews, then download clean listing images with a plan.
  5. Keep what you still need. If you use ArtPlacer for exhibitions or collectors, you can run both.

Questions

Is there an ArtPlacer alternative for Etsy sellers?

Yes. WallMock is built for print-on-demand and wall art sellers on Etsy and Shopify who list many designs and want each one shown as a framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed print in curated rooms. ArtPlacer is a wider marketing platform for artists, galleries and art advisors.

How much does ArtPlacer cost?

On 2026-10-26 ArtPlacer's Artist plans were Basic $9, Standard $16 and Plus $27 a month, or $96, $168 and $276 a year ($8, $14 and $23 a month) billed yearly. Gallery plans cost more. Check its pricing page for today's prices.

How many artworks does ArtPlacer's Instant Mockups take?

ArtPlacer's Instant Mockups page says you select up to three artworks and get 12 room mockups, with a Refresh button for a new set of rooms. In WallMock, you add a whole collection and every artwork is made in every room you chose.

Does ArtPlacer limit how many artworks I can store?

Yes, by plan. On its pricing page, Artist plans hold up to 50, 100 or 500 artworks. WallMock plans are priced by images made each month instead.

Does WallMock change my artwork?

No. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI.

Can I use WallMock today?

Anyone can try free watermarked previews with no sign-up, then create a free account with Google or an email code.

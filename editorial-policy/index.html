Editorial policy

How we write our guides

Who writes them, where the facts come from, how AI tools are used, and how we fix mistakes.

By AK · Updated 21 November 2026

WallMock is a small company run by AK, an Etsy wall-art seller since 2023. Our guides answer questions sellers ask us, or ones we hit in our own shop. We draft with the help of AI writing tools. Platform rules, supplier specs and prices are checked against the platform's or supplier's own page, with the date we checked it next to the source; design advice, such as hanging heights, is our own. Room images are actual WallMock outputs. AK decides what is published.

We sell the product these guides mention

WallMock is our product, so we are not neutral when it comes up. We say where it fits and where it doesn't, and comparison pages show other tools' prices and features as their own sites state them, with the date. We take no payment for placements and use no affiliate links.

How a guide is made

  1. The question. It comes from sellers who write to us, from search data, or from our own listings.
  2. The sources. Etsy, Shopify and supplier help pages come first, because their rules and product sizes are what a listing has to match. Each source is listed at the end of the guide with the date we read it.
  3. The draft. We use AI writing tools to help draft and tidy the text. They are not a source: a fact that can't be traced to a page we read, or to something we did ourselves, is cut.
  4. The images. Mockups are made in WallMock from the artwork file shown, and the caption says so. We don't use stock photos of rooms.
  5. The check. Before a guide goes live, numbers, prices and limits are checked again against those sources. AK decides what is published.

What we test ourselves

Where a guide reports our own measured check, such as the exact-art test, the method is on the testing page, along with what that check does not tell you. Our November 2026 review of where art sits in each WallMock room was done by eye, room by room; the placement guide sets out the rules it produced. Things only a physical sample can show, such as print colour and frame finish, we tell you to check with a sample.

Updates and corrections

Each guide shows the date it was last updated. When Etsy or a supplier changes a rule or a price, we update the guide and the date. To report a mistake, email hello@wallmock.com.

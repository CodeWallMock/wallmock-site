Metal print or aluminum framed print: which should you show?

Both are metal, but they are different products. Show the one you ship.

  • Metal print: the artwork is printed on the aluminium panel itself. No frame, no mat, and no glass. It suits bold colour, photography and modern rooms.
  • Aluminum framed print: a paper print behind a slim aluminium frame in black, gold or silver, with or without a mat. See metal frame mockups.

The metal print vs metal frame guide compares the two side by side.

Match your provider's metal print

Metal prints vary between print-on-demand providers. Before you make the photo, check these on your provider's product page, then order one sample:

  1. Finish: gloss, matte or brushed metal. WallMock shows Gloss (the default), High gloss or Matte, not brushed metal.
  2. Edges and corners: rounded or square corners, and whether the edge is bare metal.
  3. Hanging: a block or frame on the back that floats the panel off the wall, or a flat mount.
  4. Sizes and shape: the ratios offered. WallMock keeps your file's own shape, from square to 1:3 tall.

When the sample arrives, hold it next to your mockup. Our sample check guide lists what to compare.

What is a metal print mockup?

A room preview of your artwork on a metal panel, usually aluminum. It shows buyers the frameless look, the thin edge and how the panel sits off the wall, before they order.

Is High gloss or Matte better for metal print listings?

Neither is better on its own: show the surface your provider sells. High gloss shows a clear window reflection, which reads best on skies and dark backgrounds. Matte shows the art with no sheen and low glare. Gloss, the default, sits between them with a soft sheen on the side the light comes from.

Which metal print surfaces does WallMock offer?

Three. Gloss is the default, with a natural, gentle sheen on the side the room light comes from. High gloss shows a clear reflection of the room's window, like a high-gloss aluminium panel. Matte has no sheen, so the artwork reads as it does on canvas. Choose in the Studio's Metal print panel (Surface: Gloss | High gloss | Matte). WallMock does not show brushed metal: if your provider sells it, say so in the listing, and check your sample against the photo.

What a metal print looks like in WallMock

A metal print is your art printed on a thin aluminium panel: no frame, small rounded corners and a thin bare-metal edge, hung on a hidden block about ½ in (12.7 mm) off the wall so it casts a soft, tight shadow. The surface is gloss white with a gentle sheen.

No frame

The art is printed on a thin aluminium panel, 1.14 mm thick. Nothing covers its edges: no frame and no mat.

Rounded corners, thin edge

Small 3.2 mm (⅛ in) rounded corners and a thin bare-metal edge, the details that tell a buyer it is metal.

Floats off the wall

A hidden block holds the panel about ½ in (12.7 mm) from the wall, so it casts a soft, tight shadow.

A gentle gloss

The surface is gloss white with a gentle sheen, easiest to see on dark art. Your artwork stays exactly as your file.

Metal print or aluminum framed print?

Both are metal, but they are different products. Show the one you ship.

  • Metal print: the artwork is printed on the aluminium panel itself. No frame, no mat, and no glass. It suits bold colour, photography and modern rooms.
  • Aluminum framed print: a paper print behind a slim aluminium frame in black, gold or silver, with or without a mat. See metal frame mockups.

Match your provider's metal print

Metal prints vary between print-on-demand providers. Before you make the photo, check these on your provider's product page, then order one sample:

  1. Finish: gloss, matte or brushed metal. WallMock shows a gloss white surface.
  2. Edges and corners: rounded or square corners, and whether the edge is bare metal.
  3. Hanging: a block or frame on the back that floats the panel off the wall, or a flat mount.
  4. Sizes and shape: the ratios offered. WallMock keeps your file's own shape, from square to 1:3 tall.

When the sample arrives, hold it next to your mockup. Our sample check guide lists what to compare.

Questions

What is a metal print mockup?

A photo of your artwork printed on a metal panel, usually aluminium, hanging on a room wall. It shows buyers the frameless look, the thin edge and how the panel sits off the wall, before they order.

Does WallMock make metal print mockups?

Yes. In the Studio, choose Metal, then Metal print. A metal print is your art printed on a thin aluminium panel: no frame, small rounded corners and a thin bare-metal edge, hung on a hidden block about ½ in (12.7 mm) off the wall so it casts a soft, tight shadow. The surface is gloss white with a gentle sheen.

Can I show a metal print in a set of 2, 3 or 4?

Not yet. Metal prints are made in WallMock's rooms, one artwork per image. They are not available in sets yet. Sets work with canvas and framed prints.

Is my artwork changed?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. The panel's corners, edge and shadow are drawn around your file; the art itself comes from your file.

What is the difference between a metal print and an aluminum framed print?

A metal print is the art itself printed on an aluminium panel, with no frame and no mat. An aluminum framed print is a paper print behind a slim aluminium frame, in black, gold or silver, with or without a mat. Show the one your provider ships.

Glossy or matte?

WallMock shows the panel with a gloss white surface and a gentle sheen. If your provider sells a matte or brushed-metal finish instead, say so in the listing, and check your sample against the photo.

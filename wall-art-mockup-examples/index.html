Questions

Are these real WallMock results?

Yes. Every image on this page was made with WallMock from the artwork file as supplied, and checked against that file. Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock.

Can I zoom in?

Yes. Click any image or "Full size" to open a 2400 px version and look at the frame corners, the floating-frame gap and the canvas edge.

What size are the images I download?

Clean images are 4096 px on the long side, large enough to crop for Etsy, Shopify, Pinterest and Instagram.

Which frame colours are there?

Black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver, for floating-frame canvas and framed prints.

Is the artwork changed?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI.

What makes a framed canvas look like a framed canvas

Buyers decide what they are ordering from the photo. Three details separate a floating-frame canvas from every other product.

The gap

A floating frame holds the canvas from behind with a few millimetres of space all round. From the front it reads as a thin dark line. No gap, and the photo looks like a framed print.

No glass, no mat

The canvas surface sits open in the frame. A white border or reflections belong to framed prints, not framed canvas.

The frame colour you ship

Show the finish the buyer will receive: black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold or silver. One wrong colour in the photo is a return waiting to happen.

Questions

What is a framed canvas mockup?

A listing photo that shows your artwork as a stretched canvas inside a frame, hanging in a room. For most print-on-demand sellers that frame is a floating frame, so the mockup should show the narrow gap between the canvas edge and the frame.

What is a floating frame for canvas?

A floating frame, or floater frame, is an open frame that holds a stretched canvas from behind with a small gap all round. From the front you see a thin line of shadow between canvas and frame, so the canvas seems to float. It has no glass and no mat.

Is a framed canvas the same as a floating frame canvas?

Usually, in print on demand. Prodigi calls its framed canvas a floater frame with a 5 mm gap, Gelato states a 12 mm gap between canvas and frame, and Printful's framed canvas reviews describe a floater frame. Check your own provider's product page, because some frames sit tight against the canvas.

Do floating frames have glass?

No. A floating frame shows the canvas surface directly, with no glass, glazing or mat. If your product has glass and a mat, it is a framed print; use the frame mockup generator instead.

Which frame colours can I show?

black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver, so you can match the finish your provider ships. Custom frame colours are also available.

What size are WallMock's images?

Short answer: Clean images are 4096 px on the long side, large enough to crop for Etsy, Shopify, Pinterest and Instagram. Free previews are smaller and watermarked.

Etsy recommends listing photos at least 2000 px on the shortest side, so a 4096 px image leaves room to crop. See the crop-safe Etsy thumbnail and full-size examples.

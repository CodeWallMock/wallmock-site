WallMock help

How do I sign in to WallMock?

Short answer: Use Continue with Google, or enter your email and type the 8-digit code WallMock emails you. There is no password. A code stops working after 10 minutes or 3 wrong tries; ask for a new one from the sign-in page.

Inside Instagram, Facebook or TikTok's own browser, Google sign-in doesn't work; use the email code, or open the page in Safari or Chrome. Sign in at app.wallmock.com.

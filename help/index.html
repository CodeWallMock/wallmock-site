Questions

How much does WallMock cost?

Lite $5 a month for 200 clean 4K (4096 px) images; Pro $12 and Studio $24 a month for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Paying yearly costs $39, $89, $179. Watermarked previews in every room are free, and you can try WallMock with no sign-up and no card.

Can I try WallMock for free?

Yes. Try WallMock free with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews made on your device. With a free account you also get watermarked previews in every room, so you can check the look before you choose a plan.

What files can I upload to WallMock?

JPEG, PNG or WebP artwork files, one at a time or a whole collection. Each file keeps its own proportions, from square to 3:1 panoramas and 1:3 tall pieces, and is never stretched or cropped to fit a room.

What size are WallMock's images?

Clean images download in 4K: 4096 px on the long side, high quality and large enough to crop for Etsy, Shopify, Pinterest and Instagram. Free previews are smaller and watermarked.

Does WallMock change my artwork?

No. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Lettering, signatures, colours and proportions stay exactly as they are in your file.

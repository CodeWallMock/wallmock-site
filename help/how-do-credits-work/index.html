How do WallMock credits work?

Short answer: One credit is one successful clean, full-size image. Watermarked previews are free. Failed, refused and cancelled images never use credits, downloading again is free, and making the same image twice isn't charged twice.

Every plan adds its credits each month, annual plans included. Credits you don't use end with their month; they don't roll over. The plans: Lite $5 a month (200 clean images), Pro $12 a month (5,000 clean images), Studio $24 a month (10,000 clean images).

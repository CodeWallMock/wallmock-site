How do WallMock credits work?

Short answer: On Lite, one credit is one successful clean 4K image, 200 a month. Pro and Studio include unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server (600 on Studio). Watermarked previews are free, failed or cancelled images never count, and downloading again is free.

Lite adds its credits each month, annual plans included; credits you don't use end with their month and don't roll over. The plans: Lite $5 a month for 200 clean 4K (4096 px) images; Pro $12 and Studio $24 a month for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. On Lite, each month's credits end with that month. Pro and Studio have no monthly image count, under the fair-use limit.

Are previews charged?

No. Watermarked previews, including the live preview while you set up a room, are free.

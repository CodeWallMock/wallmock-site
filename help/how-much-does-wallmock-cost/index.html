How much does WallMock cost?

Short answer: Lite $5 USD a month for 200 clean 4K (4096 px) images; Pro $15 USD a month for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Paying yearly costs $39, $144. From 1 Jan 2027, new subscribers pay Lite $9 a month or $79 a year and Pro $19 a month or $179 a year. Subscribe before then and keep today's price for as long as you stay subscribed. Free accounts and Try Free include 50 previews every 30 days (only Try Free previews are watermarked), a free account gets 10 watermark-free 4K (4096 px) downloads every 30 days, and you can try WallMock with no sign-up and no card. Paid plans have unlimited previews.

Every paid plan includes the eligible rooms and products, with clean 4K (4096 px) downloads. Pro includes unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: paid plans can open any room in the library. Up to 300 images a day can be made on WallMock's server; images made in your browser, on your device, do not count toward that limit. Reference prices are USD. Pricing cards and the app set USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR or BRL automatically from your connection; existing subscriptions keep their billing currency.

From 1 January 2027 new subscribers pay Lite $9 USD a month or $79 a year, Pro $19 a month or $179 a year, and Studio $29 a month or $279 a year; subscribe before then and keep your price for as long as you stay subscribed.

See the pricing page for the plan details and the refund policy.

Is there a free plan?

Free account: 50 previews every 30 days on your device, with no watermark, in your included rooms; paid plans have unlimited previews. Usage safeguards and device limits apply. You get 10 watermark-free 4K (4096 px) downloads every 30 days; unused downloads do not carry over. The first 10 free downloads over your account's lifetime can use all 500+ rooms; after that, 8 free rooms remain. When the 10 downloads are used, previews continue in your included rooms, up to 50 every 30 days. Refills do not reopen the full library. Free downloads are for single artworks. Sets and listing kits need a paid plan; listing videos need Pro. Send to Shopify is in limited beta on Pro and the WallMock Studio plan; when it opens it will be on every plan. Paid plans add more images: Lite 200 a month, Pro unlimited. Try WallMock free with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews made on your device.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel in Billing & plans, then Manage subscription. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

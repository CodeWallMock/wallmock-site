How much does WallMock cost?

Short answer: Lite $5 a month for 200 clean 4K (4096 px) images; Pro $12 and Studio $24 a month for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Paying yearly costs $39, $89, $179. Watermarked previews are free, a free account gets 10 watermark-free downloads (1024 px) every 30 days, and you can try WallMock with no sign-up and no card.

Every plan includes every room, frame and product, with clean 4K (4096 px) downloads. Pro and Studio include unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server (600 on Studio). Prices in USD; the app shows local prices in EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR and BRL.

See the pricing page for the plan details and the refund policy.

Yes. Cancel in Billing & plans, then Manage subscription. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

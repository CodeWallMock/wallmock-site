WallMock help

Does WallMock change my artwork?

Short answer: No. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Lettering, signatures, colours and proportions stay exactly as they are in your file.

The room, frame, mat and shadows are drawn around your artwork; the artwork itself is placed as supplied. Resizing to the room, perspective and image compression affect how it is presented, never what it is. Read why your art must never be redrawn.

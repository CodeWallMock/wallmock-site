Refund policy

WallMock refund policy

WallMock · Alberta, Canada · Updated 4 November 2026

Short answer: Cancel any time and your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

  • First subscription: ask within 7 days of your first payment and we refund the unused days.
  • Renewals aren't refundable; cancel before your renewal date.
  • Duplicate or mistaken charges are always refunded in full.
  • Failed images never use credits.
  • Free credits and rewards have no cash value.

Cancel any time

Cancel yourself in Billing & plans (Manage subscription). Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then stops renewing. Cancelling doesn't refund the current period.

7-day refund on your first subscription

If WallMock isn't right for you, email hello@wallmock.com from the address you sign in with, within 7 days of your first WallMock payment (monthly or annual). We refund the unused days of that period: what you paid, divided by the days in the period, times the days left. The day you paid counts as a used day. This applies once per customer, to your first subscription.

Examples (USD). Pro monthly, $15 for a 30-day month, asked on day 3: 27 unused days, $13.50 refunded. Pro annual, $144 for 365 days, asked on day 5: 360 unused days, $142.03 refunded.

The refund isn't available once more than 100 clean images have been downloaded, or 100 or more different rooms have been opened, in that period. When a refund is made, the plan ends straight away and its remaining credits are removed. Clean images you've already downloaded keep their commercial-use licence.

Renewals

A renewal pays for a new period, so renewals (monthly or annual) aren't refundable; cancel before the renewal date if you don't want it. If a renewal happened by accident and you haven't downloaded any clean images since it renewed, email us within 7 days of it and we'll refund it in full and cancel the plan.

Duplicate charges, mistakes and service problems

  • Duplicate or mistaken charges are refunded in full. Send us the date and amount.
  • Failed images: an image that fails, is cancelled or can't be confirmed never uses a credit. If you think a credit was used wrongly, tell us and we'll check and return it.
  • Service problems: if WallMock doesn't work for you for a long stretch because of a fault on our side, write to us and we'll put it right with credits or a refund for the affected time.
  • If we stop offering WallMock, or close your account for no fault of yours, we refund the unused days of your current paid period.
  • Not refunded: plan changes (upgrades, downgrades, monthly to annual) in cash; free, promotional, invite and reward credits, which have no cash value; and a plan on an account closed for breaking the terms or for payment or refund abuse.

How to ask, and how long it takes

Email hello@wallmock.com from the address you sign in with. Tell us the payment date and amount and, if you like, what didn't work for you; it helps us improve. We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, within 15 days of approval; your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it. We never ask for your card number.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't take away any right to cancel or get a refund that the law gives you, for example under Alberta's Consumer Protection Act. If you live in the EU or the UK, you have a 14-day right to cancel a new subscription. WallMock starts as soon as you subscribe, so by subscribing you ask us to start straight away; if you cancel under that right, you pay for the days you've had and we refund the rest. Where your law gives you more, we follow the law.

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank: we may pause your account while a dispute is open, and we can usually refund faster ourselves. See Chargebacks and payment disputes.

Purchases made before 4 November 2026 keep the refund rule that applied when they were made.

Questions

Does WallMock give refunds?

Yes, on a first subscription. Within 7 days of your first payment, WallMock refunds the unused days of that period, unless more than 100 clean images have been downloaded or 100 or more different rooms have been opened.

How is the refund worked out?

By days: what you paid, divided by the days in the period, times the days left. The day you paid counts as used. For example, Pro monthly at $15 for a 30-day month, asked on day 3, refunds $13.50.

Are renewals refundable?

No. Cancel any time before your renewal date. An accidental renewal with no clean downloads since it renewed is refunded in full if you write within 7 days.

How do I ask for a refund?

Email hello@wallmock.com from the address you sign in with, with the payment date and amount. Refunds go back to your card through Stripe.

Testing and examples

What we check before a listing image goes out

Start with the problem the image needs to solve. Our published artwork comparison checks detail against source files. Size and crop tools answer numerical questions. A physical sample checks print colour, material and frame finish. Each has a different job; the examples below show how to use the right check before publishing a listing.

Small lettering and fine lines stay part of the artwork

The recorded example set compares 68 placed panels from 30 artwork files across 61 room images. It includes single pieces and sets. The renderer’s reports and a separate internal comparison check detail and colour values against the source files, with a flipped-image control to test whether a similar-looking picture could be mistaken for the original.

Read the methods and results, or inspect the measurement ledger. These are historical example measurements from October 2026, not a fresh test of every current download. A source-file comparison does not establish physical print colour or supplier-frame accuracy.

A panorama needs a shape check before a room check

A 6000 × 2000 px file has a 3:1 ratio. A 36 × 12 inch print has the same ratio, so it needs no composition crop. A 30 × 12 inch product is 5:2; filling that shape with the same file requires a crop or a border. The ratio calculator makes the difference visible before you choose a room.

Measure the artwork opening in a front-on view. In an angled photograph, perspective changes the visible width and height; the image-plane bounding box is not a ratio test. See panoramic and tall artwork for room choices and crop checks.

The frame is part of what the buyer orders

Match the product family first: stretched canvas, floating-frame canvas, framed paper print or metal panel. Then compare face width, gap, depth, mat and finish with the specific product your provider ships. Premium proportions is the default visual setting. Frame width and Floating gap adjust the visual proportions. Check exact gap, depth and finish against your provider’s specifications.

A walnut swatch on a screen is not a stained-wood sample. Use a sample and side-by-side photos for that decision, and keep the sample date and provider product with your listing records. Print colour and screen colour explains the other part of the comparison.

A collection needs an output count and a final review

We also ran a collection through the live Studio: three source files, three rooms and nine verified Free-plan downloads. The walkthrough includes the actual ZIP, file checks and the room-access limit encountered.

Four separate artworks in five compatible rooms make 20 single-artwork images. A pair of artworks together in those five rooms makes five set images. Check the planned count and unavailable combinations before generating; review the finished files and download them before refreshing the session.

That is a worked example, not a timed run. The bulk checklist explains saved choices, representative previews and grouped downloads. Paid plans include clean 4K (4096 px) downloads. Free accounts include 10 watermark-free 1K (1024 px) single-artwork downloads every 30 days.

Check the listing after the images leave WallMock

Open the marketplace draft and select each size, frame and mat option. Check which image the buyer sees, the thumbnail crop and the written dimensions. Repeat after a provider sync or a listing update. Our listing proof checklist walks through this final step.

Our calculators have automated checks for their documented examples, and release checks compare public plan facts with the app’s offers. Those checks cover calculations and commercial terms; they are separate from artwork measurements, physical samples and customer outcomes.

Questions

What has WallMock measured?

The published historical example set contains 61 room images, 30 source artworks and 68 placed panels. Recorded renderer and separate internal checks compare the artwork with its source. The ledger identifies the example bytes and comparison methods.

Does the artwork test prove the frame matches my supplier?

No. It compares artwork regions. Use the supplier’s current dimensions and a physical sample to check frame construction, finish, mat and canvas depth.

Does a worked example count as a timed collection test?

No. Multiplying artworks by compatible rooms explains the expected output count. A performance result also needs the actual files, device, browser, app version, completed outputs and elapsed time.

Can you show that these mockups increase sales?

We have not published a controlled sales study. The founder’s shop experience explains the product decisions; it does not establish a conversion lift for another shop.

Why do sellers' mockups matter to a print lab?

Your sellers' listings are the first time a buyer sees your product. Many sellers make room photos with generic templates: a frame of the wrong width or colour, no depth, no gap around a floating canvas, no shadow. If the listing shows a different frame from the one you ship, buyers can be surprised when the order arrives.

Mockups made from your own frame range keep the listing and the parcel in step, and save each seller from building their own templates.

How does a print lab pilot work?

  1. Talk to us

    Tell us what you make, where your sellers list and roughly how many images they need. We say honestly what a pilot can and cannot cover today.

  2. Share your range

    Send the frames you want to start with: profile, face width, depth, finish, mat options and product photos. A pilot starts with a handful of frames.

  3. Judge the samples

    We build presets in WallMock's renderer and send sample images made with an artwork you choose. You compare them with your own product photos.

  4. Connect

    If the frames look right to you, we agree how images reach your sellers: the API (early access), a partner entry, or store delivery. Terms are agreed per partner.

How are sellers' files handled?

  • Exact art. Placed as supplied, never redrawn, with an exact-art check on every room photo.
  • Deleted after processing. Artworks are deleted after processing and never used for training.
  • Signed result links. Finished images are fetched from signed links, not public URLs.
  • Sandbox first. Build and test your integration against watermarked images.

Questions

Can a print lab give its sellers mockups of its own frames?

That is what WallMock's partner frame catalogue is for, and it is in development. In a partner pilot we build presets from your frame specifications and product photos and check them with you before sellers see them. To discuss a pilot, talk to us.

Is there a white-label mockup API for print labs?

Not yet from WallMock. White-label output is planned for partner agreements, and the API is in early access, not available yet. In the current build, live images carry no WallMock watermark, and sandbox images are watermarked.

Which products can WallMock show?

Floating canvas, stretched canvas, framed prints with or without a mat, metal prints in Gloss, High gloss or Matte, and aluminium frames in black, gold or silver.

Does it keep the seller's art exact?

Yes. The artwork is placed as supplied and never redrawn. Every room photo gets an exact-art check against the uploaded file, and the result comes back with each image.

Does WallMock connect to our print-on-demand integration?

WallMock makes the images; it does not place orders. Through the API (early access), your system can request images and receive them by webhook and signed links. For sellers, Send to Shopify (beta) adds images to their Shopify products, and Send to Etsy is coming soon.

What happens to sellers' artwork?

Artworks are deleted after processing and never used for training.

How many images can it make?

In development tests, about 3 to 5 seconds per 4K image per worker. Production throughput is measured in a pilot.

What does it cost a print lab?

Prices are not published. Pilot terms are agreed with each partner, and the API will be pay per image, with volume plans.

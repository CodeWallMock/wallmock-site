Shopify integration for wall art mockups · Beta

Send to Shopify: mockups onto your products, without re-uploading.

Send to Shopify is WallMock's native Shopify integration for wall art sellers, in beta. Connect your Shopify store and your WallMock mockups go straight onto your products, hero first, with no downloading or re-uploading. It asks for product-edit permission only and never deletes your photos. It is included in Pro (1 store, up to 10 products a month) and the WallMock Studio plan (up to 3 stores, unlimited products). It is not in the Shopify App Store; you connect your store from WallMock.

How does Send to Shopify work?

  1. Choose a plan

    Pro for 1 store and up to 10 products a month, or the WallMock Studio plan for up to 3 stores with unlimited products.

  2. Connect your store

    Approve one permission: editing products. WallMock asks for nothing else.

  3. Send your mockups

    Images are added to your Shopify products, hero first. Your existing photos stay; nothing is deleted.

Does it work with print-on-demand products?

Send to Shopify adds images to the products in your Shopify store. It does not connect to your print provider. If a print-on-demand app created or manages a product, check that app's publish settings so a later sync does not replace your room photos, and keep your original image files.

What does it never touch?

  • Your existing photos: never deleted.
  • Orders, customers and payments: no access asked; the only permission is editing products.
  • Your artwork: placed as supplied, never redrawn.

Questions

Is there a Shopify app that adds wall art mockups to my products automatically?

Send to Shopify does this, in beta: connect your Shopify store and your WallMock mockups go straight onto your products, hero first, with no downloading or re-uploading. It is included in Pro and the WallMock Studio plan and is not in the Shopify App Store; see plans.

Can WallMock upload mockups to Shopify automatically?

With Send to Shopify, in beta on Pro and the WallMock Studio plan: your mockups go from WallMock onto your Shopify products without downloading and re-uploading them.

Will Send to Shopify delete or replace my product photos?

No. It only adds images. It never deletes your photos.

What Shopify permission does Send to Shopify ask for?

Only permission to edit products, which it needs to add images to them.

In what order are the images added?

Hero first, followed by the rest of your images.

Does Send to Shopify work with print-on-demand products?

It adds images to the products in your Shopify store and does not connect to your print provider. If a print-on-demand app manages a product, check that app's publish settings so a later sync does not replace your photos.

Which plan includes Send to Shopify?

Pro includes 1 store and up to 10 products a month. The WallMock Studio plan includes up to 3 stores with unlimited products. A product counts once in your plan month, when it first gets an image; sending again to the same product does not count. You can change stores 2 times a month. Lite and Free do not include Send to Shopify.

Is Send to Shopify in the Shopify App Store?

No. It is in beta on Pro and the WallMock Studio plan, and it is not listed in the Shopify App Store. You connect your store from WallMock.

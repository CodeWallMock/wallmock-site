Send to Shopify: mockups onto your products, without re-uploading.

Send to Shopify is WallMock's native Shopify integration for wall art sellers, in beta testing. It adds your WallMock mockups straight onto your Shopify products, hero first. It asks for product-edit permission only and never deletes your photos. It is not generally available and is not in the Shopify App Store; request beta access by email. When it launches, Pro includes 1 store and up to 25 products a month, and the WallMock Studio plan (coming soon) includes 5 stores with unlimited products.

Does it work with print-on-demand products?

Send to Shopify adds images to the products in your Shopify store. It does not connect to your print provider. If a print-on-demand app created or manages a product, check that app's publish settings so a later sync does not replace your room photos, and keep your original image files.

What does it never touch?

  • Your existing photos: never deleted.
  • Orders, customers and payments: no access asked; the only permission is editing products.
  • Your artwork: placed as supplied, never redrawn.

Questions

Is there a Shopify app that adds wall art mockups to my products automatically?

Send to Shopify does this, in beta testing: WallMock mockups go straight onto your Shopify products, hero first. It is not generally available and not in the Shopify App Store; request beta access.

Can WallMock upload mockups to Shopify automatically?

With Send to Shopify, in beta testing by request and not generally available: your mockups go from WallMock onto your Shopify products without downloading and re-uploading them.

Will Send to Shopify delete or replace my product photos?

No. It only adds images. It never deletes your photos.

What Shopify permission does Send to Shopify ask for?

Only permission to edit products, which it needs to add images to them.

In what order are the images added?

Hero first, followed by the rest of your images.

Does Send to Shopify work with print-on-demand products?

It adds images to the products in your Shopify store and does not connect to your print provider. If a print-on-demand app manages a product, check that app's publish settings so a later sync does not replace your photos.

Which plan will include Send to Shopify?

Planned: when Send to Shopify launches, Pro includes 1 store and up to 25 products a month, and the WallMock Studio plan (coming soon) includes 5 stores with unlimited products. During the beta, access is by request. The WallMock Studio plan has no price yet.

Is Send to Shopify in the Shopify App Store?

No. It is in beta testing with sellers who request access, and it is not listed in the Shopify App Store.

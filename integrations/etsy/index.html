What can you automate on Etsy today?

The slow part of Etsy listing photos is making them, not uploading them. The WallMock web app makes every artwork in every room you saved, in one run, so a collection's photos come out together and look consistent.

Five photos of one artwork in different frames, made with the WallMock API pilot (early access, not yet available) from founder AK's own artwork. The web app makes the same kind of room photos today. Open any image for a larger view.

  1. Save your rooms and frames once. Heart the rooms that suit your shop.
  2. Upload the collection. Each artwork keeps its own shape and is never redrawn.
  3. Download the images. On paid plans, the listing kit adds crops for a 4:3 Etsy first photo, alt text and a listing checklist.
  4. Upload in Etsy's listing editor. This is the step Send to Etsy is planned to remove.

Why isn't Send to Etsy available yet?

An app that sends photos to Etsy listings uses the Etsy API, and Etsy has to approve it first. Our app is waiting for that approval. Until it is approved, Send to Etsy stays “coming soon” on this site.

Questions

Can I send mockups to Etsy listings automatically?

Not with WallMock yet. Send to Etsy is coming soon, pending Etsy's approval of our app. Today, download your WallMock images and upload them in Etsy's listing editor.

When will Send to Etsy be available?

When Etsy approves our app. We do not have a date, and we will not call it available before then.

Is WallMock endorsed by Etsy?

No. The term 'Etsy' is a trademark of Etsy, Inc. This application uses the Etsy API but is not endorsed or certified by Etsy, Inc.

Which plan will include Send to Etsy?

The WallMock Studio plan: Send to Etsy will be added to it at no extra cost when it launches. Pro includes Send to Shopify only, for 1 store and up to 10 products a month.

How can I automate Etsy listing photos for wall art today?

Automate the part that takes longest: making the photos. In WallMock, save your rooms and frames once and make every artwork in every chosen room in one run. On paid plans, the listing kit ZIP adds crops for a 4:3 Etsy first photo, alt text for every photo and a listing checklist. Then upload them in Etsy's listing editor.

Does it work with print-on-demand?

WallMock makes photos for the products you sell, whoever prints them. It does not connect to your print provider.

Is there a Shopify integration?

Yes, in beta: Send to Shopify adds mockups to your Shopify products, hero first. It is included in Pro (1 store, up to 10 products a month) and the WallMock Studio plan (up to 3 stores, unlimited products).

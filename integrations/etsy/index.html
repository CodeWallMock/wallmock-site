How WallMock fits an Etsy listing workflow

The slow part of Etsy listing photos is making them, not uploading them. WallMock makes every artwork in every room you saved, in one run, so a collection's photos come out together and look consistent.

Five photos of one artwork in different frames, made with the WallMock API pilot (early access, not yet available) from founder AK's own artwork. The web app makes the same kind of room photos today. Open any image for a larger view.

  1. Save your rooms and frames once. Heart the rooms that suit your shop and choose the frames you actually sell.
  2. Upload the collection. Each artwork keeps its own shape and is never redrawn.
  3. Download. Images are 4,096 px on the long edge. On paid plans, the listing kit adds crops for a 4:3 Etsy first photo, alt text and a listing checklist.
  4. Check every image at 100%. Corners, edges, signatures and small marks, before anything goes on Etsy.
  5. Upload in Etsy's listing editor. Hero first (drag to reorder), then paste the alt text. Add one unedited photo of the real item unless your product has an exception.

From my shop

I have sold print-on-demand wall art on Etsy as AntarmadaArtPrints since 2023, and I make my own listing photos in WallMock. Each artwork gets about 10 room images: the hero room first, a frame close-up card second. Every image is checked at 100% before I upload it, and nothing with a known defect goes in. I built WallMock because choosing rooms, matching frames and exporting the same designs again and again was part of every collection; uploading the finished set is the quick part.

Why doesn't WallMock connect to Etsy?

We applied to Etsy for access to its API, so WallMock could add photos to your listings directly. Etsy didn't approve it, so WallMock works the way most Etsy sellers already work: make the photos, download them, upload them. Your shop, orders and listings stay entirely between you and Etsy.

If you also sell on Shopify, Send to Shopify (beta) can add the same photos to your products for you.

Questions

Does WallMock connect to my Etsy shop?

No. WallMock has no connection to Etsy: it never asks for your Etsy login or any permission on your shop. You make the photos in WallMock, download them and upload them in Etsy's listing editor.

Can WallMock upload photos to my Etsy listings for me?

No. Uploading is a step you do in Etsy's listing editor: add the photos, drag the hero to the first slot and add the alt text. The slow part, making a consistent set of photos for every design, is what WallMock does in one run.

What size should Etsy listing photos be?

Etsy recommends at least 2,000 px in width and height, and the first photo should be landscape or square. WallMock downloads are 4,096 px on the long edge, and I keep every listing photo at least 2,000 px on its shortest side.

What is in the listing kit?

On paid plans, the listing kit ZIP adds crops for a 4:3 Etsy first photo, alt text for every photo and a listing checklist, next to the full-size images.

Can I use WallMock mockups on Etsy?

Yes, as long as they accurately show what the buyer receives. Most listings also need one unedited photo of the actual item; Etsy describes an exception for authorised mockups of print-on-demand items. See using WallMock mockups on Etsy.

Is WallMock endorsed by Etsy?

No. Etsy is a trademark of Etsy, Inc. WallMock is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy.

Is there a Shopify integration?

Yes, in beta: Send to Shopify adds mockups to your Shopify products, hero first. In the beta it is on Pro (1 store, up to 10 products a month) and the WallMock Studio plan (up to 3 stores, unlimited products); when it opens to all stores it will be on every plan, with up to 10 products a month on Free, Lite and Pro and unlimited products on the WallMock Studio plan.

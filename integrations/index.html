Questions

Is there a Shopify app that adds mockups to my products automatically?

WallMock's Send to Shopify does this, in beta testing: your WallMock mockups go straight onto your Shopify products, hero first. It is not generally available and it is not in the Shopify App Store. When it launches, Pro is planned to include 1 store and up to 25 products a month, and the WallMock Studio plan (coming soon) 5 stores with unlimited products; request beta access.

Can I send mockups to Etsy listings automatically?

Not with WallMock yet. Send to Etsy is coming soon, pending Etsy's approval of our app. Today, download your WallMock images and upload them in Etsy's listing editor; on paid plans, the listing kit ZIP includes crops for a 4:3 Etsy first photo, alt text for every photo and a listing checklist.

Does it work with print-on-demand?

Send to Shopify adds images to the products in your Shopify store; it does not connect to your print provider. If a print-on-demand app manages a product, check that app's publish settings so a later sync does not replace your photos.

Will it delete or replace my product photos?

No. Send to Shopify only adds images. It never deletes your photos.

What Shopify permission does WallMock ask for?

Only permission to edit products, which it needs to add images to them.

Which WallMock plan will include Send to Shopify and Send to Etsy?

Planned: when Send to Shopify launches, Pro includes 1 store and up to 25 products a month. The WallMock Studio plan (coming soon) includes Send to Shopify for 5 stores with unlimited products; Send to Etsy; one-click listing sets for whole collections; sending a whole collection onto all products; saved frame and partner presets; and 20% off API images. None of this is live yet, and the WallMock Studio plan has no price yet. Shopify: beta, request access. Etsy: coming soon.

Who are the integrations for?

Print-on-demand wall art sellers, print shops, and anyone selling framed canvas, posters, framed prints or metal prints on Shopify or Etsy.

Is there an API for print companies and platforms?

The WallMock API is in development and not available yet, with a private pilot coming. Early access is by request.

Can I try WallMock now?

Yes. Try Free needs no sign-up and no card: it makes watermarked previews with up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms. A free account adds 10 watermark-free 1K (1024 px) downloads every 30 days.

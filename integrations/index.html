What does it do, step by step?

  1. Make your mockups

    Upload a collection to WallMock, pick your rooms and frames once, and generate every artwork in every chosen room.

  2. Connect your store

    For Shopify, approve one permission: editing products. Send to Etsy follows once Etsy approves our app.

  3. Send

    Images are added to your Shopify products, hero first. Your existing photos stay; nothing is deleted.

Questions

Is there a Shopify app that adds mockups to my products automatically?

WallMock's Send to Shopify does this, in beta: connect your Shopify store and your WallMock mockups go straight onto your products, hero first, with no downloading or re-uploading. It is included in Pro (1 store, up to 10 products a month) and the WallMock Studio plan (up to 3 stores, unlimited products). It is not in the Shopify App Store; you connect your store from WallMock. See plans.

Can I send mockups to Etsy listings automatically?

Not with WallMock yet. Send to Etsy is coming soon, pending Etsy's approval of our app. Today, download your WallMock images and upload them in Etsy's listing editor; on paid plans, the listing kit ZIP includes crops for a 4:3 Etsy first photo, alt text for every photo and a listing checklist.

Does it work with print-on-demand?

Send to Shopify adds images to the products in your Shopify store; it does not connect to your print provider. If a print-on-demand app manages a product, check that app's publish settings so a later sync does not replace your photos.

Will it delete or replace my product photos?

No. Send to Shopify only adds images. It never deletes your photos.

What Shopify permission does WallMock ask for?

Only permission to edit products, which it needs to add images to them.

Which WallMock plan includes Send to Shopify and Send to Etsy?

Pro includes Send to Shopify for 1 store and up to 10 products a month. The WallMock Studio plan includes Send to Shopify for up to 3 stores with unlimited products, up to 25 saved custom frame colours, priority email support and 20% off API images when the API launches. Send to Etsy, one-click listing sets, sending a whole collection and saved presets are coming soon; Send to Etsy and listing sets will be added to the WallMock Studio plan at no extra cost when they launch. You can change stores 2 times a month. Shopify: beta. Etsy: coming soon.

Who are the integrations for?

Print-on-demand wall art sellers, print shops, and anyone selling framed canvas, posters, framed prints or metal prints on Shopify or Etsy.

Is there an API for print companies and platforms?

The WallMock API is in development and not available yet, with a private pilot coming. Early access is by request.

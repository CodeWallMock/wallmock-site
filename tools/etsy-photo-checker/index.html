Fixing a photo that fails

  • Too small: export again from the original file at a larger size; enlarging a small JPEG only makes a bigger blurry file. The print resolution checker shows how large your artwork file really is.
  • Vertical first photo: use a landscape or square room photo first and move the tall shot later in the listing.
  • Art cut off in the thumbnail: step back: more wall and furniture around the art leaves room for a square crop.
  • WebP or TIFF: save as JPEG (photos) or PNG.

Which photo goes first

The first photo is the one buyers see in search, so it does the most work. For wall art it is usually a calm room photo that shows the size, followed by a close-up, a size guide and other rooms. Questions

What size should Etsy listing photos be?

Etsy's help article recommends listing photos with a width and height of at least 2000 pixels, so buyers can zoom in. It also says the first photo should be at least 635 pixels wide and tall to avoid showing up lower in searches.

What shape should the first Etsy photo be?

Etsy asks for a horizontal (landscape) or square first photo. Vertical photos are fine later in the listing, but as a first photo they lose more of the picture when thumbnails are cropped.

Why does my Etsy thumbnail cut off the art?

Thumbnails are cropped, and Etsy advises leaving enough border that an image can be cropped to square. Keep the artwork inside the dashed square the checker draws, away from the edges of a landscape photo.

Is there a file size limit for Etsy photos?

Etsy's image help article notes that images larger than 1 MB may not finish uploading, especially on a slower connection. The checker flags files over 1 MB as worth knowing, not as a failure: a sharp 4096 px export is often larger and uploads fine on a good connection.

Which file types does Etsy accept?

Etsy's help article lists .jpg, .gif, .png, .svg and .heic. Save WebP, TIFF or AVIF files as JPEG or PNG before you upload them.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The checker reads each image's size and type in your browser and draws the preview on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

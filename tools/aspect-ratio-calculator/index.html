Most sizing mistakes happen here. A seller lists 8 × 10 and 11 × 14 from one file, or treats A4 as a small 2:3 print, and the print lab quietly trims the difference. The last column is how much of the art is cut off when a file made for one size fills the other, rounded to one decimal.

Two rules save most of the trouble. 11 × 14 is not 4:5: a 4:5 file at 11 × 14 loses 0.2 in of width, harmless for most art but not for a border or a signature on the edge. A sizes are not 2:3: a 2:3 file printed at A4 loses 1.8 cm of its height, enough to clip a signature. If you sell both shapes, make a separate crop for each with the crop to print size tool and look at what it cuts, or make all five common ratios at once with the printable size pack maker.

To find any ratio by hand, divide both sides by their greatest common divisor: 1440 × 810 divides by 90 to 16 × 9. When the numbers don't divide neatly, compare long ÷ short with the table above. A file like 4200 × 5000 (1.19) sits a few percent off 4:5 and needs a small crop before it fits 8 × 10 or 16 × 20.

The ratio on the listing photo

The ratio matters on the listing photo too. WallMock places your file as it is, so a 3:1 panorama stays 3:1 and is never redrawn to fit a frame. Questions

How do I find the aspect ratio of an image?

Divide the width and height by their greatest common divisor: 4000 × 6000 px becomes 2:3. The calculator does it for you and also names the nearest standard print ratio when the numbers don't divide neatly.

What aspect ratio is 8 × 10?

8 × 10 is 4:5, the same shape as 16 × 20 and 24 × 30. It is not the same shape as 8 × 12 (2:3).

What aspect ratio is A4?

All ISO A sizes share one shape, 1:√2, about 1:1.414. A4 at 300 DPI is 2480 × 3508 px.

Does the unit matter?

No. The ratio of 4000 × 6000 px is the same as 12 × 18 in or 30.48 × 45.72 cm. Choose the unit only so the same-ratio size comes out in it.

How do I calculate the aspect ratio from width and height?

Divide the width and the height by their greatest common divisor. 1920 × 1080 divides by 120 to give 16:9; 4000 × 6000 divides by 2000 to give 2:3. If they don’t divide neatly, compare the long side ÷ short side with a standard ratio: 1.5 is 2:3, 1.333 is 3:4 and 1.25 is 4:5.

What ratio is 18 × 24?

18 × 24 is 3:4, the same shape as 12 × 16 and 30 × 40 in. A 4:3 phone photo fits it without cropping.

What is a 4:3 aspect ratio in pixels?

Any size where the width is 4/3 of the height: 1024 × 768, 1600 × 1200 or 4032 × 3024. For prints, 4:3 is the landscape version of 3:4 sizes such as 16 × 12 and 24 × 18 in.

What aspect ratio is 11 × 14?

11:14, about 1:1.27. It is close to 4:5 (8 × 10, 16 × 20) but not the same shape, so a 4:5 file loses about 1.8% of its width at 11 × 14. The only other common size in the family is 22 × 28 in.

Is A4 the same ratio as 2:3?

No. A4 and every other A size are 1:√2, about 1:1.414, while 2:3 is 1:1.5. A 2:3 file printed at A4 loses about 5.7% of its height, 1.8 cm. A sizes are much closer to 5:7, only 1.0% apart.

What aspect ratio is 24 × 36?

2:3, the same shape as 4 × 6, 8 × 12, 12 × 18, 16 × 24 and 20 × 30 in. At 300 DPI it needs 7200 × 10800 px.

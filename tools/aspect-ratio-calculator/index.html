Aspect ratio calculator for prints

Short answer: enter a width and a height in pixels, inches or centimetres. It also finds a same-ratio size for any width.

Questions

How do I find the aspect ratio of an image?

Divide the width and height by their greatest common divisor: 4000 × 6000 px becomes 2:3. The calculator does it for you and also names the nearest standard print ratio when the numbers don't divide neatly.

What aspect ratio is 8 × 10?

8 × 10 is 4:5, the same shape as 16 × 20 and 24 × 30. It is not the same shape as 8 × 12 (2:3).

What aspect ratio is A4?

All ISO A sizes share one shape, 1:√2, about 1:1.414. A4 at 300 DPI is 2480 × 3508 px.

Does the unit matter?

No. The ratio of 4000 × 6000 px is the same as 12 × 18 in or 30.48 × 45.72 cm. Choose the unit only so the same-ratio size comes out in it.

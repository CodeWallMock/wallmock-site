Gallery wall spacing calculator

Plan a pair, trio, row or 2×2 grid above furniture or on an open wall: the total width, even gaps and where each piece goes. Works in inches or centimetres.

Why even gaps matter

Equal gaps make a group read as one intentional piece. Gaps of about 3–6 inches (8–15 cm) are a common guideline; keep them equal. See our gallery wall guide for alignment tips.

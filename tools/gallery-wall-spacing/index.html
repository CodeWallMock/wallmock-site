How to hang a pair or trio with even gaps

  1. Find the centre. Measure the furniture, mark its middle on the wall and draw a light pencil line straight up.
  2. Set the height. The calculator puts the bottom of the group in the middle of the usual gap above the furniture (8 in over a sofa back) and gives each hook's height from the floor.
  3. Mark the nails. Measure each "from the left end" distance along the wall, or each "from the centre line" distance either side of your pencil line, at the hook height.
  4. Check on paper. Tape paper cut to each frame size at the marks, step back, then hang the middle frame first and work outwards.

Choosing the gap

Gaps of 3 to 6 inches (8 to 15 cm) are the usual guideline. What matters most is that every gap is the same, between rows as well as across. Keep the whole group about two-thirds the width of the furniture: the calculator checks the 60–75% band and says when a group is narrow, wide or wider than the furniture itself. To pick the frame size first, use the art size over a sofa calculator.

Grids, tall frames and the ceiling

A grid doubles the height of the group, so tall frames over a sofa can climb close to the ceiling: a 2 × 2 grid of 30 × 40 inch frames is over 82 inches tall. Enter your ceiling height and the calculator warns when the top edge comes within 6 inches (15 cm) of it or goes past it. Questions

How far apart should pictures be hung in a gallery wall?

About 3 to 6 inches (8 to 15 cm) between frames is a common guideline, with every gap the same. Smaller frames sit well at the low end, large ones at the high end. Wider gaps make the pieces read as separate; narrower ones look cramped.

How do I space three pictures over a sofa?

Treat the three as one piece about two-thirds the width of the sofa. Over an 84 inch sofa, three 16 × 20 inch frames with 3 inch gaps make a 54 inch group. Their nails are 19 inches apart. With a 34 inch sofa back and the bottom of the frames 8 inches above it, the tops of the frames are 62 inches from the floor; each nail goes that high minus the frame's hook drop.

How far apart are the nails for frames of the same size?

One frame width plus one gap, measured centre to centre, for frames hung from a single central wire or hook. Three 16 inch frames with 3 inch gaps have nails 19 inches apart. For frames with two hooks each, measure the hook positions on each frame and offset them from its centre.

Should the gaps be the same between rows?

Yes. In a grid, use the same gap vertically as horizontally so the group reads as one block. The calculator gives the row-to-row spacing for each grid.

What if the group is wider than the sofa?

Use smaller frames, fewer frames or a smaller gap. The calculator says so plainly and measures every position from the centre line instead of the sofa's end.

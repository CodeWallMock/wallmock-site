How the calculator works

Most interior guidelines size art from the furniture below it, not from the wall: about two-thirds to three-quarters of the furniture's width, measured across the outside of the frame. Over an 84 inch sofa that is 56 to 63 inches. The calculator lists each common size in the shape you choose with its share of the width, so you can see exactly where it lands:

  • In range: two-thirds to three-quarters of the width.
  • A little narrow: 60% to two-thirds. Fine for a calm, airy look or a busy wall.
  • A little wide: three-quarters to 80%. Fine for a bold statement piece.

Pairs and trios are measured as one group with a 3 inch (8 cm) gap between pieces, the same default as the gallery wall spacing calculator. In centimetres the calculator lists metric sizes such as 60 × 90 and 100 × 150 cm rather than converted inch sizes.

When to break the guideline

The numbers are starting points. A tall ceiling can carry a taller piece; a low sofa under a high window may want a wide panorama instead of a tall portrait; a sideboard with lamps and objects often looks best with art a little narrower than two-thirds. For several frames, the gallery wall layout planner draws the whole group to scale. Before you print, check your file in the print size calculator so the size you pick prints sharply.

Questions

What size art goes over an 84 inch sofa?

About 56 to 63 inches wide, frame included: two-thirds to three-quarters of the sofa. A 40 × 60 inch landscape hung wide (60 in), a 20 × 60 inch panorama, or a trio of 18 × 24 inch portraits with 3 inch gaps (60 in) all fit.

What size art goes over a queen bed?

A US queen headboard is about 60 inches wide, so aim for 40 to 45 inches: a 30 × 40 inch landscape, a 14 × 42 inch panorama, or a pair of 20 × 30 inch portraits with a 3 inch gap (43 in). Measure the headboard if it is wider than the mattress.

Does the frame count in the width?

Yes. The two-thirds guideline is about what you see on the wall, so measure the outside of the frame. For a pair or trio, measure the whole group, gaps included.

How high should art hang over a sofa?

Hang the bottom of the frame about 6 to 10 inches (15 to 25 cm) above the sofa back, 8 to 12 inches (20 to 30 cm) above a headboard and 4 to 8 inches (10 to 20 cm) above a sideboard. The calculator shows the gap for the furniture you choose.

Can art be wider than three-quarters of the sofa?

Yes. These are common interior guidelines, not rules, and a bold oversized piece can work. Art wider than the sofa itself usually looks crowded, so the calculator lists sizes up to 80% and labels the ones outside two-thirds to three-quarters.

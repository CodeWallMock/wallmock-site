What size art over a sofa, bed or sideboard?

Enter the width of the furniture below the wall. You get an art width range, standard print sizes that fit, and a hanging height. These are common interior guidelines, not rules: big art can look great too.

How the numbers are worked out

Width: art (including its frame) usually looks balanced at about two-thirds to three-quarters of the furniture width. Many sellers like larger art in their listing photos, so treat the top of the range as a good default for a first photo.

Height: hang the bottom of the frame about 6–10 in (15–25 cm) above the furniture. Without furniture, a common gallery guideline puts the centre of the art about 57–60 in (145–152 cm) from the floor.

Sets: leave 2–3 in (5–8 cm) between panels and treat the whole group as one piece.

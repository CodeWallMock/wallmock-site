Questions

Which ratios should a printable wall art pack include?

Five ratios cover almost every standard frame: 2:3 (4 × 6 to 24 × 36), 3:4 (6 × 8 to 18 × 24), 4:5 (8 × 10, 16 × 20), 11:14 and ISO A (A5 to A1). Buyers print the size they need from the file of the right shape.

Is the pack made at full resolution?

Yes. Each crop keeps the original pixels of the area it covers; nothing is resized or enlarged. PNG is lossless; JPEG at quality 95 makes smaller files.

Can I choose what each crop keeps?

Yes. The slider moves every crop along the side that is cropped, from one edge (0%) through the centre (50%) to the other (100%). Check the previews before you download.

Is my artwork uploaded?

No. The file is opened, cropped and zipped in your browser; nothing leaves your device.

How large a file can it handle?

Files up to about 40 megapixels work on most computers. Phones limit how large an image the browser can make, so very large packs may fail there; use a computer for those.

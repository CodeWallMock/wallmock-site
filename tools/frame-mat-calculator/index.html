Frame and mat size calculator

Enter the print size and the mat border you want. You get the mat opening (slightly smaller than the print so the mat holds it) and the outside size of the frame. Works in inches or centimetres.

How mats are sized

The mat opening is cut slightly smaller than the print so the mat holds the print's edges; about ⅛ inch (3 mm) on each side is common. A slightly deeper bottom border (bottom weighting) is a traditional gallery touch. Read more in our frame and mat proportions guide.

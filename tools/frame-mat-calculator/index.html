How mats and frames are sized

  1. Mat opening. The window is cut a little smaller than the print, about 1/8 inch (3 mm) on each edge, so the mat holds the print's edges.
  2. Mat outer size. Add the border on every side of the opening. This is also the frame's rebate (inside) size: the size of the glass and backing.
  3. Outside of the frame. Add the frame face on each side. This is the size that hangs on the wall, and the one to use in the art size over a sofa calculator.

With bottom weighting the bottom border is wider than the top and sides, so the rebate is taller than a plain border would make it.

Ready-made or custom

A custom frame can be any rebate size. A ready-made frame comes in fixed sizes, so the calculator also names the smallest standard frame that gives at least your border, and the borders it really gives with the print centred. The standard frame sizes chart lists those sizes in inches, centimetres and A sizes.

Choosing a border

Wider borders give small prints more presence and make a calm, gallery look; narrow ones suit busy walls and large prints. There is no single right width: the guide to frame and mat proportions compares common choices with examples. If you sell framed prints, show the mat in your listing photos so buyers know what arrives.

Questions

How much smaller should a mat opening be than the print?

About 1/8 inch (3 mm) on each edge, so the mat holds the print: 1/4 inch (6 mm) less in each direction overall. An 8 × 10 inch print gets a 7.75 × 9.75 inch opening.

What size frame do I need for an 8 × 10 print with a mat?

With 1.5 inch borders the mat and frame rebate are 10.75 × 12.75 inches, so a custom frame is that size. The nearest ready-made frame is 11 × 14 inches, which gives about 1.625 inch borders at the sides and 2.125 inches top and bottom.

What is bottom weighting?

Making the bottom border slightly wider than the top and sides, a traditional framing touch that stops the art looking as if it is sliding down. The calculator offers a bottom border a quarter wider or half as wide again.

What is the rebate size of a frame?

The inside size of the frame's lip: the size of the glass, mat and backing it holds. Order a custom frame by its rebate size; the outside is bigger by the width of the frame face on each side.

Can I frame a print without a mat?

Yes. Enter a border of 0: the frame's rebate is then the print size itself, and the calculator tells you whether that is a ready-made frame size.

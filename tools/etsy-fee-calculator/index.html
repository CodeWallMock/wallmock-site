How the calculator works

It adds up every Etsy fee on one sale, then takes the fees and your provider's cost from what the buyer pays you:

  1. Order total = item price + the shipping you charge.
  2. Etsy fees = listing fee + 6.5% of the order total + processing (a percentage of the order total and any tax, plus a fixed amount) + any regulatory operating fee + any Offsite Ads fee, plus VAT on those fees if you pay it.
  3. Profit = order total − Etsy fees − the provider's product and shipping cost.
  4. Margin = profit ÷ order total.

Worked example (US): a USD 45 framed print with free shipping, made for USD 22 plus USD 7 shipping. Etsy takes USD 0.20 + USD 2.93 (6.5%) + USD 1.60 (3% + USD 0.25) = USD 4.73. Profit is USD 45 − USD 4.73 − USD 29 = USD 11.27, a 25.0% margin. The break-even price is USD 32.55: below it, the sale loses money.

Each fee is rounded to the cent. To set a price that leaves the margin you want, the guide to how to price wall art on Etsy turns the same fees into a formula, size by size.

What the calculator leaves out

  • Etsy Ads inside Etsy search: Etsy says their fees vary, so count your ad spend separately.
  • Currency conversion: if you list in a currency other than your bank account's, Etsy charges 2.5% to convert your sales.
  • Shipping labels, the one-time shop set-up fee and income tax.
  • Refunds and discounts: enter the price the buyer actually paid after a sale or coupon.

Print-on-demand costs differ by size, frame and country, so run the calculator for each size you sell. Before you set prices, check that your file is large enough for each size with the print resolution checker.

Questions

How much does Etsy take from a sale?

In the US, Etsy takes 6.5% of the item price plus shipping, payment processing of 3% + USD 0.25, and USD 0.20 for the listing. On a USD 45 print with free shipping that is USD 4.73, about 11% of the sale. An Offsite Ads sale adds 12% or 15%.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee, payment processing and the Offsite Ads fee are all charged on the shipping you charge as well as the item price, so free shipping built into the price costs the same in fees as a separate shipping charge.

What is the Offsite Ads fee?

When a buyer clicks one of Etsy's ads on another website and buys within 30 days, Etsy charges 15% of the order total, or 12% if your shop made USD 10,000 or more in the past 365 days. It is never more than USD 100 for an order.

Why does the calculator ask for an exchange rate?

Etsy states the listing fee (USD 0.20) and the Offsite Ads cap (USD 100) in US dollars. If your shop uses another currency, type how many units of your currency one US dollar costs today; the calculator never guesses a rate.

How do I find my break-even price?

Enter your provider's product and shipping cost. The break-even price is the lowest item price that covers those costs and every Etsy fee on the sale, with your shipping charge unchanged. Price above it to make a profit.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs in your browser; nothing you type leaves your device.

Standard frame sizes chart

Short answer: common ready-made frames include 5 × 7, 8 × 10, 11 × 14, 16 × 20, 18 × 24 and 24 × 36 inches, and 13 × 18, 30 × 40, 50 × 70 cm and the A sizes. A matted frame usually holds the next size down, such as an 8 × 10 print in an 11 × 14 frame. Enter your print below to find frames that fit.

Find a frame for your print

  • Without a mat: an 8 × 10 in frame holds the print edge to edge.
  • With a mat: an 11 × 14 in frame, borders about 1.6 in at the sides and 2.1 in top and bottom.
  • With a mat: a 12 × 16 in frame, borders about 2.1 in at the sides and 3.1 in top and bottom.

Mat borders assume the mat overlaps the print by about 1/8 in (3 mm) on each edge. Brands vary, so check the mat opening of the frame you choose.

How to use the chart

If you sell prints, sizes that fit ready-made frames let buyers frame a print without a custom order, so many sellers list them first. Check that your file's shape matches: the ratio column shows each size's shape, and where a size is not an exact common ratio it shows how much a file in the nearest ratio loses to cropping. The print size calculator lists every standard size your file fits.

Mats and mat openings

The "usually holds" column is the conventional pairing, such as an 8 × 10 print in an 11 × 14 frame. Mat openings vary by brand, so check the one you recommend. For a custom size, or to see the exact borders, use the frame and mat size calculator.

Ratios behind the sizes

Sizes come in families that share a shape: 8 × 12, 12 × 18 and 24 × 36 are all 2:3; 12 × 16 and 18 × 24 are 3:4; A4 to A0 are all 1:1.414. The guide to print ratios and sizes for wall art explains which families suit which art.

Questions

What are the standard frame sizes?

In the US and Canada, common ready-made frames include 5 × 7, 8 × 10, 11 × 14, 12 × 16, 16 × 20, 18 × 24 and 24 × 36 inches. In the UK, Europe, Australia and India they include 13 × 18, 30 × 40, 40 × 50, 50 × 70 and 70 × 100 cm plus the A sizes. The chart lists each with its ratio.

What size print goes in an 11 × 14 frame with a mat?

Usually an 8 × 10 inch print. With the mat overlapping the print by about 1/8 inch, that leaves borders of about 1.6 inches at the sides and 2.1 inches top and bottom.

Is 11 × 17 a 2:3 size?

No. 11 × 17 is 1:1.55, so a 2:3 file printed at 11 × 17 loses about 3% to cropping. 12 × 18 is the 2:3 size nearby.

What size is A3 in inches?

A3 is 29.7 × 42 cm, about 11.7 × 16.5 inches. Every A size has the same 1:1.414 ratio, so one file prints at all of them without cropping.

Is 70 × 100 cm the same shape as A sizes?

Nearly but not quite: 70 × 100 is 7:10 (1:1.43) and A sizes are 1:1.414, about 1% apart. A file made for A sizes loses a sliver when printed at 70 × 100.

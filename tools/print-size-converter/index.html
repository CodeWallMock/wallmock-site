The formulas

  • Centimetres = inches × 2.54.
  • Pixels = inches × DPI, or centimetres ÷ 2.54 × DPI.
  • Inches = pixels ÷ DPI.

DPI here means pixels per inch of the print. The DPI saved in a file's settings does not change its pixels; only the pixel size and the print size matter.

Questions

How many pixels is 8 × 10 at 300 DPI?

2400 × 3000 px. Multiply each side in inches by the DPI: 8 × 300 and 10 × 300.

How do I convert inches to centimetres?

Multiply by 2.54: 24 × 36 in is 61 × 91.4 cm. To go from centimetres to inches, divide by 2.54.

What DPI should a print be?

300 DPI is the usual target for prints seen up close. Large prints seen from further away often look fine at 150 to 200 DPI; check yours with the print resolution checker.

How many pixels is A4 at 300 DPI?

2480 × 3508 px (21 × 29.7 cm).

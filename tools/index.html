Free tools for wall art sellers

16 quick tools that run in your browser. No sign-up, and nothing is uploaded. Work out Etsy fees and profit, check print sizes, ratios and resolution, convert inches, centimetres and pixels, crop art to a print size or make a printable size pack, draw a size comparison chart, split a panorama into a triptych, pull a colour palette from your art, plan frames and mats, lay out a gallery wall and check your Etsy photos.

Art color palette generator

Free art color palette generator: extract the six main colours from your artwork or painting, with HEX and RGB codes and each colour's share. Nothing is uploaded.

Aspect ratio calculator for prints

Free aspect ratio calculator for wall art: enter pixels, inches or cm to get the exact ratio, the nearest standard print ratio and the print sizes in that family.

Free tool to crop an image for print: 8 × 10, 2:3, 3:4, 5:7, 11 × 14, square, A4 or 3:1 panorama, at full resolution, with the print size at 300 DPI. Nothing uploaded.

Free Etsy fee calculator for wall art and print-on-demand sellers: every Etsy fee, your profit, margin and break-even price, with Etsy's rates for 64 countries.

Etsy listing photo checker

Free Etsy listing photo checker: see each image's pixel size, shape, file type and square thumbnail crop against Etsy's own photo guidance. Nothing is uploaded.

Free frame and mat size calculator: enter a print size and mat border to get the mat opening, the frame size to order and the nearest ready-made frame. Inches or cm.

Standard frame sizes chart

Standard frame sizes in inches and cm, A sizes and poster sizes, with each size's ratio and the print a matted frame usually holds. Find a frame for your print.

Free gallery wall layout planner: enter frame sizes, gaps and your sofa width to get each frame's position, nail height and a to-scale diagram. Inches or cm.

Free gallery wall spacing calculator for pairs, trios, rows and grids: even gaps, the total width, each nail's position and hook height above a sofa or bed. Inches or cm.

Free triptych split calculator: split a panorama into 2 or 3 panels with gaps, get each panel's size and position, and cut your image into panels in the browser.

Free print resolution checker for wall art sellers: your file's DPI at every common size, 5×7 to 24×36 and A5 to A1, and how much each crops. Nothing uploaded.

Free print size calculator: enter your artwork's pixel size to find its ratio and every standard print size it fits without cropping, with the DPI of each. Inches and cm.

Free print size converter: inches to cm, cm to inches and either to pixels at 72 to 300 DPI, with a table of common print sizes and A sizes in pixels.

Questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. They are free, need no account and have no usage limit on our side.

Do the tools upload my artwork?

No. Tools that read an image, such as the crop tool and the colour palette generator, do all their work in your browser; the file never leaves your device.

What do the tools have to do with WallMock?

They answer the questions sellers ask before listing: print sizes, ratios, resolution, crops, size charts, colours, frames, wall layouts and Etsy fees. WallMock then shows your exact artwork in realistic rooms for the listing photos.

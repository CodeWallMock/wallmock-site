How to read the chart

Each panel shows the same wall and furniture at one scale, with one artwork hung with its bottom 8 in (20 cm) above the furniture, or centred at eye level (57 in, 145 cm) on a bare wall. The percentage is the artwork's width as a share of the furniture's width. About two-thirds to three-quarters usually looks balanced over a sofa or bed.

Sizes are drawn without a frame; a frame or mat adds its width on every side.

Questions

How do I make a wall art size chart for Etsy?

Enter the sizes you sell, pick a piece of furniture, and download the PNG. Every size is drawn to the same scale, so buyers can compare them at a glance. Add it as one of your listing photos, next to your room mockups.

Is the chart drawn to scale?

Yes: every artwork and the furniture share one scale. It is a diagram, though, not a photo of a room, and the image says so. Furniture sizes are typical retail sizes, so a buyer's own sofa or bed may differ.

What size art goes over an 84 inch sofa?

Art about two-thirds to three-quarters of the sofa's width, 56 to 63 inches across, looks balanced; the chart marks each size against that guide. A 40 × 60 in landscape covers 71%.

Does it work in centimetres?

Yes. Switch the unit to cm; the chart labels each size in both units, and ISO sizes such as A3 and A2 are named.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The chart is drawn and saved in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Gallery wall layout planner

Short answer: treat the gallery wall as one picture. Keep equal gaps of 3 to 6 inches (8 to 15 cm), make the group about two-thirds the width of the furniture below it, and centre it about 57 inches (145 cm) above the floor. Heights are measured from the floor. "Centre across" is how far each frame's centre sits from the middle of the group, which you line up with the middle of the furniture.

How to hang it

  1. Measure the furniture. Note its width and the height of its top (or the sofa back). For an open wall, skip this and pick a centre point.
  2. Choose sizes and a gap. Enter your frame size and an even gap; the gallery wall spacing calculator covers gaps on their own. Aim for a group about two-thirds the furniture width; the planner tells you if it is narrow or wide.
  3. Mark the centre line. Find the middle of the furniture and draw a light pencil line straight up. Every "Centre across" value is measured from it.
  4. Mark each nail. Measure the hook drop on the back of each frame, enter it, then mark each frame's hook height at its distance left or right of the line.
  5. Test on paper first. Cut paper to each frame size, tape it up at the marks and step back before you drill.
  6. Hang the middle first. Hang the centre frame or row, check it is level, then work outwards keeping the gaps equal.

Layouts the planner handles

  • Row: 1 to 9 frames side by side, the classic look over a long sofa or bench.
  • Grid 2 × 2, 2 × 3 and 3 × 3: equal frames in rows. With an odd count, the short last row is centred.
  • Trio: a large centre frame with two smaller frames on its centre line, a calm choice over a bed.

These numbers are common guidelines, not rules. Ceiling height, a tall headboard or a low sofa can all change what looks right, so trust the paper test.

Questions

How high should a gallery wall be hung?

A common guideline is to put the centre of the whole group about 57 inches (145 cm) above the floor, the eye-level line many galleries use. Over a sofa or sideboard, keep the bottom frames 6 to 10 inches (15 to 25 cm) above the furniture, ideally about 8 inches (20 cm), even if that moves the centre a little. The planner warns you if the gap is too small, too large or the frames overlap the furniture.

How wide should a gallery wall be over a sofa?

About two-thirds of the sofa's width is the usual guideline, and up to three-quarters still looks planned. Over an 84 inch sofa that is roughly 56 to 63 inches for the whole group, frames and gaps included.

How do I work out the nail height for each frame?

Take the frame's top-edge height from the table and subtract the drop: the distance from the top of the frame down to the taut wire or hook on the back. Enter that drop in the planner and it gives the hook height for every frame.

Can I plan in centimetres?

Yes. Switch the unit to cm and the planner converts what you typed. The default 57 inch centre becomes 145 cm and the default 3 inch gap becomes 8 cm; other values are converted exactly.

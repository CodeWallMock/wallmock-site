Why provider mockups are rarely enough for canvas

Same scenes as everyone

Every seller on the same provider can use the same stock scenes, so your listing looks like the next one.

Frame details get lost

A floating frame's gap and the frame colour decide what the buyer expects. Generic scenes often show neither clearly.

One design at a time

A collection of 50 designs in 8 rooms is 400 images. Making them one by one doesn't scale.

Canvas and framed products come from several print providers, so frame type and colours depend on the provider you pick. Printful

Printful sells a framed canvas "in a high-quality wooden frame"; customer reviews on the product page describe a floater frame and a shadow gap (Printful framed canvas, checked 2026-10-29). Show the gap in your mockup so the photo matches.

Gelato

Gelato's framed canvas comes in 13 sizes from 8×10 in to 24×36 in with black, natural wood or dark brown frames, on a slim 2–3 cm canvas (Gelato framed canvas FAQ), with a 12 mm gap between canvas and frame (Gelato framed canvas). Prodigi

Prodigi's framed canvas is a floater frame with a 5 mm gap and a 12 mm frame face, 53 mm deep from the wall, in black, white, antique gold, antique silver, natural and brown, from 4×6 in to 55×55 in (Prodigi framed canvas).

Questions

What is the best mockup tool for print on demand canvas?

One that shows the canvas you actually sell: stretched canvas (gallery wrap), or framed canvas with the floating-frame gap, in the frame colour your provider ships, with your design unchanged. WallMock is built for exactly that, for whole collections, in 500+ curated rooms.

Can I use my own mockups instead of my provider's?

Yes. Your provider's mockups are a starting point; on Etsy and Shopify you choose the listing photos. Many sellers keep one provider image for the product details and lead with lifestyle room photos made elsewhere.

Are print on demand mockups accurate?

They show the product type, but room scenes and frame details vary by tool. Order a sample of your best seller and compare the frame colour, depth and gap with your listing photos before you scale.

Does Printify make room mockups for framed canvas?

Printify's AI Mockups are included with Printify Premium. Its page lists canvases among the products, and doesn't mention framed canvas or framed prints (checked 2026-10-29).

Is Gelato's Mockup Studio free?

Gelato says Mockup Studio is available for Gelato+ subscribers, and users without a premium subscription can try it before deciding (checked 2026-10-29).

Which framed canvas should my mockup show?

The one your provider makes. Prodigi's is a floater frame with a 5 mm gap; Gelato states a 12 mm gap and black, natural wood or dark brown frames; Printful's reviews describe a floater frame. Pick the matching finish and show the gap if the frame floats.

What resolution do canvas mockups need for Etsy?

Etsy recommends listing photos at least 2000 px on the shortest side. WallMock exports 4K (4096 px) images, so there is room to crop to Etsy's square thumbnail.

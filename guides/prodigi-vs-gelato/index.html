Prodigi vs Gelato for framed prints and canvas: UK, EU and US sellers

By AK, founder of WallMock and an Etsy wall-art seller since 2023 · Updated 14 November 2026 · How we write our guides

Short answer: Choose Prodigi if you sell framed prints with mats, large framed canvas (up to 55×55 in) or gold and silver frames: it states its mount options, offers six framed canvas colours to Gelato's three, and fulfils from the UK, EU, US and Australia. Choose Gelato if you want a bold 12 mm floating gap, framed canvas that arrives with its hanger attached, and listings that start as Etsy drafts for you. Either way, order a sample to each region you sell to most, because both change frame materials or depth by region.

Both make framed prints and framed canvas, and both route each order to a lab near the buyer. The differences that matter are the frame, the mount and the region. Below: each provider's own facts, and how to match either one in a frame mockup generator or with framed canvas mockups.

Which to choose

  • Framed prints with a mat: Prodigi. It states optional mounts in three colours on classic and box frames; the Gelato help pages we checked don't mention mats.
  • Gold or silver framed canvas, or anything above 24×36 in: Prodigi. Its framed canvas comes in antique gold and antique silver and runs to 55×55 in; Gelato's stops at 24×36 in.
  • A clear, dark floating gap: Gelato. Its 12 mm gap reads as a band; Prodigi's 5 mm reads as a fine line.
  • Ready to hang out of the box: Gelato's framed canvas has a sawtooth hanger attached. Prodigi's framed canvas comes without hanging equipment.
  • Buyers in many countries: don't decide on Gelato's 32-country headline. Check fulfilment for the exact product and destination; neither provider lets you pick the lab.

Framed canvas: gap, depth and colour

Both are floating-style framed canvases. On a wall they don't look alike.

  • Prodigi (framed canvas): a 5 mm shadow gap and a slim 12 mm frame face around a deep 38 mm canvas, standing 53 mm from the wall. Hanging equipment is not included.
  • Gelato (canvas guide, FAQ): a wider 12 mm gap, a 9–14 mm frame front, and a slim 2–3 cm canvas. It arrives ready to hang with a sawtooth hanger attached.

For your listing photos, the gap is the detail to get right. Show it at a size where a buyer can see it, and never photograph a 12 mm band for a product with a 5 mm line. Colour choice is wider at Prodigi, including antique gold and silver; Gelato keeps to black and two woods. For how these finishes look side by side, see frame colours for wall art.

Don't pick from the spec table alone

The gap width, the wood tone and the mat are exactly where these two differ, and they are the details a buyer compares when the parcel opens. Read the table, then let a sample settle it; the floating frame guide shows why the gap matters so much on a framed canvas.

Framed prints, mounts and glazing

Prodigi lists classic frames (20 mm face, 22 mm from the wall, eight colours, 6×6 in to 40×40 in) and box frames (20 mm face, 33 mm from the wall, black, white or natural, 5×7 in to 50×50 in), each with Perspex, float glass or moth-eye glaze (classic, box). Mounts are optional on both, in three colours, 25–50 mm wide depending on frame size (source).

Gelato offers framed posters in four wood colours and four metal colours (source), with a 10–14 mm frame and plexiglass (source). Its premium wood frames are 20 mm wide in natural oak, black or white (source). By default, frame and poster arrive unmounted in one package; Ready-to-Hang mounts them before shipping (source).

If your style depends on a mat, start with Prodigi; with Gelato, confirm in its current catalogue before you photograph one. Our frame and mat proportions guide explains how a mat changes the look.

UK, EU and US: the regional differences

  • Prodigi frame materials change by region. In the UK and EU, black and white framed canvas frames are obeche with a painted finish, and natural is real oak laminate. In the US, black and white are poplar or pine with a vinyl foil finish, and natural and brown are real wood with an oak-effect laminate (source, checked 2026-10-29). A US sample may not look exactly like a UK one.
  • Gelato's framed canvas depth changes by region: 42 mm in North America and 32 mm in Europe. Gelato also notes that black frames in Australia show the wood grain (source).
  • Gelato's premium wood frames: the 11×17 in size is listed for North America only (source).
  • Both route orders for you. Prodigi fulfils from the lab nearest your customer, and Gelato says you can't choose the country. Selling to both the UK and the US? Order a sample shipped to each. A UK sample tells you nothing certain about the US frame.

Etsy, samples and getting started

Etsy: Prodigi says you can't yet create listings in Prodigi and sync them to Etsy; you make the listing on Etsy, then import it (source). Gelato creates the listing for you as a draft, with mockups from its scenes (source). Either way you can add your own room photos on Etsy. Our Etsy integrations guide covers the steps.

Samples: Prodigi offers 50% off your first sample order, refunded after you email the order number, one product type per order, and customs charges may apply (source). Its frame pages also offer a sample pack of mouldings in each colour. Gelato has no free samples; new sellers get 30% off the product cost of a first sample order within 48 hours of sign-up, up to 50€ off (source).

Account: Prodigi says it is free to use, with no monthly subscription (source). Gelato's Mockup Studio is for Gelato+ subscribers, with a way to try it first.

A worked example: 16 × 20 in for UK and US buyers

Say you sell a 4:5 design at 16 × 20 in to buyers in the UK and the US. The ratio fits both providers, so it doesn't decide anything. Work through the product instead:

  1. Paper print? Settle the frame and mat first: do you need a mat, and does the frame arrive assembled? That points to a candidate at each provider. Then check the current catalogue for the size, artwork opening, glazing and assembly. A mat our dated sources don't mention is a question to ask Gelato, not proof it can't.
  2. Canvas? Compare gap, frame face and canvas depth, then check the size and destination on the current product page. For these two markets, the regional material and depth differences matter more than any country count.
  3. Order the samples. One to the UK, one to the US. Note the product, finish, size, destination and date, and compare the delivered frame, edge and crop with the listing you plan to publish.

Only then compare total product and delivery cost for the same destination. The sample checklist covers what to record.

Matching either one in WallMock

WallMock covers both product families: framed canvas in black, white, natural oak, natural wood, walnut, espresso, gold or silver, stretched canvas, and framed prints with or without a mat. Map Prodigi's antique gold and silver to gold and silver and natural to natural oak. Map brown, or Gelato's dark brown, to walnut or espresso only after comparing with your sample. The Frame width and Floating gap settings adjust the visual proportions, so set the gap to look like your provider's 5 mm or 12 mm rather than leaving the default. Frame colours are visual approximations, not a physical finish sample.

Posters and paper

Rechecked on 8 November 2026: Gelato lists five poster papers, from classic matte at 170 gsm to archival matte at 250 gsm, plus two semi-glossy weights, and ships the poster with its frame or hanger unmounted (source). Prodigi’s classic frames arrive framed and ready to hang, in eight colours, with Perspex, float glass or moth-eye glaze and an optional mount (source). Questions

Is Prodigi or Gelato better for framed prints?

Prodigi, if you need a mat or a wide choice of frames: it lists classic and box frames, eight classic frame colours and optional mounts in three colours. Gelato offers framed posters in four wood and four metal colours through a 32-country network, but the help pages we checked don't mention mats. Order a sample either way.

Which has the wider gap on framed canvas?

Gelato states a 12 mm gap between canvas and frame. Prodigi's floater framed canvas has a 5 mm gap.

Does Gelato offer mats or mounts?

The Gelato help pages we checked don't mention mats (checked 2026-10-29). Prodigi offers optional mounts in snow white, off-white and black on its classic and box frames.

Where do Prodigi and Gelato print?

Prodigi has its own facilities in the UK, EU and USA plus partners in over 10 countries, and fulfils wall art from the UK, EU, US and Australia. Gelato produces locally in 32 countries and says you can't choose the production country.

Which offers bigger framed canvas sizes?

Prodigi's framed canvas runs from 4×6 in to 55×55 in. Gelato's comes in 13 sizes from 8×10 in to 24×36 in.

Do Prodigi and Gelato give sample discounts?

Prodigi offers 50% off a first sample order. Gelato offers new sellers 30% off the product cost of a first sample order within 48 hours of sign-up, up to 50€ off.

Is Gelato or Prodigi better for posters?

Gelato offers more paper choices (five, 170–250 gsm) and ships frame and poster unmounted unless you choose Ready-to-Hang. Prodigi’s classic frames arrive ready to hang, with a choice of glazing and an optional mount. Choose by the finish your buyers expect, then order a sample.

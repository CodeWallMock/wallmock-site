Prodigi vs Gelato for framed prints and canvas: UK, EU and US sellers

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Prodigi suits sellers who want detailed specs and more frame choice: a floater framed canvas with a 5 mm gap in six colours, box and classic frames, and optional mounts in three colours, fulfilled from the UK, EU, US and Australia. Gelato suits sellers who want a wide local network (32 countries) and a simpler range: framed canvas in three colours with a 12 mm gap, and framed posters in wood or metal. Check the regional differences, then order a sample.

Prodigi and Gelato both make framed prints and framed canvas, and both route orders to a lab near the buyer. They differ in frame choice, mounts and regional details. Below are the facts from each provider's own pages, and how to match each in a frame mockup generator or with framed canvas mockups.

Framed canvas: gap, depth and colour

Both are floating-style framed canvases, but they look different on a wall.

  • Prodigi (framed canvas): a 5 mm shadow gap and a slim 12 mm frame face around a deep 38 mm canvas, standing 53 mm from the wall. Hanging equipment is not included.
  • Gelato (canvas guide, FAQ): a wider 12 mm gap, a 9–14 mm frame front, and a slim 2–3 cm canvas. It arrives ready to hang with a sawtooth hanger attached.

For your listing photos, the gap is the detail to get right. A 12 mm gap reads as a clear dark band; a 5 mm gap reads as a fine line. Show it at a size where a buyer can see it, and don't promise the wrong one. Colour choice is wider at Prodigi, including antique gold and silver, while Gelato keeps to black and two woods. For how these finishes look side by side, see frame colours for wall art.

Framed prints, mounts and glazing

Prodigi lists classic frames (20 mm face, 22 mm from the wall, eight colours, 6×6 in to 40×40 in) and box frames (20 mm face, 33 mm from the wall, black, white or natural, 5×7 in to 50×50 in), each with Perspex, float glass or moth-eye glaze (classic, box). Mounts are optional on both, in three colours, 25–50 mm wide depending on frame size (source).

Gelato offers framed posters in four wood colours and four metal colours (source), with a 10–14 mm frame and plexiglass (source). Its premium wood frames are 20 mm wide in natural oak, black or white (source). By default, frame and poster arrive unmounted in one package; Ready-to-Hang mounts them before shipping (source).

If your style depends on a mat, Prodigi states its mount options; with Gelato, check its catalog first. Our frame and mat proportions guide explains how a mat changes the look.

UK, EU and US: the regional differences

  • Prodigi frame materials change by region. In the UK and EU, black and white framed canvas frames are obeche with a painted finish, and natural is real oak laminate. In the US, black and white are poplar or pine with a vinyl foil finish, and natural and brown are real wood with an oak-effect laminate (source, checked 2026-10-29). A US sample may not look exactly like a UK one.
  • Gelato's framed canvas depth changes by region: 42 mm in North America and 32 mm in Europe. Gelato also notes that black frames in Australia show the wood grain (source).
  • Gelato's premium wood frames: the 11×17 in size is listed for North America only (source).
  • Both route orders for you. Prodigi fulfils from the lab nearest your customer, and Gelato says you can't choose the country. If you sell to both the UK and the US, order a sample shipped to each region you sell to most.

Etsy, samples and getting started

Etsy: Prodigi says you can't yet create listings in Prodigi and sync them to Etsy; you make the listing on Etsy, then import it (source). Gelato creates the listing for you as a draft, with mockups from its scenes (source). Either way you can add your own room photos on Etsy. Our Etsy integrations guide covers the steps.

Samples: Prodigi offers 50% off your first sample order, refunded after you email the order number, one product type per order, and customs charges may apply (source). Its frame pages also offer a sample pack of mouldings in each colour. Gelato has no free samples; new sellers get 30% off the product cost of a first sample order within 48 hours of sign-up, up to 50€ off (source).

Account: Prodigi says it is free to use, with no monthly subscription (source). Gelato's Mockup Studio is for Gelato+ subscribers, with a way to try it first.

Which fits your shop, and matching it in WallMock

  • Your buyers are spread across many countries: Gelato's 32-country network may print closer to more of them.
  • You want a bold floating gap: Gelato's 12 mm gap is wider; Prodigi's 5 mm gap is subtler.

Questions

Is Prodigi or Gelato better for framed prints?

Prodigi lists more frame types and colours and optional mounts in three colours. Gelato offers framed posters in wood and metal colours through a 32-country network. Choose by the frame you want and where your buyers are, then order a sample.

Which has the wider gap on framed canvas?

Gelato states a 12 mm gap between canvas and frame. Prodigi's floater framed canvas has a 5 mm gap.

Does Gelato offer mats or mounts?

The Gelato help pages we checked don't mention mats (checked 2026-10-29). Prodigi offers optional mounts in snow white, off-white and black on its classic and box frames.

Where do Prodigi and Gelato print?

Prodigi has its own facilities in the UK, EU and USA plus partners in over 10 countries, and fulfils wall art from the UK, EU, US and Australia. Gelato produces locally in 32 countries and says you can't choose the production country.

Which offers bigger framed canvas sizes?

Prodigi's framed canvas runs from 4×6 in to 55×55 in. Gelato's comes in 13 sizes from 8×10 in to 24×36 in.

Do Prodigi and Gelato give sample discounts?

Prodigi offers 50% off a first sample order. Gelato offers new sellers 30% off the product cost of a first sample order within 48 hours of sign-up, up to 50€ off.

Start a print-on-demand wall art shop on Etsy: a beginner's plan for 2026

By Akshit, founder of WallMock and an Etsy wall-art seller since 2023 · Published 16 November 2026 · How we write our guides

Short answer: Start narrow: one niche, one product from one print partner, and 10 listings in one consistent style. Order a sample of every frame before you list it, price from your real cost with Etsy's fees built in, and give every listing about 10 photos that show exactly what arrives. Then leave it a month and read Etsy Stats before you add more.

This page puts the whole start in order, from choosing a niche to reading your first month of stats. Each step links to a deeper guide; the order matters more than any single trick, because most beginner problems come from doing a later step first: writing tags before the product is settled, or making photos of a frame nobody has sampled.

1. Pick a narrow niche and a house style

A niche is a buyer and a room, not just a subject. “Calm botanical prints for a bedroom” tells you the palette, the frame colours and the rooms for your photos. “Flowers” tells you nothing.

Plan the first designs as a collection: related subjects in the same palette and finish. A collection can be offered as pairs and trios, and one set of rooms can serve every listing in it, so the shop looks like a shop rather than a pile of one-offs. Consistent Etsy shop mockups shows how to plan that room set.

From my shop: for calm decor pieces, the art direction I prefer is one calm subject, lots of empty space, a muted palette with one accent colour, and a soft painted finish rather than a photographic one. That is one style among many. The point is to choose one and hold it across the collection.

If AI is part of how you make the art, Etsy allows seller-prompted work where you made an individual creative contribution, and asks you to say so in the description. Read selling AI art prints on Etsy before you list.

2. Choose one product and a print partner

Start with one product. Framed canvas, stretched canvas and framed prints need different photos, sizes and prices; launching all three on day one triples the work before you know what sells. Add the second product once the first ten listings are clean.

The shortlist from our provider comparison (each provider's own wording, checked 2026-10-26):

  • US buyers, framed posters: Printful, from its own US, Mexico and Canada centres.
  • US buyers, floating-frame canvas: Lumaprints, for black, oak or walnut floating frames on its 1.25-inch canvas.
  • UK and EU buyers, or more than canvas: Prodigi, with its own facilities in the UK, EU and USA.
  • Buyers in many countries: Gelato, which says it produces locally in 32 countries.
  • You want to choose the factory: Printify, where you are really choosing a print provider inside Printify.

Pick two, order the same artwork from both, and let the samples decide. Then order a sample of every frame colour you plan to sell. A frame colour on screen is only an approximation, and the sample also gives you the unedited photo Etsy asks for on most listings. The sample check lists what to compare when the parcel arrives.

3. Prepare your files in one ratio

Choose one ratio for the collection and offer sizes from that family, so nothing gets cropped. An 8×12 and a 24×36 in print are both 2:3; an 8×10 is 4:5 and would crop a 2:3 design.

2:38×12, 12×18, 16×24, 24×3620×30, 40×60, 60×90
3:412×16, 18×2430×40, 45×60, 60×80
4:58×10, 16×20, 24×3040×50, 80×100
ISO AA4, A3, A221×29.7, 29.7×42, 42×59.4

Buyers in the US and Canada mostly think in inches; buyers in the UK, Europe, Australia and India more often frame in A sizes or centimetre sizes such as 30×40 and 50×70 cm. Three to five sizes from one family, from a small print to one statement size, is plenty for a first listing.

Then check the pixels. At 300 DPI an 18×24 in print needs 5,400 × 7,200 px and a 24×36 in print needs 7,200 × 10,800 px. Many sellers accept 150–200 DPI at large sizes, because large prints are seen from further away, but say clearly what you supply. In my shop, print masters are upscaled before printing; the mockup never is, because WallMock places the file as it is. The print resolution checker shows the largest size your file prints well at.

4. Set up the shop and connect the partner

  1. Add the production partner. In Shop Manager: Settings, Partners you work with, Add a new production partner. Etsy requires you to disclose a print-on-demand partner on your listings.
  2. Match your shipping. Set shipping locations to where the partner ships from.
  3. Connect the partner's Etsy app. Printify publishes the listing; Printful and Gelato publish it as a draft; with Prodigi you create the listing on Etsy first. A draft is the best moment to add your own photos.
  4. Check what a re-sync does to your photos before you upload your own. Some apps can republish their mockups over yours; the Etsy print-on-demand integrations guide lists what each provider sends.

Etsy also charges a one-time shop set-up fee that varies by location. Check it for your country before you open.

5. Price from your cost, not from competitors

Etsy's fees for a US shop, from Etsy's help pages (checked 2026-10-29):

FeeAmount
Listing feeUSD 0.20 per listing, for 4 months
Transaction fee6.5% of the item price plus shipping
Payment processing (US)3% + USD 0.25
Offsite Ads15%, or 12% if you sold USD 10,000 or more in the past 365 days; only on orders from Etsy's ads on other sites, never more than USD 100 per order

Work back from your cost: price = (cost + 0.45) ÷ (1 − 0.095 − margin), where cost is the provider's product plus shipping. For a 30% margin, divide by 0.605. With an example cost of USD 76, that gives USD 126.36; round to 129. Price every size and frame separately, and recheck when the provider changes a price. The pricing guide has a worked table by size, and the Etsy fee calculator does the sum for your country.

6. Make the photos before you write the listing

Etsy's photo rules changed in late 2026, and they decide what your gallery must contain. As our detailed guide to the 2026 rules reads them:

  • Most listings need one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. It doesn't have to be first, and you need one per listing, not one per variation.
  • Other photos may be edited, as long as the item doesn't look different.
  • Print-on-demand items made by a production partner have a narrow allowance for authorised stock-photo mockups. It is not a blanket pass for every rendered room.
  • Etsy plans proactive enforcement of the photo rules in early 2027, with advance notice and no exact date yet.

The technical side: up to 20 photos and 2 videos per listing, at least 2,000 px recommended, and a landscape or square first photo, because it becomes the thumbnail and is cropped in several places.

About 10 well-chosen images cover a wall art listing: a hero room first, a close-up of the frame second, then a scale room, the flat artwork, a few other rooms and a sizes chart. The listing photo set explains each slot, and the crop-safe thumbnail guide covers the first photo. Choose rooms where the art is large in the photo; wide, empty rooms shrink it to a stamp at thumbnail size.

7. Write the listing: title, tags, attributes

  • Title: what it is first, then the art. For example: “Misty Mountain Framed Canvas Wall Art, Panoramic Blue Landscape, Oak Floating Frame”.
  • 13 tags, as phrases: cover the product, the subject, the shape or size, the room or style, and an occasion, each under 20 characters.
  • Attributes: choose the most specific category, then fill every attribute that applies.
  • Description: the first sentence says what it is and what is included (frame or not, hanging hardware, sizes), and who makes and ships it.
  • Alt text: up to 250 characters per photo, describing what that photo shows.

Only use words that are true: “framed” on a listing where the frame is optional wins the click and loses the review. Etsy SEO for wall art has full tag sets.

8. Your first 10 listings: a checklist

Ten listings from one collection is enough to learn which designs and search terms bring visits, and few enough to check every one properly. Before each one goes live:

  • A sample is ordered and photographed for every frame colour you offer.
  • The frame, mat and canvas depth in every mockup match what the partner ships.
  • Every size is in the artwork's ratio, and the file fills the largest at the DPI you state.
  • Every size and frame has its own price from the formula.
  • The production partner is selected, and shipping matches where it ships from.
  • About 10 photos: hero room, frame close-up, scale, flat art, rooms, sizes.
  • One unedited photo of the real item, unless an exception covers your product.
  • Every image is at least 2,000 px on its shortest side and checked at 100%: corners, edges, signature.
  • Title product-first, 13 tags as phrases, attributes filled, alt text on every photo.
  • AI use is disclosed in the description if the artwork is AI-created.

The proof checklist for wall art listings goes deeper on checking images, and wall art listing mistakes lists the ones that cause returns.

9. Month one: what to measure

  • Search terms. In Etsy Stats, choose Etsy search under How shoppers found you. Move terms that bring visits into the titles and tags of similar listings.
  • Keep the numbers apart. Search impressions, visits and listing views answer different questions; don't read a click problem from listing views alone.
  • Change one thing at a time, such as the first photo on one listing, so you know what made the difference.
  • Recheck prices whenever the provider changes a price or you add a frame colour.
  • Plan the seasons ahead. The wall art selling calendar shows when to list for each.

When the first ten are clean, add the next collection the same way. A second collection built on what month one taught you is worth more than fifty unrelated designs.

Questions

How do I start a print-on-demand wall art shop on Etsy?

Pick a narrow niche and one product, sample two print partners, prepare your files in one ratio, add the partner as a production partner on Etsy, price from cost with Etsy's fees included, then publish about 10 listings with accurate photos and read Etsy Stats after a month.

How many listings should a new Etsy wall art shop start with?

Ten from one collection is a sensible start: enough to see which designs and search terms bring visits, and few enough to sample, photograph and check every one properly.

What sizes should I sell wall art in on Etsy?

Sizes from your artwork's ratio, so nothing is cropped: for a 2:3 file, 8×12, 12×18, 16×24 and 24×36 in. US buyers mostly think in inches; UK and European buyers more often in A sizes or centimetres. Check your file has the pixels for the largest size you offer.

How many mockups should an Etsy wall art listing have?

About 10 well-chosen images: a hero room first, a frame close-up second, then a scale room, the flat artwork, other rooms and a sizes chart. Etsy allows up to 20 photos and 2 videos per listing.

Do I need an unedited photo if I sell print on demand?

Most listings need one unedited photo of the actual item. Etsy's policy has a narrow allowance for authorised stock-photo mockups of print-on-demand items; check that it covers your product, and order a sample anyway so you know what buyers receive.

Does WallMock connect to my Etsy shop?

No. WallMock makes the listing photos; you download them and upload them in Etsy's listing editor. It never asks for your Etsy login or any permission on your shop.

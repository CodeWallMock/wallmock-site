Framed canvas vs stretched canvas: which to sell, and how to show each

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Sell stretched canvas when you want the widest size range and a relaxed, frameless look. Sell framed canvas when you want a finished, premium piece in common sizes. Stretched canvas goes much larger at the providers we checked: Printful lists canvas up to 40×60 in but framed canvas only up to 24×36 in (checked 2026-10-29). Show stretched canvas with its edge and wall shadow, and framed canvas with its floating gap and frame colour.

This is narrower than canvas vs framed print: both products here are the same stretched canvas, and one adds a floating frame. To show both in the same room, use the canvas print mockup generator.

The difference in one table

Stretched canvasFramed canvas
What it isCanvas pulled over a wooden stretcher and hung bareThe same canvas set in a floating frame
Edge you seeThe canvas side: image, mirror copy or a plain colourA thin gap, then the frame
LookRelaxed, painterly, modernFinished, premium, gallery-like
SizesSmall to very largeUsually up to about 24×36 in
CornersExposedProtected by the frame
Photo must showDepth, edge style and wall shadowThe gap and the frame colour

The image on the front is the same. The edge, the size range and the price you can charge are what change.

Which to sell

  • By size. Selling 30×40 in and up? At Printful and Gelato that means stretched canvas, because their framed lists stop at 24×36 in. Prodigi's framed canvas goes to 55×55 in.
  • By art. Large abstracts, textured and painterly work look natural unframed. Classic paintings, photography, botanicals and pieces bought as gifts often look more finished framed.
  • By buyer. A buyer furnishing a whole room on a budget may want stretched canvas. A buyer looking for one statement piece may pay for the frame.
  • Both. Many shops offer framed canvas as an upgrade. Etsy lets you add up to 3 variation types, such as size and frame, and link a photo to each option (up to 20 options with photos) (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29).

If you aren't sure the buyer knows what a floater is, add one line: "Framed canvas: the canvas sits inside a floating frame with a small gap, no glass." Our guide to what a floater frame is has fuller wording.

How to show stretched canvas

A stretched canvas has no frame to show, so the photos must show the canvas itself:

  • A room photo with a soft shadow on the wall, so the canvas reads as an object with depth, not a print stuck flat to the wall.
  • A close-up of a sample at an angle, showing the side and a corner. This is where the buyer sees the edge style and the depth.
  • The edge style and depth in words: for example "image wraps around the sides, 1.5 in (38 mm) deep". The canvas wrap edges guide explains the options, and canvas depth covers thin vs deep canvas.

How to show framed canvas

  • The gap. A thin, even dark line between canvas and frame on every side. Without it, the photo reads as a framed print.
  • The frame colour of each variant. One photo per finish you sell, in the same room, makes the choice easy.
  • A corner close-up from a sample, so the buyer sees the frame profile and the gap up close.

The numbers for the gap, frame face and depth are in floating frame canvas mockups.

Questions buyers ask, answered in the listing

Two questions come up for both products: is it ready to hang, and is the frame included? Providers answer them differently, so check yours (checked 2026-10-29):

ProviderHanging, as the provider states it
PrintfulCanvas: mounting brackets included. Thin canvas: wall mounts attached. Framed canvas: hanging hardware attached
GelatoFramed canvas arrives ready to hang, with hanging hardware included
ProdigiHanging equipment not included, for its stretched and framed canvas

Put the answer in the first lines of the description: "Ready to hang, hardware included" or "Hanging hardware not included". For framed canvas, also say "frame included" and name the colour, so nobody expects a bare canvas, or a frame with glass.

Same room, same art, one change

When you offer both, keep everything the same except the product: same room, same artwork, same size on the wall. Buyers can then compare like with like. A different room for each option makes the frame look like a style change rather than an upgrade.

Questions

Does a canvas print need a frame?

No. A stretched canvas is ready to hang as it is, with its sides finished. A floating frame is an optional upgrade that adds a finished edge and protects the corners.

Which looks more premium, framed or stretched canvas?

Framed canvas usually reads as more finished and premium. Stretched canvas reads as relaxed and modern, and suits very large pieces.

Can a buyer frame a stretched canvas later?

Yes, with a floating frame made for that canvas size and depth. Tell buyers the exact canvas size and depth so they can order the right frame.

What is the largest framed canvas size?

It depends on the provider. Printful's and Gelato's framed canvas lists stop at 24×36 in; Prodigi lists framed canvas up to 55×55 in (checked 2026-10-29).

Should I offer framed and stretched canvas in one listing?

You can. Use the product as a variation and link a photo to each option, ideally the same room with only the product changed.

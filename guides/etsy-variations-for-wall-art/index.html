Etsy variations for wall art: size, frame colour and a photo for every option

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Use Etsy variations for size and frame colour, and give each frame colour its own linked photo, made in the same room so only the frame changes. Etsy lets you link a photo to each option of a variation, for up to 20 options. Sell canvas and framed prints as separate listings, because they need different photos, titles and prices. Show sizes in one size guide image rather than one photo per size.

Variations let one listing sell many sizes and finishes, but a buyer who picks "walnut" expects to see walnut. This guide sets up the variations and plans a photo for every option you sell; an Etsy mockup generator for framed canvas and prints makes the matching set of room photos.

What Etsy lets you vary

Etsy's help page on variations sets these limits (checked 2026-10-29):

RuleEtsy's limit
Variation types per listingUp to 3, such as size, colour or style
Option combinationsUp to 4,900 with 1 or 2 variation types; up to 2,500 with 3
Different prices, SKUs or quantitiesFor up to 400 option combinations
Photos linked to optionsOne photo per option; if a variation has more than 20 options, only 20 can have photos
Photos and videos per listingUp to 20 photos and 2 videos of 5 to 15 seconds
Digital listingsVariations are not supported

Sources: Etsy: How to add variations, Etsy: How to create a listing.

Two more details matter for wall art. The variation names you can pick depend on the category you chose. And buyers can't filter search results by a custom variation, so use Etsy's own option names where your category offers them. If a print-on-demand app creates your listings, it may support fewer variation types than Etsy does; check the app's own limits.

A variation plan for framed canvas and framed prints

The cleanest set-up for most wall art shops is two variation types in one listing per product:

  1. Size. Only sizes in your artwork's ratio family, so no size crops the art. A 2:3 piece fits 12 × 18, 16 × 24, 20 × 30 and 24 × 36 in; a 3:4 piece fits 12 × 16, 18 × 24 and 30 × 40 in. The print size calculator lists the family for your file.
  2. Frame colour. Only the finishes your print provider makes, named the way you will describe them in the photos.

Four sizes × five frame colours is 20 combinations, well inside Etsy's limits, and each can have its own price. In the variation settings, turn on the price toggle for both types if bigger sizes and some frames cost you more to make; our guide to pricing wall art on Etsy works through the numbers by size.

Canvas or framed print: one listing or two?

You can use a third variation type such as style for "framed canvas" and "framed print". It is usually better as two listings:

  • Different photos. A floating-frame canvas shows a gap around the canvas; a framed print shows a frame over the paper, often with a mat. One set of photos can't show both honestly.
  • Different titles and tags. "Framed canvas wall art" and "framed print with mat" are different searches. One title can't lead with both.
  • Simpler choices. Three drop-downs on a phone is a lot to ask, and every extra choice is one more way to order the wrong thing.

Link the two listings in each description so a buyer who wants the other product can find it. The guide to canvas vs framed print mockups shows what each product looks like on the wall.

A photo for every frame colour

On Etsy.com, the steps from Etsy's help page are:

  1. Add all the photos to the listing first, in the Photos and video section.
  2. Open Variations and choose Manage variations.
  3. Select the pencil icon on the frame colour variation and turn on Link photos to this variation.
  4. Pick the photo for each option, then select Done and Apply.

Make the linked photos a matched set:

  • Same room, same wall position and same artwork size in every colour, so only the frame changes. A smaller picture with a heavier frame looks like a different size.
  • Name the photo files by colour (for example pier-walnut.jpg) so you link the right one.
  • Check each colour against your provider's sample. "Natural" from one provider can be paler than another's oak.

Etsy's own SEO advice also suggests adding images that show different variations of your item (source).

Fit the options into 20 photos

Linked photos come out of the same 20-photo allowance as the rest of the listing. A plan for a framed canvas listing with five frame colours:

PhotosWhat to show
1Hero room in your best-selling colour
2Scale above a sofa or bed
3Size guide image with every size you sell
4Close-up of the frame corner and canvas edge
5An unedited photo of a sample you ordered
6–10The five frame colours in one room, each linked to its option
11–14Other rooms: bedroom, dining room, a bold wall
15–20Spare: a matching set, a seasonal room, care and hanging

The hero room can double as one of the colour photos. Etsy needs at least one unedited photo of the actual item for most listings, with an exception for authorised stock mockups of print-on-demand items (Etsy). The 20-slot listing photo plan covers every other slot.

Questions

How many variations can an Etsy listing have?

Etsy's help page says up to 3 variation types, such as size and colour. Listings with 1 or 2 types can have up to 4,900 option combinations, and listings with 3 types up to 2,500.

Can I add a different photo for each Etsy variation?

Yes. Turn on Link photos to this variation, then pick a photo for each option. If a variation has more than 20 options, only 20 can have photos.

Should frame colour or size get the linked photos?

Frame colour, because it changes how the piece looks. Sizes are clearer in one size guide image that shows them side by side.

Can I sell canvas and framed prints in one Etsy listing?

You can add a third variation for the product, but separate listings are usually clearer: each gets photos, a title and tags that match exactly what it is.

Can digital downloads have variations on Etsy?

No. Etsy says variations are not supported for digital listings, so list each file set as it is or describe the included ratios in the listing.

Do Etsy variations need different prices?

Only if your costs differ. Etsy lets you set different prices, SKUs and quantities for up to 400 option combinations.

Prodigi wall art mockups: framed canvas, box frames and mounts

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Prodigi makes 3D rendered mockups in its dashboard for wall art such as classic frames, box frames and stretched, framed and rolled canvas, and AI scene mockups (15 free to try). Its framed canvas is a floater frame with a 5 mm gap and a 12 mm frame face; its box frames stand 33 mm from the wall; mounts come in snow white, off-white or black. Use the 3D views for product detail, and add room photos that show the same frame, gap and mount.

Prodigi publishes more frame measurements than most print-on-demand providers, which makes matching your listing photos easier. Here is what its mockup tools give you, the numbers to match, and how a frame mockup generator or framed canvas mockups fill in the rooms.

Prodigi's mockup options

Prodigi's mockups page lists two kinds (checked 2026-10-29):

  • 3D rendered mockups. For wall art it lists classic frames, box frames, stretched canvas, framed canvas and rolled canvas. To use them, create an order in the dashboard, add your artwork, and switch to Mockup view at the top left. Prodigi's 2024 announcement says each 3D mockup gives three images, angled, close-up and face-on, and that you can download the full-size image (source).
  • AI scene mockups. You choose lighting and describe the setting. Prodigi says you can try 15 AI mockups free and that Prodigi Pro merchants get more access.

The mockups page doesn't say whether you can upload your own room or scene. Its Etsy FAQ says you can also use mockups made in other apps, as long as you assign Prodigi as the fulfilment partner (source).

A floating-frame mockup only suits the first one. Prodigi also says a listed frame size is the size of the glaze, not including the frame (source), so the outer size is larger than the name suggests.

Mounts: colours and widths

Prodigi's mounts (mats) come in snow white, off-white and black, cut from 2.4 mm acid-free, conservation-grade card. They are optional on classic and box frames. The width depends on the frame size (source):

Frame sizeMount width
Up to 250×250 mm (10×10 in)25 mm
Up to 280×355 mm (11×14 in)38 mm
300×400 mm (12×16 in) and above50 mm

Worked example: a 30×40 cm frame takes a 5 cm mount. If that border runs on all four sides, the print window is about 20×30 cm. A mockup that shows a thin 2 cm border on that frame would set the wrong expectation. Work out your own sizes with the frame and mat size calculator, and see frame and mat proportions for how mat width changes the look.

When to add your own room photos

Prodigi's 3D views are good for detail: the angle shows depth, the close-up shows the frame. Room photos do a different job. They show the piece at a believable size, in a style the buyer recognises, and they keep a collection consistent across your shop.

On Etsy, Prodigi says you can't yet create listings in Prodigi and sync them to Etsy. You create the listing on Etsy first, then import it into Prodigi (source). So the photos on the listing are the ones you upload. Etsy requires at least one unedited photo of the actual item for most listings, with a print-on-demand exception for stock photo mockups the owner has authorised (Etsy Help). A photo of a Prodigi sample covers that safely.

Room photos also help when you sell a matching pair or trio in Prodigi frames. Hung together in one room at believable spacing, the set reads as a set, which a single product render can't show. Our guide to pairs and trios covers layouts and gaps, and the guide to Etsy print-on-demand integrations compares how Prodigi's import differs from Printify, Printful, Gelato and Gooten.

Samples and frame packs

Prodigi offers 50% off your first sample order. Place it through the manual order form, then email the order number to Prodigi's support team for a 50% refund. It applies to first sample orders only, one product type per order, under a fair use policy, and customs charges may apply (source, checked 2026-10-29). Its framed canvas and frame pages also offer a frame sample pack with moulding samples in each colour.

The moulding pack is the cheapest way to see six or eight frame colours side by side before you choose which to photograph. Then follow our sample checklist to compare a full sample with your mockup.

Questions

Does Prodigi have a mockup generator?

Yes. Prodigi makes 3D rendered mockups in its dashboard image editor, with three views per product, and AI scene mockups where you choose the lighting and describe the setting.

Are Prodigi mockups free?

Prodigi says you can try 15 AI mockups free and that Prodigi Pro merchants get more access (checked 2026-10-29). Its 3D mockups are in the dashboard when you create an order.

What is the gap on Prodigi's framed canvas?

5 mm. Prodigi's framed canvas is a floater frame with a 12 mm frame face, 53 mm deep from the wall, around a 38 mm canvas.

What colours are Prodigi mounts?

Snow white, off-white and black, cut from 2.4 mm acid-free card. The width is 25, 38 or 50 mm depending on the frame size.

Can I create Etsy listings from Prodigi?

Not currently, according to Prodigi's Etsy FAQ. Create the listing on Etsy first, then import it into Prodigi and switch on fulfilment for each product.

Does Prodigi framed canvas come with hanging hardware?

No. Prodigi's framed canvas page says hanging equipment is not included, so say so in your listing.

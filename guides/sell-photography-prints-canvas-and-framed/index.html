How to sell photography prints as canvas and framed prints: mockups for photographers

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Choose the product per photo: framed prints with a mat for fine detail, black-and-white and smaller sizes; canvas or floating-frame canvas for big, soft, painterly scenes. Check the file: a 24-megapixel image (6000 × 4000 px) prints 20 × 13.3 inches at 300 DPI. Crop to standard ratios, order a sample, then list each photo with a room hero, a close-up of the frame and a scale shot, through a print-on-demand partner on Etsy or Shopify.

A photograph competes with a huge supply of other images, so the product and the room photo do most of the selling. Plan both, and make the listing images with a frame mockup generator that keeps your photo exactly as you shot and edited it.

How big can your files print?

Print size in inches = pixels ÷ DPI. Prodigi suggests 300 DPI for print-on-demand products, and says 150 may be enough for work without fine details (Prodigi, checked 2026-10-29).

Any crop reduces these numbers, so measure the final, cropped file. A detailed photo meant for a framed print viewed up close should stay near 300 DPI. A soft canvas seen from across a room can go lower, but not below 150. The free print resolution checker does the sums for your own file.

Crop to ratios people can frame

Most camera files are 3:2, which is the same family as 4×6, 8×12, 12×18, 16×24, 20×30 and 24×36 inches. Those sizes print without cropping. Popular sizes such as 8×10, 11×14 and 16×20 belong to other ratio families, so selling them means cropping the photo or adding a border.

Decide this per photo, before you make any mockups. A crop that cuts a pier's end or a horizon's balance changes the photograph. If you sell several ratios, check each crop, and make the main mockups at the ratio most buyers will order. The print ratio guide lists every family.

Order a sample and photograph it

Etsy requires at least one unedited photo for most items; for print-on-demand items it allows an authorised stock mockup instead (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29). Even so, photographers have an advantage here: you can photograph a sample well.

  • Order one sample per product, such as a framed print and a canvas.
  • Compare its colours with your screen in daylight. If the print is darker, adjust your print file, not the mockups.
  • Shoot the sample straight on, then a corner close-up showing the frame, mat or canvas edge.
  • Note the paper or canvas type and frame depth for your description.

Edit for print, not for screen

Screens glow; paper and canvas don't. A photo that looks right on a bright laptop often prints darker and flatter. Compare your sample with the screen in daylight and, if needed, make a print version of the file with slightly lifted shadows. Keep it separate from your web version.

In the description, say what the buyer gets: paper or canvas type, frame colour and depth, mat or no mat, and every size. If you sell a limited edition, state the edition size and how you number it, and keep to it.

Listing photos that sell a photograph

Room mockups do the job a gallery wall does: they show a buyer what the photo will feel like at home. A plan that works:

  1. Hero room that matches the photo's mood: a calm neutral room for black and white, a light coastal room for seascapes.
  2. Scale: the print above a sofa, bed or sideboard.
  3. Close-up of the frame corner, mat or canvas edge.
  4. The photograph itself, flat and large.
  5. Two to four alternative rooms, including one bold wall.
  6. Sizes and options, and the series if you sell sets.

Where to sell: Etsy, Shopify or both

On Etsy, a print-on-demand provider is a production partner you list in your shop, and the photo rules above apply. On Shopify you run your own store and add a provider's app; see how to sell wall art on Shopify. Our guide to choosing a print-on-demand provider compares what each one makes.

If a photo shows a recognisable person, a private property or a trademark, check whether you need permission before selling it. This guide isn't legal advice.

Questions

How do I sell my photography as prints?

Choose a product for each photo, check the file is big enough, crop to standard ratios, order a sample, and list through a print-on-demand partner on Etsy or Shopify with room mockups and a sample photo.

How big can I print a 24-megapixel photo?

A 6000 × 4000 px file prints 20 × 13.3 inches at 300 DPI, or about 40 × 26.7 inches at 150 DPI, before any cropping.

Should I sell photos as canvas or framed prints?

Framed prints with a mat suit fine detail, black and white and smaller sizes. Canvas suits large, soft, painterly scenes. Many photographers offer both.

Which ratio should I crop photos to?

Camera files are usually 3:2, which prints at 4×6, 8×12, 12×18, 16×24, 20×30 and 24×36 inches without cropping. Sizes like 8×10 and 16×20 need a crop.

Can I use mockups for photography listings on Etsy?

Yes, if they accurately show what the buyer receives. Most items also need an unedited photo; print-on-demand items may use an authorised stock mockup instead.

Print on demand wall art: how to choose a provider

Published by WallMock · Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer: No single print-on-demand provider is best for every wall art shop. Choose by three things: the exact product you sell (framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed print), where your buyers live, and whether it connects to Etsy or Shopify. Printify, Printful, Gelato, Prodigi, Lumaprints and Gooten all list wall art and connect to both platforms, but their frames and print locations differ. Order samples before you list. Then make listing photos that match the product you chose.

Picking a print-on-demand provider decides what your buyer receives, so it decides what your listing photos must show. Once you have chosen, an Etsy mockup generator for print-on-demand wall art makes the room photos.

Decide these four things first

Comparing providers is quicker once you know what you need. Write down:

  1. Product. Framed canvas, stretched (gallery-wrapped) canvas, framed print with or without a mat, or unframed poster. Not every provider makes every one, and frame colours differ.
  2. Buyers' countries. A provider that prints near your buyers usually means cheaper, faster shipping. Printify says the same when it explains how to choose a print provider (source).
  3. Sales channel. Etsy, Shopify or both. Check the integration before you build a catalogue.
  4. Sizes and ratios. Your artwork ratio (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, ISO A) has to match sizes the provider offers, or you'll crop the art.

What the table doesn't tell you

  • Printify varies by provider. Printify says its print providers differ in location, prices, shipping, product models, print areas and print methods (source). Two "framed canvas" listings on Printify can come from different factories, so sample the exact provider.
  • Printful picks the facility. Printful chooses the fulfilment centre for each order automatically, and you can't select one (source). Its UK centre only ships to UK addresses.
  • Gelato frames differ by region. Its framed canvas frame is 42 mm deep in North America and 32 mm in Europe (source). Your listing text should state the frame depth for each region.
  • US-only production. Lumaprints names two US sites. That suits US buyers. For buyers abroad, check shipping before you list.

A checklist before you list

  • Order a sample of every frame colour you plan to sell, and photograph it.
  • Check the provider's sizes against your artwork ratio with the print ratio guide.
  • Check your file is big enough for the largest size with the print resolution checker.
  • Stay inside the variation limits. For products it publishes to Etsy, Printify lists up to two options per listing, 70 choices per option and 400 variants in total (source); Etsy itself now allows up to three variation types (see Etsy variations for wall art).
  • Add the provider as a production partner on Etsy. Etsy names Printful, Printify and Gooten as examples of print-on-demand partners (source).
  • Set shipping origins to the partner's location, as Etsy asks.
  • Make room photos that show the same frame, mat and finish as the sample.

Why listing photos matter more with print on demand

When many shops sell the same base product from the same provider, the product is the same. What differs is your art and your photos. Provider mockups are available to everyone who sells that product. Your own room photos are something a competitor doesn't have.

Good wall art photos show the piece in a believable room, at a believable size, in the frame the buyer will get. For a slot-by-slot plan, see Etsy listing photos for wall art. For the frame choice itself, see canvas vs framed print mockups.

Questions

What is the best print on demand for wall art?

It depends on your product, your buyers' countries and your sales channel. Shortlist providers that make your exact product (framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed print), print near your buyers and connect to Etsy or Shopify, then order samples and compare.

Printful vs Printify for posters: what's the difference?

Printful runs its own fulfilment centres and chooses one per order. Printify connects you to a network of print providers, so quality, price and location depend on the provider you pick. Both offer framed and unframed posters and connect to Etsy and Shopify.

Does Printful or Printify offer framed canvas?

Yes. Printify lists framed canvas with a pine wood frame. Printful lists framed canvas alongside standard and thin canvas. Frame colours depend on the product and provider, so check before you make mockups.

Which print on demand providers print in the UK or Europe?

For wall art, Printful lists centres in Spain and the UK (UK addresses only); Riga handles apparel. Prodigi says it has its own facilities in the UK and EU. Gelato says it produces locally in 32 countries. Printify depends on the print provider you choose.

Do I have to list my print provider on Etsy?

Yes. Etsy requires sellers who use production assistance to disclose it on the relevant listings. Add the provider under Shop Manager, Settings, Partners you work with, then choose it on each listing.

Can I use my own mockups with a print on demand provider?

Yes. Printify lets you upload custom mockups to its Mockup Library. With Printful you add them directly to your Etsy or Shopify listing. Make sure the mockup matches the real product.

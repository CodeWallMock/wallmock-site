Framed Print Mockups for UK Wall Art Sellers

Published by WallMock · Updated 5 November 2026

A printed border and a window mount need different images

A printed border is part of the paper. A window mount is a separate board that sits in front of the print. The opening can hide some of the paper, so the same artwork may show less of its edge in the mounted variant. theprintspace explains how its framed variants handle crops, borders and window mounts.

For an illustrative example, a 210 × 297 mm A4 sheet behind a centred 200 × 287 mm opening loses 5 mm of visible paper on each edge. Ask for your supplier’s actual opening before laying out lettering or a signature near the edge.

Match each listing image to an option the buyer can select

  1. Write down the frame colour, mount colour and paper size for each option.
  2. Keep the artwork and room the same when comparing mounted and unmounted looks, so the buyer can see the change.
  3. Pair the room images with a product detail that shows the actual mount and frame. Label both the paper and outside frame sizes.

Inkthreadable’s September 2026 update is one example of why to check again before reusing older mockups. It announced mount options for A2, A3 and A4 frames and new frame colours. Confirm the combination you intend to sell in your supplier’s current catalogue.

For Etsy, check its image requirements for your listing type. For the steps to organise the options, see the wall art variations guide.

Try your mounted print in WallMock

Try the prepared artwork for your A-size print, then compare the mounted and unmounted variants in the same room. Check the print edges and the mount opening against the supplier’s measurements.

WallMock supports square 1:1, standard portrait and landscape ratios (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:7 and their reverses), and long formats (1:2, 2:1, 1:3 and 3:1). Choose a room that fits the artwork shape. For other print sizes such as 11 × 14 in, prepare the file to the intended print opening and check the result in the preview. See the print ratios and sizes guide.

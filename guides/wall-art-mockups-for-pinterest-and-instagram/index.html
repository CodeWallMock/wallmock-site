Wall art mockups for Pinterest pins and Instagram: 2:3 and 4:5 crops

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Pinterest recommends 2:3 images at 1000 × 1500 px, so crop your wall art mockup to a tall 2:3 with the whole frame inside. For an Instagram feed post, use 4:5 portrait: Meta's Instagram publishing documentation accepts 4:5 to 1.91:1 and scales images down to 1440 px wide, so 1440 × 1800 px is the largest 4:5. Crop from your full-size export, keep space around the art, and link each pin to the listing.

Pinterest and Instagram favour tall images, and most room mockups are square or landscape. This guide crops one mockup for both without cutting the art. For the room photos themselves, use the Etsy mockup generator, then crop.

Pinterest's ad specs add a warning worth following for normal Pins too: Pins taller than 2:3 might get cut off in people's feeds (Pinterest).

How to crop a room mockup to 2:3 and 4:5

Crop from the full-size export, never from a small preview, then resize down. With example export sizes:

Starting image2:3 crop (Pinterest)4:5 crop (Instagram)
4096 × 4096 px square2731 × 4096 px, then resize to 1000 × 15003277 × 4096 px, then resize to 1440 × 1800
4096 × 3072 px landscape (4:3)2048 × 3072 px, then resize to 1000 × 15002458 × 3072 px, then resize to 1440 × 1800

A landscape room loses the most to a tall crop: a 2:3 crop of a 4:3 image keeps only half its width. So the art must sit in the middle half of a landscape room for a 2:3 pin to keep the whole frame. Portrait art in a tall room crops best of all; wide panoramas suit Instagram's landscape shape better than a pin.

  • The whole frame is inside the crop, with wall on all four sides.
  • The art is the largest thing that isn't wall, and the furniture still shows scale.
  • Text, if you add any, stays off the artwork.
  • The file is sRGB, so frame finishes keep their colour.

Match the room to the art's shape. Portrait art fills a 2:3 pin naturally. Landscape art in a 2:3 pin ends up small, with a lot of wall above and furniture below; give it a room where the furniture fills the lower part, or save landscape pieces for Instagram's wider shapes.

The same safe-area thinking applies to Etsy's thumbnails; see the crop-safe Etsy thumbnail.

Pins that bring buyers back to the listing

  • Link every pin. Pinterest suggests adding a URL that drives traffic back to your site (Pinterest). Link the exact listing, not the shop home page.
  • Use the Etsy Share & Save link. If the listing is on Etsy, a trackable listing link counts toward Share & Save, which refunds 4% of the eligible order total when a buyer orders within 30 days of clicking (Etsy, checked 2026-10-29).
  • Title and description. Pin titles can be up to 100 characters and descriptions up to 800 (Pinterest). Say what it is and where it goes: "Misty mountain framed canvas for a living room".
  • Text, used lightly. Pinterest notes that well-performing Pins can use stylised effects or text (Pinterest). On wall art, keep it to one short line, such as "framed canvas, 5 sizes", placed on the wall or a band, never over the art.
  • Several pins per artwork. A hero room, a bedroom and a close view each make a different pin for the same listing.
  • Boards by room or style, such as "Japandi living room art" or "Coastal bedroom prints", matching how buyers search.

Instagram: posts, carousels and the grid

  • Use 4:5 for single posts. It is the tallest shape in Meta's documentation, so it fills more of a phone screen than a square or landscape post.
  • Carousels tell the listing's story: the hero room, a scale photo, a close-up of the frame, then another room. Keep every slide the same shape.
  • Keep the frame whole. Your profile grid may show a cropped version of each post, so check the grid view after posting and keep the art in the centre.
  • One look per collection. Posts from the same room set read as one collection on your profile; see one look for the whole shop.

Questions

What size should a Pinterest pin be for wall art?

Pinterest recommends a 2:3 image at 1000 × 1500 px. Crop the room mockup so the whole frame sits inside with wall around it.

What size should an Instagram post of wall art be?

Use 4:5 portrait. Meta's Instagram publishing documentation accepts 4:5 to 1.91:1 and scales images down to 1440 px wide, so 1440 × 1800 px is the largest 4:5.

Can I use the same mockup for Pinterest and Instagram?

Yes. Crop one full-size room image twice: a 2:3 crop for Pinterest and a 4:5 crop for Instagram, keeping the art centred in both.

Should Pinterest pins link to Etsy?

Link each pin to the exact listing. On Etsy, a Share & Save trackable link earns a 4% refund on eligible orders placed within 30 days of a click.

What about video pins?

Pinterest recommends 9:16 at 1080 × 1920 px for video, with space left at the top and bottom for the player controls.

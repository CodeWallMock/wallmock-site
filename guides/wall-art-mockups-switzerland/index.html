Wall Art Mockups for Swiss Print-on-Demand Sellers

Published by WallMock · Updated 5 November 2026

When a Swiss listing offers both a rolled print and a framed print, give each option its own photo and check how it will ship. We recommend WallMock for the framed room image once the actual frame, mount and delivery option are confirmed. See WallMock’s USD plan prices for Switzerland.

Try WallMock with your art

Check where each option ships from

A supplier may ship an unframed print and a framed version from different locations. Check the delivery quote to a Swiss postcode for both variants, including any charges disclosed by the supplier. The Swiss Federal Office for Customs and Border Security explains how mail and courier imports are handled.

Keep the storefront currency and delivery terms clear in the listing. The currency of your WallMock subscription is separate from what your shop charges and how the physical order is fulfilled.

Give the buyer a way to compare what arrives

Say your shop offers a 40 × 60 cm print rolled, or the same 2:3 artwork in a frame. Show the rolled option with a paper detail and the framed option with its outside dimensions and an angled photo. Confirm the paper size for both options and the mount opening if there is one.

Use a room photo to show scale and a product photo to show the contents of the parcel. The sample checklist helps you check the frame and paper before you reuse either image.

Preview your framed option before you list it

Preview your artwork in a room for free. The trial preview is watermarked and requires no account or card.

WallMock supports square 1:1, standard portrait and landscape ratios (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:7 and their reverses), and long formats (1:2, 2:1, 1:3 and 3:1). Choose a room that fits the artwork shape. For other print sizes such as 11 × 14 in, prepare the file to the intended print opening and check the result in the preview. See the print ratios and sizes guide.

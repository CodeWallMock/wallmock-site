Printable wall art vs print-on-demand framed prints: profit per sale and listing photos

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Printables keep most of a small price; print-on-demand framed prints and canvas keep a smaller share of a much larger one. With Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee and 20-cent listing fee, our example keeps about 7 US dollars from an 8-dollar printable and about 23.50 from a 60-dollar framed print that costs 30 to make and ship: one framed sale is worth about three and a half printable sales. Printable photos show the file framed; print-on-demand photos show the exact product.

Both models can sell the same artwork. What changes is what the buyer gets, what you keep and what the photos must prove. For printables, see our guide to printable wall art mockups; for physical products, a frame mockup generator shows the frame you ship.

Etsy's fees, checked 29 October 2026

From Etsy's help page on fees (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29):

  • Listing fee: 20 US cents to create or renew a listing.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the total order amount, in your listing currency.
  • Payment processing: a set rate plus a percentage, which varies by country. Check the rate for your country.
  • Offsite Ads: if you made less than 10,000 US dollars on Etsy in the past 365 days, a 15% fee on orders that come from those ads.

What changes besides profit

  • Effort per sale. A printable delivers itself. A physical order can be late, damaged or lost, and you handle the buyer's questions either way.
  • Buyer. A printable buyer is happy to print and frame. A framed-print buyer wants something ready to hang the day it arrives.
  • Sizes. A printable can include every ratio's files. A physical listing offers the sizes your provider makes. See the print ratio guide.
  • Risk. Printables have no stock and no shipping. Print on demand has no stock either, but you pay the provider for every order.
  • Reviews. With print on demand, a buyer reviews the print quality, the frame and the delivery too, so order samples and choose the provider carefully.
  • Copying. A downloaded file is easy to pass on after purchase. A framed print isn't.

Price checks before you list

Run these sums for every size and product, not just the one you expect to sell most:

  1. Find the full provider cost for the size, including shipping to your main market.
  2. Add Etsy's fees: 6.5% of the order, your country's payment processing, and the listing fee.
  3. Check the Offsite Ads case. If a 15% fee would leave you with almost nothing, raise the price or drop that size.
  4. Set a floor. Decide the least you're willing to keep per sale, and price every variant above it.
  5. Recheck when the provider changes prices, which providers do from time to time.

For printables, the sums are simpler, so the work goes into the files: include every ratio buyers need and test that each one prints cleanly at its largest size with the print resolution checker.

Listing photos for printables

Show a printable the way buyers will display it: in a simple black, white or oak frame with a white mat, in three to five rooms. Say "digital download" clearly on an image and in the first line of the description. Etsy asks for a preview of the actual content, so include the artwork itself and an image listing the files and ratios. Our printable wall art mockups guide has the full plan.

Listing photos for print-on-demand framed prints and canvas

Here the photo promises a product, so it must match it exactly: the frame colour, mat or no mat, the canvas's wrapped edge or the floating frame's gap, and a believable size. Add a close-up of a corner and, ideally, a photo of a sample. Our guide to canvas vs framed print mockups shows how each product should look.

Selling both from one artwork

Many artworks can be sold both ways. Keep them as separate listings so each has the right photos, description and delivery details. Use the same rooms for both, so your shop looks consistent, and change only the product: a thin frame with a mat for the printable, the exact frame or canvas for the physical version.

Questions

Is it more profitable to sell printables or print on demand?

Per sale, print on demand usually keeps more money; for effort, printables are simpler. In our example a framed print kept about as much as three and a half printables. Work it out with your own prices and provider costs.

What fees does Etsy charge on a sale?

Etsy's help page lists 20 US cents to create or renew a listing, a 6.5% transaction fee on the total order amount, a payment processing fee that varies by country, and 15% on Offsite Ads orders for shops under 10,000 US dollars a year.

Can I sell the same artwork as a printable and a framed print?

Yes, as separate listings, each with photos and a description that match what the buyer receives.

Do printables need mockups?

Yes. Buyers want to see the art framed in a room. Etsy also asks digital listings to include a preview of the actual content.

Do print-on-demand listings need a photo of the actual product?

Etsy requires at least one unedited photo for most items. For print-on-demand items, it allows a stock photo mockup if its owner has authorised its use.

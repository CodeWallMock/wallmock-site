Printify framed canvas: products, sizes, frames and listing photos

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Printify sells framed canvas through several print providers, so the frame, sizes and depth depend on the product you pick. Its framed canvas page lists gallery canvas wraps in black or walnut frames (17 portrait sizes from 5×7 to 32×48 in), a landscape version up to 60×20 in, and an eco matte canvas in a black pine frame. Find the depth in each product's size guide, order one sample, then make room photos in the same frame colour.

"Framed canvas" on Printify is not one product. This guide sorts out the versions, what to check before you list one, and how to make framed canvas mockups that match it. Selling on Etsy? The Etsy mockup generator covers the rest of the listing photos.

Depth, gap and frame width: where to find them

The framed canvas page doesn't state the depth, the frame face width or whether the canvas floats with a gap inside the frame (checked 2026-10-29). Those details sit in each product's size guide: Printify says to open the product in the Printify Catalog and scroll down to the measurements (size guides).

Check them for every provider you consider. Printify says its print providers differ in location, product and shipping prices, product models and variants, print areas, and printing equipment and methods (provider differences). Two framed canvases with the same name can come from different factories.

For each candidate, write down:

  • outer size for each variant you plan to sell;
  • frame face width and depth from the wall;
  • whether there is a visible gap between canvas and frame, and roughly how wide;
  • the frame colour names (for example black or walnut);
  • hanging hardware and bumpers;
  • which countries it ships to.

For comparison, some providers publish these numbers on the product page. Prodigi's floater framed canvas states a 5 mm gap and a 12 mm frame face (see our Prodigi mockups guide). That tells you what to look for in a Printify size guide.

Where it ships from, and where it can't go

Printing happens at the print provider's location. Printify says a local provider can mean lower shipping costs and faster delivery (source). Its shipping restrictions page lists limits that matter for canvas sellers (checked 2026-10-29):

  • Jondo: certain larger sizes of Matte Canvas, Framed (Multi-color) ship only to six named countries.
  • merchOne: its stretched canvases (0.75 in and 1.5 in) ship within the US only.
  • Prima Printing: larger sizes of Classic Canvas ship within Australia only.

If most of your buyers are outside the US, check this page before you pick a provider. It's no use building listing photos for a size your buyers can't order.

Order one sample before you photograph anything

Printify doesn't give free samples. Sample orders are placed at regular pricing: go to My Products or Orders, click Create order and choose Sample. Your account address is filled in as the shipping address (sample ordering, checked 2026-10-29). Printify also says its mockups are representations, and that you should order samples from your chosen providers to see what customers receive (source).

Order the frame colour you plan to show most. When it arrives, check:

  • Frame colour. "Walnut" can read warm brown or near-black in daylight. Your mockup should match what you see, not the name.
  • Gap. Is there a dark line between canvas and frame, or does the canvas touch the frame?
  • Edges and depth. Look from the side. That is what a buyer sees when the canvas hangs next to a doorway.
  • Back. Hardware and bumpers, so your description is accurate.

When you publish, keep within Etsy's limits. Printify says Etsy allows up to 20 mockups per product across all colour variants, and publishing fails above that (source). Our guide to replacing Printify mockups shows how to keep your room photos when you republish.

Questions

Does Printify have framed canvas?

Yes. Printify's framed canvas page lists gallery canvas wraps with square, vertical and horizontal frames, and an eco-friendly matte canvas in a black frame. More framed canvas listings from other providers appear in the catalog.

What frame colours does Printify framed canvas come in?

The gallery canvas wraps come in black or walnut, and the eco matte canvas in black pine only (checked 2026-10-29). Other providers in the catalog may offer different colours, so check the product you choose.

How deep is a Printify framed canvas?

Printify's framed canvas page doesn't say. Open the product in the Printify Catalog and scroll down to its size guide, and check each print provider separately, because they differ.

Does Printify framed canvas come ready to hang?

Printify says the gallery canvas wraps have hanging hardware and felt bumpers fitted, and the eco matte canvas comes with a sawtooth hanger.

Can I get a free sample from Printify?

No. Printify says sample orders are placed at regular pricing. Choose Sample after clicking Create order, and it ships to your account address.

Does Printify's AI Mockups tool make framed canvas scenes?

Its page lists canvases among supported products and doesn't mention framed products (checked 2026-10-29). AI Mockups are included with Printify Premium.

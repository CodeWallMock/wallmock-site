Print on demand posters and framed posters: paper, frames and listing photos

Published by WallMock · Updated 8 November 2026

Short answer: Choose a poster supplier by three details your buyer will notice: the paper (matte, luster or semi-gloss, and its weight), the frame (material, colour and whether a mat is offered) and how it arrives (ready to hang, or frame and poster in one box). Printful, Printify, Gelato, Prodigi and Printseekers all make framed posters but differ on each of those. Posters are the cheapest way into wall art, and the easiest product to photograph badly: a framed poster in a stock room looks like every other listing from the same supplier. This guide compares what five print-on-demand suppliers actually make, then shows how to choose sizes and make framed poster mockups that match the product you ship.

Poster, framed poster or framed print?

Suppliers use the words loosely, so decide which of three products you are selling before you compare prices:

  • Poster (unframed). A print on photo or matte paper, shipped rolled or flat. The buyer frames it. Cheapest to make and ship, and the easiest to undercut.
  • Framed poster. The same paper in a ready-made frame, usually with an acrylic or plexiglass front rather than glass. This is what most print-on-demand “framed art” is.
  • Framed fine-art print. Heavier archival or fine-art paper, often with a mat (a mount) and a choice of glazing. Prodigi’s classic frame is an example. It costs more and photographs as a more finished piece.

Your title, your photos and your price should all describe the same one. A buyer who sees a mat in the photo and receives a frame without one has a fair complaint.

Choosing the paper: matte, luster or semi-gloss

Paper decides how the poster looks on a wall more than the frame does.

  • Matte has no sheen. It suits illustration, line art, muted palettes and anything that will hang opposite a window, because there is nothing to reflect. Under an acrylic front, the front still reflects; matte paper just doesn’t add a second shine.
  • Luster or semi-gloss has a soft sheen with deeper blacks and stronger colour. It suits photography and saturated art, and shows fingerprints and glare more.
  • Archival or fine-art matte (Gelato’s 250 gsm archival, Prodigi’s fine-art papers) is heavier and has no optical brighteners, so whites look slightly warmer. Say so if your art relies on a bright white background.

Weight (gsm) changes how the poster feels in hand, not how it looks framed. If you sell unframed posters, a heavier paper is worth naming in the listing; for framed posters, name the finish instead.

What your listing photos must show

When dozens of shops sell the same supplier’s framed poster, the frame is identical; your art and your photos are what differ. Match each photo to the product:

  1. The frame colour of each variant. If you sell black, white and oak, show each in the same room, so a buyer comparing variants sees only the frame change.
  2. Mat or no mat, exactly as shipped. A mat makes a poster look more expensive in a photo; only show one if the product has one.
  3. A believable size. An 8×10 and a 24×36 should not look the same above a sofa. Use a size chart image for the range and keep room photos at a size you sell.
  4. A close view of the corner. It shows the frame profile and the paper surface, and answers “is this cheap?” before the buyer asks.
  5. The real thing once. Etsy asks most listings for at least one unedited photo of the actual item; print-on-demand items can use an authorised mockup instead (Etsy’s listing image requirements, as checked on 2026-11-06). A photo of your sample is still the strongest proof. See how to photograph artwork for Etsy.

Questions

What is the best print on demand for framed posters?

It depends on the frame and paper you want and where your buyers are. Prodigi lists optional mounts and three glazing choices; Gelato offers five papers and wood or metal frames; Printseekers lists many frame finishes and sizes up to 60×40 in; Printful and Printify offer wood frames in common colours. Order a sample of the exact product before you list it.

Do print-on-demand framed posters arrive ready to hang?

Printful says its framed posters arrive mounted and ready to hang, and Prodigi’s classic frames arrive ready to hang. Gelato ships the poster and frame together but unmounted unless you choose its ready-to-hang service. Printify depends on the print provider. Say which in your listing.

Should I choose matte or luster paper for posters?

Matte for illustration, line art and rooms with strong light, because it adds no sheen. Luster or semi-gloss for photography and saturated colour, accepting more glare. Under an acrylic front, the front reflects either way.

What size file do I need for a 24×36 poster?

At 300 ppi, 7,200 × 10,800 pixels; at 150 ppi, 3,600 × 5,400. Check your supplier’s file guide and test the file with a print resolution checker before you list the size.

Can WallMock make unframed poster mockups?

No. WallMock shows framed posters and framed prints, with or without a mat, and slim aluminium frames. For an unframed poster, show the flat artwork and a photo of your sample.

Wall art size guide image: how to make the "how big is it?" photo

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: A wall art size guide image shows every size you sell side by side, drawn in proportion to each other, each labelled in inches and centimetres. Show only sizes in your artwork's ratio, say whether the numbers are the artwork or the framed outside size, and pair it with one room photo above familiar furniture for real-life scale. Make it the same shape as your first photo, and repeat the sizes in the description.

"How big is it?" is a question every wall art buyer has, and a single clear image answers it before they message you. This guide covers the chart, the scale photo and the numbers, with an Etsy mockup generator for the room photos.

Two images do the job

Buyers judge size two ways, so show both:

ImageWhat it showsAnswers
Size guide (chart)Every size you sell, side by side, in proportion, labelled in inches and cm"How much bigger is 24 × 36 than 16 × 24?"
Scale photoOne size above a sofa, bed or sideboard"Will it look right in my room?"

A room photo alone can't give exact sizes, because the room's real dimensions are unknown to the buyer. A chart alone feels abstract. Together they answer the question. Put the scale photo second in the listing and the size guide third, as in the 20-slot listing photo plan.

What to put on the size guide

  • Only sizes you sell. If you offer four sizes, show four. A chart with sizes you don't stock invites the wrong order.
  • Only sizes in your ratio. A 2:3 file fits 8 × 12, 12 × 18, 16 × 24, 20 × 30 and 24 × 36 in without cropping. A 16 × 20 in print from a 2:3 file loses part of the art. Check your family with the print size calculator.
  • Both units. Inches first for US buyers, centimetres beside them: 16 × 24 in is about 41 × 61 cm.
  • Artwork size or outer size, stated. Write "artwork size" or "framed size" on the image itself.
  • The real artwork. Draw your own art at each size rather than grey boxes, so buyers see their piece.
  • Drawn in proportion. A 24 × 36 in rectangle should be three times as tall as an 8 × 12 in one.

Artwork size vs framed outer size

Many size complaints come from this gap. A frame, a floating gap or a mat makes the piece bigger than the print:

ProductOuter sizeWorked example (example frame widths)
Stretched canvasSame as the canvas16 × 24 in canvas: 16 × 24 in
Floating-frame canvasCanvas + 2 × (gap + frame face)16 × 24 in canvas, 0.25 in gap, 0.5 in face: 17.5 × 25.5 in
Framed print with matPrint + 2 × mat + 2 × frame face (about)12 × 18 in print, 2 in mat, 0.75 in face: about 17.5 × 23.5 in

Gap, face and mat widths differ by provider, so take them from your provider's product page or measure a sample. The frame and mat size calculator works out a matted size for you. If you list framed sizes, say so in the variation names too.

The scale photo

Put one size above furniture buyers know: a sofa, a bed or a sideboard. Use your most popular size, and say which size it is in the caption or the photo description.

  • Pick furniture that suits the size: a large piece above a sofa, a small one above a console or desk.
  • Show the art at a believable width. Interior guides often suggest art about two thirds to three quarters of the width of the sofa or bed below; the art size over a sofa tool works it out.
  • Don't change the size from photo to photo. Keep one size in all room photos and let the size guide show the others.

Room mockups are a feel for scale, not a measurement. Etsy's rules ask that every listing photo accurately represents what the buyer receives (Etsy), so never show a size you don't sell.

Shape, size and description

  • Shape: Etsy says the first listing photo should be landscape or square, and that it sets the shape of the photos that follow (Etsy, checked 2026-10-29). Make the size guide the same shape as your first photo, so it shows the same way as the rest.
  • Pixels: at least 2000 px on each side, Etsy's recommendation, so the labels stay sharp when buyers zoom.
  • Text: large enough to read on a phone. If a label is hard to read at full width, it is unreadable in the app.
  • Alt text and description: Etsy's alt text can be up to 250 characters, and for size charts it suggests describing the chart in detail or putting the sizing in the listing description (Etsy). Do both: list every size in the description.

A sizes block for the description can be this simple (example frame widths, as in the table above):

Artwork sizes: 12 × 18 in (30 × 46 cm), 16 × 24 in (41 × 61 cm), 24 × 36 in (61 × 91 cm). The floating frame adds 1.5 in to the width and to the height: a 16 × 24 in canvas measures 17.5 × 25.5 in (44 × 65 cm) outside.

Questions

What should a wall art size chart include?

Every size you sell, drawn in proportion to each other, labelled in inches and centimetres, with a note saying whether the numbers are the artwork or the framed outer size.

Should I show sizes in inches or centimetres?

Both. Lead with the unit most of your buyers use and put the other beside it, for example 16 × 24 in (about 41 × 61 cm).

Do frame sizes include the frame?

Say which. A floating frame or a mat adds to the outer size, so a 16 × 24 in canvas in a floating frame measures more than 16 × 24 in outside.

Is a room mockup enough to show size?

No. A room photo gives a feel for scale, but buyers can't measure from it. Add a size guide image with the exact sizes, and list them in the description.

Is WallMock's size guide image true to scale?

It draws the sizes in proportion to each other, but it is an example comparison, not your art measured on a wall. Check the sizes against what you sell before publishing.

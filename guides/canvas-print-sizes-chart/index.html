Canvas print sizes chart: stretched and framed canvas, by ratio

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Canvas sizes come in ratio families, and one file prints every size in its family without cropping. Square: 12×12, 16×16, 20×20, 24×24, 30×30 in. 2:3: 8×12, 12×18, 16×24, 20×30, 24×36, 40×60. 3:4: 9×12, 12×16, 18×24, 24×32, 30×40. 4:5: 8×10, 16×20, 24×30. Panoramic: 1:2 (12×24, 20×40) and 1:3 (12×36, 20×60). Framed canvas lists are shorter: Printful and Gelato stop at 24×36 in (checked 2026-10-29).

The print ratios guide covers paper; this chart is for canvas, where sizes run larger and framed canvas has its own, shorter list. Show each size you sell with the canvas print mockup generator.

Framed canvas sizes

Framed canvas comes in fewer sizes than stretched canvas, and usually stops lower. From each provider's own page (checked 2026-10-29):

ProviderFramed canvas sizes

Gelato13 sizes from 20×25 to 60×90 cm (8×10 to 24×36 in), listed above
ProdigiFrom 4×6 to 55×55 in
PrintifyGallery frames in 17 sizes from 5×7 to 32×48 in (vertical) and 7×5 to 60×20 in (horizontal)

Sources: Printful framed canvas, Gelato framed canvas FAQ, Prodigi framed canvas, Printify framed canvas.

The frame also makes the piece bigger on the wall. Prodigi lists a 5 mm gap and a 12 mm frame face. If a size names the canvas, that adds about 34 mm (1.3 in) to each side length: a 24×36 in canvas would be about 25.3×37.3 in outside the frame. Check your provider's stated outer size before you put it in a listing.

Panoramic and tall canvas

1:2 and 1:3 canvases suit long furniture: a 20×60 in panorama over a long sofa, or a 12×36 in strip over a bed. Turned upright, they suit narrow walls between doors and windows. Not every room suits them, so show each panoramic size in a room with furniture wide enough to carry it. The panoramic and tall art guide shows where they work.

Pick sizes for the wall

A common interior guideline is that art above furniture looks balanced at about two-thirds to three-quarters of the furniture's width, frame included. Some worked examples:

  • An 84 in sofa: 56 to 63 in of art. A 40×60 in landscape canvas (60 in) or a 20×60 in panorama fits.
  • A 60 in queen headboard: 40 to 45 in. A 30×40 in landscape (40 in) fits; a 24×48 in panorama is a little wide.
  • A 48 in console: 32 to 36 in. A 24×36 in landscape (36 in) is the upper end.

The art size over a sofa calculator does this for any furniture width and lists the standard sizes that fit.

Show the size you sell

How many sizes to offer? Three to five within one ratio family is a common pattern, because one file covers them all and the size choice stays simple. For a 2:3 artwork, for example, 12×18, 16×24, 24×36 and 40×60 in run from a shelf piece to a statement piece. Add a second family only if you prepare a second file for it.

Each size deserves an honest photo. Show the artwork at its true ratio, in a room whose furniture suits the size, and add a size guide with the dimensions in inches and centimetres. For very large pieces, see large and oversized canvas mockups.

Questions

What are the standard canvas sizes?

Common ones include 8×10, 11×14, 12×12, 12×16, 16×20, 18×24, 20×30, 24×36, 30×40 and 40×60 in. In centimetres: 30×40, 40×50, 50×70, 60×80 and 60×90 cm.

Which canvas sizes are 2:3?

8×12, 12×18, 16×24, 20×30, 24×36, 32×48 and 40×60 in, and 20×30, 40×60 and 60×90 cm. One 2:3 file prints all of them without cropping.

Is 16×20 the same ratio as 8×10?

Yes, both are 4:5, as is 24×30. 11×14 and 20×24 are different ratios and need their own file or a crop.

What is the biggest standard canvas size?

It depends on the provider. Printful's canvas list goes up to 40×60 in, and Prodigi's framed canvas up to 55×55 in (checked 2026-10-29). Check your provider before listing oversized pieces.

Are framed canvas sizes the size of the frame or the canvas?

Providers differ, so check. The frame and its gap add to the outside size: with Prodigi's 5 mm gap and 12 mm face, about 34 mm on each side length.

What size canvas goes over a sofa?

About two-thirds to three-quarters of the sofa's width. Over an 84 in sofa that is 56 to 63 in, such as a 40×60 in landscape.

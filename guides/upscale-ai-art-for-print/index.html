How to upscale AI art for large canvas and framed prints, without artefacts

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Work out the pixels first: print inches × 300 for prints seen up close, or × 150 for large canvas seen from across a room, the lowest Prodigi and Gelato suggest. Fix defects and crop to the final ratio at the original size, then upscale in one or two 2× to 4× steps with a gentle setting. Inspect at 100% for invented texture, smeared lettering and halos, and keep a lossless master.

AI images usually come out far smaller than a large print needs, so most sellers upscale. Done carelessly, upscaling invents detail you never made. Here is a careful, tool-neutral method, and a canvas print mockup generator that shows the finished file exactly as it is.

Work out the pixels you need

Pixels needed = print size in inches × DPI (dots per inch), for each side. In centimetres, divide by 2.54 first.

Which column applies? Providers' guidance, checked 2026-10-29:

  • Prodigi suggests 300 DPI for print-on-demand products, and says 150 may be enough for work without fine details (Prodigi). Its framed canvas page lists 300 DPI (framed canvas).
  • Gelato's canvas guide says files should ideally be 300 DPI at the final print size, and images under 150 DPI are not recommended (Gelato).

So plan on 300 DPI for framed prints and small canvases viewed up close, and never go below 150 DPI even for a big canvas. The free print resolution checker shows the largest size your current file prints well, and the print ratio guide lists the sizes in each ratio family.

Start from the cleanest original

An upscaler enlarges whatever it is given, flaws included. Before you enlarge:

  1. Export the largest, least compressed version your image tool offers. Never upscale a screenshot or a social-media copy.
  2. Crop to the final ratio now. If you sell 2:3 sizes, crop to exactly 2:3 at the original size, so you don't enlarge pixels you'll throw away.
  3. Fix defects at the original size. Extra fingers, warped lines, garbled text and odd seams are easier to repair small, and an upscaler will sharpen them into permanent features.
  4. Save as PNG or TIFF. Each JPEG save adds blocky artefacts, which upscalers read as texture and enlarge.

Upscale in steps, with a gentle setting

Most AI upscalers offer 2× and 4× and a strength or "detail" setting, and many have separate modes for photos and for art or illustration. For paintings and illustrations, choose the art mode and a low detail or creativity setting. High settings are where invented texture comes from.

A worked example: say your file is 1,536 × 2,304 px, a 2:3 shape. A single 4× step gives 6,144 × 9,216 px. At 300 DPI that prints 20.5 × 30.7 inches; at 150 DPI, about 41 × 61 inches. A 24 × 36 inch print at 300 DPI needs 7,200 × 10,800, so you'd need another step, or accept about 256 DPI, which is still well above the 150 DPI floor.

Two habits help:

  • Compare the AI result against a plain enlargement (bicubic or Lanczos) of the same file at 100%. If the AI version adds things that aren't in the original, lower the strength.
  • Upscale once from the clean original rather than stacking several runs on top of each other. Each pass can add its own artefacts.

Artefacts to look for at 100%

Gelato suggests making sure an image still looks good when zoomed in three times (Gelato, checked 2026-10-29). Zoom in and work through this list:

For canvas, also check the surface. Gelato warns that designs with a built-in texture may clash with the canvas pattern when printed (Gelato). Don't let the upscaler add a fake canvas weave.

Proof before you order

The screen hides problems that paper shows. Two cheap checks:

  • Print a crop at full size. Take a section of the final file and print it at 100% on a home printer, at the DPI you'll order. Hold it at normal viewing distance. If it looks right there, the full print will too.
  • Order one sample of your largest size before you list it. It also gives you the unedited product photo Etsy asks for on most physical items.

Keep the master as a lossless file, and export each provider's format from it. If you used AI to make the art, disclose it where your marketplace requires; see our guide to selling AI art prints on Etsy.

Questions

What DPI do I need for a canvas print?

Aim for 300 DPI at the final size. Prodigi says 150 may be enough for work without fine detail, and Gelato advises against going under 150 DPI.

How many pixels do I need for a 24 × 36 inch print?

7,200 × 10,800 pixels at 300 DPI, or 3,600 × 5,400 at 150 DPI.

Is it OK to upscale AI art for print?

Yes, if you check the result at 100% for artefacts and fix defects before enlarging. The artwork also has to be yours to sell, and Etsy asks you to disclose AI-made items.

Should I upscale 2× or 4×?

Use the smallest step that reaches the pixels you need. One 4× step from a clean original is usually better than several stacked passes.

Why does my upscaled art look plastic?

The upscaler's strength or detail setting is too high, so it smooths and repaints. Use the art mode, lower the strength, or blend in a plain enlargement.

Does WallMock upscale my art?

No. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Upscale your print master yourself, then make mockups from it.

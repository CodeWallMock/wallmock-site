Printify mockups for framed canvas and posters

Published by WallMock · Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer: Printify gives you free product mockups in its Mockup Library, and you choose which ones publish to Etsy or Shopify. For wall art they are mostly plain, generic scenes. You can upload your own room mockups in the same library (JPG, PNG or SVG). Or add them on Etsy and leave the Mockups box unticked when you republish, so Printify doesn't replace them. Keep the frame, size and finish in your mockup the same as the Printify product you sell.

Printify's Mockup Library covers the product; buyers also want to see the art in a room. Here is how to fix mockups that don't show, add your own and match them to the product, with a canvas print mockup generator for the room photos.

What Printify's built-in mockups give you

Every Printify product comes with mockups. You pick them in the Mockup Library: open a product in My products and choose View all mockups. According to Printify's help page, you can:

  • filter mockups by colour, size and type, and select up to the maximum allowed for the listing;
  • choose different mockups for each colour group, and set the main mockup for each colour;
  • download mockups with a white or transparent background;
  • upload your own custom mockups.

For wall art, Printify's product pages list stretched canvas, gallery-wrapped canvas and framed canvas with a pine wood frame (canvas page), and framed posters in black or white wooden frames as well as unframed posters (posters page). What you can choose depends on the print provider you pick.

There are two extras. A custom background lets you put your own image behind some mockups, but Printify says it only works for a limited number of products. It recommends a 2048 × 2048 px background (source). AI mockups come with Printify Premium, for select products only, with 25 generation attempts every 24 hours (source).

Printify also says its mockups are representations, and suggests ordering samples to see what customers receive (source). For framed canvas and posters, that sample is also your best source of a real photo.

Printify mockup not showing on Etsy: what to check

Most "missing mockup" problems come from publishing settings, not from the image itself. Work down this table. Each fix is from Printify's own help pages.

Still stuck? Printify's help page says a general publishing error with no detail should go to its support team. Send them the product so they can check your account.

Printify mockup image size

There are two numbers to know:

  • Uploading to Printify: custom mockups can be JPG, PNG or SVG, up to 30,000 × 30,000 px (Printify).
  • Showing on Etsy: Etsy recommends listing photos at least 2000 px in width and height. It also says the first photo should be at least 635 px, should be landscape or square, and works best in sRGB. Transparent parts of a PNG show as black (Etsy).

So a large, square or landscape JPG in sRGB is the safe choice for a framed canvas or poster hero photo. Don't use a transparent PNG. Etsy also warns that files over 1 MB may not finish uploading on a slow connection. The free Etsy listing photo checker checks each file's size and shape in your browser.

How to add your own room mockups to a Printify listing

You have two routes. Pick one per product and stick with it, so a later republish doesn't surprise you.

Route A: upload the mockups in Printify

Best when you want Printify to stay in charge of the listing. Steps from Printify's help page:

  1. In Printify, go to My products and open the product.
  2. Click View all mockups (top right).
  3. Choose Upload in the left menu and pick your file.
  4. Select the variant the mockup belongs to, for example the 16×20 black-framed version.
  5. Change the image order if needed, then click Save selection.
  6. Publish to your store with the Mockups option ticked.

Route B: add the photos on Etsy or Shopify

Best when you edit listings in your store. Printify's selective publishing page says you can keep manually uploaded mockups by leaving Mockups unticked when you republish. Printify recommends making every other change in Printify, and names mockups as the exception.

  1. Publish the product from Printify once.
  2. In Etsy Shop Manager, edit the listing and add your room photos. Etsy allows up to 20 photos and 2 videos per listing (Etsy).
  3. Drag your best room photo to the first slot. It becomes the thumbnail.
  4. Next time you change price or tags in Printify, publish with Mockups unticked.

Printify warns that changing the design, its placement or the variants and then publishing updates the whole product (source). Before a big edit, keep copies of your room images so you can put them back.

Match the mockup to the Printify product

Printify asks that a custom mockup closely matches the real product, and its content rules for designs apply to mockups too (source). Etsy's rules say listing images must accurately show what the buyer gets. Most listings also need at least one unedited photo of the real item, with a print-on-demand exception for authorised stock mockups. Read Etsy's page to see how it applies to your product. Before you upload, check:

  • Same product: framed canvas, gallery-wrapped canvas or framed poster, exactly as listed.
  • Same frame colour as the variant, for example black or white for framed posters.
  • Mat or no mat, as the provider makes it.
  • Same shape: a 2:3 file shown as 2:3, never stretched or cropped to fit a scene (see the print ratio families).
  • A believable size on the wall for the variant the photo belongs to.
  • The artwork itself unchanged: lettering, signature and colours exactly as printed.
  • A real photo of a sample you ordered, if Etsy's rules need one for your product.

Questions

Why are my Printify mockups not showing on Etsy?

Check the publishing settings first. Publishing fails if more than 20 mockups are selected. New variants arrive without a photo if you republished with Mockups unticked. An expired Etsy connection, often after a password change, stops all publishing until you click Reconnect in Printify.

How do I change from a Printify mockup to my own product photo?

Either upload your photo in Printify's Mockup Library (View all mockups, then Upload) and publish with Mockups ticked, or add it on Etsy and leave Mockups unticked when you next republish from Printify.

Will republishing from Printify delete my own Etsy photos?

It can. Printify says publishing its mockups replaces your Etsy mockups, including videos. Use selective publishing and leave the Mockups box unticked to keep your own photos.

What size should Printify mockups be?

Printify accepts uploads up to 30,000 × 30,000 px in JPG, PNG or SVG. For Etsy, aim for at least 2000 px in width and height, square or landscape for the first photo, in sRGB, with no transparency.

Does Printify offer framed canvas?

Yes. Printify's canvas page lists stretched, gallery-wrapped and framed canvas, and its posters page lists framed posters in black or white wooden frames. Options vary by print provider, so check the product you choose.

Can I use WallMock mockups with Printify products?

Yes. Use the same artwork file, choose the matching product and frame in WallMock, then upload the images to Printify's Mockup Library or straight to your Etsy or Shopify listing.

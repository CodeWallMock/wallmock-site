Canvas depth: 0.75 in vs 1.5 in, and how to show depth in a listing

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Thin canvas is about 0.75 in (19 mm) deep and gallery canvas about 1.5 in (38 mm). Thin suits small and medium sizes, framing and a flatter look. Deep canvas stands further off the wall, suits large pieces hung without a frame and shows more of its edge. Providers differ: Printful's standard canvas is 1.25 in, Gelato makes 2 cm and 4 cm, Prodigi 19 mm and 38 mm (checked 2026-10-29). State the exact depth and show it in a side close-up.

Depth is easy to get wrong in a listing, because a front-on photo hides it. Check your provider's number, then show it next to your canvas print mockups.

Watch the word "standard". Printful's regular canvas is 1.25 in, not 1.5 in, so a listing that says "1.5 in gallery wrap" for it would be wrong by a quarter of an inch.

Thin or deep: which to sell

Thin canvas (0.75 in) is lighter and sits flatter against the wall. It suits small and medium sizes, shelves and leaning displays, and framing: Gelato's framed canvas uses a slim 2–3 cm canvas (checked 2026-10-29). Printful's thin canvas comes in 13 sizes from 8×10 to 24×36 in, while its 1.25 in canvas goes up to 40×60 in. Thin is a small-to-medium product.

Deep canvas (1.5 in) stands out from the wall like a block. On a large piece hung without a frame it looks proportionate: on a 40 × 60 in canvas, a 0.75 in edge looks like a sheet, while 1.5 in looks like an object. It shows more of the side, so the edge style matters more. Our guide to canvas wrap edges covers mirror, image and white or black sides.

A simple rule if you offer one depth: thin for prints up to about 16 × 20 in that may be framed, deep for anything sold large and unframed.

Depth changes your file

With image wrap, the picture covers the sides, so the deeper the canvas, the more picture you need. Add the depth to every side:

  • A 24 × 36 in canvas, 0.75 in deep, needs 25.5 × 37.5 in of picture: 3825 × 5625 px at 150 DPI.
  • The same canvas, 1.5 in deep, needs 27 × 39 in: 4050 × 5850 px at 150 DPI.

Mirror wrap and plain white or black sides need only the front. The full table by size is in what DPI a canvas print needs, and the print resolution checker reads your own file.

How depth shows in a photo

Front-on, depth is invisible except for one clue: the shadow. A deeper canvas stands further from the wall, so under the same light it casts a slightly wider, softer shadow on the side away from the light. A canvas with no shadow at all looks like a poster stuck flat to the wall.

To prove the depth, you need a second view:

  • A close-up of a sample at an angle, showing one side and a corner.
  • A photo of the side next to something everyone knows the size of, such as a hand or a pencil.
  • A simple diagram with the depth marked in inches and millimetres.

Framed canvas depth

For framed canvas, the buyer sees the frame from the side, not the canvas. Providers state it differently (checked 2026-10-29):

  • Prodigi: a 38 mm canvas in a floater that stands 53 mm off the wall (source).
  • Gelato: a frame 42 mm deep in North America and 32 mm in Europe, around a slim canvas (source).
  • Printful: a pine frame 1.25 in (3.18 cm) thick (source).

The frame has to suit the canvas: a floater is built around one canvas depth, so a deep 1.5 in canvas needs a frame made for it, as Prodigi's 53 mm floater is for its 38 mm canvas. If you sell a framed upgrade, check that the provider frames the same depth you sell unframed.

Give the frame depth for the region you ship from, and show it in a corner close-up. The floating frame mockup guide explains how the frame's depth shows in a room photo.

Common depth mistakes

  • Rounding up. Calling a 1.25 in canvas "1.5 in gallery wrap" because it sounds better.
  • Mixing up canvas and frame depth. For framed canvas, the buyer sees the frame from the side; give that number.
  • One number for every region. Gelato's frame is 42 mm deep in North America and 32 mm in Europe (checked 2026-10-29).
  • Forgetting the wrap. Switching to a deeper canvas with image wrap without adding picture to the file loses more of the front.
  • Showing the wrong sample. A close-up of a deep canvas on a listing for thin canvas.

How to state depth in a listing

  • Put it in the first lines: "Canvas depth: 1.5 in (38 mm). Image wraps around the sides."
  • Name variations by depth if you sell both: "Thin canvas, 0.75 in" and "Gallery canvas, 1.5 in".
  • Use your provider's number, not a rounded one.
  • Show the side of the depth you sell, never a deeper sample than the product.

Etsy's image rules say all images must accurately reflect the item's true appearance, including colour, scale and materials (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29). Depth is part of that.

Questions

Is 0.75 or 1.5 inch canvas better?

Neither is better in general. Thin 0.75 in canvas suits smaller pieces and framing; deep 1.5 in canvas suits large pieces hung without a frame.

What is a gallery depth canvas?

A deep stretched canvas, usually about 1.5 in (38 mm), hung without a frame with its sides finished. Prodigi's stretched canvas is 38 mm and Gelato's thick canvas 4 cm.

How thick is a standard canvas print?

It depends on the provider. Printful's standard canvas is 1.25 in (3.18 cm), Prodigi's stretched canvas 38 mm and its slim canvas 19 mm (checked 2026-10-29).

Can a 1.5 inch canvas go in a floating frame?

Yes, if the frame is made for that depth. Prodigi's floater, for example, holds a 38 mm canvas and stands 53 mm off the wall.

Does canvas depth change the file size I need?

Only with image wrap. Then the picture covers the sides, so add the depth to every side: a 24 × 36 in canvas, 1.5 in deep, needs 27 × 39 in of picture.

Canvas depth 0.75 vs 1.5 inch: which to sell, and how to show it

By Akshit, founder of WallMock and an Etsy wall-art seller since 2023 · Updated 14 November 2026 · How we write our guides

Short answer: Sell 1.5 in (38 mm) gallery canvas for large pieces that hang without a frame, and 0.75 in (19 mm) thin canvas for small and medium pieces, shelves and anything going into a frame. Then use your provider's real number, not the label: Printful's standard canvas is 1.25 in, Gelato makes 2 cm and 4 cm, Prodigi 19 mm and 38 mm (checked 2026-10-29). Put that exact depth in the listing and prove it with a side close-up of a sample.

A front-on photo hides depth completely, which is why it's so easy to get wrong. Check the number first, then pair it with your canvas print mockups.

Don't trust the word "standard". Printful's regular canvas is 1.25 in, not 1.5 in. Call it a "1.5 in gallery wrap" and your listing is wrong by a quarter of an inch, on the one detail a buyer can check with a ruler.

Thin or deep: which to sell

Thin canvas (0.75 in) is lighter and sits flatter against the wall. Use it for small and medium sizes, shelves and leaning displays, and for anything that goes into a frame: Gelato's framed canvas uses a slim 2–3 cm canvas (checked 2026-10-29).

Deep canvas (1.5 in) stands off the wall like a block, and that's the point of it on a large unframed piece. The deeper side also shows more canvas edge for the same front size, so the edge style matters more. Our guide to canvas wrap edges covers mirror, image and white or black sides.

Size often makes the decision for you. Printful's thin canvas comes in 13 sizes from 8×10 to 24×36 in, while its 1.25 in canvas goes up to 40×60 in. Go bigger than 24×36 in unframed at Printful and you're selling the 1.25 in canvas, whether you planned to or not.

Offer both depths only if you'll show both. Each depth is another variation, and each needs its own side photo.

Depth changes your file

With image wrap, the picture covers the sides, so a deeper canvas needs more picture. Add the depth to every side:

  • A 24 × 36 in canvas, 0.75 in deep, needs 25.5 × 37.5 in of picture: 3825 × 5625 px at 150 DPI.
  • The same canvas, 1.5 in deep, needs 27 × 39 in: 4050 × 5850 px at 150 DPI.

Those figures cover front and sides only, before any fold or bleed the supplier adds, so upload to the supplier's own template rather than these numbers. Mirror wrap and plain sides keep the whole front composition, but still follow that template. How depth shows in a photo

Front-on, there's one clue: the shadow. A deeper canvas stands further from the wall, so under the same light it casts a slightly wider, softer shadow on the side away from the light. A canvas with no shadow at all looks like a poster stuck flat to the wall.

That's also the limit of a room mockup. WallMock's stretched canvas renders are front-on: they show the wall shadow, not the wrapped side or its edge colour. The depth itself has to come from a photo of your sample:

  • A close-up at an angle, showing one side and a corner.
  • The side next to something everyone knows the size of, such as a hand or a pencil.
  • A simple diagram with the depth marked in inches and millimetres.

Put the side close-up early

On a canvas listing, the close-ups are where you prove construction, and for stretched canvas that means the side. Don't bury it at slot 15. My listings give the second slot, straight after the hero room, to a close-up card. If the product is new to you, order the sample before you write the depth into a listing.

Framed canvas depth

On a framed canvas, the buyer sees the frame from the side, not the canvas. Providers state it differently (checked 2026-10-29):

  • Prodigi: a 38 mm canvas in a floater that stands 53 mm off the wall (source).
  • Gelato: a frame 42 mm deep in North America and 32 mm in Europe, around a slim canvas (source).
  • Printful: a pine frame 1.25 in (3.18 cm) thick (source).

A floater is built around one canvas depth, so a deep 1.5 in canvas needs a frame made for it, as Prodigi's 53 mm floater is for its 38 mm canvas. If you sell a framed upgrade, check the provider frames the same depth you sell unframed. Give the frame depth for the region you ship from and show it in a corner close-up; the floating frame mockup guide explains how frame depth reads in a room photo.

Common depth mistakes

  • Rounding up. Calling a 1.25 in canvas "1.5 in gallery wrap" because it sounds better.
  • Mixing up canvas and frame depth. For framed canvas, give the frame's number.
  • One number for every region. Gelato's frame changes between North America and Europe.
  • Forgetting the wrap. Moving to a deeper canvas with image wrap without adding picture to the file eats more of the front.
  • Showing the wrong sample. A close-up of a deep canvas on a thin canvas listing.

How to state depth in a listing

  • Put it in the first lines: "Canvas depth: 1.5 in (38 mm). Image wraps around the sides."
  • Name variations by depth if you sell both: "Thin canvas, 0.75 in" and "Gallery canvas, 1.5 in".
  • Use your provider's number, not a rounded one.
  • Show the side of the depth you sell, never a deeper sample than the product.

Etsy's image rules say all images must accurately reflect the item's true appearance, including colour, scale and materials (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29). Depth is part of that.

Questions

Is 0.75 or 1.5 inch canvas better?

For large pieces hung without a frame, 1.5 in. For small and medium pieces, shelves and anything going into a frame, 0.75 in. Then check your provider's exact depth: Printful's standard canvas is 1.25 in, not 1.5 in.

What is a gallery depth canvas?

A deep stretched canvas, usually about 1.5 in (38 mm), hung without a frame with its sides finished. Prodigi's stretched canvas is 38 mm and Gelato's thick canvas 4 cm.

How thick is a standard canvas print?

It depends on the provider. Printful's standard canvas is 1.25 in (3.18 cm), Prodigi's stretched canvas 38 mm and its slim canvas 19 mm (checked 2026-10-29).

Can a 1.5 inch canvas go in a floating frame?

Yes, if the frame is made for that depth. Prodigi's floater, for example, holds a 38 mm canvas and stands 53 mm off the wall.

Does canvas depth change the file size I need?

With image wrap, extra picture covers the sides: a 24 × 36 in front and 1.5 in sides cover 27 × 39 in before supplier-specific folds and bleed. Other edge styles can preserve the front, but still follow the full product template.

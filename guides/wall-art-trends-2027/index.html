Wall art trends for 2027, and the rooms to show them in

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: No one can measure 2027 yet, so treat every trend list as observation. The firmest data is Etsy's: searches for wall art decor rose 110% and gallery prints 80% in the three months to 10 February 2026, year on year, among US signed-in shoppers. The directions we see carrying into 2027 are gallery-wall sets, nostalgic art, deep wall colours, warm natural materials, big statement pieces and quiet photography. Show each in a room that suits it.

Trend lists are easy to write and hard to check. This one separates the little data there is from our observations, and pairs each direction with rooms you can use today in WallMock's library of mockup rooms.

What the data says, and what it doesn't

Etsy's trend expert published Etsy's spring and summer 2026 trends in the Etsy Community. One of them, "Everyday Exhibits", is about curated displays at home. Etsy says searches for wall art decor were up 110% and searches for gallery prints up 80%, as shoppers build curated and sometimes nostalgic gallery walls. The figures compare the three months to 10 February 2026 with the same period a year before, and cover US signed-in users (Etsy Community, read 2026-10-29).

That is useful, but it is not a forecast for 2027, and we found no newer Etsy trend post when we checked. Everything below that isn't from that post is our observation, labelled as such. Use it to choose what to test, not as a promise of sales.

1. Gallery walls and sets (backed by Etsy's data)

Sets of two, three and four sell a whole wall rather than one picture, and Etsy's gallery-print figure points the same way. Show sets on one wall with equal gaps, then each piece alone. Our guide to presenting a set of 3 wall art covers spacing and layouts.

Rooms to show it: Parchment library reading bay and Chocolate and travertine home office both support pairs, trios and 2×2 sets; the Burgundy collector's sitting room takes a trio of gold canvases.

2. Nostalgic, vintage-feeling art (partly backed)

Etsy's own wording mentions "sometimes nostalgic" gallery walls. Our observation: muted oil landscapes, classical still lifes, old botanical studies and vintage-style posters keep appearing in styled interiors. They look most convincing in a gold or dark wood frame, often with a mat.

Rooms to show it: Classical fireplace salon, Olive and ivory panelled bedroom and the Parchment library reading bay.

3. Deep, colour-drenched walls (observation)

Our observation: burgundy, aubergine, chocolate and deep green walls have moved from accent to whole-room colour in a lot of interior styling. Pale art, black-and-white photography and gold frames stand out on them. Use a deep room as an alternative image, not the hero, unless your buyers love drama; our room styles and wall colours guide suggests a mix.

Rooms to show it: Aubergine wall and oak credenza, the Burgundy collector's sitting room and the Chocolate and travertine home office.

4. Warm natural materials (observation)

Our observation: plaster and limewash walls, oak and walnut furniture and stone surfaces continue to define calm, premium rooms. Natural oak and walnut frames suit them, as do earthy abstracts, botanicals and soft landscapes.

Rooms to show it: Ivory plaster feature wall, Japandi low bed and Walnut sideboard dining room.

5. Big statement pieces, and 6. quiet photography (observation)

Our observation: one large canvas or a wide panorama over a sofa or credenza is a simple way to make a room feel finished, and buyers who choose it want to see the scale. Show it above furniture so the size reads; our guide to hanging art over a sofa has the proportions.

Calm black-and-white and minimal photography fits the same warm, natural rooms. Framed with a mat or as a floating-frame canvas, it reads as gallery work.

Rooms to show them: Mid-century modernist house credenza, Charcoal wall and ivory boucle, Japandi tea room and Cozy alpine-cabin lounge.

How to use trends without chasing them

  • Keep the hero neutral. Put a trend room in slot three or four, where it adds mood without risking the click.
  • Test one change at a time. Swap one listing's alternative room for a trend room and compare favourites and visits over a few weeks.
  • Refresh by season. Our wall art selling calendar shows when to swap hero images.
  • Change rooms, not your art. Your style is the reason buyers follow you. Rooms are the cheap part to update.

How to tell if a trend works for you: Etsy's shop stats show visits and favourites per listing. Note them for four weeks before you swap a room, then for four weeks after. If nothing moves, the trend isn't your buyers' trend, and that's useful to know too. Check the Etsy Community again in the new year, when a newer trend post may give fresher data than anything in this guide.

Questions

What are the wall art trends for 2027?

No one has 2027 data yet. Etsy's latest trend post shows strong search growth for wall art decor and gallery prints. We also see gallery-wall sets, nostalgic art, deep wall colours, warm natural materials, statement pieces and quiet photography carrying on.

Is gallery wall art still popular?

Etsy reported searches for gallery prints up 80% and wall art decor up 110% in the three months to 10 February 2026, compared with a year earlier, among US signed-in users.

Are dark walls good for wall art mockups?

Yes, as an alternative image. Pale art, black-and-white photos and gold frames stand out on deep walls. Keep a neutral room for the first photo.

Should I change my art to follow trends?

Usually not. Change the rooms and frames you show it in, which is cheaper and keeps your style consistent.

Where does Etsy publish trend data?

Etsy's trend expert posts seasonal trend reports in the Etsy Community, in the Etsy Success forum. Check there for newer reports.

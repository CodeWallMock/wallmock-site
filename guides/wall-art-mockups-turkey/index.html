Wall Art Mockups for Print-on-Demand Sellers in Türkiye

Published by WallMock · Updated 5 November 2026

If you run a wall art shop from Istanbul or elsewhere in Türkiye (Turkey), choose the print size by buyer market before you make the listing images. We recommend WallMock for checking your European and US crops in the same room and exporting a separate image for each option. See WallMock’s USD plan prices for Türkiye.

Try WallMock with your art

Compare an A3 option with a 12 × 18 in option

If your printer asks for 300 pixels per inch before bleed, export A3 at about 3,508 × 4,961 pixels and 12 × 18 in at 3,600 × 5,400 pixels. Preview both crops before exporting. Keep a little room above and below the subject in the 2:3 master so you can trim for the shorter A3 shape.

Make a note for each buyer market with the supplier code, paper size, frame and delivery estimate. Put the actual size on each listing image and keep the original art file for future variants. The EU print guide covers checking the mount opening as well.

Keep shop and subscription currencies separate

Etsy lists Türkiye as eligible for Etsy Payments and explains the country’s PayPal limitation. Check Etsy’s currency guide for the options in your shop. Check which currency each service shows before you pay for a subscription or a supplier sample.

Preview your EU and US artwork crops

Preview your artwork in a room for free. The trial preview is watermarked and requires no account or card.

WallMock supports square 1:1, standard portrait and landscape ratios (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:7 and their reverses), and long formats (1:2, 2:1, 1:3 and 3:1). Choose a room that fits the artwork shape. For other print sizes such as 11 × 14 in, prepare the file to the intended print opening and check the result in the preview. See the print ratios and sizes guide.

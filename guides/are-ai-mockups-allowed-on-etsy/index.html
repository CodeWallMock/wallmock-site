Are AI mockups allowed on Etsy? The accuracy rules, and why your art must never be redrawn

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Yes, within limits. Etsy lets you edit listing photos, including with AI, as long as they accurately show what the buyer will receive. Most items also need at least one unedited photo of the actual item; for print-on-demand items, Etsy allows a stock photo mockup if its owner has authorised its use. So an AI-made room can be fine, but AI-redrawn art is not: if the art in the photo differs from the print, the photo misrepresents the item.

Etsy doesn't ban mockups or AI. It bans photos that misrepresent the item. Here is what its rules say, checked on 2026-10-29, and how to keep the images from an Etsy mockup generator on the right side of them.

Mockup, AI room or AI-redrawn art: three different things

"AI mockup" covers three different images. Etsy's accuracy rule treats them very differently.

ImageWhat happens to your artAccuracy risk
Staged room with your file placedYour exact file sits in a digitally staged roomLow, if frame, size and colours match what you ship
AI-made room, your file placed on topThe room is generated; your file is placed as a separate layerLow to medium: check perspective, scale and light
AI regenerates the whole pictureThe model redraws your art along with the roomHigh: lettering, signature, colours and crop can change

The first two can meet Etsy's rule. The third often can't, because the picture in the photo is no longer quite your picture. Our guide to AI mockup generators for wall art shows how to test any tool with your own file.

Why your art must never be redrawn

A wall art buyer pays for the image itself. When a tool redraws it, the changes are small enough to miss at a glance and big enough to matter when the print arrives:

  • Lettering in a quote or city print gets respelled or restyled.
  • A signature moves, changes or disappears.
  • Colours drift toward the room's palette, which breaks Etsy's rule that images reflect true colour.
  • The crop changes, so a 2:3 print appears as 4:5 and the buyer expects a different picture.
  • Sets come out slightly different from each other, so the trio no longer matches.

Every mismatch is a reason for a return or a complaint, and it's avoidable. Keep the art pixel-for-pixel your file, and let only the room be staged. After you upload, zoom your listing photo to 200% and compare it with your file side by side.

The unedited photo: what to shoot

For most physical items, Etsy wants at least one photo that shows the actual item against an actual background, with no edits at all. For a print or canvas, that is a photo of a sample:

  1. Order one sample of each product type you sell, such as a framed print and a framed canvas.
  2. Hang it on a plain wall near a window and shoot straight on in daylight.
  3. Add a close shot of a corner, showing the frame, the mat or the canvas edge.
  4. Don't filter, retouch or crop it beyond what Etsy allows.

Print-on-demand sellers have the stock-mockup exception above, but a sample photo is still the best proof of quality. For digital downloads, the preview of the actual content does this job; our guide to printable wall art mockups shows what to include.

What to write in the description

Etsy recommends noting in your listing description whether images were edited (Etsy, checked 2026-10-29). One plain line covers it, for example:

"Room photos are digitally staged mockups. The artwork is shown exactly as printed; frame colour and size are as selected."

That line is about the photos. Whether the artwork itself was made with AI is a separate rule: Etsy asks sellers to disclose in the description when an item is created with AI (What can I sell on Etsy?, checked 2026-10-29). Our guide to selling AI art prints on Etsy covers that side.

A self-check before you publish

  • The art in every photo is your exact file: zoom to 200% on text and signature.
  • Width ÷ height of the art matches your file.
  • The frame colour, mat and canvas type match each variant.
  • The size looks believable against the furniture, and a sizes image states the real sizes.
  • There's an unedited sample photo, or you rely on an authorised print-on-demand mockup.
  • You have the rights to every room scene you use.
  • The description says the room photos are mockups, and discloses AI-made art if it is.

Questions

Are mockups allowed on Etsy?

Yes. Etsy allows edited images, including mockups, as long as they accurately show what the buyer receives. Most physical items also need at least one unedited photo of the actual item.

Can I use AI-generated mockups on Etsy?

You can use AI to make or edit the room, as long as the item is shown accurately. Don't let a tool redraw the artwork itself, because the photo then shows a different picture from the print.

Do I need an unedited photo of my print on Etsy?

For most items, yes. For print-on-demand items, Etsy says you may use a stock photo mockup if its owner has authorised its use. A photo of a sample is still the strongest proof.

Do I have to say my photos are mockups?

Etsy recommends noting in the description whether images were edited. One plain line saying the room photos are digitally staged mockups covers it.

Is AI-redrawn art in a mockup against Etsy's rules?

If the redrawn art differs from what you ship, the photo no longer accurately represents the item, which Etsy's image rules require. Keep the artwork exact and stage only the room.

Can I use WallMock images on Etsy?

Yes. WallMock's terms allow clean downloads in shop listings. The artwork is your file, placed as it is, and you should still follow Etsy's photo rules.

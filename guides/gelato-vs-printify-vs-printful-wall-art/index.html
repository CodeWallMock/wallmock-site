Gelato vs Printify vs Printful for canvas and framed prints

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: For wall art, the three differ most in how much they tell you about framed products. Gelato states its framed canvas specs (a 12 mm gap, 13 sizes, three frame colours) and produces in 32 countries. Printful runs its own centres and gives canvas depths of 1.5, 1.25 and 0.75 in. Printify connects you to many print providers, so specs vary by product. All three connect to Etsy and Shopify. Decide by your buyers' countries and the exact frame, then order a sample.

Most "Gelato vs Printify vs Printful" pages compare T-shirts. This one compares only canvas, framed canvas and framed prints, using each provider's own pages. When you've picked one, make print-on-demand canvas mockups that match it, or start from the canvas print mockup generator.

How this comparison works

Every fact below comes from the provider's own website or help centre, checked on 2026-10-29. We leave out product prices because they change often and depend on size. This is not a ranking, and WallMock has no affiliate link with any of the three. For Prodigi, Lumaprints and Gooten as well, see how to choose a print-on-demand provider.

One difference shapes everything else. Printify is a marketplace of print providers: it says providers differ in location, prices, product models, print areas and equipment (source). Gelato and Printful sell one version of each product and route the order for you. So with Printify you compare listings; with Gelato and Printful you compare products.

Framed prints and posters

  • Gelato offers framed posters in four wood colours and four metal colours, on five poster papers (source). Its premium wood frames come in natural oak, black and white, 20 mm wide, with plexiglass (source). By default the frame and poster ship unmounted in one package; a Ready-to-Hang option mounts the poster before shipping (source). The Gelato help pages we checked don't mention mats.
  • Printful prints framed posters on matte or luster photo paper, in sizes from 8×10 in to 24×36 in and 21×30 cm to 61×91 cm (source). Its catalog also lists a matte paper framed poster with a mat.
  • Printify lists framed posters in black or white wooden frames, on archival or 200 gsm paper with archival or semi-gloss finishes (source). Mats and sizes depend on the provider.

If a mat matters to your style, compare the actual mat products, not the frame colours. Our frame and mat proportions guide shows how mat width changes the look.

Samples

  • Gelato: no free samples. New sellers get 30% off the product cost of a first sample order within 48 hours of sign-up, up to 50€ off (source).
  • Printful: sample orders get a 20% discount for store owners with a connected store, starting at one sample order a month with up to 3 items. Framed posters can only be sampled at 8×10 in and canvas at 12×12 in, and samples may not be available to all accounts (source).
  • Printify: no free samples; sample orders are at regular pricing (source).

Then compare the sample with your listing photos using our sample checklist.

Which one fits your shop

  • You want framed canvas specs stated up front: Gelato states gap, frame width and regional depth. Note the depth differs between North America and Europe.
  • You want in-house production in North America and Europe, and a matted poster: Printful, as long as your buyers are near its centres.
  • You want to choose the factory, or compare prices across makers: Printify, but sample the exact provider, because two listings with the same name can differ.

Questions

Is Gelato, Printify or Printful better for canvas prints?

It depends on where your buyers are and which frame you want. Gelato states framed canvas specs and produces in 32 countries, Printful makes its own canvases in its centres, and Printify lets you pick between print providers whose specs vary. Order a sample from your shortlist.

Which one has a floating frame canvas?

Gelato states a 12 mm gap between canvas and frame (checked 2026-10-29). Printful's framed canvas page states no gap, though customer reviews describe a floater frame. On Printify it depends on the provider, so check the size guide and a sample.

How deep are Printful's canvases?

Printful's blog gives 1.5 in for canvas, 0.75 in for thin canvas and 1.25 in for framed canvas including the frame. The post is dated March 2025, so confirm on the product page.

Can I use my own mockups with Gelato, Printify and Printful?

Yes. Printify lets you upload custom mockups to its Mockup Library. Printful and Gelato push their own mockups, and you add your room photos directly to the Etsy or Shopify listing.

Do Gelato, Printify or Printful give free samples?

Gelato and Printify say they don't. Gelato offers 30% off a first sample order for new sellers, and Printful offers a 20% sample discount with monthly limits.

Which prints closest to buyers in Europe?

Gelato routes orders to local partners in 32 countries. Printful has centres in Barcelona and Birmingham, and Birmingham only ships to UK addresses. On Printify, choose a provider located in Europe.

Selling abroad · 13 markets compared

Selling wall art in other countries: sizes, labels and currencies

By Akshit, founder of WallMock and an Etsy wall-art seller since 2023 · Updated 14 November 2026 · How we write our guides

Selling abroad changes three things: the sizes buyers expect (A-series and centimetres across Europe, inch sizes for North American buyers), the words on your size image, and the currency your buyer pays versus the one you are paid in. It does not change the room photo, so make one clean room image per product build, keep all text off it, and carry the local sizes on a separate size image that gives cm and in together.

Do the size maths before you make a single mockup. A3, A2, 50 × 70 cm and 12 × 18 in look alike in a thumbnail, but they are different products, and a crop that fits one can clip a signature on another. The table covers 13 markets; the sections after it hold only what is actually different in each.

What changes, and what stays the same

  • Sizes. A-series sheets (A4 is 210 × 297 mm, A3 is 297 × 420 mm, A2 is 420 × 594 mm) sit between 3:4 and 2:3, so neither common art shape fills them without a border or a crop. Inch sizes such as 12 × 16 in (3:4) and 12 × 18 in (2:3) take those files exactly.
  • Labels. A framed piece has up to three numbers: the paper or canvas size, the visible opening if there is a mount, and the outside frame size. Buyers need them in their own language, and they need to know which is which.
  • Currency. What your buyer sees, what Etsy deposits to you and what you pay for software can be three different currencies. Track them separately.
  • Not the room photo. If the product is the same build, the same room image works in every market. Make a new one only when the frame, mount, canvas wrap, crop or finished proportions change.

Do the size maths before the mockup

Same shape is not the same size, and same label is not always the same size either. Prodigi’s printing FAQ gives the example of a print listed as 45 × 60 cm and 18 × 24 in that comes out at a different physical size depending on the lab. These are the fits that come up most when one design is sold in several markets:

FitWhat happens
2:3 artwork on A3 (297 × 420 mm)Scaled to 420 mm high it is 280 mm wide, leaving 8.5 mm each side if centred. To fill the sheet, scale to 297 mm wide and crop 25.5 mm of image height in total.
30 × 40 cm (3:4) artwork on A3Fitted to the 297 mm width it is 396 mm high, leaving 12 mm of paper above and below if centred.
50 × 70 cm (5:7) design on A2 (420 × 594 mm)Scaled to 420 mm wide it is 588 mm high, leaving 3 mm above and below. To fill the page, fit the height and check the small trim on either side.
A4 sheet (210 × 297 mm) behind a centred 200 × 287 mm mount openingAn illustrative case: 5 mm of visible paper is lost on each edge. Ask for your supplier’s actual opening.
12 × 16 in vs 30 × 40 cmBoth 3:4. 12 × 16 in is exactly 30.48 × 40.64 cm.
16 × 24 in vs 40 × 60 cmBoth 2:3. 16 × 24 in is 40.64 × 60.96 cm.
18 × 24 in vs 45 × 60 cmBoth 3:4. 18 × 24 in is exactly 45.72 × 60.96 cm, so a 45 × 60 cm catalogue entry is a different physical size.
Pixels, if your printer asks for 300 ppi before bleedA3 is about 3,508 × 4,961 px; 12 × 18 in is 3,600 × 5,400 px.

Two habits save most of the trouble. Keep a 2:3 master with a little room above and below the subject, so you can trim it for the shorter A3 shape without losing anything. And keep lettering and signatures clear of the trim, the frame’s lip and any mount opening. Run the visible opening through the aspect ratio calculator when a mount is included; your print file still follows the printer’s own trim and bleed instructions.

Market by market: only what is different

United Kingdom: printed border or window mount

The UK question is the mount. A printed border is part of the paper. A window mount is a separate board in front of the print, and its opening hides some of the paper, so the mounted variant shows less of the artwork’s edge. theprintspace explains how its framed variants handle crops, borders and window mounts. Ask for the actual opening before you put lettering near the edge.

Write down the frame colour, mount colour and paper size for each option. Compare mounted and unmounted in the same room with the same artwork, so the buyer sees only the change. Then add a product detail of the real mount and frame, labelled with paper and outside frame sizes. Recheck the catalogue before reusing old mockups: Inkthreadable’s September 2026 update announced mount options for A2, A3 and A4 frames and new frame colours. On Etsy, check the image requirements for your listing type.

European Union: the variant for the region you ship from

Use the supplier’s product code and dimensions for the actual fulfilment region, not the first one you found. Prodigi’s classic frame catalogue shows dimensions and fulfilment locations by variant. Before you duplicate a product across markets, write down four things: supplier code, finished dimensions, available frame colours, and whether a mount is included. Paying for tools in EUR says nothing about where your supplier prints and ships.

Keep the room, crop and frame finish consistent across the featured photos. Shopify recommends consistent aspect ratios for featured product images so collection tiles display at the same size.

Germany: A2 is not 50 × 70 cm

They look close in a thumbnail and they are two products. If you sell both, make an A2 image and a 50 × 70 cm image (the fit is in the size table above). On the size image, Papierformat is the paper format and Rahmenaußenmaß the outer frame; repeat the measurements beside the product options. theprintspace lists its Düsseldorf location; confirm the lab can supply your exact size and finish before the option goes live.

France: say whether the passe-partout is included

A passe-partout is a window mount over the print; a border printed on the sheet has no separate layer. If you sell affiche seule, affiche encadrée and avec passe-partout, each option image must show its own construction; use a close photo of the actual mounted sample to show the cut edge. A mount can cover more of a 30 × 40 cm composition on A3 than the 12 mm border suggests, depending on its opening, so check the edges at final size. Gelato’s French catalogue for frames and posters shows which combinations exist; confirm the visible opening on the exact product. For the size image, Format de l’impression and Dimensions extérieures du cadre separate print from frame.

Netherlands and Belgium: one gallery, two languages

If the product is identical, reuse the room photo for Dutch and Belgian buyers and translate only the labels: Afdrukformaat with Buitenmaat lijst in Dutch, the French pair above for French-language listings. That only works if the room image carries no text. Shopify Translate & Adapt and Etsy’s manual listing translations handle product text, so open the live page in each language and check which image, labels and variants actually appear.

A 50 × 70 cm poster size is 5:7. Keep that crop for the art and compose a square gallery tile with enough wall to show all four corners of the frame: the square tile and the 5:7 print come from one mockup. If the frame also changes by market, see the listing variations guide.

Romania: leu prices, euro payouts

The European Commission identifies the leu as Romania’s currency, but Etsy’s payment currency guide says it sends deposits to Romanian bank accounts in EUR. Keep separate fields for the currency your buyers see, the one you receive and the one you pay for software in.

If you sell a 30 × 40 cm print in Romania and a 12 × 16 in variant elsewhere, one 3:4 file fits both, but give each its supplier’s stated dimensions. Dimensiunea printului and Dimensiunea exterioară a ramei separate print from outside frame. Add a new image when the mat, frame or finished proportions change.

Bulgaria: replace images with BGN prices in them

Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 January 2026, according to the European Commission’s Bulgaria and the euro page. Updating the price field is not enough if a BGN price is baked into a gallery image. Check old comparison cards, delivery banners and collection covers too, then verify the live storefront price against Etsy’s supported currencies and shop currency guidance. From now on, keep the room image price-free; if you run a promotion card, list it as an asset to replace when the promotion ends.

A 40 × 50 cm artwork sold as a print and a canvas starts from one 4:5 crop but needs one room photo per build, with the paper print and the canvas depth labelled separately. After saving, check the thumbnail and every photo linked to a variant.

Switzerland: rolled and framed may ship differently

A supplier may send the unframed print and the framed version from different locations. Get a delivery quote to a Swiss postcode for both, including any charges the supplier discloses; the Swiss Federal Office for Customs and Border Security explains how mail and courier imports are handled. Keep the storefront currency and delivery terms plain in the listing.

For a 40 × 60 cm print sold rolled, or the same 2:3 artwork framed, show the rolled option with a paper detail and the framed one with its outside dimensions and an angled photo. Confirm the paper size for both and the mount opening if there is one. Use the room photo for scale and a product photo for what is in the parcel; WallMock’s part is the framed room image, once the frame, mount and delivery option are confirmed.

Türkiye: two buyer markets, two crops

If you sell from Istanbul or elsewhere in Türkiye, choose the print size by buyer market before making images: A3 for European buyers, 12 × 18 in for US buyers, each exported at its own pixel size (see the table). Preview both crops in the same room, export a separate image for each option, and put the actual size on each. Keep a note per buyer market with supplier code, paper size, frame and delivery estimate, and keep the original art file for future variants.

Etsy lists Türkiye as eligible for Etsy Payments and explains the country’s PayPal limitation. Check which currency each service shows before you pay for a subscription or a supplier sample.

Ukraine: Payoneer payouts, and check what can ship

Etsy’s Payoneer guide lists Ukraine among the countries where sellers use Payoneer for Etsy Payments. That covers your payouts, not your fulfilment. Before you promise an option or a date, check the provider’s current delivery policy against the buyer’s postcode; Printful publishes current country and region restrictions.

Say a framed 30 × 45 cm print ships to one destination and only an unframed print to another. Both take a 2:3 image. Keep the frame in the first gallery, show the paper and size in the second, and use the same composition so buyers recognise the design. Recheck the delivery quote, sizes and linked photos before every listing update.

India: GSTIN and Payoneer for a new Etsy shop

According to Etsy’s India seller guidance, new shops in India can sell internationally and must verify a GSTIN and use Payoneer for Etsy Payments. For an Indian Shopify store, start from the products and sizes your print partner can deliver to your buyers.

A 30 × 40 cm print and a 12 × 16 in option for another market share a 3:4 file and are still different sizes, so each variant gets its own size label. With a mount, record the visible opening separately from the outside size.

Australia: two framed canvases with similar names

Prodigi’s Australian fulfilment catalogue lists both framed canvas and classic framed canvas. The names are close enough to confuse a buyer, and the two builds look different from the side.

ProductConstruction and photo check
Framed canvas38 mm stretched canvas in a floating frame, with a gap of about 5 mm. Show the gap at a corner and the depth in an angled photo.
Classic framed canvas19 mm stretched canvas in a classic picture frame. Check the profile and depth before reusing a mockup made for a floater.

A product family in the Australian catalogue does not establish which options can be produced there, so check size, finish and fulfilment location on your exact SKU. A 40 × 60 cm canvas face and a 16 × 24 in face share a 2:3 file; list the one you sell, and record the canvas face and outside frame separately. Match the canvas face, frame finish and gap to a sample photo or the supplier’s product view before putting the artwork in a room. In the room image, leave space around the outer frame so it survives a storefront’s thumbnail crop, then add a corner or angled detail for depth. The guide to Prodigi’s mockups goes further.

Canada: one canvas, two fulfilment countries

Prodigi’s framed canvas product page lists both Canadian and US fulfilment, so where an order is made depends on the product and the buyer’s address. Verify size, frame colour and shipping quote for the specific variant in each market, and keep one row per variant in your notes: destination, supplier product code, canvas size, edge finish and photo set.

Check the wrap before you crop the artwork. Keep a title, signature or a subject’s face inside the front of the canvas: in an image wrap, part of the image runs round the side, while a solid or mirrored wrap looks different in an angled photo. Prodigi’s canvas guide explains all three; use your supplier’s template for the actual bleed. Use a 3:4 file for an 18 × 24 in canvas and a 2:3 file for 16 × 24 in. If only the unit labels change between markets, reuse the room view; if the frame or wrap changes, make a new product detail. The canvas edge guide shows what to compare.

Make one set of room photos work everywhere

  • One room image per build, not per country. Same artwork, frame and room for every market that gets the same product.
  • No text on the room image. Prices go stale, as Bulgaria’s switch to the euro showed, and size labels need translating. Both belong on a separate card and in the listing text.
  • One labelled size image per language. cm and in together, the print or canvas size, the visible opening if there is a mount, and the outside frame size. The size image guide covers the layout, and WallMock’s studio has a size guide image button that draws your artwork at the sizes you enter, in proportion and labelled in inches and centimetres.
  • A frame or mount detail. The close-up answers the question the room photo cannot: what the edge, gap or mount really looks like. The frame and mount guide shows where to measure.
  • Keep images large. Keep every listing photo at least 2,000 px on its shortest side. WallMock downloads are 4,096 px on the long edge. When you cut a room photo to square, trim the sides only, so the art keeps its size.

On billing: WallMock sets your pricing region from your connection, and that is separate from where your buyers are. Prices are shown by region (EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD and INR among them); Romania, Switzerland, Türkiye and Ukraine see USD. A Free account gives 10 clean 4K downloads every 30 days, with the room access limits listed on the pricing page; Lite includes 200 clean 4K images a month and Pro unlimited clean 4K images for one shop.

The mistake that costs you in any country

It is a listing image that shows a different product from the one that arrives. No translation fixes a wood tone that doesn't match. Order a sample of anything you haven't handled; the sample checklist covers what to compare, and this is why I built WallMock for my own shop.

Questions

Do I need different mockups for each country?

Not if the product is the same build. Reuse the same room image and translate the size image instead. Make a new room image only when the frame, mount, canvas wrap, crop or finished proportions change for that market.

Is A3 the same as 12 × 18 in?

No. A3 is 297 × 420 mm and sits between 3:4 and 2:3. 12 × 18 in is a 2:3 size, so a 2:3 file fills it exactly but needs a border or a crop on A3: scaled to 420 mm high it is 280 mm wide, leaving 8.5 mm each side.

Is 50 × 70 cm the same as A2?

No. A2 is 420 × 594 mm. A 50 × 70 cm design scaled to 420 mm wide is 588 mm high, leaving 3 mm above and below on A2. Sell them as two products with two images.

Which currency will Etsy pay me in?

It depends on your bank country, so check Etsy’s currency guide for yours. Two examples: Etsy says it sends deposits to Romanian bank accounts in EUR, and new shops in India must use Payoneer for Etsy Payments. Keep the currency buyers see, the one you receive and the one you pay for software in as separate fields.

How should I label sizes for international buyers?

Put cm and in together on a separate size image, and say which number is the print or canvas, which is the visible mount opening and which is the outside frame. Translate the labels for each storefront language, for example Papierformat and Rahmenaußenmaß in German.

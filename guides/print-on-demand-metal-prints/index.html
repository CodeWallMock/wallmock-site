Print on demand metal prints: suppliers, surfaces and listing photos

Published by WallMock · Updated 8 November 2026

Short answer: A metal print is art printed onto an aluminium panel, usually by dye sublimation, and hung on a hidden mount so it floats off the wall. Suppliers differ in the base (white, silver or brushed), the surface (gloss, matte or lustre), the size range and where they ship: Printful and Prodigi’s gloss aluminium prints ship to the US only, while Gelato lists over 60 sizes. Choose the panel, order a sample, then show that surface and edge in your photos.

  • Four suppliers checkedPrintful, Prodigi, Gelato and Lumaprints, from their own pages on 8 November 2026.
  • Base changes the artOn a brushed or silver base, white and transparent areas show bare metal; on a white base they stay white.
  • Gloss reflectsGloss panels show window reflections. Matte or lustre surfaces cut glare but look less punchy.
  • Mockups that matchWallMock metal prints come in Gloss (default), High gloss or Matte, with 3.2 mm corners, one artwork per image.

Metal prints look modern and sharp, and they cost more than posters, so buyers look harder at the photos. This guide compares what four print-on-demand suppliers make, explains the details that change how the art looks, and shows how to make metal print mockups that match your supplier.

Metal print or aluminium framed print?

“Metal” covers two different products:

  • Metal print: the image is fused into a coated aluminium panel. No frame, no glass; the panel hangs on a hidden block or subframe a little off the wall.
  • Aluminium framed print: a paper print behind glazing in a slim metal frame, often with a mat. It photographs like a framed print, not like a metal print.

This guide is about the first. For the difference in photos, see metal print vs metal frame.

The details that change how the art looks

  • The base colour. Gelato notes that white ink isn’t used, so on its brushed panel any white or transparent area of your design shows the silver metal. That can be beautiful on a moody landscape and wrong on a bright botanical with a white background. Lumaprints’ Glossy Silver base behaves the same way. If your art has white space, choose a white base, or test a sample.
  • The surface. Gloss gives deep blacks and strong colour but mirrors windows and lamps. Matte or lustre (Prodigi’s dibond options) cuts glare and reads closer to paper. See the three surfaces side by side in glossy vs matte metal print mockups.
  • The edge and corners. Prodigi’s aluminium prints have a rounded edge; check your supplier, because square corners read differently in a close-up.
  • The stand-off. ½ in, 15 mm or ¾ in off the wall sets how much shadow the panel casts. A deeper float looks more dramatic; a shallow one looks flatter.
  • Who it ships to. Two of the products above ship to US addresses only. If you sell worldwide, list metal prints only where your supplier delivers, or use a supplier with a wider network.

Sizes and file resolution

Metal prints are viewed close and show detail sharply, so be stricter with resolution than for canvas. Aim for about 300 ppi at the printed size: a 16×20 in print needs 4,800 × 6,000 pixels, and a 24×36 in print 7,200 × 10,800. If your file is smaller, offer fewer large sizes rather than upscaling art you can’t inspect. Check with the print resolution checker, and keep sizes in your file’s ratio with the print size calculator.

Listing photos for metal prints

  1. A hero room in your supplier’s surface. If your panel is matte, don’t lead with a glossy reflection.
  2. A corner close-up showing the thin edge, the corner and the shadow under the panel.
  3. Scale above familiar furniture, at a size you sell.
  4. A note on the base if it is silver or brushed, with a sample photo showing how white areas look.
  5. Your sample, once. Etsy asks most listings for one unedited photo of the actual item; print-on-demand items may use an authorised mockup instead (as checked on 2026-11-06).

Questions

Which print-on-demand companies make metal prints?

Among the suppliers we checked on 8 November 2026: Printful (glossy aluminium, six sizes, US only), Prodigi (gloss aluminium prints from the US, and dibond in matte, gloss or lustre from the EU, UK and US), Gelato (3 mm dibond, white or brushed silver base, over 60 sizes) and Lumaprints (glossy white or silver aluminium, 5×5 to 40×60 in). We did not find a metal print on Printify’s site that day.

Do metal prints show white?

Only if the base is white. Gelato notes white ink isn’t used, so on its brushed silver base white and transparent areas show the metal. Choose a white-base panel for art with white backgrounds, or test a sample.

Is a metal print better glossy or matte?

Neither on its own: show the surface your supplier sells. Gloss gives the strongest colour and reflects windows; matte cuts glare and looks closer to paper or canvas.

How far off the wall does a metal print hang?

It depends on the mount: Printful says ½ in, Prodigi 15 mm for its aluminium prints, and Lumaprints’ inset frame ¾ in. WallMock draws its metal print about ½ in (12.7 mm) off the wall.

Etsy vs Shopify vs your own site for wall art sellers

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Etsy is better when you need buyers now: shoppers already search it, and you pay per listing and per sale (USD 0.20 a listing, 6.5% of each sale, plus card processing). Shopify, or your own site, is better once you can bring your own traffic from Pinterest, Instagram, email or ads: you pay a monthly plan and card fees, and you own the brand. A common path is to start on Etsy and add a store later, with the same print-on-demand provider and photos.

The platform decides who finds your art and what each sale costs. This guide compares them for print-on-demand framed canvas, framed prints and posters, with Etsy's and Shopify's own numbers. Whichever you choose, the Etsy mockup generator makes room photos that work on both.

Etsy: buyers first, fees per sale

Etsy's strength for wall art is search. Shoppers type "framed canvas wall art" or "japandi print" and your listing can appear on day one. In return you pay on every sale (checked 2026-10-29):

  • USD 0.20 per listing, which lasts 4 months.
  • 6.5% of the item price plus the shipping you charge.
  • Card processing, which varies by country: 3% + USD 0.25 in the US.
  • Offsite Ads on orders that came from Etsy's ads elsewhere: 15%, or 12% for shops with USD 10,000 or more in sales over the past 365 days, never more than USD 100 per order.

On a USD 129 framed canvas with shipping included, the US fees come to USD 12.71, about 10% of the price. Our guide to pricing wall art on Etsy works it through for every size.

If you bring your own traffic to Etsy, Share & Save refunds 4% of the eligible order total when a buyer orders within 30 days of clicking a link you shared (Etsy).

Shopify and your own site: your traffic, your brand

A Shopify store is your own shop on your own domain. Nobody is browsing it until you send them. In return:

  • Costs are mostly fixed. You pay a monthly plan plus card processing. Shopify's pricing page lists an extra 2% on Basic when you use a third-party payment provider instead of Shopify Payments (1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced). Plan prices depend on your country and on monthly or yearly billing, so check Shopify's pricing page.
  • No per-listing fee and no marketplace cut. Above a certain monthly volume this costs less than Etsy's percentages; below it, the plan fee dominates.
  • Your brand, your buyers. Your design, your domain, your email list and your rules, with no other sellers' art on the same page.

You also take on the work Etsy did for you: the shop's design, its policies pages, answering buyers, and getting found by search engines and on social media. For a wall art shop, Pinterest is a natural first traffic source, because people use it to save ideas for their rooms; see wall art mockups for Pinterest and Instagram.

Other website builders work the same way: a monthly plan, card fees and your own traffic. Choose by whether your print-on-demand provider connects to it.

A simple break-even test

Compare one month's costs, using your own numbers:

  1. Etsy: your monthly sales × about 9.5% (transaction + processing), plus USD 0.45 per order and any Offsite Ads fees.
  2. Shopify: your plan's monthly price plus your card processing on the same sales.

Example: 20 orders at USD 129 is USD 2,580 in sales. Etsy's fees are about 20 × 12.71 = USD 254 a month before Offsite Ads. If your Shopify plan and card fees for the same sales come to less, Shopify is cheaper, but only if those 20 orders still happen without Etsy's search. That is the real question: can you bring the buyers yourself?

What changes for wall art listings

  • Options. Both handle size and frame colour. Etsy links photos to up to 20 options of a variation; Shopify gives each variant one image from the product's media. See Etsy variations for wall art and Shopify wall art product images.
  • First image shape. Etsy wants a landscape or square first photo (Etsy); Shopify suggests a consistent aspect ratio across main images (Shopify). Square room photos suit both.
  • Print-on-demand sync. On both, a provider's republish can replace your own photos. Keep your image files and check each variant after a sync.
  • Disclosure. Etsy requires sellers who use a production partner to disclose it on the listing (Etsy). On your own site, say who makes and ships the piece in your policies.

Running both, with one set of photos

A common set-up is to keep Etsy for search and add a Shopify store for repeat buyers, Pinterest and Instagram traffic. With print-on-demand there is no stock to split: the same provider can fulfil both. What you need twice is the listing work, so make the photos once and reuse them.

Questions

Is Etsy or Shopify better for selling wall art?

Etsy is better for finding buyers quickly, because shoppers already search it. Shopify is better once you can bring your own traffic, because you pay a monthly plan instead of a percentage on every sale and you own the brand.

How much does Etsy charge to sell a print?

For a US shop, USD 0.20 per listing, 6.5% of the item price and shipping, and 3% plus USD 0.25 for card processing. Orders from Offsite Ads add 15%, or 12% for larger shops, capped at USD 100 per order.

Does Shopify charge a fee per sale?

Shopify charges card processing on each sale. Its pricing page also lists an extra fee on Basic (2%) when you use a third-party payment provider instead of Shopify Payments.

Can I sell the same print-on-demand wall art on Etsy and Shopify?

Yes. The main print-on-demand providers connect to both, so one provider can fulfil orders from each. Use the same listing photos on both.

Should I start on Etsy or Shopify?

If you don't have an audience yet, Etsy is usually the faster start. Add a Shopify store once you have repeat buyers or traffic from Pinterest, Instagram or email.

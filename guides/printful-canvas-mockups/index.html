Printful canvas mockups: what the generator shows and what to add

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Printful's free mockup generator shows your design on its canvas products, from clean studio backgrounds to lifestyle scenes, and sends the mockups you pick to your store. It can't take mockups made elsewhere, so room photos go straight into your Etsy or Shopify listing. Match the product: Printful gives 1.5 in depth for canvas, 0.75 in for thin canvas and 1.25 in for framed canvas, with black, brown or white frames.

Our Printful mockups guide covers framed posters and fixes for mockups that won't update. This one is about canvas: which Printful canvas you're showing, what its generator gives you, and the room photos to add from a canvas print mockup generator.

The three Printful canvases

Printful sells standard canvas, thin canvas and framed canvas (canvas range). Its help centre says the design is printed on satin-finish canvas, hand-stretched over a pine frame (source). The numbers that change how a mockup should look:

CanvasThin canvasFramed canvas
Depth1.5 in0.75 in1.25 in including the frame

What your mockup should showA deep edge and a soft shadowA slimmer edge, closer to the wallThe frame colour, and the gap if your sample has one

Depths and frame colours come from Printful's blog post on canvas vs metal prints, dated March 2025. Hanging details come from Printful's custom canvas page and thin canvas page. All checked 2026-10-29.

Printful's framed canvas page describes "a high-quality wooden frame" but doesn't list colours, depth or a gap (checked 2026-10-29). Customer reviews on that page describe a floater frame with a shadow gap; that is buyers' wording, not a stated spec. Check the variant selector and your own sample before you decide how the frame looks in your photos. Printful's canvas range runs from 6×6 in up to 24×36 in for framed canvas.

What Printful's mockup generator gives you

Printful's mockup generator is free for its products; you sign up and work from your dashboard. Styles range from lifestyle shots to clean studio backgrounds (checked 2026-10-29). When you publish, you choose:

  • a main mockup, the style shown first;
  • additional mockups, extra styles shown alongside it (source).

For Etsy, Printful pushes up to 10 images (source). To keep files for your own use, open a published product, select styles, click Generate files, then Download mockups. Mockup previews on product templates can't be changed.

Two limits for canvas sellers. Printful's AI mockups currently cover select apparel products only, with 10 attempts a day (source). And Printful says it isn't possible to upload mockup images to Printful itself (source). So the generator covers the product view; for room photos at the right size, in the right frame colour, you add images in your store.

The photos to add yourself

A canvas listing works best when the photos answer what buyers ask: how big is it, what does the edge look like, and does it suit my room? Add these to what Printful sends:

  1. A room photo at a believable size for each size you sell. A 12×12 in canvas and a 24×36 in canvas shouldn't look the same over a sofa. The art size over a sofa calculator helps you judge it.
  2. A frame or edge close-up. For framed canvas, the frame colour and the gap. For canvas and thin canvas, the edge depth.
  3. Two or three different rooms so buyers can picture it in their style. Our guide to room styles and wall colours shows a balanced mix.
  4. A photo of your sample for Etsy's unedited-photo rule (see below).

Make the first photo a room photo: it becomes the listing thumbnail on Etsy (source).

Adding room photos to a Printful-synced listing

Etsy

  1. Publish from Printful. The product arrives in Etsy as a draft.
  2. In Shop Manager, open the draft and add your room photos.
  3. Drag your best room photo into first place.
  4. Leave at least one of the 20 photo slots free. Printful says it can't push a new mockup to a listing that already has 20 images (source).

Shopify

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Products and open the product.
  2. Add your files in the Media section. The first media item is the featured image; drag a room photo there. A product can have up to 250 images, 3D models or videos (Shopify Help).
  3. To show a room photo for a size or frame colour, click the image icon next to that variant. Each variant can have one image (source).

If you later refresh Printful's mockups, check the listing straight after. Printful says some integrations add new mockups without removing the old ones (source). Our guide to replacing Printify and Printful mockups covers this in detail.

Order a sample canvas first

Printful's sample orders come with a 20% discount for store owners with a connected store. Accounts start with one sample order a month, up to 3 items, and samples may not be available to all accounts. For canvas, only the 12×12 in size can be ordered as a sample, and framed posters only at 8×10 in (source, checked 2026-10-29). The article doesn't say which framed canvas sizes can be sampled.

A 12×12 in sample tells you about colour, edge, frame and texture, not about how a 24×36 in canvas fills a wall. Use it to check your mockups' frame and colour, and use size tools for scale. Our sample checklist walks through each check.

Questions

Is Printful's mockup generator free?

Yes. Printful says mockups for its products are free. You need a Printful account and make them from your dashboard.

Can I upload my own canvas mockups to Printful?

No. Printful's help centre says it isn't possible to upload mockup images to Printful. Add room photos directly to your Etsy or Shopify listing.

How thick is Printful's canvas?

Printful's blog gives 1.5 in for canvas, 0.75 in for thin canvas and 1.25 in for framed canvas including the frame. The post is dated March 2025, so confirm on the product page before you list.

What colours does Printful's framed canvas come in?

Printful's blog lists black, brown and white frames. The framed canvas product page doesn't list colours in its text, so check the variant selector.

Does Printful make AI mockups for canvas?

Not at the moment. Printful says AI mockups cover select apparel products only (checked 2026-10-29).

What size canvas sample can I order from Printful?

Only 12×12 in, according to Printful's sample order article, with a 20% discount and monthly limits.

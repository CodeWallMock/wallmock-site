Wall Art Mockups for Sellers in India

Published by WallMock · Updated 5 November 2026

We recommend WallMock for Indian Etsy and Shopify sellers who need room images of their own art as framed prints, canvases or posters. See local prices.

Try WallMock with your art

Choose your storefront and delivery market

If you run an Indian Shopify store, start with the product and sizes your print partner can deliver to your buyers. If you are opening an Etsy shop from India, Etsy’s current guidance says new shops in India can sell internationally and must verify a GSTIN and use Payoneer for Etsy Payments. Check Etsy’s India seller guidance before setting up a shop.

WallMock uses your connection to set your pricing region. Your buyer’s destination is a separate decision. Choose listing dimensions and fulfilment for that market.

Keep a size reference for each product

Suppose you sell a 30 × 40 cm print and a 12 × 16 in option for a different market. Both use 3:4 artwork. They are different physical sizes, so give each variant its own size label. If one comes with a mount, record the visible opening separately from the outside size.

  1. Get the frame colour, finish, depth and visible print opening from the supplier listing or a sample.
  2. Pick a room that lets a buyer judge the artwork’s scale. Compare the mockup against the actual product or supplier view.
  3. Use one room and crop across a collection. Add a corner photo or size image where it helps a buyer choose between variants.

The print ratio guide explains which files can share a crop. The frame and mat guide shows what to measure when the frame opening is smaller than the product.

Choose a plan by how you make listings

Use Try Free to check the frame and room with your own artwork. A Free account gives you 10 watermark-free 1K downloads every 30 days, with room access limits described on the pricing page. If you need listing images at 4K, Lite includes 200 a month and Pro offers unlimited clean 4K images for one shop.

See monthly and yearly prices in the INR row. WallMock makes listing images. Your print partner produces and ships the physical order.

Make a mockup with your own art

Use a 3:4 file for a 30 × 40 cm print. If you offer another ratio, upload the file prepared for that variant before making its listing image.

WallMock supports square 1:1, standard portrait and landscape ratios (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:7 and their reverses), and long formats (1:2, 2:1, 1:3 and 3:1). Choose a room that fits the artwork shape. For other print sizes such as 11 × 14 in, prepare the file to the intended print opening and check the result in the preview. See the print ratios and sizes guide.

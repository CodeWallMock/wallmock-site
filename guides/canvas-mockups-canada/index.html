Canvas Mockups for Canadian Print-on-Demand Sellers

Published by WallMock · Updated 5 November 2026

We recommend WallMock for showing Canadian and US buyers your canvas at the shape they can order. Use a room image for scale and an angled supplier image to show the wrap. See local prices.

Try WallMock with your art

Separate your billing currency from your print routing

CAD is a WallMock billing currency. Where your orders are produced depends on your print partner, the selected product and your buyer’s delivery address. For example, Prodigi’s framed canvas product page lists both Canadian and US fulfilment. Verify the available size, frame colour and shipping quote for the specific variant in each market.

Keep one row per variant in your listing notes: destination, supplier product code, canvas size, edge finish and photo set. If the sample differs by market, update the description and images for the version that will ship.

Check the wrap before you crop the artwork

Keep a title, signature or a subject’s face inside the front of the canvas. In an image wrap, part of the image can extend around the side. A solid or mirrored wrap looks different in an angled image. Prodigi’s canvas guide explains all three edge treatments. Use your supplier’s template for the actual amount of bleed.

Put the canvas face dimensions in your listing. If your supplier specifies 18 × 24 in, the exact conversion is 45.72 × 60.96 cm. A catalogue entry sold as 45 × 60 cm is a different physical size even though the artwork ratio is the same.

Decide which images can serve both markets

Start with a room view that fits the whole canvas. Add a close view for the wrap and a size image that states what the buyer gets. If only the unit labels change, you may be able to reuse the room view. If the frame or wrap changes, make a new product detail. The canvas edge guide and supplier sample checklist show what to compare.

Preview your canvas in WallMock

Use a 3:4 file for an 18 × 24 in canvas or 2:3 for a 16 × 24 in canvas. For a tall or panoramic listing, choose a room that leaves space around the artwork.

WallMock supports square 1:1, standard portrait and landscape ratios (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:7 and their reverses), and long formats (1:2, 2:1, 1:3 and 3:1). Choose a room that fits the artwork shape. For other print sizes such as 11 × 14 in, prepare the file to the intended print opening and check the result in the preview. See the print ratios and sizes guide.

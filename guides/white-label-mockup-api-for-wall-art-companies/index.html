White-label mockup API for wall art companies: what to look for

Published by WallMock · Updated 6 November 2026

Short answer: Look for exact art with a per-image proof, renders of your real frames rather than flat templates, enough curated rooms, listing-pack output, white-label and private presets, webhooks and signed links, a sandbox, file deletion with no training, and throughput measured on stated hardware. Then pilot it on your own frames before you sign. WallMock's API is in early access, not available yet; white-label is planned for partner agreements.

If your sellers will see these images under your name, the provider behind them matters. This checklist covers what to ask, and where WallMock stands today.

The checklist

Use these questions with any provider. The last column says where WallMock stands on 6 November 2026.

What “white-label” usually covers

  • Images with no provider watermark or logo.
  • Links and downloads that do not show the provider's name to sellers.
  • Your product names on frames, finishes and photo slots.
  • Your seller-facing screens, if the provider offers an entry your sellers use.

Ask which of these are available now and which are planned. At WallMock, live API images carry no watermark and presets can use your frame names; fuller white-label is planned for partner agreements, not available yet.

Questions to settle in a pilot

  1. Do the frames match your product photos, corner by corner?
  2. Does every image come back with its art check, and what happens when a check fails?
  3. How many images per hour on production hardware, at the size you need?
  4. How do results reach your system, and how long do links last?
  5. Who owns presets, and what happens to them if you stop?

Red flags

  • Art that is “enhanced”, regenerated or upscaled by AI without asking.
  • No way to show that a given image contains the seller's exact file.
  • Speed figures with no hardware, size or batch stated.
  • Frames that look the same in every finish, or no shadow at all.
  • A long contract before you have seen your own frames rendered.

Questions

What is a white-label mockup API?

A service your systems call to make product mockups, where the images and links reach your sellers under your brand rather than the provider's.

What should wall art companies look for in a mockup API?

Exact art with a per-image proof, renders of your real frames rather than flat templates, enough curated rooms, listing-pack output, white-label and private presets, webhooks and signed links, a sandbox, deletion of files after processing with no training, and throughput measured on hardware you can verify.

Does WallMock offer a white-label mockup API?

Not yet. White-label output is planned for partner agreements. In the current early-access build, live images carry no WallMock watermark and presets can use your frame names.

Can a mockup API show our own frames?

Ask for a pilot on your frames before you sign. At WallMock, presets of a partner's actual frames are built in partner pilots; the partner frame catalogue is in development.

How do I test a mockup API before committing?

Use its sandbox to build the integration, then run a pilot with your own frames and a few artworks you have the rights to, and compare the images with your product photos.

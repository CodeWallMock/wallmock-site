Floating frame mockup tips: how to make floater frames look right

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: A floating frame mockup looks right when five details match the product: a thin, even gap between canvas and frame, the right colour inside that gap, a slim frame face, a shadow that fits the frame's depth, and the frame finish your provider ships. Use real numbers: Prodigi lists a 5 mm gap and 12 mm face, Gelato a 12 mm gap (checked 2026-10-29). Show no glass and no mat, and nothing covering the front of the canvas.

This is the how-to for floater frames, with numbers you can check. To have the images made for you, the floating frame canvas mockup generator draws the gap in 7 frame colours, and the canvas print mockup generator covers stretched canvas too.

The five details buyers read in a floating frame

A floating frame, also called a floater frame, holds a stretched canvas from behind, so nothing covers the front of the canvas. Buyers recognise it from five details. Get all five right and the photo reads as framed canvas. Miss one and it reads as a framed print, or as fake.

  1. The gap. A thin, even reveal between the canvas edge and the inside of the frame, on all four sides.
  2. The colour inside the gap. Usually dark: you are looking at the inner wall of the frame and its shadow, not the wall behind.
  3. A slim frame face. The front border of a floater is narrow, about 9 to 14 mm at the providers below.
  4. Depth and shadow. The frame stands off the wall, so it casts a soft shadow that agrees with the room's light.
  5. The finish you ship. Black, white, wood or metal, matching the variant the buyer orders.

What is missing matters too: no glass, no reflections, no mat and no frame lip over the art. If you are not sure how a floater differs from other frames, read what is a floater frame first.

Get the gap width right, in millimetres and pixels

The gap is a fixed physical size, so how wide it looks in your image depends on how big the canvas is. Here is what three print-on-demand providers state on their own pages (checked 2026-10-29):

Sources: Prodigi framed canvas, Gelato canvas and framed canvas, Printful framed canvas.

To turn millimetres into pixels, divide the canvas width in pixels by its width in millimetres, then multiply by the gap. A worked example: a 24 × 36 in canvas is 610 mm wide. If it appears 600 px wide in your mockup, that is about 1 px per mm, so a 5 mm gap is about 5 px and a 12 mm face about 12 px. Now show a 12 × 16 in canvas (305 mm wide) at the same 600 px. Every millimetre becomes about 2 px, and the same 5 mm gap is about 10 px.

Two rules follow. The gap should look thinner on big canvases and wider on small ones, never the same pixel width at every size. And it should be even on all four sides. A gap that is wide at the top and thin at the bottom looks like a canvas that has slipped.

Show depth with the shadow, not a thicker frame

From the front you can't see how deep a floater is. Buyers judge depth from the shadow it casts and, in a close-up, from the side of the frame. Prodigi's framed canvas stands 53 mm off the wall; Gelato's frame is 42 mm deep in North America and 32 mm in Europe (checked 2026-10-29). Under the same light, a deeper frame throws a slightly wider shadow.

  • Put the shadow on the side away from the room's main light. If the window is on the left, the shadow falls to the right and below.
  • Keep it soft and short. A hard black outline looks pasted on.
  • Don't widen the frame face to suggest depth. A chunky front border reads as a different, heavier frame.
  • If you list the frame depth, give inches and millimetres, and use your provider's number for the region it ships from.

Seven mistakes that make floater mockups look fake

  • The canvas touches the frame. With no gap, it reads as a framed print.
  • A mat or glass inside a floater. Floating frames for canvas have neither.
  • A frame lip over the art. In a floater the whole front of the canvas shows; none of your artwork should be cut off.
  • One gap width for every size. Use the pixel rule above.
  • The shadow on the wrong side, or the same hard shadow in every room.
  • Art stretched or cropped to fit a template. A 2:3 canvas must stay 2:3. The canvas print sizes chart groups sizes by ratio.
  • A frame colour or depth you don't sell. That is a return waiting to happen.

Check it at two sizes

Look at every photo twice. At thumbnail size, about as wide as a search result on a phone, the frame colour should be clear and the gap should show as one thin line. At 100% zoom, check one corner: the gap should run straight into the corner, the frame should meet in a clean mitre (the angled corner joint), and the canvas edge should be crisp.

Then compare against a photo of your sample, if you have ordered one. Etsy's image rules say all images must accurately reflect the item's true appearance, including colour, scale and materials (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29). A floater mockup that shows a different gap, finish or depth breaks that rule as surely as a wrong colour.

Questions

How wide is the gap in a floating frame?

It depends on the frame. Prodigi lists a 5 mm gap and Gelato a 12 mm gap for their framed canvas (checked 2026-10-29). In a mockup, the gap should scale with the canvas, so it looks thinner on large canvases.

Should a floating frame mockup show glass or a mat?

No. Floating frames for canvas have no glass and no mat. Reflections or a white border tell buyers it is a framed print.

What colour should the gap be?

Usually dark, because you see the inside of the frame and its shadow. Printful says its black and brown framed canvases have a black inside and its white frames a white inside, so check your own sample.

Does a floating frame cover part of the artwork?

No. The frame surrounds the canvas without touching its front, so the whole front of the canvas shows. Only the sides are mostly hidden.

A seasonal selling calendar for wall art, by country

Published by WallMock · Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer: List new collections and refresh mockups six to eight weeks before each peak: by early December for the new year, late December for Valentine's Day, mid-January for Mothering Sunday in the UK, mid-March for Mother's Day in May, late April for Father's Day in June, and October for Christmas. For many shops, October to December is the biggest season of the year.

Wall art sells all year, but gifting dates and home seasons move demand. Listings and search take time to warm up, so plan new collections and refreshed mockups about six to eight weeks before each peak; a bulk mockup generator turns a whole-shop refresh into one run.

List early, refresh often

Marketplace search and social platforms need time to pick up new listings. A Mother's Day collection listed the week before is usually too late. Aim to have new pieces and seasonal mockups live six to eight weeks ahead, then refresh the hero image as the date approaches.

Seasonal rooms help: a light, airy room in spring and summer; a warm, cosy room in autumn and winter (see room styles and wall colours). Swap the hero image rather than rebuilding the listing.

Gift seasons need gift signals

For gifting dates, show the art in rooms that match the recipient (a nursery for a new parent, a study for a graduate), mention printable gifts and last-order dates in the first line of the description, and make sure the size and frame information is clear: gift buyers can't ask the recipient. The Etsy listing photo plan has a slot for sizes.

Prepare for the fourth quarter early

For many shops, October to December is the biggest period of the year. Finish new collections by September, refresh hero images with warm, cosy rooms in October, and decide your Black Friday and Christmas offers well before the rush.

Questions

When should I list Christmas wall art?

By October, so listings have time to appear in search before the December peak. Start on cosy seasonal mockups in September.

Is Mother's Day on the same date everywhere?

No. It is in March in the UK and Ireland, the first Sunday of May in Spain and Portugal, the second Sunday of May in the US, Canada, Australia, India and much of Europe, and late May or early June in France.

How far ahead should I list seasonal wall art?

About six to eight weeks before the peak, then refresh the hero image as the date approaches.

A seasonal selling calendar for wall art, by country

By AK, founder of WallMock and an Etsy wall-art seller since 2023 · Updated 14 November 2026 · How we write our guides

Short answer: Have new pieces and fresh mockups live six to eight weeks before each peak: October for Christmas, late December for Valentine's Day, mid-January for Mothering Sunday in the UK, mid-March for Mother's Day in May and late April for Father's Day in June. Your own order history and your provider's cutoffs decide which of those dates matter to your shop.

Wall art sells all year, but gifting dates and home seasons move demand. The six-to-eight-week figure is a production buffer, not a search-engine waiting period: it covers what has to happen before a new piece is listed. A bulk mockup generator turns the seasonal photo refresh into one run.

What the six to eight weeks are for

The buffer is not padding. Here is what goes into that time:

  • A sample, if the product is new to you. A seasonal rush is the worst time to find out the wood tone is off.
  • The print file. Print masters are upscaled before printing. The mockup is not: it uses the file as it is.
  • About 10 room images. Hero room first, a frame close-up card second, every image at least 2,000 px on its shortest side.
  • A check at 100%. Corners, edges, signatures and small marks, on every image, before upload. Anything with a known defect is not shown.

Then add your provider's production and shipping time to the destination. The right buffer depends on the product and the country, so take the "list by" column below as a planning start and move it earlier if your supplier is slow or ships far.

Change the hero, not the whole listing

Keep your calm, neutral rooms as the core of every listing all year. For the season, swap the hero image: a light, airy room in spring and summer, a warm, cosy one with glowing lamps and throws in autumn and winter. Bold rooms belong here too. My rule of thumb is about 15% bold rooms in a shop, and seasonal art is one of the subjects they are kept for. See room styles and wall colours for the full mix and Christmas and seasonal mockups for festive rooms.

Gift seasons need gift signals

For gifting dates, show the art in rooms that match the recipient (a nursery for a new parent, a study for a graduate), put last-order dates and any printable gift option in the first line of the description, and make size and frame information unmissable. Gift buyers cannot ask the recipient. The Etsy listing photo plan has a slot for sizes.

Start the fourth quarter in September

If your own orders show a year-end gifting peak, work backwards from the provider's delivery cutoff. Finish new collections by September, refresh hero images with warm, cosy rooms in October, and decide your Black Friday and Christmas offers before the rush, not during it.

Refresh the whole shop in one pass

A seasonal refresh across a whole shop is where a batch workflow pays off: choose the seasonal rooms once, apply them to every artwork and download the new hero images together. In WallMock, favourite rooms and saved setups make that a repeatable task.

Questions

When should I list Christmas wall art?

By October, as a planning starting point, then check your production and delivery cutoff. Start on cosy seasonal mockups in September.

Is Mother's Day on the same date everywhere?

No. Mothering Sunday falls in March or early April in the UK and Ireland, the first Sunday of May in Spain and Portugal, the second Sunday of May in the US, Canada, Australia, India and much of Europe, and late May or early June in France.

How far ahead should I list seasonal wall art?

About six to eight weeks before the peak, then refresh the hero image as the date approaches.

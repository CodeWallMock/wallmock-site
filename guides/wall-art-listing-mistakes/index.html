Wall art listing mistakes that cause returns and bad reviews

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Many wall art returns start with a listing that promised something different: a frame that isn't included, a size that looks bigger in the mockup, colours brighter than the print, a different frame finish, or art cropped to fit a size. On Etsy, an item that differs from its photos or description can become a case where the seller refunds the item, the original shipping and the return shipping. Make every photo and line match what arrives.

A bad review on wall art is often not about the art at all. It is about the gap between the listing and the parcel. Here are the common gaps, why buyers notice, and how to close each one, with a canvas print mockup generator that shows the exact product.

What happens when a listing doesn't match

Etsy's rules make the cost clear (checked 2026-10-29):

  • "Not as described" can include, among other things, a colour, design or model different from what the listing description and photos showed, or a condition that was misrepresented (Etsy).
  • Who pays: if Etsy finds the item doesn't match the listing, the seller must refund the cost of the item, the original shipping and the return shipping (Etsy).
  • Purchase Protection covers qualifying orders where the item differs significantly from the description or photos (Etsy).
  • Your return policy is yours to set; Etsy says sellers aren't required to accept returns unless their policy says so (Etsy). A mismatch with the listing is a different matter.
  • Photos must accurately represent what the buyer will receive (Etsy).

For a framed canvas, a refund including return shipping can cost more than the sale earned. Prevention is cheaper.

Size and crop mistakes

Size causes a lot of the disappointment, because a room mockup is a feeling, not a measurement. Three habits fix most of it:

  1. One size in every room photo. Use your most popular size at a believable width over the furniture, and don't change it from photo to photo.
  2. A size guide image. Every size you sell, side by side and labelled in inches and centimetres. See how to make a size guide image.
  3. Only sizes that fit the ratio. A 2:3 file printed at 16 × 20 in loses part of the art. The print size calculator lists the sizes your file fits, and the print resolution checker shows how large it prints sharply.

Product mistakes: frame, finish, mat and edge

When a print-on-demand provider makes the item, your photos must show its product, not a generic one:

  • Frame or no frame exactly as shipped. If the frame is an option, every photo of the unframed option shows no frame.
  • Finish names that match: if the provider calls it "natural wood", don't call it walnut.
  • Mat or no mat as the provider makes it.
  • Floating gap or no gap. A floating-frame canvas and a canvas in a regular frame look different up close.
  • Canvas edge as printed.

Order a sample of each finish and photograph it. Etsy needs at least one unedited photo of the actual item for most listings, with an exception for authorised print-on-demand mockups (Etsy), and it is the best check that your mockups match. Etsy also requires you to disclose a production partner on the listing (Etsy).

Colour mistakes

  • Export in sRGB. Etsy converts listing images to sRGB and suggests converting before upload if colours look wrong (Etsy).
  • Don't flatter the art. Extra saturation or warmth in a mockup makes the print look dull when it arrives.
  • Compare with a sample. Hold the print next to your screen in daylight. If they differ, adjust the file you send to print, not just the mockup.
  • Say it once. A short note that colours vary slightly between screens and prints sets a fair expectation; it doesn't excuse a real mismatch.

A pre-publish check

  1. The first photo shows exactly the product, frame and finish the default option ships with.
  2. The title and first line say framed or unframed, and the product type.
  3. Every size listed is in the file's ratio and prints sharply.
  4. Artwork size and outer size are both stated for framed items.
  5. A size guide image and a scale photo are in the listing.
  6. An unedited photo of a real sample is in the listing (unless your product qualifies for Etsy's exception).
  7. Processing and delivery times match your provider.

Questions

What counts as 'not as described' on Etsy?

Etsy's help page says it can include an item whose colour, design or model differs from what the listing description and photos showed, the wrong quantity, or a misrepresented condition.

Who pays for returns when wall art is not as described?

If Etsy decides the item doesn't match the listing, the seller must refund the item, the original shipping and the return shipping.

Do I have to accept returns on Etsy?

Etsy says sellers aren't required to accept returns, exchanges or refunds unless their return policy says so. An item that doesn't match its listing can still lead to a case and a refund.

How do I stop 'it's smaller than I expected' reviews?

Show one realistic size in every room photo, add a size guide with every size labelled in inches and centimetres, and state the outer size for framed items.

Are mockups the cause of returns?

Not by themselves. Returns come from mockups that show a different product, size, finish or colour from what arrives. A mockup that matches the item is fine.

Framed Poster Mockups for German POD Sellers

Published by WallMock · Updated 5 November 2026

A2 and 50 × 70 cm are different products, even though their proportions look close in a thumbnail. Confirm the supplier’s paper and outer frame dimensions for each size. We recommend WallMock for comparing both options in the same room. See WallMock’s EUR plan prices for Germany.

Check the crop before you duplicate the poster

A2 is 420 × 594 mm. A 50 × 70 cm design scaled to 420 mm wide becomes 588 mm high, leaving 3 mm above and below on A2. To fill the A2 page edge to edge, fit the height and check the small trim on either side. Keep any lettering clear of the trim and the frame’s lip.

Use the aspect ratio calculator on the visible opening when a mount is included. Your print file still needs to follow the printer’s trim and bleed instructions.

Check the exact frame option at the lab

theprintspace lists its Düsseldorf location and publishes a guide to setting up framed variants. Confirm that the lab can supply the size and finish you have chosen before putting that option in your listing.

Make an A2 image and a 50 × 70 cm image if you sell both. For a German listing, labels such as Papierformat and Rahmenaußenmaß distinguish the paper format from the outer frame. Put the measurements beside the product options too.

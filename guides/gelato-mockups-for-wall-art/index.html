Gelato mockups for wall art: Mockup Studio vs room mockups

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Gelato gives you two mockup tools. Every product gets scenes to choose from when you publish to Etsy or Shopify. Mockup Studio, built for wall art, lets you pick a background scene, size and place the product, add layers and download the result; Gelato says it is for Gelato+ subscribers and that others can try it. Use them for product views, and add your own room photos when you want the same rooms and frame across a whole collection.

Gelato's mockups are made for its own products, which is a good start. This guide covers what each tool does, the Gelato frames your photos must match, and where a frame mockup generator or framed canvas mockups add what Gelato's scenes don't.

To open Mockup Studio, log in, go to your dashboard and click Mockup Studio on the left. Gelato's article states no usage limits or pixel dimensions, and doesn't mention AI (checked 2026-10-29).

Gelato also has a Multiple products flow: upload your designs, review the generated previews, choose products and their mockups, then publish or save as templates (source).

How many Gelato mockups reach your listing

  • Etsy: Gelato sends up to 10 mockups per product, including the main image. It makes mockups only for the main colour you pick; other colours are uploaded to Etsy by hand. Etsy links variant images to one option only, such as Size or Style (source).
  • Shopify: you choose the mockups and the main image, and whether they upload as PNG, WebP or JPG. Shopify trial stores don't receive mockup images while the trial is active, because Shopify doesn't allow adding product images by URL for trial stores (source).

Etsy itself allows up to 20 photos per listing (source). That leaves 10 slots for your own room photos, a close-up and a photo of your sample.

Two details catch sellers out. Framed posters ship as a separate frame and poster in one package unless you turn on Ready-to-Hang (source). And the Gelato help pages we checked don't mention mats, so show a framed print without a mat unless your product has one. For how frame width and mats change the look, see frame and mat proportions.

When Gelato's mockups are enough, and when to add room photos

Gelato's scenes are a quick way to show the product. You'll want your own room photos when:

  • You sell a collection. Buyers browsing your shop should see the same few rooms and the same frame across every design, so the shop looks planned.
  • The frame needs to be exact. Gelato's 12 mm framed canvas gap and its frame colour are what the buyer gets; the photo should show both clearly.
  • You sell sets. A pair or trio should hang together in one room at believable spacing. Our guide to pairs and trios covers layouts.
  • You need more photos. Gelato sends up to 10 mockups to Etsy; the other 10 slots are yours.

Adding your own room photos to Gelato listings

Etsy

  1. Add the product from Gelato (Stores, your Etsy store, Add product). Gelato saves it as a draft, which avoids Etsy listing fees until you publish (source).
  2. Open the draft in Shop Manager, add your room photos, and drag your best one into first place.
  3. If you connect an existing Etsy listing instead, Gelato asks whether it should upload photos. Decline if you want to keep yours. Don't change the SKU of a connected product in Shop Manager (source).

Shopify

  1. In Shopify admin, open the product. To remove a Gelato mockup, hover over it in the Media section, tick it and click Remove (Gelato).
  2. Add your room photos in the Media section and drag one to the first position: the first media item is the featured image (Shopify Help).

Check each image's size and shape before uploading with the free Etsy listing photo checker.

Questions

Is Gelato Mockup Studio free?

Gelato says Mockup Studio is available for Gelato+ subscribers, and users without a premium subscription can still try it (checked 2026-10-29).

What is Gelato Mockup Studio for?

Gelato says it is designed for the wall art category. You pick a background scene, size and position the product, add layers such as a logo or text, and download the mockup or save it for your products.

How many mockups does Gelato send to Etsy?

Up to 10 per product, including the main image. Etsy allows 20 photos per listing, so you can add your own room photos alongside.

Can I use my own mockups with Gelato products?

Yes. Add them to the Etsy or Shopify listing. When connecting an existing Etsy listing, Gelato asks whether it should upload photos, so you can keep your own.

Does Gelato's framed canvas have a gap?

Yes. Gelato's canvas guide states a 12 mm gap between the canvas and the frame, with a frame front 9–14 mm wide.

Do Gelato framed posters arrive assembled?

Not by default. Gelato ships the frame and poster separately in one package, unless you turn on its Ready-to-Hang option.

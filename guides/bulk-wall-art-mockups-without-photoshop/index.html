How to make mockups for 100 printables without Photoshop

By Akshit, founder of WallMock and an Etsy wall-art seller since 2023 · Updated 21 November 2026 · How we write our guides

Short answer: Save the rooms and the framed-print look you want once, as Favourites, then add the collection and generate it together: every printable is made in every saved room, with no PSD templates or smart objects. A collection of printables repeats the same decisions a hundred times: the same rooms, the same frame, the same export. This guide makes those decisions once. It is how WallMock's bulk flow actually works, with the counts and limits from our pricing page and no time promises.

What a printable listing has to show

A buyer of a digital download gets a file, prints it and puts it in a frame, so the listing has to show that finished result: the art framed on a wall, in a frame they could buy. A thin black, white or natural oak frame, with or without a white mat, covers most of what printable buyers hang. Etsy also asks digital download listings to include a preview of the content, so one photo should show the artwork itself, with the frame and room labelled as not included.

The number that decides your plan is printables multiplied by rooms. Work it out before you open anything, because it also decides how many photos each listing gets.

Prepare the files, and name them once

Most printable listings include several ratio files (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 11:14 and ISO A are the usual set), but the room photos only need one file per design: the ratio most buyers print, which for many shops is 2:3. Upload another ratio only when you want it shown framed. WallMock keeps each file's own shape and never stretches it, so a 4:5 file goes into rooms that suit 4:5.

Before you upload, give every file a short artwork ID, the name you will use for that design everywhere: sage-botanical-07_2x3.png. Keep three folders apart: the untouched source art, the ratio files the buyer downloads, and the listing photos. A file name that says which design it is saves you from the worst bulk mistake: a photo of the wrong printable in a listing.

Save the rooms once, as Favourites

Heart the rooms you want for this collection; they go into your Favourites and stay there for the next one. Three to five is plenty for most printables: a hero living room, a bedroom, and one room that fits the subject, such as a kitchen for botanicals or a nursery for children's prints. Keep most of them neutral; a bold wall is for art that suits it. The room mockups by style page is a quick way to shortlist.

Then choose the product once: a framed print, its frame colour and the mat. On Lite or Pro you can save that look as a reusable product profile, with up to five custom frame colours. Rooms show which artwork shapes they support, so a room reviewed for portrait art may not offer a square or panoramic file.

Add the collection and generate it together

Add the printables, check the previews and generate: every printable is made in every Favourite room in one run. I built this flow for my own collections of canvas and framed prints, and it works the same way for printables with a framed-print look. Before you generate, look at the previews of three files: a pale one, a dark one and a colourful one. That is where a frame that disappears on a pale wall or a room that fights a bright palette shows up. Then read the planned image count and any combinations a room can't make. Run a big collection in batches you can check in one sitting, rather than all at once; how long a run takes depends on your device, the files and the number of rooms.

Downloads, folders and file names for Etsy

Downloads are 4K (4096 px on the long edge), as single JPEGs, a ZIP per artwork or one ZIP for the batch. Uploads and results belong to the current session, so download before you refresh the page; your Favourite rooms and saved looks stay.

Unzip each printable's photos into its own folder and rename them in the order they go into the listing, using one pattern: artwork ID, ratio, product and finish, photo role, revision. For example sage-botanical-07_2x3_print-oak-whitemat_hero_v01.jpg. File names are for finding the right photo, not for search; the file-naming guide has the full system. On paid plans the listing kit ZIP adds crops for a 4:3 Etsy first photo, 4:5, 2:3, 1:1 and 16:9, alt text for every photo and a listing checklist.

WallMock doesn't connect to Etsy, so the last step is yours: upload the photos in Etsy's listing editor.

The honest limits

  • Free: 50 previews and 10 watermark-free 4K downloads every 30 days, single artworks only. The first 10 downloads can use all 500+ rooms; after that, 8 free rooms remain.
  • Lite: 200 clean 4K images a month. Enough for 100 printables in 2 rooms, or 40 in 5.
  • Pro and the WallMock Studio plan: no monthly image count, with the fair-use line above. Pro adds listing videos.
  • Sets and listing kits need a paid plan.
  • A computer: on a phone WallMock makes one artwork in one room at a time; bulk batches are made on a computer.
  • Your device does the work: images are made in your browser, so speed depends on your computer, the files and the batch size. Try a representative file before a big run.

A checklist for the next collection

  1. Count: printables × rooms, and the plan that covers it.
  2. Name each file with an artwork ID; one file per design for the room photos.
  3. Open Favourites: the rooms and the framed-print look are already saved.
  4. Preview a pale, a dark and a colourful file; generate the batch.
  5. Download, unzip into one folder per printable, rename in listing order.
  6. Check a few photos at 100%, then add the preview of the artwork itself and upload.

Questions

Can I make mockups for 100 printables without Photoshop?

Yes. In WallMock you save your rooms and the framed-print look once, add the printables and generate them together; every file goes into every saved room, with no PSD templates, smart objects or actions.

How many images do 100 printables need?

Printables multiplied by rooms. 100 printables in 2 rooms is 200 images, which Lite's 200 a month covers; in 5 rooms it is 500, which needs Pro or the WallMock Studio plan, which have no monthly image count.

Is there a daily limit?

Fair use: paid plans can open any room in the library. Up to 300 images a day can be made on WallMock's server; images made in your browser, on your device, do not count toward that limit.

Which frame should printables be shown in?

The frame your buyers are most likely to use: a thin black, white or natural oak frame, with or without a white mat. Say in the listing that the frame is not included.

Can I upload the files straight to Etsy from WallMock?

No. WallMock doesn't connect to Etsy. You download the photos (4096 px on the long edge) and upload them in Etsy's listing editor.

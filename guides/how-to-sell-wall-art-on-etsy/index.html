How to sell wall art on Etsy: products, photos and mockups

Published by WallMock · Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer: To sell wall art on Etsy, decide whether you sell printables or physical prints. For prints, make them yourself or use a print provider you list as a production partner. Then build listings with up to 20 photos. Etsy needs images that show exactly what buyers receive, and usually at least one unedited photo of the real item. Add room mockups that match your frame and size, and disclose AI if you used it to make the art.

Selling wall art on Etsy comes down to three decisions: what you sell, who makes it, and how your photos show it. This guide covers each, with Etsy's own rules linked, and where a wall art mockup generator built for Etsy and Shopify sellers saves time.

1. Choose what you'll sell

Etsy groups items by how they're made. Your own designs, sold as digital downloads or printed by a third party, fall under "Designed by a seller" (Etsy). For wall art that means three common models:

ModelWhat the buyer getsWhat to check
Printable (digital download)Files they print themselvesRatios and sizes included; previews of the real files. See mockups for printable wall art
Print on demandA framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed print made and shipped by a partnerProduction partner listed; sample ordered; mockups match the partner's frames. See how to choose a provider
You print and shipPrints you make or have made locallyYour own photos of the real item; packaging and shipping

For physical prints, also choose the format. A framed canvas, a stretched canvas, a framed print with a mat and an unframed poster are four different products with different photos. Canvas vs framed print mockups explains how to show each one.

2. Set up your production partner

If a print-on-demand company prints your work, Etsy requires you to disclose it on the listings (Etsy). Etsy's own examples of print-on-demand partners include Printful, Printify and Gooten. The steps on Etsy.com:

  1. Shop Manager → Settings → Partners you work with.
  2. Add a new production partner: enter the partner's name and location. You can show a descriptive title instead of the name.
  3. Describe how you work with them, then save.
  4. Choose the partner on each listing, or in bulk with Listings → Editing options → Change production partners.

Etsy also asks you to set shipping locations to match where the partner ships from.

3. Know Etsy's photo rules before you make mockups

Etsy's listing image rules (checked 2026-10-26). The key points from Etsy's help page and image requirements:

  • All images must show the item's true colour, condition, scale and materials.
  • Most listings need at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. Some item types have exceptions. For print-on-demand items, Etsy describes using an authorised stock photo mockup. Read the Listing Image Requirements policy for your product.
  • Other photos can be edited, including with AI, to change lighting, crop or background, as long as the item doesn't look different.
  • Make the first photo the clearest, most realistic view of what buyers get.

The technical side:

RuleEtsy's guidance
Photos per listingUp to 20 photos and 2 videos of 5–15 seconds (source)
SizeAt least 2000 px in width and height recommended; first photo at least 635 px (source)
First photo shapeHorizontal (landscape) or square, so the thumbnail crops well
File types.jpg, .gif, .png, .svg or .heic; no animated GIFs; transparent areas show as black
ColourConvert to sRGB if colours look wrong
ThumbnailYour first photo; reorder by dragging in the listing editor (source)

For framed canvas and posters, the simplest way to meet the unedited-photo rule is to order a sample and photograph it in daylight. Then use room mockups for the other slots.

4. Plan the photos: a sample, then rooms

A wall art listing has to answer three questions: what does it look like at home, how big is it, and what exactly arrives? A practical order:

  1. Hero room: your best room mockup, art clearly the focus. If Etsy's rules make your unedited photo the clearest view, put that first.
  2. Scale: the art above a sofa, bed or sideboard, at the size you sell.
  3. Sample photo: the real print, frame corner or canvas edge.
  4. Other rooms: bedroom, living room, office or nursery, so different buyers see their home.
  5. Options: frame colours, sizes and sets, one clear image each.

The full 20-slot version is in Etsy listing photos for wall art, and the how-to is in how to make wall art mockups for Etsy. For size photos, use the art size over a sofa tool.

5. Mockups that match the print

A mockup is only useful if the buyer receives what it shows. Check each image against this list before you upload:

  • Same product: framed canvas, stretched canvas, framed print or poster.
  • Same frame colour and mat as the variant.
  • Same artwork ratio, not cropped or stretched to fit the room.
  • Believable size for the size you sell.
  • Artwork untouched: text, signature and colours exactly as printed.
  • At least 2000 px, sRGB, no transparency.

Check that your file can print at your largest size with the print resolution checker.

6. If AI was part of making the art

Etsy allows AI-assisted art when the seller plays a clear creative role, and requires you to disclose AI use in the listing description (Etsy). That rule is about the artwork you sell. Editing your other listing photos is covered by the photo rules above. Our guide to selling AI art prints on Etsy covers disclosure and print quality. Questions

How do I sell wall art on Etsy?

Choose printables or physical prints. For prints, use your own production or a print-on-demand partner listed on your listings. Make listings with up to 20 photos that accurately show the item, usually including one unedited photo of the real item, plus room mockups that match the frame and size.

How many photos can an Etsy listing have?

Up to 20 photos and 2 videos of 5 to 15 seconds, according to Etsy's help page on creating a listing.

What size should Etsy listing photos be?

Etsy recommends at least 2000 pixels in width and height, and at least 635 pixels for the first photo. Use a landscape or square first photo, in sRGB, without transparency.

Are mockups allowed on Etsy?

Yes, as long as they accurately show what the buyer receives. Most listings also need at least one unedited photo of the real item; Etsy describes exceptions, including authorised stock mockups for some print-on-demand items. Read Etsy's Listing Image Requirements for your product.

Do I need to list my print provider on Etsy?

Yes. If a print-on-demand company makes your items, add it as a production partner in Shop Manager and select it on each listing.

Do I have to disclose AI on Etsy?

If an item is created with AI, Etsy requires you to say so in the listing description. That applies to the artwork itself. Edited listing photos have separate rules: they must not make the item look different. Etsy also recommends noting in your description if any listing photos were edited with AI.

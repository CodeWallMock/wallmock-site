How to add mockups to Shopify products automatically (wall art)

Published by WallMock · Updated 6 November 2026

Short answer: By hand, upload the mockups to each product in the Shopify admin, drag the hero first and assign one image per frame variant. To do it automatically, use a script on Shopify's Admin API or an app with product-edit permission. WallMock's Send to Shopify, in beta, adds your mockups to your products hero first and never deletes your photos.

Uploading mockups is the dull half of listing wall art on Shopify. This guide covers the manual steps, what an automatic method needs, and what to check before you connect anything to your store.

The manual way: upload in the Shopify admin

Every Shopify seller starts here. For each product:

  1. Make or download the mockups for that artwork, and name each file by artwork and frame colour.
  2. In your Shopify admin, open Products and the product.
  3. Upload the images to the product's media, then drag them into order: the hero first, then the other rooms and close-ups.
  4. If the product has frame variants, click the image icon next to each variant and choose its matching photo.
  5. Save, then check the product page on a phone.

Shopify allows one assigned image per variant, chosen from the product's media, and up to 250 media items per product. Square images around 2048 × 2048 px usually display best. For a handful of products this is fine. For a collection of 30 designs it is hundreds of uploads, and the order is easy to get wrong.

What “automatic” really means on Shopify

Images reach a Shopify product in one of three ways:

MethodHow it worksGood for
By handUpload in the admin, drag into order, assign variant imagesA few products
Your own scriptCode that uses Shopify's Admin API with permission to edit productsShops with a developer
A connected appAn app you approve sends images to your products for youCollections, without code

Whichever you use, check three things before you connect anything to your store:

  • Permissions. Adding images needs product editing. Be wary of anything that asks for more than the job needs.
  • Existing photos. Does it add images, or replace the ones you have? Replacing can wipe photos you made by hand.
  • Order. Does the hero land first? Buyers see the first image on collection pages and in search.

The WallMock way (beta): Send to Shopify

Beta Send to Shopify is in beta testing. It is not generally available and not in the Shopify App Store.

  1. Make the mockups. Upload a collection to WallMock, heart your rooms and pick your frames once. Every artwork is made in every chosen room, and your art is never redrawn.
  2. Connect your store. Approve one permission: editing products.
  3. Send. The images are added to your Shopify products, hero first. Your existing photos stay; nothing is deleted.

If a print-on-demand app manages your products

A print-on-demand app may publish its own mockups to the products it creates, and a later sync can replace photos you added. Before adding room photos by any method, check the app's publish settings, and keep your original image files so you can restore them. Our Shopify wall art product images guide covers fixing images after a sync.

A checklist before you send a collection

  • One hero per artwork, with the art large and centred.
  • The same room for every frame variant, so only the frame changes.
  • One shape for every main image, usually square, so collection pages line up.
  • File names by artwork and frame, so a variant never points to the wrong photo.

Questions

Is there a Shopify app that adds mockups to my products automatically?

WallMock's Send to Shopify does this, in beta testing: mockups go straight onto your Shopify products, hero first, and your existing photos are never deleted. It is not generally available and not in the Shopify App Store; request beta access.

How do I add mockups to Shopify products by hand?

Open the product in your Shopify admin, upload the images to its media, drag them into order with the hero first, then assign one image to each frame variant and save.

How many images can a Shopify product have?

Up to 250 media items per product, with one assigned image per variant.

What permission does an app need to add product images?

Permission to edit products. Send to Shopify asks for that permission only.

Will automatic uploads delete my existing photos?

It depends on the tool, so check before you connect it. Send to Shopify only adds images; it never deletes your photos.

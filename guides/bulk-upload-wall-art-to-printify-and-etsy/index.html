Bulk-upload a wall art collection to Printify and Etsy with your own room mockups

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Build one Printify product as a template, duplicate it for each artwork and swap the design: Printify keeps the variants, prices, descriptions and provider settings. Make every room photo in one batch, named by artwork. Attach them per product in Printify's Mockup Library, or add them on Etsy and publish later updates with Mockups unticked. Printify can publish selected products together and Etsy can bulk-edit text fields, but photos go on one listing at a time.

Listing a collection one artwork at a time repeats the same steps over and over. Most of them can be batched; a few can't. This guide shows which is which, using a bulk mockup generator for the photos and Printify's own tools for the products. WallMock makes the images only; it doesn't upload to Printify or Etsy.

Step 1: prepare the collection

  • Name files by artwork: misty-pier.jpg, fig-branch.jpg. You'll use the same name for the Printify product, the photo folder and the Etsy listing.
  • Group by ratio. A 2:3 file and a 4:5 file need different print sizes. Check yours with the print ratio guide.
  • Check resolution for the largest size you'll offer with the print resolution checker.
  • Pick the Printify product and frame colour once, for example a gallery canvas wrap in black or walnut. Our Printify framed canvas guide compares the options.
  • Order one sample before you make photos for twenty listings.

Step 2: make every room photo in one batch

In WallMock, heart the rooms that suit the collection, choose the product (framed canvas in the finish that matches your sample: black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold or silver), and add all the artworks. Each artwork comes out in every saved room at its own proportions, as 4096 px images. Download them as individual JPEGs, per-artwork ZIPs or one batch ZIP.

Plan the count first: artworks × rooms. With 20 artworks and 6 rooms that's 120 images, within Lite's 200 clean 4K images a month; Pro and Studio include unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Free watermarked previews in every room.

Then plan each listing's 20 photo slots (Etsy allows 20 photos and 2 videos):

  • slot 1: your strongest room photo (the thumbnail);
  • slots 2–6: the other room photos;
  • a close-up or provider mockup of the frame;
  • a photo of your sample, for Etsy's unedited-photo rule;
  • size and detail images, keeping the total at 20 or fewer.

Keep a folder per artwork with files numbered in that order, such as misty-pier/01-living-room.jpg.

Step 3: build one template product, then duplicate it

  1. Create the first product in Printify completely: provider, sizes, prices, description, tags and shipping.
  2. Check it against Etsy's limits: up to two options per listing, 70 choices per option and 400 variants in total. Printify itself limits a listing to 100 variants (source).
  3. Save the artwork position as an artwork template if you want identical placement on each product: select the elements on the canvas and click Save as template (source).
  4. In My Products, click the Duplicate icon for each new artwork. Printify says the copy keeps the variants, pricing, descriptions, mockups and print provider settings (source).
  5. Open each copy, replace the design, and rename it with the artwork's name.

A duplicate keeps the original's mockups. If you uploaded custom room photos to the first product, remove them from each copy and add that artwork's own photos, or your listing will show the wrong artwork.

Step 4: attach your room photos (one route per collection)

Route A: in Printify, before publishing

For each product: My products, open it, View all mockups, Upload, choose the variant the photo belongs to, set the order, Save selection. Printify accepts JPG, PNG or SVG up to 30,000 × 30,000 px (source). Keep to 20 mockups per product for Etsy (source).

Route B: on Etsy, after publishing

Publish first, then add your photos to each Etsy listing and drag the best one first. For every later update from Printify, publish with Mockups unticked so your photos stay (source). Our guide to replacing Printify mockups covers this routine.

Route A keeps everything in Printify, so republishing is simpler. Route B is quicker if you're already editing each Etsy listing by hand. If you group products into a Multi-Product Listing on Etsy, Printify manages its mockups in that listing, and you reselect them after changing a design file (source).

Step 5: publish together, then finish on Etsy

  1. In Printify's My Products, select the products, click Publish, tick the details to send (with Mockups ticked for Route A), and click Confirm (source).
  2. Production partner: if your Etsy shop already has a partner named "Printify", Printify assigns it to listings when it publishes; otherwise the listing defaults to "Made by seller" and you change it on Etsy (source).
  3. On Etsy, go to Listings, tick the new listings and use Editing options to set production partners, tags, titles, descriptions, prices or shipping profiles in one go (source).
  4. Open each listing once to add or reorder photos (Route B) and check the thumbnail.

If you later edit titles or tags on Etsy, Printify's Update from Etsy can pull them back for up to 24 products at once. It doesn't bring photos or videos back (source), so keep your photo folders.

Questions

Can I bulk upload designs to Printify?

Printify's help centre describes duplicating a product and changing its design, saving artwork templates, and publishing many products at once. We found no article on uploading many designs as separate products in one step (checked 2026-10-29).

Does duplicating a Printify product copy the mockups?

Yes. Printify says a duplicate keeps the variants, pricing, descriptions, mockups and print provider settings, so replace any custom mockups with the new artwork's photos.

Can I add photos to many Etsy listings at once?

No. Etsy's bulk editing covers titles, tags, descriptions, prices, custom options, production partners, renewal options, processing and shipping profiles. Photos are added and reordered per listing.

How many mockups can I publish from Printify to Etsy?

Up to 20 per product, across all colour variants. Publishing fails above that.

How many room images will my collection need?

Artworks multiplied by rooms. 20 artworks in 6 rooms is 120 images. WallMock's Lite plan includes 200 clean 4K images a month; Pro and Studio include unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images.

Does WallMock upload to Printify or Etsy?

No. WallMock makes the room photos in bulk; you add them to Printify's Mockup Library or to your Etsy listings yourself.

Framed print with a mat or without: which to offer, and how to show the mat

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Offer a mat when the art is small, detailed or photographic, or when the buyer wants a gallery look: photography, botanicals, line art and smaller prints. Skip it for bold graphic posters, large prints and edge-to-edge designs. A mat shrinks the visible art inside a given frame, so state both the print size and the frame size. In mockups, show the mat at the width you ship: Prodigi uses 2.5 to 5 cm mounts depending on frame size (checked 2026-10-29).

Mat or no mat changes the look, the size and the price of a framed print. Decide per product, then show the same choice with a frame mockup generator that makes framed prints with or without a mat.

A mat also has a practical job: it keeps the glazing off the print. Prodigi lists protection as a reason to use one, along with presentation (checked 2026-10-29).

How a mat changes the size

A mat takes space inside the frame, so the same frame shows a smaller picture. The mat opening is also cut a little smaller than the print, about 1/8 in (3 mm) on each edge, so the mat holds the print in place. Some worked examples:

PrintFrameMat border (sides × top and bottom)
5×7 in8×10 inAbout 1.6 × 1.6 in
8×10 in11×14 inAbout 1.6 × 2.1 in
12×16 in16×20 inAbout 2.1 × 2.1 in
11×14 in16×20 inAbout 2.6 × 3.1 in

Two things follow. In your listing, give both numbers: "Print 8×10 in, frame 11×14 in with white mat". And keep signatures and important detail at least 1/8 in from the print's edge, or the mat will cover them. How to show the mat in a mockup

  • Match the width you ship. A 2 in mat in the photo and a 1 in mat in the box is a different product.
  • Match the colour. White and off-white look different next to each other; use the one your provider names.
  • Keep the art size honest. With a mat, the art is smaller than the frame. Don't show a matted 8×10 print filling an 11×14 frame.
  • Check the edge. A pale print in a white mat can disappear at thumbnail size. Look at the photo small before you publish.
  • Keep it consistent. Use the same frame and mat across the listing, so the buyer knows what arrives.

On colour, Prodigi's advice is that white and off-white mounts are classic for good reason, and that a coloured mount can pick up tones in the art or add contrast. It also says a double mount, two layers of card, adds depth and a more premium, gallery-style finish (checked 2026-10-29). Only show a double or coloured mat if your provider makes it.

Offering both in one listing

If your provider makes both, offer them as variations and show each one. Etsy lets you add up to 3 variation types, such as size, frame and mat, and link a photo to each option, up to 20 options with photos (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29).

Make the photos in the same room with the same artwork, changing only the mat. The buyer then sees exactly what the upgrade does. If you sell in several frame colours too, see which frame colour to show.

Printables and mats

Digital-download buyers frame prints themselves, often in a ready-made frame with a mat. Tell them which print size fits which frame: an 8×10 print fits an 11×14 frame with a mat, and an 11×14 print fits a 16×20 frame. Show the printable in a simple frame with a mat, as most buyers will hang it; our guide to mockups for printable wall art covers the rest.

Questions

Does a mat make a framed print look more expensive?

It usually makes it look more finished and gallery-like, especially for photography and small, detailed art. Bold posters often look better without one.

What size mat for a 16×20 frame?

It depends on the print. A 12×16 in print gets about 2.1 in of mat on every side; an 11×14 in print gets about 2.6 in at the sides and 3.1 in at top and bottom.

Does the mat cover part of the print?

Yes, a little. The opening is usually cut about 1/8 in (3 mm) smaller than the print on each edge, so keep signatures and key detail away from the edge.

Should a mat be white or off-white?

Either works; white is crisper and off-white softer. Use the colour your provider ships, and show the same one in every photo.

Can I sell framed prints with and without a mat in one listing?

Yes. Add the mat as a variation and link a photo to each option, ideally the same room with only the mat changed.

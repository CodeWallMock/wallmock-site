Etsy thumbnail for wall art: how to make a crop-safe first photo

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Your Etsy thumbnail is your first listing photo, and Etsy crops it to square, portrait and landscape views in different places. For wall art, use a landscape or square room photo at least 2000 px wide, taken from further back than feels natural, with the whole artwork in the centre and clear space on every side. Then preview it with Etsy's Adjust Thumbnail tool and fix the crop before you publish.

A thumbnail has one job: make a buyer recognise your art at a glance in a grid of other wall art. This guide covers the shape, the size and the safe area for that first photo. The Etsy mockup generator makes the room photos, and the free Etsy listing photo checker tests the crop.

What Etsy says about the first photo

Etsy's image help page and its photo-editing page set the rules (checked 2026-10-29):

  • The first photo is the thumbnail for your listing.
  • It should be horizontal (landscape) or square, and it sets the shape of the photos that follow.
  • It should be at least 635 px wide and tall, or the listing may show up lower in search. Etsy recommends at least 2000 px for listing photos.
  • Thumbnails are cropped to square, portrait and landscape views in different places on Etsy. Leave enough border that each crop keeps the whole product.
  • Etsy's tips: shoot further back than you think you need, keep the most important part in the centre, and upload horizontal images rather than square crops.

Sources: Etsy: Requirements and best practices for images, Etsy: How to edit your listing photos.

Etsy also asks that the first photo is the clearest, most realistic view of what the buyer receives (Etsy). For wall art, that means the frame and finish you actually ship.

The safe area: where the art must sit

Three crops of one photo leave one area that survives all of them: the middle. On a landscape photo, the square crop is the tightest from the sides, and the portrait crop tighter still. Work it out for your file:

Etsy doesn't publish the exact portrait shape, so treat the square as the minimum and leave more wall than that. A simple rule for wall art: keep the frame inside the middle half of the photo's width (wide art no wider than about 60% of it), with clear wall above and below. What makes a wall art thumbnail work

  • The art is the biggest thing that isn't wall. A sofa, bed or sideboard gives scale, but it shouldn't outshine the picture.
  • A calm room. Plain walls and few objects read better at a small size than shelves and plants.
  • Contrast with the wall. A pale print on a white wall disappears in a thumbnail. Choose a wall that is lighter or darker than the art's edges.
  • The frame is visible. A buyer should be able to tell canvas from framed print even at thumbnail size. Our guide to canvas vs framed print mockups shows the difference.
  • The whole artwork. Never crop into the art itself. A thumbnail that cuts off the frame looks like a mistake.

Panoramas, tall art and sets

Shape decides how much room you need around the art:

  • Panoramas (3:1, 2:1): the art is wide, so the side crops bite first. Use a room shot from further back, with the panorama spanning no more than about 60% of the photo's width.
  • Tall art (1:2, 1:3): a landscape photo leaves the art small. Use a tall wall between doors or windows, shot from far enough back that the top and bottom of the frame clear the landscape crop.
  • Pairs and trios: the group is wide. Keep the whole set inside the middle and accept that each piece is smaller; the next photos can show them closer. The guide to panoramic and tall art mockups has room ideas.

Check the crop before you publish

  1. Run the file through the photo checker: size, shape, file type and the square crop.
  2. Upload it as the first photo. On Etsy.com, edit the listing, choose Adjust Thumbnail, use the slider to zoom, and position the image so the art is clear in all three views. Then select Apply (Etsy).
  3. Look at the listing in search on your phone. If you can't name the artwork in one second, choose a calmer room or step further back.
  4. Keep the same first-photo style across a collection, so buyers recognise your shop in a grid. See one look for the whole shop.

Etsy's page warns that crops you save in its editor can't be undone; you have to upload the original again. Crop in your own files where you can.

Questions

What size is an Etsy thumbnail?

Etsy doesn't give a single thumbnail size, because it crops the first photo to square, portrait and landscape views in different places. It recommends listing photos of at least 2000 px, and at least 635 px for the first photo.

Should my first Etsy photo be square or landscape?

Etsy says the first photo should be landscape or square, and its thumbnail tips recommend horizontal images. A landscape room photo with the art in the centre survives the crops best.

Why is my Etsy thumbnail cutting off my art?

The art is too close to the edge of the photo. Use a photo taken from further back, keep the artwork in the middle, and adjust the thumbnail in the listing editor.

How do I change my Etsy thumbnail?

Drag the photo you want into the first position, then use Adjust Thumbnail in the listing editor to zoom and position it.

Can the thumbnail be a mockup?

Yes, if it accurately shows what the buyer receives. Most listings also need at least one unedited photo of the actual item; print-on-demand items can use an authorised stock mockup instead, according to Etsy.

Replace Printify and Printful mockups on Etsy and Shopify without breaking sync

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Both Printify and Printful let you use your own photos in Etsy or Shopify; republishing over them and editing variants or SKUs are the real risks. With Printify, either upload your images in its Mockup Library, or add them on Etsy or Shopify and untick Mockups every time you republish. Printful can't take your files, so add them in your store, keep a photo slot free on Etsy, and check the listing after any mockup refresh. Make every other change inside Printify or Printful.

Default provider mockups get your listing live; your own room photos show buyers how the piece looks at home. This guide shows how to swap them on Etsy and Shopify without losing the connection, using images from an Etsy mockup generator or the print-on-demand canvas mockups page.

What breaks sync, and what doesn't

Both providers' help pages treat photos as something you may change in your store. The risks they warn about are republishing over your photos and changing variants or SKUs. Here is what each help centre says (checked 2026-10-29):

Printify also notes that Update from Etsy brings back titles, descriptions and tags, but not mockups, images or videos (source). Your photos live only in your store and on your computer, so keep the files.

Printify on Etsy: pick one route per product

Route A: upload your photos into Printify

  1. In Printify, go to My products and open the product.
  2. Click View all mockups and choose Upload from the left menu.
  3. Select the variant the photo belongs to, adjust the order, and click Save selection.
  4. Publish with the Mockups option ticked (source).

Printify accepts JPG, PNG or SVG up to 30,000 × 30,000 px, and asks that custom mockups closely match the real product. Etsy takes up to 20 mockups per product across all colours; above that, publishing fails (source).

Route B: add your photos on Etsy

  1. Publish once from Printify.
  2. In Shop Manager, open the listing and add your room photos. Drag the best one into first place: the first photo is the thumbnail (Etsy Help).
  3. From then on, when you change prices, tags or descriptions in Printify, publish with Mockups unticked. Printify says this keeps manually uploaded mockups.

If you add a new size or colour and republish without ticking Mockups, Printify says that variant publishes without an image (source). Add a photo for it on Etsy straight away. Troubleshooting for mockups that don't appear is in our Printify mockups guide.

Printify on Shopify

Selective publishing works the same way on Shopify: untick Mockups, tick only what you changed, publish. Printify adds variant visibility options for Shopify and Etsy (source). In Shopify:

  1. Go to Products and open the product.
  2. Add your files in the Media section. The first media item is the featured image shown on collection pages, in the cart and at checkout, so drag your best room photo there (Shopify Help).
  3. To give a size or frame colour its own photo, click the image icon next to the variant. Each variant can have one image (source).

Shopify allows up to 250 media items per product, and image files up to 20 MB and 25 megapixels (source). Two Printify warnings: Shopify categories are overwritten on every republish, so reassign them after each one (source), and avoid changing IDs or SKUs on connected products (source).

Printful on Etsy and Shopify

Printful says it isn't possible to upload mockup images to Printful; you upload them directly to your store (source).

  1. Etsy: Printful adds the product as a draft. Open it in Shop Manager, add your room photos and put the best one first (source).
  2. Keep a slot free on Etsy. Printful can't push a new mockup to a listing that already has 20 images (source).
  3. Shopify: add your photos in the product's Media section and drag one into first place, as above.

If you need to refresh Printful's own mockups later (a new design, say), Printful's steps are: Stores, open the product, select all variants, change the print file without changing the design, go to the mockups step, choose the mockups and mark one as the primary image, keep Published updated mockups on, and continue (source). Then open the listing and check it. Printful notes that some integrations add the new mockups without removing old ones (source), so delete duplicates and put your room photo back in first place.

A safe routine for every product

  • Keep one folder per artwork with your room photos, named in listing order.
  • Choose Route A or Route B per product and write it down.
  • Change variants, sizes, SKUs and designs only in Printify or Printful, never in Etsy or Shopify.
  • Republish from Printify with Mockups unticked unless you mean to replace photos.
  • After any republish or mockup refresh, open the live listing and check the first photo.
  • Stay under 20 photos on Etsy, with one slot free for Printful listings.
  • Check your files first: Etsy recommends at least 2000 px in width and height, sRGB, and warns that files over 1 MB may not finish uploading on a slow connection (source). The Etsy listing photo checker tests each file in your browser.

Questions

Will replacing Printify mockups break the sync?

Not by itself. Printify's selective publishing keeps manually uploaded mockups as long as you leave Mockups unticked when you republish. Changing variants, IDs or SKUs in your store is what causes sync problems.

How do I stop Printify overwriting my Etsy photos?

Publish updates with only the changed details ticked and Mockups unticked. Printify says publishing its mockups replaces your Etsy mockups, including videos.

Can I upload my own mockups to Printful?

No. Printful says it isn't possible to upload mockup images to Printful. Add them directly to your Etsy or Shopify listing.

Why are my old Printful mockups still on the listing?

Printful says some integrations add new mockups without removing the old ones. Delete the old images in your store, then put your room photo back in first place.

How many photos can I add to a Shopify product?

Shopify allows up to 250 images, 3D models or videos per product, with one image per variant.

Does WallMock upload photos to Printify, Printful or Etsy?

No. WallMock makes the room photos; you upload them to your provider's mockup library or your store yourself.

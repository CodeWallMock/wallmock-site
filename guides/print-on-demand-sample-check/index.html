Order a print-on-demand sample and check it against your mockup

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Order one sample of your best seller in the frame colour you'll show, then compare it with your mockup in daylight: frame colour and width, depth, the gap on a floating frame, mat width, edges, colours and sharpness. Change the mockup to match the product, not the other way round. Printify and Gelato don't give free samples; Printful, Gelato and Prodigi offer sample discounts with conditions. Photograph the sample too: Etsy wants an unedited photo for most items.

A mockup is a promise about the product. A sample is how you check the promise. This guide covers how to order one from the main wall art providers and what to compare, so your photos from a canvas print mockup generator or framed canvas mockups show what buyers will get.

What to order

  • Your best seller, or the design you'll photograph most. One good sample beats five rushed ones.
  • The frame colour you'll show in listing photos. If you offer several finishes, a moulding sample pack (Prodigi offers one) is a cheap way to see every colour.
  • A size you can compare. Printful's sample sizes are fixed (8×10 in framed posters, 12×12 in canvas), so you'll check colour and frame, not scale.
  • A design with your darkest and lightest colours, so you can see how both print.

Printify recommends ordering samples from your chosen providers to see what customers receive, and says its mockups are representations (source). If you're choosing between providers, see Gelato vs Printify vs Printful first.

How to compare colour fairly

  • Check by a window in daylight, out of direct sun. Warm bulbs make every frame look browner.
  • Set your screen to a normal brightness, not the maximum. Bright screens make prints look dull.
  • Compare the same small area of print and file, such as a sky or a skin tone, not the whole picture.
  • Look at the frame next to something you know: a white wall, a black phone.
  • Printify says minor colour and placement differences can occur because providers use different equipment (source). Note what you see and decide whether it matters for your buyers.

Photograph the sample

Etsy requires at least one unedited photo for most items: the actual item, against a real background, not edited in any way. For print on demand, Etsy says you may use a stock photo mockup if its owner has authorised it (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29). Read Etsy's page for your product. A photo of your sample is the simplest way to meet the rule.

Take four shots while it's on the wall:

  1. straight on, in daylight, with some wall around it;
  2. a 45° angle showing the edge or the frame depth;
  3. a close-up of the frame corner or the canvas texture;
  4. the back, showing the hanger.

Use the first as the unedited photo and the others as detail slots. Our guide to Etsy listing photos for wall art shows where each fits.

Fix your mockups to match

If the sample and mockup disagree, change the mockup. In WallMock that means changing the saved look: a different finish (walnut or espresso for a dark brown frame), mat on or off, framed or stretched canvas. Then run the collection again with the same rooms.

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Judge print colours against your original file rather than a room photo, because any room scene shows the art in that room's light. For frame finishes side by side, see frame colours for wall art. Questions

Is it worth ordering a print-on-demand sample?

Yes, for at least your best seller. It's the only way to check the frame colour, depth, gap and print colours before buyers see them, and it gives you a photo of the real item for Etsy.

Does Printify give free samples?

No. Printify says sample orders are at regular pricing. Choose Sample after clicking Create order, and it ships to your account address.

What sizes can I order as a Printful sample?

Printful says framed posters can only be sampled at 8×10 in and canvas at 12×12 in, with a 20% discount (checked 2026-10-29).

How do I get Prodigi's sample discount?

Place your first sample order through Prodigi's manual order form, then email the order number to its support team for a 50% refund. It's limited to one product type per order.

Does Gelato offer a sample discount?

New sellers get 30% off the product cost of a first sample order within 48 hours of sign-up, up to 50€ off. Gelato also runs seasonal sample discounts.

Do I need a photo of the real item on Etsy?

For most items, Etsy requires at least one unedited photo of the actual item. Print-on-demand sellers may use an authorised stock photo mockup instead; a photo of your sample is the simplest option.

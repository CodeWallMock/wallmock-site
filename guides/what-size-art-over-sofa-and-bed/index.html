What size art over a sofa, bed or sideboard? A simple size guide

Most sizing mistakes are art that is too small. A few simple proportions, and the furniture widths most homes have, tell you which print sizes look balanced, in a listing and on a real wall.

By WallMock · Published 5 October 2026

The two-thirds guideline

A common interior guideline is that art above furniture looks balanced at about two thirds to three quarters of the furniture's width, including the frame. A single piece, a pair or a trio counts as one group: measure the whole group.

It is a guideline, not a law. Oversized art can be dramatic, and a smaller piece can work in a quiet corner. But if a print looks lost above a sofa, it is almost always too small.

Hang the bottom of the frame about 6–10 inches (15–25 cm) above the back of the sofa, so the two read as one arrangement.

Measure the headboard rather than the mattress if it is wider. Leave enough space above the headboard that nobody hits the frame when sitting up: about 8–12 inches (20–30 cm).

Above a sideboard, console or desk

Sideboards and consoles are often styled with lamps or objects, so the art can be a little narrower: about half to two thirds of the furniture's width. Hang it lower than you think, about 4–8 inches (10–20 cm) above the top, so the art and the objects work together.

On an open wall

Without furniture, a common gallery guideline puts the centre of the art about 57–60 inches (145–152 cm) from the floor, roughly eye level. On a narrow wall, such as a pier between doors, choose a tall format and centre it on that wall.

What this means for listing photos

Buyers judge size from the furniture in your mockups. Show your main size at a believable scale above familiar furniture, and use a separate size image for the exact dimensions. If a buyer orders a 12×16 print after seeing it fill the wall above a sofa, the listing has set the wrong expectation.

Questions sellers ask

How big should art be over a couch?

About two thirds to three quarters of the couch's width, including the frame. For an 84-inch sofa that is roughly 56–63 inches of art, or a group of pieces that wide.

How high should art hang above a sofa?

The bottom of the frame about 6–10 inches (15–25 cm) above the sofa back.

What size art goes above a queen bed?

About 40–45 inches (100–115 cm) wide, such as a 30×40 landscape, a 14×42 panorama or a pair of 16×20 landscapes.

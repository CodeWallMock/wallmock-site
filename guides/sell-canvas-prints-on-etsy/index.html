How to sell canvas prints on Etsy: sizes, depth, frames and photos

Published by WallMock · Updated 8 November 2026

Short answer: Decide the canvas you sell first: thin (about 0.75 in) or gallery depth (about 1.5 in), how the edge is printed, and whether it comes in a floating frame. Offer sizes from your artwork’s ratio family, with at least 150 and ideally 300 pixels per inch at each size. Use a print-on-demand partner or make them yourself and disclose it, set size and frame as variations with a photo for each, and lead with a clean room photo, then scale, the edge and the frame options.

  • DepthThin canvas is about 0.75 in (19 mm); gallery canvas about 1.5 in (38 mm). Say which.
  • Ratio families2:3, 3:4, 4:5, square and panoramic. 11×14 and 20×24 need their own crop.
  • Pixels300 ppi is the common target; a 16×20 in canvas needs 4,800 × 6,000 px.
  • PhotosUp to 20 photos and 2 videos per listing; give each frame option its own photo.

Canvas is the product many wall art buyers expect to find, and one of the most crowded on Etsy. What sets a canvas listing apart is the detail around the art: the depth, the edge, the frame and the photos. This guide walks through each decision in order, with links to deeper guides and free tools, and ends with a wall art mockup workflow for Etsy.

1. Choose the canvas you sell

  • Thin or gallery depth. Thin canvas (about 0.75 in) suits small and medium sizes and framing; gallery depth (about 1.5 in) stands further off the wall and looks finished without a frame. Details in canvas depth 0.75 vs 1.5 inch.
  • The edge. Image wrap (the art continues round the side), mirror wrap, or a solid colour. Image wrap loses part of the composition to the sides, so it changes your file. See canvas wrap edges.
  • Stretched or floating frame. A floating frame adds a wood or painted border with a gap around the canvas and usually a higher price. See framed canvas vs stretched canvas and what a floater frame is.

2. Pick sizes from your artwork’s ratio

A file fits every size in its ratio family without cropping. These are the stretched canvas sizes Printful lists, grouped by ratio (checked 2026-10-29; other suppliers differ):

3:49×12, 12×16, 18×24, 24×32, 30×4018×24: 5,400 × 7,200

Two traps: 11×14 and 20×24 are not 4:5, so each needs its own crop. Providers name 300 DPI as ideal and Gelato says files under 150 DPI are not recommended (checked 2026-10-29). For an image-wrapped canvas, add the depth to every side before you check pixels. The full chart is in canvas print sizes, and the DPI guide works through the wrap. Test a file with the print resolution checker.

3. Decide who makes it

With print on demand, a production partner prints and ships each order. Etsy asks you to list the partner in your shop and on each listing (see Etsy print-on-demand integrations). Compare suppliers on the exact canvas you want: depth, edge options, frame colours and where they print, in how to choose a print-on-demand provider. Order a sample of each depth and frame colour you plan to sell; it settles questions no product page answers and gives you the real photo for your listing.

If you make canvases yourself, the same decisions apply, and the photo of your own piece is the unedited photo Etsy asks most listings for. How to photograph artwork for Etsy covers it.

4. Set up variations and price

  • Size and frame as variations. Etsy allows up to three variation types per listing and lets you link a photo to each option. Keep one depth per listing, or make depth its own variation with its own photo. See Etsy variations for wall art.
  • Price each size from cost. Large and framed canvas cost much more to make and ship than small ones. Work out the margin per size with the Etsy fee calculator and the pricing guide.
  • Title, tags and attributes. Put the product (“canvas print”, “framed canvas”), the subject and the style in the title; use attributes for size and orientation. See Etsy SEO for wall art.

5. Make photos that match the canvas

Etsy allows up to 20 photos and 2 videos per listing. For canvas, these slots do the most work:

SlotWhat to showWhy

4–6The same room in each frame colour you offerLinked to each variation option

Questions

Is selling canvas prints on Etsy worth it?

It can be, if your art and photos stand out: canvas is a crowded category on Etsy, and the base product is often identical between shops using the same supplier. Price each size from cost, show the depth and edge clearly, and give buyers a reason to choose your art.

What canvas sizes sell best on Etsy?

We don’t publish sales rankings. Offer sizes from your artwork’s ratio family that suit common furniture: for example 16×20 or 18×24 in above a small sofa, and 24×36 in or larger above a large one. Use a size guide image so buyers can compare.

What resolution do I need for canvas prints?

Aim for 300 pixels per inch at the printed size; providers treat about 150 as the minimum. A 16×20 in canvas needs 4,800 × 6,000 pixels at 300, or 2,400 × 3,000 at 150. Add the depth to each side for an image-wrapped edge.

Should I sell stretched or framed canvas?

Many shops offer both: stretched canvas as the lower price and a floating frame as the finished option. Show each in the same room so buyers see only the frame change.

Do I need a real photo of my canvas on Etsy?

Etsy asks most listings for at least one unedited photo of the actual item; print-on-demand items made by a production partner can use an authorised mockup instead (as checked on 2026-11-06). A photo of your sample is still the best proof.

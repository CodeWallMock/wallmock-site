Canvas wrap edges: mirror wrap vs image wrap vs colour edge

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: The edge is the side of a stretched canvas. Image wrap, often called gallery wrap, folds part of the picture round the sides, so the front loses a strip on every side unless your file has extra image. Mirror wrap copies the outer strip onto the sides, so the front stays whole. A colour edge leaves the sides plain white or black. Choose mirror or colour for art with detail near its edges, and image wrap when the picture can spare a border.

Buyers can't see the edge in a front-on room photo, so they rely on your words and one close-up. Here is what each wrap does, which providers offer which, and how to show the edge next to your canvas print mockups.

The three edge styles at a glance

All three are stretched the same way; only the printing on the sides changes. Once a canvas goes into a floating frame, the sides are mostly hidden, so the edge matters most for canvas sold unframed.

Image wrap: plan the strip you lose

With image wrap, the picture continues round each side. The canvas needs picture for the front and the sides, so add the depth to every side before you prepare the file.

A worked example: a 16 × 20 in canvas that is 1.5 in deep needs 16 + 1.5 + 1.5 by 20 + 1.5 + 1.5, which is 19 × 23 in of picture. If your artwork is composed for exactly 16 × 20 in, the outer 1.5 in on every side ends up on the sides, and the front shows only the middle. On a 0.75 in thin canvas the file must cover 17.5 × 21.5 in, and about 0.75 in disappears round each side.

Providers add a little more for the fold. Gelato's own example: for a 200 × 300 mm front on its slim 2 cm canvas, the file must be 298 × 398 mm (34 mm of wrap plus 15 mm of bleed on each side). On its 4 cm canvas it is 310 × 410 mm (40 mm plus 15 mm) (Gelato Support, checked 2026-10-29). Printify's canvas guide says a 1.5 to 2 inch bleed is generally ideal for most canvas prints (Printify, checked 2026-10-29).

The extra area also needs pixels. The canvas print DPI guide works out how many.

Mirror wrap: the whole front, a copied edge

Mirror wrap keeps your full composition on the front. The provider takes a strip from the edge of your image and reflects it onto the side, so the side looks like a continuation of the picture. Prodigi describes it as "the edge of the image is repeated on the sides" (checked 2026-10-29).

It looks natural with skies, water, soft textures and painterly backgrounds. It looks odd when a strong line, a face or lettering runs into the edge, because the mirror copy doubles it back at the corner. Check your edges before you choose it.

Colour edges: white or black sides

A colour edge prints nothing from your image on the sides. White is the cleanest and reads like a museum piece; Prodigi calls its white edge a museum wrap. Black looks sharp on dark or graphic art. If you want any other colour on the sides, ask your provider whether it offers one before you promise it.

Gelato's upload guide describes the simplest version: place the artwork inside the inner box of the template and the sides stay white (checked 2026-10-29). The front keeps the whole picture, and the file only needs to cover the front.

Names that confuse buyers

"Gallery wrap" means two things. At Prodigi it is the name for image wrap. Many sellers and printers use it for any thick stretched canvas, whatever is printed on the sides. "Museum wrap" is just as loose.

So don't rely on the label. Say what the buyer will see:

  • "The image continues around the sides."
  • "The sides show a mirror image of the edge, so none of the picture is lost."
  • "White sides" or "black sides".

Add the depth in the same sentence, in inches and millimetres. Our guide to canvas depth lists what providers mean by thin and deep canvas.

How to show the edge in your listing

  1. Room photos for the front. They sell the look and the size. The wall shadow tells buyers the canvas has depth.
  2. One close-up of a sample at an angle. Show a corner and one side in daylight. This is the only photo that proves the edge style.
  3. Words in the title or first lines. Edge style plus depth, as above.
  4. Match photos to what ships. If you sell mirror wrap, don't show an image-wrap sample, and the other way round.

Questions

What is the difference between gallery wrap and mirror wrap?

With gallery (image) wrap, part of your picture folds round the sides, so the front loses a strip. With mirror wrap, the edge of the picture is copied onto the sides, so the front stays whole.

Which canvas edge is best?

Mirror wrap or a colour edge if your art has detail, faces or text near its edges. Image wrap if the picture has a soft border it can spare, such as sky, water or texture.

Does image wrap cut off part of my art?

From the front, yes, unless your file includes extra image for the sides. On a 1.5 in deep canvas, plan about 1.5 in of extra picture on every side.

What is a museum wrap?

Usually a canvas with plain white sides. Prodigi uses the name for its white edge. Other printers use it loosely, so describe the sides in plain words.

Does the edge matter for a framed canvas?

Much less. In a floating frame the sides sit inside the frame, so they are mostly hidden from the front.

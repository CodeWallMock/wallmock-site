How to price framed canvas and framed prints on Etsy

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: To price wall art on Etsy, add your provider's product and shipping cost, then divide by what is left after Etsy's percentage fees and your target margin. In the US, Etsy takes 6.5% of the item price and shipping, 3% plus USD 0.25 for card processing, and USD 0.20 per listing. For a 30% margin, price = (cost + 0.45) ÷ 0.605. Price every size and frame separately, because each costs a different amount to make.

Print-on-demand wall art has real costs on every order, and Etsy's fees are a percentage of the whole price. This guide turns both into a price for each size, with worked numbers. For the photos that justify a framed price, see the frame mockup generator.

Etsy's fees on a wall art sale

Etsy's fees for a US shop, from Etsy's help pages (checked 2026-10-29):

Processing rates differ by country; a UK shop, for example, pays 4% + GBP 0.20. Etsy also charges a one-time shop set-up fee that varies by location, and some countries pay a regulatory operating fee. Check the pages above for your country.

The pricing formula

Add up what one order costs you, then work back from the price:

  1. Cost = the provider's product price + the provider's shipping to the buyer.
  2. Fixed Etsy fees = USD 0.20 listing + USD 0.25 processing = USD 0.45.
  3. Percentage fees = 6.5% + 3% = 9.5% of the price (when shipping is included in the price).
  4. Target margin = the share of the price you want to keep, for example 30%.

Price = (cost + 0.45) ÷ (1 − 0.095 − margin). For a 30% margin, divide by 0.605. Example: a cost of USD 76 gives (76 + 0.45) ÷ 0.605 = USD 126.36; round to USD 129.

Free shipping or a separate shipping charge makes no difference to Etsy's fees: the transaction and processing fees apply to shipping as well as the item.

Two things the table shows. First, Offsite Ads can halve the profit on an order, so a margin that only just works on normal orders loses money on those. Second, small framed prints leave the least room: a few dollars of cost change moves the margin a lot.

Leave room for sales and problems

  • Sales: a 20% discount on the 18 × 24 in canvas takes the price to 103.20 and the profit to 16.94. If you plan to run sales, set the full price so the sale price still pays.
  • Damage and remakes: large framed pieces sometimes arrive damaged. Read your provider's reprint policy and budget for the refunds it doesn't cover.
  • Samples: order one of each frame colour you sell. It is a cost, but it gives you the real photo Etsy asks for on most listings.
  • Your own traffic: Etsy's Share & Save refunds 4% of the eligible order total when a buyer orders within 30 days of clicking a link you shared (Etsy, checked 2026-10-29).
  • Listing photos: on WallMock's Lite plan ($5 a month for 200 images), each image costs about 2.5 cents, so ten room photos add about 25 cents to a listing once, not to every order.

Price each size and frame separately

Bigger sizes and framed versions cost more to make, so they need their own prices. Etsy lets you set different prices for up to 400 option combinations in one listing (Etsy). The guide to Etsy variations for wall art sets up size and frame colour as variations.

Two habits keep the price list tidy:

  • Work out each size's price with the formula, then round to a clean ladder such as 49, 69, 89, 129, 199, and check the margin again after rounding.
  • Recheck when your provider changes a price or you add a frame colour. A provider price rise of a few dollars on a large canvas can wipe out the margin on a sale.

Make the photos match the price

A higher price needs photos that show why: the frame finish, the floating gap, the mat and a believable size above furniture. Buyers comparing a framed canvas with a cheaper unframed print need to see the difference at thumbnail size.

Questions

How much does Etsy take from a wall art sale?

For a US shop: USD 0.20 per listing, 6.5% of the item price and shipping, and 3% plus USD 0.25 for card processing. On a USD 129 sale with shipping included, that is USD 12.71. Offsite Ads add 15% (or 12%) on orders that came from those ads.

What margin should I aim for on print-on-demand wall art?

There is no fixed rule. Many sellers work back from a target such as 30% of the price, then check that sales and Offsite Ads orders still make money. The formula in this guide works for any margin you choose.

Should I offer free shipping on framed canvas?

It doesn't change Etsy's fees, because they apply to shipping too. Include shipping in the price if that suits your buyers, but price from the full cost either way.

Why price each size separately?

Each size and frame costs a different amount to make and ship. A single price across sizes either overcharges small pieces or loses money on large ones.

Does Etsy charge fees on sales tax?

Etsy's payment processing fee is charged on the total sale price, including shipping and any applicable sales tax. The transaction fee applies to the item price, shipping and gift wrap.

Shopify wall art product images: variant photos for size, frame and canvas

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Give each frame colour variant its own image from the product's media, made in the same room so only the frame changes, then group the extra photos for that colour next to it in the gallery. Shopify allows one assigned image per variant, up to 250 media items per product and up to 2,048 variants. Square images around 2048 × 2048 px usually display best. After a print-on-demand sync, check every variant still points to the right photo.

On a Shopify product page, the variant image is what changes when a buyer clicks "Walnut". If it doesn't change, or shows the wrong frame, the page feels broken. This guide sets up options and images for framed canvas and prints, with a bulk mockup generator for the matching room photos.

What Shopify allows

From Shopify's help pages (checked 2026-10-29):

RuleShopify's limit or advice
Options per productUp to 3, such as size, colour and style
Variants per productUp to 2,048. Some themes, apps and sales channels may not support more than 100
Media per productUp to 250 items, such as images
Image per variantOne, chosen from the product's media list. No videos or 3D models on variants
Image size2048 × 2048 px usually displays best for square images; up to 5000 × 5000 px or 25 megapixels
File sizeUnder 20 MB
File typePNG, then JPEG, for most product images

Sources: Shopify: Adding variants, Shopify: Adding images to product variants, Shopify: Product media types.

Shopify also says to use a consistent aspect ratio for your main product images, so they appear at the same size on collection pages. Pick square or one other shape for every wall art product and stick to it.

Set up options for wall art

Three options are enough for almost any wall art shop:

  1. Size, limited to sizes in the artwork's ratio family, so none of them crops the art (check with the print size calculator).
  2. Frame, with the finishes your print provider makes: for example black, white, natural oak and walnut.
  3. Format only if you sell several products on one page, such as framed canvas and framed print.

Four sizes × four frames × two formats is 32 variants, comfortably under the 100 that some themes and apps handle. Keep an eye on that number before you add a fifth size or a sixth colour.

As on Etsy, a separate product for canvas and for framed prints is often clearer, because each needs its own photos and title. Our Etsy variations guide explains the same choice for an Etsy listing.

Assign one image to each variant

Upload all the photos to the product first. Then, from Shopify's help page:

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Products and open the product.
  2. In the Variants section, click the image icon next to a variant.
  3. Select an existing product image, or upload a new one.
  4. Click Done, then Save.

When a buyer selects that variant, its image becomes the preview, and they can still scroll the rest of the gallery. Since each variant gets one image, Shopify suggests arranging related images in sequence so they appear together. For wall art that means:

Gallery positionImageAssigned to
1Hero room, black frameAll black variants
2Same room, natural oak frameAll oak variants
3Same room, walnut frameAll walnut variants
4Same room, white frameAll white variants
5Size guide imageNone (shared)
6–10Close-up, sample photo, other roomsNone (shared)

Sizes share one image per frame. A size guide image explains the sizes better than one photo per size; see how to make a size guide image.

Fixing images after a print-on-demand sync

Print-on-demand apps create the Shopify product for you, with their own mockups. Trouble starts when you add your own room photos and the app publishes again. Common symptoms and the fix in Shopify:

What you seeLikely causeWhat to do
A new size or colour shows no imageThe app added the variant without a pictureOpen the variant, click the image icon and assign the matching photo
Your room photos were replacedThe app republished its mockupsRe-upload from your saved copies; next time publish without the app's mockups
Clicking a colour shows the wrong frameThe variant points to the wrong media itemReassign the image; name files by colour so it's obvious
Photos for one colour are scatteredThe images are in upload order, not grouped by colourDrag related images next to each other

Each app has its own publish settings. Our Printify mockups guide shows which setting keeps your own photos, and the Printful mockups guide covers refreshing Printful's mockups and adding your own in the store. Whatever the app, keep the original image files yourself, so a bad sync costs minutes instead of a re-shoot.

Image sizes and shape

  • Shape: square is the safe default for a Shopify theme's product and collection grids. If your theme crops to another shape, use that shape for every main image.
  • Size: at least 2048 px on each side, the size Shopify says usually displays best for square images. WallMock's 4,096 px exports are inside Shopify's 5000 × 5000 px limit.
  • Colour: export in sRGB so frame finishes look the same on the page as in your files.
  • Art in the centre: keep the artwork whole and near the middle, so a theme that crops for collection grids still shows it.

Questions

Can a Shopify variant have more than one image?

No. Shopify allows one assigned image per variant, chosen from the product's media. Put the other photos for that variant next to it in the gallery so buyers see them together.

What size should Shopify product images be?

Shopify says 2048 × 2048 px usually displays best for square product images. Images can be up to 5000 × 5000 px or 25 megapixels, and must be under 20 MB.

How many variants can a Shopify product have?

Up to 2,048 variants and 3 options. Shopify warns that some themes, apps and sales channels might not support more than 100 variants.

Why did my Printify or Printful sync replace my Shopify photos?

Publishing the app's mockups again can replace the images on the product. Use the app's publish settings to leave images alone, and keep your own copies so you can reassign them quickly.

Does WallMock upload images to Shopify?

No. WallMock makes the room images and you download them, then add them to the Shopify product and assign one to each variant.

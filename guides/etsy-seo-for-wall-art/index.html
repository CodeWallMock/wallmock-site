Etsy SEO for canvas and framed wall art: titles, tags and attributes

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: Etsy search reads your title, tags and attributes. For canvas and framed wall art, start the title with what the item is ("Misty Mountain Framed Canvas Wall Art"), keep it under 15 words, and use all 13 tags as multi-word phrases of up to 20 characters for product, subject, style, room and size. Fill every attribute your category offers, then let tags cover what attributes can't. Check Etsy Stats for the searches that actually find you.

A title that only says "print" undersells a framed canvas. If you sell a framed canvas or a matted framed print, saying so exactly is your edge, in search and in the click. Good photos still close the sale; the Etsy mockup generator handles those.

What Etsy search reads

Etsy's own help pages (checked 2026-10-29) describe the parts that matter:

  • Titles, tags and attributes. When a shopper searches, Etsy's system "scans titles, tags, and attributes" for matching listings (Etsy Stats help).
  • Title: up to 140 characters. Etsy suggests fewer than 15 words, describing the item clearly at the start, and avoiding repetition and subjective words such as "perfect" (Etsy).
  • Tags: up to 13 per listing, each up to 20 characters, using letters, numbers and spaces. Short phrases work, and tags are most useful where no attribute matches (Etsy).
  • Attributes: the category you choose decides which attributes you can add, and they help buyers find items in search (Etsy).
  • Outside Etsy: your title becomes the listing's page title, and search engines such as Google show only about the first 50 to 60 characters (Etsy).

Write the title: product first, then the art

Lead with what the buyer is searching for, which for wall art is subject + product. Then add the details that separate your listing.

ProductExample title
Floating-frame canvasMisty Mountain Framed Canvas Wall Art, Panoramic Blue Landscape, Oak Floating Frame
Framed print with matSailboat at Dusk Framed Print, Coastal Wall Art, Black Frame with White Mat
Stretched canvasAbstract Neutral Canvas Wall Art, Gallery Wrapped Canvas Print, Textured Beige

Each is under 15 words, and the first 50 to 60 characters still say what it is. Product words that help canvas and framed sellers:

  • Framed canvas: framed canvas, floating frame, floater frame, canvas wall art.
  • Stretched canvas: canvas print, gallery wrapped, stretched canvas.
  • Framed prints: framed print, framed wall art, with mat, matted print.
  • Size words: large, oversized, long, panoramic, tall, or an exact size buyers search, such as 24x36.

Only use words that are true for the item. "Framed" on a listing where the frame is an option, not included, is the quickest way to a bad review; see listing mistakes that cause returns.

Use all 13 tags, as phrases

Tags are where you add the searches your title couldn't fit. For the mountain canvas above, one set of 13, each under 20 characters:

framed canvas art · floating frame art · mountain wall art · panoramic print · misty mountains · above bed wall art · long horizontal art · living room decor · japandi wall art · oak framed canvas · large canvas print · blue landscape art · housewarming gift

Cover five groups so the tags don't repeat each other:

GroupExamples
Productframed canvas art, floating frame art, oak framed canvas, large canvas print
Subjectmountain wall art, misty mountains, blue landscape art
Shape and sizepanoramic print, long horizontal art
Room and styleabove bed wall art, living room decor, japandi wall art
Occasionhousewarming gift

If your category has an attribute for something, such as a colour, fill the attribute and spend the tag on a phrase attributes can't express.

Attributes, category and variations

  • Category: choose the most specific one that fits the product. It decides which attributes you can fill in.
  • Attributes: fill every one that applies. If an exact option doesn't exist, Etsy suggests choosing the closest one; its example is picking yellow for a lemon-coloured item.
  • Variations: buyers can't filter search results by a custom variation, so use Etsy's own variation names where your category offers them (Etsy). The guide to Etsy variations for wall art sets up size and frame colour.

Description and alt text

Etsy advises describing the item clearly in the first sentence of the description, and not repeating the same phrases over and over (Etsy). For wall art, the first lines should say:

  1. What it is: "A misty mountain panorama as a framed canvas in a natural oak floating frame."
  2. What is included: frame or no frame, hanging hardware, sizes (artwork and outer size).
  3. Who makes and ships it. If a print-on-demand partner prints it, Etsy also requires you to disclose that on the listing (Etsy).

Add alt text to every photo. Etsy allows up to 250 characters, asks you to describe what each image shows, and says shop names, titles and tags in alt text bring no extra SEO benefit (Etsy). For example: "Misty blue mountain panorama, a 20 × 60 inch canvas in a natural oak floating frame, shown above a linen daybed".

Habits that waste the 13 tags

  • Single words. "Canvas", "art" and "print" alone match everything. Use phrases buyers type, such as "large canvas print".
  • Repeats. Five tags that all start with "wall art" spend five slots on one idea. Vary the product, subject, room and style words.
  • Subjective words. Etsy advises leaving out words like "perfect"; buyers don't search for them.
  • Words that aren't true. "Framed" on an unframed print, or "original" on a print, wins the click and loses the review.
  • Keyword lists in the description. Etsy advises against repeating the same phrases; write for the buyer instead.

Check what works, then adjust

In Etsy Stats, choose Etsy search under How shoppers found you to see the search terms that brought buyers to your listings (Etsy). Every few weeks:

  • Move search terms that bring visits into the title or tags of similar listings.
  • Replace tags that never match a search term in your stats.
  • Check the first photo of listings with views but no clicks: the thumbnail may be the problem, not the words (see the crop-safe Etsy thumbnail).

Questions

How many tags can an Etsy listing have?

Up to 13 tags, each up to 20 characters. Etsy's help page says short phrases work and that tags are most useful where no attribute matches.

How long should an Etsy title be for wall art?

Etsy allows 140 characters but suggests fewer than 15 words, with the item described clearly at the start. Search engines such as Google show only about the first 50 to 60 characters of it.

Should I put 'framed' in the title if the frame is optional?

Only if the item in the photos and the default option is framed. If the frame is an add-on, say 'frame option' or describe it in a variation, so buyers aren't misled.

Does Etsy search read the description?

Etsy's help page on Stats says search scans titles, tags and attributes. Etsy still advises a clear first sentence in the description, and search engines such as Google read the whole page.

Do attributes count as tags on Etsy?

They are separate fields, but both help buyers find your listing. Fill the attributes your category offers, then use tags for phrases attributes can't express.

Etsy print-on-demand integrations for wall art: Printify, Printful, Gelato, Prodigi and Gooten

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: All five connect to Etsy, but they work differently. Printify, Printful and Gelato create the listing for you, and Printful and Gelato add it as a draft. Prodigi imports listings you already made on Etsy. Gooten can push a draft or link an existing listing. Each handles photos its own way, so add your room photos the way your provider expects, list it as a production partner, and stay within Etsy's 20 photos.

The integration decides how your listing is built and what happens to its photos when you update it. Here is how each of the five works for wall art, and where your own room photos from an Etsy mockup generator fit in. For canvas sellers, see also print-on-demand canvas mockups.

Drafts matter for money: Gelato says a draft avoids Etsy's listing fee until you publish, and Printful says Etsy charges its fee when you publish the draft. A draft is also the best moment to add your room photos, before the listing goes live.

Step one for every provider: add it as a production partner

Etsy defines a production partner as a company outside your shop that physically produces your items. It names Printful, Printify and Gooten as examples of print-on-demand partners. To add one: Shop Manager, Settings, Partners you work with, then Add a new production partner. You can add partners to listings while editing them, or in bulk from the Listings page. Etsy also asks you to update shipping locations to show where items ship from (Etsy Help, checked 2026-10-29).

  • Printify can't create the partner for you. If a partner named "Printify" already exists in your shop, Printify assigns it to listings when it publishes; if not, the listing defaults to "Made by seller" and you fix it on Etsy (source).
  • Printful says you must select it in the listing details each time you publish a product (source).
  • Gelato, Prodigi and Gooten: set the partner on Etsy the same way.

Etsy's photo rules that apply to print on demand

  • Up to 20 photos and 2 videos of 5–15 seconds per listing (source).
  • Photos at least 2000 px in width and height; the first photo landscape or square and at least 635 px; images converted to sRGB; transparent areas show as black; files over 1 MB may not finish uploading on a slow connection (source).
  • At least one unedited photo of the actual item for most listings. For print on demand, Etsy says you may use a stock photo mockup if the stock photo's owner has authorised its use (source, checked 2026-10-29).

Read Etsy's page for how the unedited-photo rule applies to your product. A photo of a sample you ordered is the safest way to cover it, and our sample checklist shows what to photograph. Check each file before uploading with the free Etsy listing photo checker.

Adding room photos, provider by provider

Printify

Either upload your images in Printify (open the product, View all mockups, Upload, pick the variant) and publish with Mockups ticked, or add them on Etsy and leave Mockups unticked every time you republish. Printify warns that publishing its mockups replaces your Etsy mockups, including videos, and that Update from Etsy doesn't bring photos back into Printify (source). Full steps are in our Printify mockups guide.

Printful

Finish the draft on Etsy and add your room photos there. Keep at least one photo slot free: Printful says it can't push a new mockup to a listing that already has 20 images (source).

Gelato

Choose Gelato's scenes when you create the product, then add your room photos to the Etsy draft. Gelato makes mockups only for the main colour you select; other colours are uploaded to Etsy by hand. Etsy links variant images to one option only, such as Size (source).

Prodigi

Prodigi says you can't yet create listings in Prodigi and sync them to Etsy, so you build the listing, photos included, on Etsy first. Its FAQ says mockups made in other apps are fine as long as you assign Prodigi as the fulfilment partner (source).

Gooten

Gooten lists images among the Etsy changes that won't disconnect a product, so you can add room photos on Etsy (source).

What breaks the connection, and what doesn't

Rule of thumb: edit photos, titles and descriptions freely, but change variants and SKUs only inside the provider's dashboard.

Questions

Which print-on-demand companies integrate with Etsy?

Printify, Printful, Gelato, Prodigi and Gooten all connect to Etsy. Etsy's own help page names Printful, Printify and Gooten as examples of print-on-demand production partners.

Do I have to add my print provider as a production partner on Etsy?

Yes. Add it under Shop Manager, Settings, Partners you work with, then select it on your listings. Printful says you must select it each time you publish a product.

Will my print provider overwrite my own Etsy photos?

It can. Printify says publishing its mockups replaces your Etsy mockups, including videos, so untick Mockups when you republish. Printful and Gelato add their mockups when they publish, so check the photo order afterwards.

How many photos can an Etsy listing have?

Up to 20 photos and 2 videos. Gelato and Printful send up to 10 mockups, and Printify up to 20, so leave room for your own photos.

Can I use mockups instead of a photo of the real item?

Etsy requires at least one unedited photo for most items, with a print-on-demand exception for stock photo mockups that the owner has authorised. Check Etsy's page for your product; a photo of your sample is the safest option.

Can WallMock publish to Etsy for me?

No. WallMock makes the room photos; you add them to your Etsy listing or to your provider's mockup library yourself.

Metal print vs metal frame: what is the difference?

Published by WallMock · Updated 31 October 2026

Short answer: A metal print is the artwork printed directly onto a coated aluminum panel, usually with no visible frame. A metal-framed print is paper artwork inside a separate metal frame. Choose the first when the product you ship is an aluminum panel; choose the second when you ship paper behind a frame. The names vary by supplier, so check the materials and sample before you write the listing.

The phrase “metal wall art” can mean two different things. The product page and the sample in your hands matter more than the short label. If you are planning a complete listing set, start with our print-on-demand mockup guide.

WallMock’s metal print is a frameless aluminum panel with rounded corners and a hidden mount that floats it off the wall. Its aluminum framed print is a paper print inside a slim black, gold or silver aluminum frame, with or without a white mat. These describe WallMock’s mockup products; your supplier’s specifications may differ.

Which look fits the artwork?

A panel can suit photography, saturated color, and clean graphic work when the supplier’s surface complements the image. A paper print in a metal frame can feel lighter and more familiar; a mat can give detailed work extra breathing room. Neither choice is universally better.

  • Try a metal panel if you want a clean, frameless edge and your provider offers a surface you like. Compare gloss and matte under the lighting where the piece will hang.
  • Try a metal frame if the visible border is part of the design, or if you want a paper print with a mat. Make sure the listing shows the frame and mat actually included.
  • Compare samples when blacks, subtle gradients, or fine detail matter. Screen mockups cannot tell you exactly how a supplier’s surface reflects light.

Check the exact supplier product

Product names are not standardized. For example, Printful’s current metal-print page describes a glossy aluminum product with a hidden MDF mounting frame; Prodigi describes its aluminum prints as sublimation-printed panels with a hidden subframe and rounded edge (checked 2026-10-31). Those are metal panels, not paper prints in metal picture frames.

Before you publish, open the exact product variant and verify:

  1. Substrate: aluminum panel or paper print?
  2. Surface: gloss, matte, or brushed—and is that finish available in the size you offer?
  3. Edge and corner: rounded or square; bare metal or finished sides?
  4. Mount: hidden float mount, backing frame, or flush hanger?
  5. Options: sizes, orientation, mat, frame color, and what is included.

Provider ranges and destinations change. Do not transfer one provider’s materials, dimensions, or durability claims to another product.

For a paper print, also check the difference a mat makes to framed prints. For either product, our sample check guide gives you a practical list of details to compare with your listing images.

Make the listing photo match

Show the product you will send. For a panel, include one room view and a close view of its edge or mount if those details distinguish the product. For a framed paper print, show the actual frame color and mat choice. Use the same artwork and room when comparing finishes so buyers can see what changed.

Questions

Is a metal print the same as a metal-framed print?

No. A metal print places the image on an aluminum panel. A metal-framed print uses paper surrounded by a separate metal frame.

Does a metal print have a frame?

Usually the product is described as frameless from the front, though it may have a hidden backing or mount. Check the supplier’s product details.

Is a metal print waterproof or scratchproof?

Claims depend on the panel coating, print process, and supplier. Use only the exact product’s current care and durability information.

Which metal product should I show in a mockup?

Show the construction you sell: a panel for a metal print, or a paper print inside a frame for a metal-framed print. Match the finish and options, then compare with a physical sample.

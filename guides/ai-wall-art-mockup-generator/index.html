What an AI mockup generator can change

Some tools ask a model to generate the whole scene, including the artwork. A thin line on a map can disappear, a signature can change, or the model can invent more artwork around the edge. WallMock uses your source file for the printed image and renders the product around it. That matters when a buyer orders the exact map, quote or signed print they saw in the listing.

There is still a difference between the source file and a photo-style render. Perspective changes the angle, light and gloss can change how bright a detail looks, and export resamples the image. To see what we actually checked, inspect the source files, example downloads and measurements. WallMock’s artwork fidelity checks.

Check each product before you list it

  1. Framed canvas. Choose the floating-frame look and compare its gap, frame colour and depth with your supplier’s sample. Check that the artwork’s edges are where you expect them. Framed canvas mockups.
  2. Framed print. Choose the wood or aluminum frame and add a mat only if the buyer receives one. Check the width of the mat and whether its opening hides a border or signature. Frame and mat options.
  3. Metal print. Choose the frameless panel and match the finish you sell. Matte, gloss and high gloss change how the room light appears on the print. Use a real close-up to show the actual panel edge and finish. Metal print surfaces.

Open the original and your full-size mockup side by side. Compare lettering and fine lines at 100%, then look at all four artwork edges. Once the product and placement look right, save the room and frame choices and reuse them for the rest of the collection. Bulk room mockups.

Etsy listing photos and the real product

Etsy generally requires at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. It can appear anywhere in the listing. Other images may be edited, including with AI tools, if the item still looks like what the buyer will receive. Etsy specifically allows an authorized stock-photo mockup when a production partner prints a seller’s original design on a standard base product. Etsy listing image requirements.

Use the room mockup to show the piece on a wall and a real product photo to show what ships. If a mockup changes the lettering, adds a mat you don’t sell, or makes a matte print look glossy, it misrepresents the item. If the artwork itself was made with AI, Etsy requires disclosure in the listing description. Etsy Creativity Standards · Etsy mockup rules for wall art.

Shopify product images for a whole collection

Shopify advises merchants to keep product images and variants accurate in Shopify Catalog, which also feeds shopping platforms and AI agents. Match each gallery image to the artwork and finish the shopper selected. A walnut framed canvas and a matte metal panel should each have their own image. Export those looks from WallMock, upload them to the matching products, and add a real close-up of the frame or surface. Shopify Catalog guidance · Adding product media to Shopify.

If you fulfil through Printify, Printful or Gelato, start with the exact product and finish in your supplier’s catalog. Use that as your reference when making WallMock images for the listing. Printify mockups · Printful mockups · Gelato mockups.

Questions

Does WallMock use AI to change my artwork?

WallMock uses the file you upload. It never redraws, repaints or upscales the artwork with AI. Perspective, lighting and export settings can affect how the artwork appears in the finished image.

How can I tell if an AI mockup generator redrew my design?

Compare the full-size mockup with the source. Check small lettering, signatures, fine lines and all four edges. A different letter or missing detail is a reason to reject the image. A shift in brightness can come from scene lighting.

Can I use WallMock mockups on Etsy?

Use accurate mockups as supplemental listing images. Etsy generally requires an unedited photo of the actual item against a real background. Etsy has a specific allowance for authorized stock-photo mockups when a production partner prints a seller’s original design on a standard base product.

What should my Shopify product photos show?

Show the exact artwork, product construction and finish the buyer will receive. Use separate images for different variants and include a real close-up of the frame or surface.

Can I use the same artwork for framed canvas, framed print and metal print?

Yes. Use the same uploaded file and select the product you actually sell. Check the floating-frame gap for canvas, the mat opening for framed paper and the surface sheen for a frameless metal print.

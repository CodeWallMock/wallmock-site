How to automate Etsy listing photos for wall art and POD

Published by WallMock · Updated 6 November 2026

Short answer: Automate the slow part first: making the photos. Save your rooms and frames once, then make every artwork in every room in one run, with crops for Etsy's 4:3 first photo and alt text on paid plans. Uploading to the listing is still a manual step with WallMock today; Send to Etsy, which will send mockups to your listings, is coming soon, pending Etsy's approval of our app.

Listing a wall art collection on Etsy means dozens of photos per design. This guide separates what you can automate today from what still needs a person, and shows where WallMock fits.

Five photos of one artwork, made in one call with the WallMock API pilot (early access, not yet available) from founder AK's own artwork. The web app makes the same kind of room photos today.

Most of the time goes on the first two rows, and those can be automated today.

The manual way

  1. Pick a room template for each photo slot you plan to fill.
  2. Place each design in each template, check the crop, and export.
  3. Crop the first photo so it survives the 4:3 search thumbnail, with the art large and centred.
  4. Write alt text for each photo.
  5. Upload the photos in Etsy's listing editor, in order, for every listing.

Etsy allows up to 20 photos and 2 videos per listing. Filled with intent, those slots answer buyers' questions; see a plan for all 20 Etsy photo slots.

The WallMock way, today

  1. Save your rooms and frames once. Heart the rooms that suit your shop and choose the frames you sell.
  2. Upload the whole collection. Each artwork keeps its own shape and is never redrawn.
  3. Generate. Every artwork in every chosen room: 30 designs in 8 rooms is 240 images.
  4. Download the images (on paid plans, as a listing kit with crops and alt text) and upload them in Etsy's listing editor.

Print on demand and Etsy photos

If a print-on-demand app publishes your Etsy listings, it may add its own product mockups. Room photos you make in WallMock sit alongside or replace those, depending on the app's settings. Our Etsy print-on-demand integrations guide explains what each kind of integration sends to a listing.

Questions

Can I send mockups to Etsy listings automatically?

Not with WallMock yet. Send to Etsy is coming soon, pending Etsy's approval of our app. Today, WallMock automates making the photos, crops and alt text, and you upload them in Etsy's listing editor.

How many photos can an Etsy listing have?

Up to 20 photos and 2 videos.

How do I make listing photos for a whole wall art collection at once?

Save your rooms and frames once in WallMock, upload the collection and generate: every artwork is made in every chosen room in one run.

Does it work with print on demand?

Yes for making the photos: WallMock makes room photos for the products you sell, whoever prints them. It does not connect to your print provider.

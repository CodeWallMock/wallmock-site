What a poster buyer needs to see

A poster is cheap to ship and easy to swap, so buyers decide fast. Your photos should answer three questions without any reading.

  1. How big is it? A poster above a bed or sofa shows scale better than a flat file on white. Pick rooms where the furniture gives a clear reference.
  2. Does it come framed? If your print provider ships a poster frame, show that frame and colour. If you ship the paper only, say "frame not included" in the first lines of your description.
  3. Will it suit my room? Calm neutral rooms work for most designs. Bold graphic or typographic posters often look better against plain white or a strong wall colour.

Poster sizes and ratios: match the mockup to the file

Most poster mistakes start with the ratio, not the room. A 2:3 design printed at 18 x 24 inches loses part of its edges, because 18 x 24 is a 3:4 shape. Your mockup should show the same shape you sell. WallMock keeps each file's own proportions and never stretches it, so a 3:4 poster looks 3:4 on the wall.

Which frame should a poster mockup show?

Show what arrives in the box. That one rule avoids most "not as pictured" messages.

Wood frame

The most common framed poster. Match the colour you sell: black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold or silver.

Aluminium frame

A slim metal frame suits modern, graphic and photographic posters. WallMock shows it with or without a mat.

Matted print

A mat (the card border inside the frame) suits fine-art and photographic posters. Add one only if the product you ship has one.

Questions

What is a poster mockup generator?

A tool that shows your poster design on a wall in a room, so a buyer can judge its size, colours and style before ordering. WallMock does this online, with no PSD file or Photoshop needed.

Can I show a poster without a frame?

Not at the moment. WallMock shows posters in a frame: wood or aluminium, with or without a mat. If you ship paper only, say "frame not included" in the first lines of your listing so the photo and the product match.

What size should a poster file be for a 24x36 print?

At 300 DPI a 24x36 inch poster needs 7200 x 10800 pixels. Many sellers accept less for large posters viewed from a distance. Check your own file with the free print resolution checker.

What ratio is an A-size poster?

A1, A2, A3 and A4 all share one shape, about 1:1.41. That is close to 5:7, but not the same as the 2:3 shape of 24x36 or the 3:4 shape of 18x24.

Will text and lettering on my poster stay sharp?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Typography, signatures and fine lines show exactly as they are in your file.

Can I mock up a whole poster collection at once?

Yes. Save the rooms you like once, add every poster in the collection, and each design is made in each saved room.

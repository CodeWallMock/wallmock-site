Five things to check in any free canvas mockup tool

"Free" can mean many things. Before you put an hour into a free tool, test it with one artwork and look at these five points. They apply to WallMock too, and the answers for WallMock are given under each.

1. The watermark

Where does it sit, and does it cover the art? A watermark across your painting makes the photo useless for a listing, so use free previews to decide on rooms and frames, not as listing photos. WallMock: every free preview is watermarked; clean images need a plan.

2. The image size

Check the pixel size of what you download. A small free file looks soft when a buyer zooms in, and leaves no room to crop a square thumbnail from a wide room. WallMock: clean images go up to 4K. Our free Etsy listing photo checker tells you if any file is big enough.

3. Whether your art is redrawn

Some generators build the whole picture with AI, art included. That can blur brushwork, bend straight lines, change colours or rewrite lettering, and then the photo no longer matches the canvas your buyer receives. Zoom in to 100% on a signature or a line of text and compare it with your file. WallMock: your file is placed as it is, never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Our guide to AI mockup generators: exact art or redrawn art shows what to look for.

4. The canvas edge

A stretched canvas is 0.75 to 1.5 inches deep, and its sides show the wrap: a mirror of the image, the image itself or a plain colour. A front-on mockup hides the side, which is fine if your listing says how the edge is finished and shows it in a photo of a sample. WallMock: stretched canvas is shown front-on with its shadow on the wall; it doesn't show the wrapped side and has no edge-colour option. Our guide to canvas wrap edges explains the three wraps.

5. The floating-frame gap

A framed canvas from a print-on-demand provider usually sits in a floating frame, with a narrow gap between the canvas and the frame. Some templates draw a flat picture frame instead, which makes a framed canvas look like a framed print. WallMock: the floating frame shows the gap, in 7 finishes: black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver. See it up close on the framed canvas mockup generator page.

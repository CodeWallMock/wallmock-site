Privacy

Privacy at WallMock

Made by the WallMock team · Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer: this website (wallmock.com) sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising trackers. Its free tools work in your browser and don't upload your files. If you email us, we receive what you send. The WallMock app at app.wallmock.com, including the free previews, is covered by its own privacy policy.

What this page covers

This notice covers the public website at wallmock.com: the home page, guides, free tools, room pages, comparisons and this page. The WallMock app at app.wallmock.com is a separate service. Anyone can open it at app.wallmock.com/try to make free watermarked previews without signing up, and sellers with a free account sign in there. The app's own privacy policy and terms and refunds explain what the app does with your information; this page does not change them.

What this website collects

The website is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, sign-up forms or comment boxes, it sets no cookies, and it has no analytics, advertising or social-media tracking scripts. Fonts are your device's own, so no font service is contacted. The only scripts are the site's free tools and the before-and-after slider on the home page, which run in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes (GitHub's data collection note). WallMock does not receive those logs.

The free tools

Tools such as the print resolution checker and the Etsy listing photo checker read the image you choose in your browser, on your device, to measure its size. The image is not uploaded to WallMock or anyone else, and nothing is kept after you close the page. Numbers you type into calculators stay in your browser too.

Emailing us

Email links such as hello@wallmock.com open your own email app with a draft addressed to hello@wallmock.com. Nothing is sent until you press send. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it to reply, for example to answer a question. Please don't send artwork files or sensitive information by email.

To ask us to delete your messages or to stop contacting you, email hello@wallmock.com.

The app and your artwork

Previews and images are made in your browser, on your device. The app's privacy policy describes this in detail, including what is kept for an account. Plans are billed securely by Stripe from $5 a month. Cancel any time; new subscribers can get a refund within 14 days, minus credits used.

Links to other websites

Guides and comparisons link to other companies' pages as sources, such as Etsy, Printify and Printful help articles. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit them.

Changes and contact

If what this website does with information changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions about privacy: hello@wallmock.com. More about who we are: about WallMock.

Questions

Does wallmock.com use cookies or analytics?

No. This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising scripts. Its only scripts are the free tools and the before-and-after slider, which run in your browser.

Do the free tools upload my artwork?

No. Tools that read an image do it in your browser, on your device, to get its size; the file is not sent to WallMock.

Where is the app's privacy policy?

The app at app.wallmock.com, including the free previews at https://app.wallmock.com/try, has its own privacy policy: https://app.wallmock.com/privacy

How do I ask WallMock to delete my email?

Email hello@wallmock.com and ask us to delete your messages or to stop contacting you.

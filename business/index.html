For business

WallMock for print labs, framers and wall-art companies.

WallMock for business is for print labs, framers, print-on-demand suppliers and wall-art shops. It brings together everything we offer companies: a wall art mockup API for listing photos (early access), a frame visualizer API that lets your shoppers preview their own art in your frames (in development), mockups of a lab's own frames for its sellers, partner pilots, and Send to Shopify (beta). What works today is the WallMock web app, which places exact art, never redrawn, in 500+ curated, digitally staged rooms with WallMock's own frames.

Status on 21 November 2026: the WallMock web app is live. The wall art mockup API is in early access, not available yet. The frame visualizer for shoppers has a working prototype and is open for free 60-day pilots by request, with early-access plans from $49 a month after the pilot. The partner frame catalogue is in development. Send to Shopify is in limited beta. No other business prices are published.

Questions

Does WallMock offer an API for businesses?

Not yet. The wall art mockup API is in development and not available yet; early access is by request. A shopper preview API, where your customers see their own art in your frames before they order, is also in development.

What can a business use today?

The WallMock web app: your exact art in 500+ curated, digitally staged rooms with WallMock's own frames, for a whole collection at once. Send to Shopify is in limited beta. The APIs, partner frame presets and white-label output are not available yet.

Can WallMock show our own frames?

That is what partner pilots are for, and it is in development. We build presets from your frame details and product photos and check them with you. Today WallMock shows its own frames and finishes.

Is the artwork changed?

No. The artwork is placed as supplied; it is never redrawn. Every room photo gets an exact-art check against the uploaded file.

What does it cost?

The frame visualizer for shoppers starts with a free 60-day pilot, then early-access plans from $49 a month (early-access pricing, may change). Prices for the mockup API and partner pilots are not published; terms are agreed with each partner. Plans for sellers using the web app are on the pricing page.

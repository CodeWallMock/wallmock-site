Questions

What does the Free plan include?

Free account: 50 watermarked previews every 30 days on your device, in your included rooms; paid plans have unlimited previews. Usage safeguards and device limits apply. You get 10 watermark-free 4K (4096 px) downloads every 30 days; unused downloads do not carry over. The first 10 free downloads over your account's lifetime can use all 500+ rooms; after that, 8 free rooms remain. When the 10 downloads are used, previews continue in your included rooms, up to 50 every 30 days. Refills do not reopen the full library. Free downloads are for single artworks. Sets and listing kits need a paid plan; listing videos and Send to Shopify need Pro. Paid plans add more images: Lite 200 a month, Pro unlimited.

What happens when I use my 10 free downloads?

Your account stays Free, with no automatic charge. Watermarked previews continue in your included rooms, up to 50 every 30 days. Your next 10 download credits arrive at your normal 30-day reset; unused credits do not roll over.

Which rooms are included in Free?

Your first 10 free downloads over your account's lifetime can use all 500+ rooms. After those downloads, 8 free rooms remain. The 30-day refill restores downloads, not full-library access.

What counts as one image?

On paid plans, one image is one clean 4K (4096 px) download of one artwork in one room. On Free, one download is a watermark-free 4K (4096 px) image of one artwork in one room. Watermarked previews use neither allowance. Free includes 50 previews every 30 days; paid plans have unlimited previews.

Is there a limit on Pro?

Pro includes unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server. Lite includes 200 clean 4K images a month.

Which plan should I pick?

Stay Free for 50 watermarked previews and 10 watermark-free 4K downloads every 30 days. Every paid plan has unlimited previews. Pick Lite for up to 200 clean 4K images a month. Choose Pro for larger collections, unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images under fair use, listing videos, priority email support and Send to Shopify for 1 store. Choose the WallMock Studio plan to send to up to 3 Shopify stores with unlimited products and save up to 25 custom frame colours.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Yearly prices are Lite $39, Pro $144 and WallMock Studio plan $240. Reference prices are USD. Pricing cards and the app set USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR or BRL automatically from your connection; existing subscriptions keep their billing currency. Annual plans are paid upfront.

What happens to an existing subscription?

Existing subscriptions keep their purchased terms.

Can I use WallMock today?

Anyone can try free watermarked previews with no sign-up, then create a free account with Google or an email code. Plans are billed securely by Stripe from USD $5 a month. Cancel any time.

What is the WallMock Studio plan?

Our plan for sellers with more than one store: $25 a month, or $240 a year ($20 a month, billed yearly), in your local currency. It includes everything in Pro, plus Send to Shopify for up to 3 stores with unlimited products, up to 25 saved custom frame colours, priority email support and 20% off WallMock API images when the API launches. Send to Etsy and one-click listing sets are coming soon; they are added to the WallMock Studio plan at no extra cost when they launch. It is a plan in the same WallMock app, not a separate app. Agency serving client stores? Contact us about an agency plan.

Which plans include Send to Shopify?

Pro includes Send to Shopify for 1 store and up to 10 products a month. The WallMock Studio plan includes up to 3 stores with unlimited products. A product counts once in your plan month, when it first gets an image; sending again to the same product does not count. You can change stores 2 times a month. Lite and Free do not include Send to Shopify.

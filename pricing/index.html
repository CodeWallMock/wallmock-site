Questions

Is there a free option?

Free account: 10 watermark-free downloads (1024 px), back to 10 every 30 days (unused ones don't carry over), with watermarked previews. Your first 10 free downloads work in all 500+ rooms; after that the free plan keeps 8 rooms. 4K (4096 px) images, watermark-free previews, every room, sets (any plan) and listing videos (Pro and Studio) need a plan. Free downloads are 1024 px; a plan gives clean 4K (4096 px) images.

What counts as one image?

One clean 4K (4096 px) download of one artwork in one room. Watermarked previews don't count.

Is there a limit on Pro and Studio?

Pro and Studio include unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server (600 on Studio). Lite includes 200 clean 4K images a month.

Which plan should I pick?

Most shops that add new designs every week start with Pro. Pick Lite if you list a few designs a month, Studio if you run several shops or make mockups for clients.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Yearly prices are Lite $39, Pro $89, Studio $179. Prices in USD; the app shows local prices in EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR and BRL.

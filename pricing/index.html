Questions

Is there a free option?

Free account: 10 watermark-free downloads (1024 px), back to 10 every 30 days (unused ones don't carry over), with watermarked previews. Your first 10 free downloads work in all 500+ rooms; after that the free plan keeps 8 rooms. 4K (4096 px) images, watermark-free previews, every room, sets (any plan) and listing videos (Pro) need a plan. Free downloads are 1024 px; a plan gives clean 4K (4096 px) images.

What counts as one image?

One clean 4K (4096 px) download of one artwork in one room. Watermarked previews don't count.

Is there a limit on Pro?

Pro includes unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server. Lite includes 200 clean 4K images a month.

Which plan should I pick?

Pick Lite for up to 200 clean 4K images a month. Choose Pro for larger collections, unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images under fair use, listing videos and priority email support.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Yearly prices are Lite $39, Pro $144. Reference prices are USD; the pricing cards and app also offer EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR and BRL. Annual plans are paid upfront.

What happens to an existing subscription?

Existing subscriptions keep their purchased terms.

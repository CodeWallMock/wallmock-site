What makes a metal frame look like metal?

A metal frame is a thin, crisp line around the art. Here is how WallMock depicts a slim metal frame.

A slim face

Aluminium frames are usually narrower than wood mouldings. In the photo the frame reads as a fine outline, so the art and the mat do most of the work.

The finish

WallMock's aluminium frame is one smooth, slightly rounded rail with a metallic shine: black, gold or silver.

Mat or no mat

A white mat gives a small print room to breathe and suits line art and botanicals. With no mat, the print runs right up to the thin frame for a graphic, modern look.

Metal or wood: which frame should the photo show?

Show the one you ship. If the product page says aluminium or metal, use the metal frame; if it says wood, use a wood frame, even when the wood is painted gold or silver. The difference shows in the photo: a metal frame is thinner and sharper at the corners, a wood frame is wider and softer.

  • Metal suits modern, graphic and photographic work, small prints that need a light touch, and rooms with clean lines.
  • Wood suits warm, traditional and natural styles, and larger prints that can carry a wider moulding. WallMock's wood frames come in black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver.
  • Gold is the classic trap. A gold wood frame and a gold metal frame are different products. Pick the matching one, or buyers who ordered the slim one may feel the photo misled them.

With a mat or without

A mat, also called a passe-partout, is the card border between frame and print. Show one only if the product includes it. Two quick rules:

  • Small prints and fine detail: a white mat. It makes an 8×10 in print look like a gallery piece and keeps line work from crowding the frame.
  • Large or bold prints: often no mat. A 24×36 in poster-style print in a thin black frame reads clean and modern.

To work out the outer frame size from your print size and mat width, use the free frame and mat calculator. For when to offer a mat at all, read framed print with or without a mat.

Match your provider's metal frame

Print-on-demand frames vary, and product names don't always say what the frame is made of. Before you make the photo, check these five things on your provider's product page, then order one sample:

  1. Material: aluminium or metal, not a wood frame with a metallic paint.
  2. Colours offered: some ship only black and white; others add gold or silver. Don't show a colour you can't send.
  3. Profile: how wide the frame face is. A very thin frame should look very thin in the photo.
  4. Mat: included, optional or not offered, and its colour.
  5. Glazing: glass or acrylic. A photo can't show it well, so say it in the listing.

When the sample arrives, hold it next to your mockup. Our sample check guide lists what to compare, from frame colour to how much of the print the mat covers.

Questions

What is a metal frame mockup?

A photo of your print inside a slim metal picture frame, usually aluminium, hanging in a room. It shows buyers the frame colour, how thin the frame is and whether there is a mat, before they order.

Which colours does WallMock's metal frame come in?

Black, gold or silver, as an aluminum framed print with a slim aluminium frame, with or without a white mat. Wood-framed prints come in 7 colours: black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver.

Is there a gold metal frame mockup?

Yes. In the Studio, choose Metal, then Aluminum framed print, then gold. The example on this page is a gold aluminium frame with a white mat on a sage green hall wall.

Aluminium or aluminum: is there a difference?

No. Aluminium is the British spelling and aluminum the American one. Both mean the same light metal frame, so match whichever word your print provider uses in its product name.

Can I show a metal frame on a canvas?

Not as an aluminum-framed canvas. In WallMock the aluminium frame is for paper prints. Canvas goes in a floating frame instead, in black, white, natural oak, natural wood, walnut, espresso, gold or silver. For metal with no frame at all, choose Metal print: your art on a frameless aluminium panel.

What is the difference between a metal frame mockup and a metal print mockup?

A metal frame mockup shows a paper print inside a slim aluminium frame, with or without a mat. A metal print mockup shows the art printed on a frameless aluminium panel, in Gloss, High gloss or Matte. Show the product your provider ships.

Can I make metal frame mockups in bulk?

Yes. Choose Aluminum framed print, the frame colour and the mat once, heart your rooms, and WallMock makes every design in every chosen room in one run. On Lite or Pro, save the look as a profile to reuse on your next collection.

Will the frame or mat cover part of my print?

A little, as in a real frame. The frame lip or the mat overlaps the outer edge of the print, so keep signatures and text away from the very edge. WallMock keeps your file's own proportions and never stretches it.

Who are partner pilots for?

Companies whose sellers, or whose own listings, need room photos of the products they actually make.

Print labs and framers

Your frame range, in rooms, for every seller who lists your products. See for print labs.

Print-on-demand suppliers

Room photos for each product your sellers create, delivered to your own systems.

Art marketplaces

Listing photos for many artists' work, with each artwork kept exact.

Wall-art brands

A whole catalogue photographed the same way, with a different hero for each artwork.

What is the partner frame catalogue?

The partner frame catalogue turns a partner's actual frame range into presets in WallMock's renderer, so a seller's listing shows the frame that will arrive at the buyer's door. It is in development, and we are building it with a few partners first.

What works today

  • WallMock's own products. Floating canvas, stretched canvas, framed prints with or without a mat, metal prints (Gloss, High gloss or Matte) and aluminium frames, in 500+ curated rooms.
  • Exact art. Never redrawn, with an exact-art check on every room photo.
  • Saved presets in the API build. Your own frame names, which slots are on and the order they come in. The frames themselves are WallMock's.
  • Listing packs. Up to 10 photos per artwork from one call, with a different hero across a batch.

What a pilot builds

  • Presets of your frames. Built from your profiles, widths, depths, finishes and mat options, and checked against your product photos.
  • Private to you. Your presets will not be added to WallMock's public frame list.
  • For your sellers and your own listings. Delivered through the API, or a partner entry we agree with you.

What does a saved preset look like?

A test preset run on the API pilot with founder AK's own painting. The slots carry the preset's own frame names, one slot is turned off, the order is changed, a walnut framed print fills one slot, and the close-up focuses on the horse. Open any image for a larger view.

The frame names are made up for this test. Today a preset renames, orders and switches WallMock's own frames; presets of a partner's actual frames are what the pilot builds.

How does a partner pilot work?

  1. Talk to us

    Tell us what you make, where your sellers list and roughly how many images they need. We say honestly what a pilot can and cannot cover today.

  2. Share your range

    Send the frames you want to start with: profile, face width, depth, finish, mat options and product photos. A pilot starts with a handful of frames.

  3. Judge the samples

    We build presets in WallMock's renderer and send sample images made with an artwork you choose. You compare them with your own product photos.

  4. Connect

    If the frames look right to you, we agree how images reach your sellers: the API (early access), a partner entry, or store delivery. Terms are agreed per partner.

What should you send us to start?

  • The frames to start with, and for each one: face width, depth, profile, finish and colour, and mat sizes if you offer mats.
  • Product photos of those frames, ideally with a corner close-up.
  • One to three artworks you have the rights to share, for the samples.
  • Where your sellers list (Shopify, Etsy, your own site) and the image sizes you need.
  • Roughly how many images a month your sellers would make.

Questions

What is a WallMock partner pilot?

A small, hands-on project with one print lab, supplier, marketplace or brand. We turn a handful of your frames into presets in WallMock's renderer, send you sample images to judge against your own product photos, and only then agree how images reach your sellers. To discuss a pilot, talk to us at hello@wallmock.com.

Can WallMock make mockups of our exact frames?

That is what the partner frame catalogue is being built for, and it is in development. In a pilot we build presets from your frame specifications and product photos, and you check them before your sellers see them. Today, WallMock renders its own frames and finishes, and a saved preset can carry your frame names and photo order.

Who are partner pilots for?

Print labs and framers with their own frame range, print-on-demand suppliers whose sellers need listing photos, art marketplaces, and wall-art brands with large catalogues.

Will partner presets be private?

That is the plan: partner presets will be private to the partner and its sellers, and will not be added to WallMock's public frame list. In the current API build (early access), saved presets belong to the account that made them.

Is the artwork changed?

No. The artwork is placed exactly as supplied; it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Every room photo gets an exact-art check against the uploaded file; close-ups are cut from a checked image.

Which products can WallMock show today?

Floating canvas, stretched canvas, framed prints with or without a mat, metal prints in Gloss, High gloss or Matte, and aluminium frames in black, gold or silver, in 500+ curated, digitally staged rooms.

How do the images reach sellers?

Through the WallMock API (early access, not available yet) into your own systems, with webhooks and signed result links. Send to Shopify is in beta, and Send to Etsy is coming soon, pending Etsy's approval of our app.

How fast is it?

In development tests, about 3 to 5 seconds per 4K image per worker. Speeds on production hardware may differ, so we measure them with you in a pilot.

Can the images be white-label?

Not yet. White-label output is planned for partner agreements. Tell us what you need and we will say what a pilot can cover today.

How much does a partner pilot cost?

Prices are not published. Pilot terms are agreed with each partner. The WallMock API will be pay per image, with volume plans.

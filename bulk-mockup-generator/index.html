Reuse your room choices for the next collection

Save a room collection from Favourites. Choose a product and finish for each room. For example, use an oak floating frame above the sofa and a walnut frame with a mat above the console. Save a second look in a room to make a separate image for another product or finish.

Each saved collection uses the room’s current looks. If you change a saved finish from oak to walnut, both collections that use that room will open with the updated finish. Check the count before you generate, especially when you mix artwork shapes or products.

How the setup compares with PSD templates

In Photoshop, Replace Contents swaps the artwork in a smart object. You can repeat those edits by hand or automate them. WallMock lets you reuse room, product and finish choices directly in the browser.

How many images will the batch make?

Start with the photos you want on one listing. With 10 rooms and one saved look in each, 10 compatible designs give you 100 images. Add a second look to nine of those rooms and the same setup gives you 190 possible images. Each supported room-and-look combination makes its own photo.

Check the count in Studio before you export. Your artwork shapes, chosen looks and included rooms affect which combinations can be made. The Free account includes 10 watermark-free 4K single-artwork downloads every 30 days. Lite includes 200 a month. See plan limits.

Questions

Can I make mockups for multiple artworks at once?

Yes. Upload your artworks together and select your rooms in Favourites. Set a product and finish for each room or add another look to make an extra image. WallMock places each artwork at its original proportions in each compatible room. Download the finished photos by artwork or as one ZIP.

How is this different from batch Photoshop mockups?

With PSD templates, each design goes into each template's smart object one by one, or you write an action or script to do it. WallMock needs no PSD files, actions or scripts: you choose rooms and the product once in your browser.

Does bulk mockup work with Printify, Printful or Gelato designs?

Yes. Upload the same artwork files you send to your print provider and choose the matching product, such as framed canvas or a framed poster. Then use the images in your Etsy or Shopify listings.

Is the quality high enough for Etsy zoom?

Paid downloads are 4K (4096 px), and your artwork is placed as supplied, never upscaled or repainted by AI. Start from a high-resolution file for the sharpest result.

How many images does a bulk run use?

Six individual artworks in four compatible rooms with one look per room use 24 downloads. Check the count before generating. Lite includes 200 clean 4K downloads a month and Pro has no monthly image limit under its fair-use terms.

Can one batch mix square, portrait and panoramic art?

Yes. Each artwork keeps its own proportions and is never stretched. Room options vary by artwork shape, so a room reviewed for portrait art may not offer a panoramic piece.

Which frame finish will a saved room use next time?

Each room uses its latest saved product and finish. If the room appears in several collections, they all reopen it with that same finish.

Can I inspect a real collection download before buying?

Yes. Open the three-artwork test to see the source files, all nine downloaded JPEGs and the export details. That test was recorded at 1K. Current Free and paid image downloads are 4K.

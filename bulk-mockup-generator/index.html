One at a time in Photoshop vs a whole collection

The usual way to batch mockups is a PSD template with a smart object: you swap your design in with Replace Contents, export, and repeat for every design and every template. It works, but the steps multiply.

Replace Contents is Photoshop's command for swapping a smart object's image (Layer > Smart Objects > Replace Contents), per Adobe's help page "Replace the contents of a smart object" (helpx.adobe.com), checked 2026-10-26. Actions and scripts can automate it, but someone still has to build and maintain them.

What a bulk run looks like, in numbers

The count is simple: designs x rooms. Say you launch a 30-piece botanical collection and want each piece in 8 rooms: a hero living room, two bedrooms, a kitchen, an office, a dining room, a hallway and one close view.

  • Template route: 30 designs x 8 templates = 240 separate swaps, checks and exports.
  • WallMock: save the 8 rooms once, add 30 files, check the free watermarked previews, then make all 240 images in one run.

240 images is more than Lite's 200 a month, and Pro has no monthly image count (fair use applies), so a shop launching collections every month usually fits Pro. Next month the 8 rooms are already saved, and the only new step is adding the new files.

Questions

Can I make multiple mockups at once?

Yes. In WallMock you save your favourite rooms once, add a whole collection, and every artwork is made in every saved room in one run. You do not repeat the setup for each design.

How is this different from batch Photoshop mockups?

With PSD templates, each design goes into each template's smart object one by one, or you write an action or script to do it. WallMock needs no PSD files, actions or scripts: you choose rooms and the product once in your browser.

Does bulk mockup work with Printify, Printful or Gelato designs?

Yes. Upload the same artwork files you send to your print provider and choose the matching product, such as framed canvas or a framed poster. Then use the images in your Etsy or Shopify listings.

Is the quality high enough for Etsy zoom?

Paid downloads are 4K (4096 px), and your artwork is placed as supplied, never upscaled or repainted by AI. Start from a high-resolution file for the sharpest result.

How many images does a bulk run use?

Designs multiplied by rooms. 30 designs in 8 rooms is 240 images. Lite includes 200 clean 4K images a month; Pro includes unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Previews are watermarked on Free and Try Free, which include 50 every 30 days; paid plans have unlimited previews.

Can one batch mix square, portrait and panoramic art?

Yes. Each artwork keeps its own proportions and is never stretched. Room options vary by artwork shape, so a room reviewed for portrait art may not offer a panoramic piece.

Do I lose my setup after the first collection?

No. Your favourite rooms and product choices are saved, so the next collection reuses them. That is where most of the time saving comes from.

Can I inspect a real collection download before buying?

Yes. The recorded three-artwork workflow includes three source PNGs, the nine downloaded JPEGs and a file manifest. It demonstrates one completed Free-plan run, recorded at 1K (1024 px) before Free downloads moved to 4K. It does not establish 4K speed, performance on your device or a physical supplier match. Guest previews are watermarked and need no sign-up.

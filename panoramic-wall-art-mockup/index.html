Panoramic and tall wall art

Panoramic wall art mockups, at their true shape.

Short answer: a panoramic wall art mockup shows a wide 3:1 or 2:1 print, or a tall 1:2 or 1:3 one, on a wall that suits its shape. WallMock keeps each file at its own proportions and only offers it in rooms reviewed for that shape: panoramas above sofas, beds and sideboards, tall prints on narrow walls between doors, in niches and on landings. Your file is placed exactly as supplied, as a floating-frame canvas, stretched canvas or framed print.

3:1 panoramic mockups above sofas, beds and sideboards

A panorama needs something long under it. Centred on a sofa, bed or sideboard, at roughly two thirds of its width and 15–25 cm (6–10 in) above it, the art and the furniture read as one piece. On an empty wall the same print looks like a thin strip.

Examples made with WallMock; each artwork is placed as supplied, not redrawn. Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock.

2:1 wide art: the easier panorama

A 2:1 print is deep enough to read from across a room and still long enough for a sofa or sideboard. It also fits walls a 3:1 overwhelms, like a bed set into a niche.

Tall 1:3 and 1:2 art on narrow walls

A tall print needs a tall, narrow wall: a pier between two doors, a niche, a landing or the strip beside a window. Centre it on that wall, not on the room. In WallMock the narrow piers and tall niches are reviewed for tall and portrait shapes only, so a panorama is never squeezed onto them.

1:3, the narrowest shape

1:2, tall but less extreme

Botanicals, single figures, herons, waterfalls and abstract columns suit tall shapes. Two tall prints side by side can also fill a wider wall as a pair; see the gallery wall mockup generator.

Listing a long piece: what to check

The first photo still has to read small

Etsy asks for a landscape or square first photo and crops thumbnails. A 3:1 panorama in a whole-room view can shrink to a line, so use a closer crop of the room for the first photo and keep the full room for the second.

Show the size with furniture

A sofa, bed or bench tells buyers how long the piece is. Pair the room photo with the dimensions in the listing; a staged room is context, not a ruler.

Same rooms for the whole series

Heart the rooms that suit your long pieces once. Every new panorama goes into the same rooms, and your square and portrait art in the same batch goes into rooms that suit its own shape.

Questions

Can WallMock make panoramic wall art mockups?

Yes. Upload a 3:1 or 2:1 file and WallMock offers it in rooms reviewed for that shape, above sofas, beds, sideboards and benches. The file keeps its own proportions and is placed exactly as supplied, never cropped, stretched or redrawn.

What about tall 1:2 and 1:3 art?

Tall files go into rooms reviewed for tall shapes: narrow piers between doors, tall niches, landings and the wall beside a window. On the 249 rooms listed on this site, 42 are reviewed for 1:2 and 38 for 1:3.

Which products can I show a panorama as?

Floating-frame canvas, stretched canvas or a framed print with a white mat, depending on the room. Each room lists the products it offers for each shape.

Will a panorama be cropped to fit a template?

No. A room either offers your shape or it does not show it; WallMock never crops or stretches the file to fit. That is also why some rooms you like for portrait art will not appear for a 3:1 piece.

Can one batch mix panoramic, tall and standard art?

Yes. Each artwork keeps its own proportions, and room options follow each artwork's shape, so a 3:1 and a 2:3 file in the same batch can land in different rooms.

Can I show a panorama split into three canvases?

Yes, on a paid plan: sets of 3 hang together on one wall in compatible framed and canvas products. Work out the panel sizes first with the free triptych split calculator.

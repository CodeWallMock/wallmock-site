Make your own batch

  1. Add the artwork files

    Upload the designs together. In the example, three files went in: a portrait, a landscape and a square.

  2. Save the rooms and frame choices

    In Rooms, heart the scenes you want. In Favourites, choose a product and finish for each room and save them as a room collection. Add another look when you want a second photo with a different finish.

  3. Check the previews

    Switch between artworks to see how each sits on the wall. Review the image count and check the frame or mat before you make the batch.

  4. Make the photos and download the batch

    WallMock fits each artwork to the room and renders the selected product. In Results, download the finished photos together as a ZIP or open the downloads for one artwork.

  5. Add the images to your listing

    Pick a clear first photo, then use a close-up or another room to explain the product. Upload the images in Etsy’s listing editor. Pro can make a short listing video from a finished mockup.

Your art, placed as supplied

WallMock places your supplied file in the room and keeps its proportions. Your brushwork, lettering and signature come from that file.

Reviewed rooms

Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms, nurseries, dining rooms, entries, home offices, cafés and studios. Each is reviewed for the shapes and products it can show.

The products you sell

Stretched canvas, framed canvas and framed prints with a mat, in finishes such as natural oak, walnut, black, white and gold.

Pairs, trios and sets of four

Show matching pieces together, and adjust the gaps and group placement where the room allows it.

Portrait to panoramic

Rooms cover portrait, square, landscape and panoramic artwork, including 3:1 panoramas and tall 1:3 pieces in rooms reviewed for them.

The rooms are digitally staged interiors, reviewed by the WallMock team for the products and proportions they can show. Room options vary by product and artwork proportions.

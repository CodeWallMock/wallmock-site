HOW IT WORKS · INVITED BETA

Room mockups for your whole collection

WallMock is built for artists and print sellers who list many artworks. Set up your rooms and frames once, then make product images of every piece in a collection.

Five steps

  1. Add your artwork

    Add several artworks for a batch of single images, or arrange one pair, trio or set of four. Your supplied image is placed into each room; it isn't redrawn by an image generator, and it keeps its proportions.

  2. Choose your rooms

    Browse reviewed rooms and heart the ones you want to use. Every room lists the products and artwork proportions it supports, from portrait and square to landscape and panoramic.

  3. Set the look once

    Choose stretched canvas, framed canvas or a framed print with a mat, and the frame finish, for each room. Save the setup as a Profile and reuse it for the next collection.

  4. Preview, then make

    Preview your artwork in the rooms before spending a credit. Check the planned images, then make clean 4096 px exports. An unsupported combination is marked as unavailable and is not charged.

  5. Download and list

    Download individual JPEGs, a ZIP for each artwork, or the whole batch, ready for your shop, your website or a marketplace. Your artwork and results stay in the current session, so download them before refreshing.

What makes it different

Whole collections, not one image at a time

Apply one set of rooms and frames to every artwork in a collection, instead of repeating the same steps per piece.

Your art, placed as supplied

WallMock places your image into the room rather than generating a new picture, and keeps its proportions.

Reviewed rooms

Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, bathrooms, nurseries, dining rooms, entries, home offices, cafés and studios. Each is reviewed for the shapes and products it can show.

The products you sell

Stretched canvas, framed canvas and framed prints with a mat, in finishes such as natural oak, walnut, black, white and gold,.

Pairs, trios and sets of four

Show matching pieces together, and adjust the gaps and group placement where the room allows it.

Portrait to panoramic

Rooms cover portrait, square, landscape and panoramic artwork, including 3:1 panoramas and tall 1:3 pieces in rooms reviewed for them.

The rooms are digitally staged interiors, reviewed by the WallMock team for the products and proportions they can show. Room options vary by product and artwork proportions.

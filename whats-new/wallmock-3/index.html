WallMock 3: a new, more realistic frame look

By Akshit, founder of WallMock · Published 17 November 2026 · How we write our guides

Short answer: WallMock 3, released on 17 November 2026, gives every frame a new, more realistic surface. Natural oak, walnut and espresso show wood grain, natural wood has a fine straight grain, gold and silver have fine brushed lines and a sheen on the side the room light comes from, and black and white are smooth satin. Every frame has a mitred corner. Your artwork is never changed.

What is new

  • Brushed gold and silver. Fine lines run along each rail, with a lit side and a shade side that follow the room light.
  • Satin black and white. A smooth painted finish with no texture, shaped by the room light: a lit edge and a darker inner edge.
  • Wood grain. Natural oak, walnut and espresso show wood grain; natural wood shows fine, straight lines.
  • A mitred corner on every frame. A thin diagonal joint where the rails meet; the grain or brushing changes direction across it.
  • Modelled on print-on-demand frames. A floating frame with a visible gap, a 1.5 in (38 mm) canvas by default, framed prints with a mat and slim aluminium frames.
  • Same sizes, same art. Frame width, gap, depth and placement are unchanged, and your artwork is placed exactly as supplied.

More detail in version 3

WallMock 3 is about the frame itself. Sizes, shapes and thickness were already right, so they stay the same. What changes is the surface and the way it takes the light, so a frame still looks right when a buyer zooms into the corner of a 4K image. Each square is a frame corner cut from a 4K (4096 px) WallMock image and enlarged 2× so the detail is easy to see; tap one to open the crop at 100%.

Gold and silver: brushed metal

Fine brushed lines run along each rail; at page size the frame reads as smooth metal, and the lines show when you zoom in. The sheen sits on the side the room light comes from, and the far rails fall into shade.

Wood grain: natural oak, walnut, espresso and natural wood

Each rail shows a wood grain under a soft satin finish, and the grain changes direction at the mitred corner. Natural wood has finer, straighter lines than natural oak.

Satin black and white

A smooth painted finish with no grain or lines. The look comes from the light: a lit edge, a darker inner edge and a small change in tone from one rail to the next.

Framed prints with a mat

Framed prints show the crisp lit edge of the frame, the bevel of the mat and the shadow the frame casts onto the mat.

Aluminium framed prints

The slim aluminium frame gets the same brushed lines as the gold and silver floating frames.

Modelled on the frames print-on-demand labs offer

WallMock’s finishes are modelled on the frames print-on-demand labs commonly offer. Labs such as Lumaprints and Printseekers list black, white, gold, silver and wood floating frames; Lumaprints also lists natural oak and walnut with a wood-grain finish and describes its silver frame as brushed (their public product pages, checked 18 November 2026). WallMock draws brushed gold and silver, satin black and white, and wood grain on natural oak, walnut, espresso and natural wood. It is not a copy of any one lab’s frame, so compare a mockup with your supplier’s sample before you list it.

  • Floating frame canvas: the canvas sits inside the frame with a visible gap all the way round.
  • Canvas depth: about 1.5 in (38 mm) by default, with 1.25 in and 0.75 in also available.
  • Four rails, 45° mitred corners, now drawn with the joint line.
  • Framed prints: a wood or painted frame, with or without a mat.
  • Aluminium framed prints: a slim aluminium frame in black, gold or silver.

Which products get the new look

  • Canvas in a floating frame, in all 8 finishes: black, white, natural oak, natural wood, walnut, espresso, gold and silver.
  • Framed prints in wood and painted frames, with or without a mat.
  • Aluminium framed prints in black, gold and silver.
  • Custom frame colours follow their type: a custom wood colour gets wood grain, a metallic colour the brushed lines, and a painted colour the satin finish.

Stretched canvas (no frame) and metal prints look the same as before.

What stays the same

  • Your artwork. WallMock places your file exactly as you uploaded it. It is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled, and every image is checked for that before you download it.
  • Sizes and placement. Frame width, floating gap, canvas depth, mats and where the art sits in each room are the same as in version 2.5.
  • Your setup. Favourite rooms, profiles, saved frame colours and plans do not change.

How to get the new look

Images you have already made stay as they are. To get the new look, make your images again: open your artwork in the Studio, keep your rooms and frame, and generate. Then download one image at 4K and zoom into a corner. WallMock version 3.0 replaces version 2.5; it is a frame look update with no price, plan or room changes. The full list of changes is on the What’s new page.

Questions about WallMock 3

Do I need to do anything to get the new frame look?

Make your images again. Images you made before 17 November 2026 stay as they were. New images use the new look; your rooms, profiles and saved colours are unchanged.

Does WallMock 3 change my artwork or its size on the wall?

No. Your artwork is placed exactly as you uploaded it and is never redrawn. Frame width, floating gap, canvas depth, mats and placement are the same as before; only the frame’s surface and lighting change.

Which frames get wood grain?

Natural oak, walnut and espresso show wood grain. Natural wood shows fine, straight grain lines. Black and white are smooth with a satin finish, and gold and silver show fine brushed lines.

Are these the same finishes print-on-demand labs offer?

They are the same kinds of finishes. Labs such as Lumaprints and Printseekers list black, white, gold, silver and wood floating frames, and Lumaprints lists natural oak and walnut with wood grain (checked 18 November 2026). WallMock does not copy any one lab’s frame, so compare a mockup with your supplier’s sample.

Does the new look apply to framed prints and aluminium frames?

Yes. Framed prints in wood and painted frames, with or without a mat, and aluminium framed prints in black, gold and silver get the new look. Stretched canvas and metal prints are unchanged.

Will buyers see the detail in my listing photos?

From a normal viewing distance a frame looks smooth and calm. The grain, brushed lines and mitred corner show when someone zooms in. Download at 4K to keep that detail.

What's new in WallMock

Published by WallMock · Updated 29 October 2026

Short answer: These are WallMock updates, newest first. The latest, on 29 October 2026: premium shine on every frame, frame shadows in four window-lit rooms now fall away from the window, a faster Studio, a Help Center and a zoomable examples page. Paid plans and open sign-up started on 28 October 2026. WallMock has been in development since July 2026.

Each update is listed under the day it reached the app or this site, newest first. To try the latest version, open the free preview; no sign-up is needed.

29 October 2026

  • Premium frame shine: richer gold, silver, black, white and wood frames on every product, with highlights that follow the room's light.
  • Frame shadows in four rooms lit by a window now fall away from the window.
  • A faster Studio: smaller files to load, and a lighter sample for the tour.
  • When you open a favourite room large, or use Size & position, you now see the framed product, not the bare artwork.
  • A new Help Center with short answers to common questions.
  • A new wall art mockup examples page. Click any image to zoom in on a 2400 px view of the frame, the mat or the canvas.

28 October 2026

  • Paid plans are open: Lite $5, Pro $12 and Studio $24 a month. See pricing.
  • Sign-up is open to everyone. Sign in with Google, or with a code we email you. There is no password.
  • Natural shine on every frame finish, plus a brushed look for silver.
  • Our terms of service and refund policy are published.

26 October 2026

  • Free watermarked previews with no sign-up at app.wallmock.com/try.

How we decide what to build

We build from what sellers tell us: what slows them down, what looks wrong and what they wish WallMock did. Before a release that changes how frames look, we render every frame finish and check it by eye, up close, before it ships. Ideas and problem reports go to hello@wallmock.com.

For what WallMock is and how we check what we publish, read about WallMock and how WallMock keeps your art exact.

Before public launch: how WallMock was built

WallMock has been in development since July 2026. Free previews opened to everyone on 26 October 2026, and paid plans on 28 October 2026. What was built, month by month:

July 2026

  • The compositing engine: your artwork is placed pixel for pixel, never redrawn, with an automatic check that the art in every image matches your file.
  • Favourite rooms and one-click generation in all of them, the start of the whole-collection workflow.
  • Sets of two artworks on one wall.

August 2026

  • More square, portrait and wide 2:1 rooms, and saved rooms filtered by the shape of your art.

September 2026

  • Every new room checked edge to edge before it joins the library.
  • Design-your-own frames in any colour.
  • Sets of three and four on one wall.
  • Deeper floating frames and mats, and several images made at once on laptops.

October 2026

  • The room library passes 500 curated rooms.
  • Room Zoom for close, styled crops, and suggested rooms for each artwork.

Questions

What can WallMock do for a print-on-demand shop?

Make listing photos for a whole collection at once: every artwork in every room and frame look you saved, with crops for Etsy, Pinterest and Instagram, alt text, a listing checklist and, on Pro, listing videos. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI.

Which frames and products does WallMock show?

Framed canvas (floating frame), Stretched canvas, Framed print, with or without a mat, Metal print (art on a frameless aluminium panel), Aluminum framed print (black, gold or silver), with or without a mat. Design your own frame: pick wood grain, metallic or painted, then any colour with the colour picker or one of 21 one-click colours (5 woods, 6 metals and 10 paints). Some rooms keep only the frame looks we reviewed for them; custom frames work in all the others. Colours are close approximations, not a supplier's finish. Framed prints come with no mat or a mat in white, warm white, cream, warm grey or black.

Is every feature in every plan?

Every plan makes clean 4K (4096 px) images with the same rooms and frames. Pro and Studio include unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Pro and Studio include listing videos. Free previews are watermarked. A free account gets 10 watermark-free downloads (1024 px) every 30 days, one free account per person; previews stay watermarked. Its first 10 free downloads work in every room, then the free plan keeps 8 rooms.

How many images can I make?

Lite $5 a month for 200 clean 4K (4096 px) images; Pro $12 and Studio $24 a month for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images. Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server (600 on Studio).

How fast is it?

In our tests, a full-size 4K (4096 px) image takes about 7 to 12 seconds in the browser, and a laptop makes up to 4 images at once.

Do I need Photoshop?

No. WallMock runs in your web browser, with no PSD files or smart objects to edit.

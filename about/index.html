About WallMock

WallMock makes listing photos for wall art sellers: your artwork, exactly as you made it, in curated rooms, for every design in your shop.

Made by the WallMock team · Updated 28 October 2026

WallMock in one line

WallMock is a wall art mockup generator for Etsy and Shopify sellers: your exact artwork in realistic styled rooms as framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed prints.

  • What it is: a web app that places your artwork file in 500+ curated rooms and makes listing-ready images, up to 4096 px, for a whole collection at once. No Photoshop or PSD files.
  • Who it's for: print-on-demand and wall art sellers on Etsy and Shopify who sell framed canvas, stretched canvas, framed fine-art prints and posters.
  • What it costs: Free watermarked previews in every room. Clean images come with a plan: Lite $5, Pro $12, Studio $24 a month (see pricing). Plans are billed securely by Stripe from $5 a month. Cancel any time; new subscribers can get a refund within 14 days, minus credits used.
  • What it isn't: a pack of template files, or an image generator that redraws your artwork. It isn't WallMockup either.

Who WallMock is for

WallMock is built for sellers who list many designs: print-on-demand shops using Printify, Printful or Gelato, framed canvas and fine-art print sellers, and poster and printable shops on Etsy and Shopify. You save your favourite rooms and frame looks once, add a collection, and every artwork appears in every chosen room, so the whole shop looks consistent. Pairs, trios and sets of four go on one wall.

It shows the products these shops sell: framed canvas (floating frame); stretched canvas; framed print, with or without a mat; metal-framed print (aluminium), with or without a mat.

Who makes WallMock

Made by the WallMock team. We build the app at app.wallmock.com and this website, and we write the guides and free tools on it. Questions, corrections and ideas go to hello@wallmock.com.

What WallMock does to your artwork

  • WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Your artwork is placed exactly as supplied, so text, signatures, fine lines and colours stay as you made them.
  • Every artwork keeps its own proportions. It is never stretched or squashed to fit a room; rooms only offer the shapes and products they were reviewed for.
  • Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock.
  • Previews and images are made in your browser, on your device.

How our examples are made

The room images on this site were made with WallMock from artworks WallMock made for its own demonstrations. None of them is customer work. Before-and-after examples show the original artwork file next to the WallMock image made from it.

How we check what we publish

  • Prices, plans and product facts come from one facts file. Automated tests compare the plans with the live app's own offers before every release, and scan every page, our structured data and our summary for AI assistants (llms.txt) for claims we can't back up. Facts last checked 28 October 2026.
  • Comparisons with other tools quote each company's own public pages and say the date they were checked. Tools change: if something is out of date, email us and we'll correct it.
  • Every guide shows the date it was last updated and links its sources.
  • No made-up proof. We don't invent customer quotes, star ratings or results, and we only show a seller's work with their written permission.

Privacy

This website has no advertising or analytics trackers, and its free tools work in your browser without uploading your files. The app has its own policy. Read privacy at WallMock, the app privacy policy and the terms and refunds.

About WallMock

WallMock makes listing photos for wall art sellers: your artwork, exactly as you made it, in curated rooms, for every design in your shop.

Made by the WallMock team · Updated 28 October 2026

WallMock in one line

WallMock is a wall art mockup generator for print-on-demand sellers on Etsy and Shopify: your exact art in styled rooms as framed or stretched canvas, or framed prints.

  • What it is: a web app that places your artwork file in 500+ curated rooms and makes listing-ready images in 4K (4096 px), for a whole collection at once. No Photoshop or PSD files.
  • Who it's for: print-on-demand and wall art sellers on Etsy and Shopify who sell framed canvas, stretched canvas, framed fine-art prints and posters.
  • What it costs: Free account: 10 watermark-free downloads (1024 px), back to 10 every 30 days (unused ones don't carry over), with watermarked previews. Your first 10 free downloads work in all 500+ rooms; after that the free plan keeps 8 rooms. 4K (4096 px) images, watermark-free previews, every room, sets (any plan) and listing videos (Pro and Studio) need a plan. Clean 4K images come with a plan: Lite $5 a month for 200 clean 4K (4096 px) images; Pro $12 and Studio $24 a month for unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images (see pricing). Fair use: up to 150 new rooms a day and 400 in 30 days, and up to 300 images a day made on WallMock's server (600 on Studio). Plans are billed securely by Stripe from $5 a month. Cancel any time; new subscribers can get a refund within 14 days, minus credits used.
  • What it isn't: a pack of template files, or an image generator that redraws your artwork. It isn't WallMockup either.

Who WallMock is for

WallMock is built for sellers who list many designs: print-on-demand shops using Printify, Printful or Gelato, framed canvas and fine-art print sellers, and poster and printable shops on Etsy and Shopify. You save your favourite rooms and frame looks once, add a collection, and every artwork appears in every chosen room, so the whole shop looks consistent. Pairs, trios and sets of four go on one wall.

It shows the products these shops sell: framed canvas (floating frame); stretched canvas; framed print, with or without a mat; metal print (art on a frameless aluminium panel); aluminum framed print (black, gold or silver), with or without a mat.

Why sellers choose WallMock

Print-on-demand and wall art shops that list many designs as framed canvas, stretched canvas or framed prints and want the same premium rooms across the whole shop, with unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images on Pro and Studio.

  • Photoshop-quality framing: the gap around floating-frame canvas, a natural shine on every frame and a soft wall shadow, in clean 4K (4096 px) images.
  • Your exact art: never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI, with a published check.
  • Whole collections: save favourite rooms once, and every artwork goes into every chosen room in one run, sets of 2, 3 or 4 included.
  • Frames: 7 finishes plus design-your-own frames in any colour; floating frames in thin, medium or bold profiles; framed prints in 20 mm wood, 31.75 mm gallery wood or slim aluminum, with or without a mat.
  • Speed: in our tests, about 7 to 12 seconds per full-size image in the browser, and a laptop makes up to 4 at once.
  • Try it with no sign-up and no card; plans from $5 a month, and unlimited clean 4K (4096 px) images on Pro from $12.

What WallMock does to your artwork

  • WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Your artwork is placed exactly as supplied, so text, signatures, fine lines and colours stay as you made them.
  • Every artwork keeps its own proportions. It is never stretched or squashed to fit a room; rooms only offer the shapes and products they were reviewed for.
  • Rooms are curated, digitally staged interiors reviewed by WallMock.
  • Previews and images are made in your browser, on your device.

How our examples are made

The room images on this site were made with WallMock from artworks WallMock made for its own demonstrations. None of them is customer work. Before-and-after examples show the original artwork file next to the WallMock image made from it.

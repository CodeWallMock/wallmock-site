Author

AK

Founder of WallMock. Etsy wall-art seller since 2023.

I run a print-on-demand wall art shop on Etsy. By 1 November 2026 my shop’s public counter showed more than 8,000 sales. I built WallMock because I was making the same room photos for every new collection, and I publish these guides for sellers doing the same work.

What I know first-hand

  • Selling framed canvas and prints made by a print partner. I check a sample of a product before I list it, match my listing photos to the frame the buyer will get, and answer buyers who ask whether the frame in the photo is the one they will receive.
  • Room photos for whole collections. My listings use several room photos per design. WallMock grew out of reusing the rooms and frame choices that worked, instead of setting up each image by hand.
  • Where art goes on a wall. In November 2026 we went through every room in WallMock and moved the default art spot in about 140 of them, mostly rooms where panels, wainscoting or a niche already mark where a picture belongs. The placement guide explains the rules we settled on.

The rest comes from reading what Etsy, Shopify and the print suppliers publish, with the date we checked it on each guide. How we write our guides explains the process, including where AI writing tools are used.

Contact

Found a mistake, or a rule that changed? Email hello@wallmock.com. When a guide is wrong, we fix it and change its date.

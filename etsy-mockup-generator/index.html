What to put in the first five photo slots

Use the first photo for a clear view of the artwork at thumbnail size. In the next photos, show how it fits a room, the frame or edge, the sizes and the finishes you sell.

  1. First photo. Lead with a clear view of what’s for sale. If you use a room photo, check that the artwork still reads in Etsy’s cropped thumbnail.
  2. Different rooms. Living room, bedroom, office or nursery, so different buyers see their home.
  3. Frame detail. Show the corner, floating gap or mat if it’s part of what the buyer receives. A close-up of your own sample is the best way to show the actual finish.
  4. Scale. The art above a sofa, bed or sideboard, to give the art room context. Include a labelled size guide for dimensions.
  5. Sets together. Pairs and trios on one wall, if you sell them as a set.

When Etsy asks for a product photo

Most physical items need at least one unedited photo of the item. Etsy allows an authorised stock photo mockup for eligible print-on-demand items where a production partner prints your original design on a standard base product. If you sell printable art, show a preview of the file the buyer receives and label the listing as a digital download. Check the rules for your item before choosing the first photo.

Questions

What is an Etsy mockup generator?

A tool that puts your artwork into realistic photos, such as a framed print on a living-room wall, so your Etsy listing shows how the piece looks at home. WallMock does this for framed canvas, stretched canvas, and framed prints and posters.

Which photos should I add to an Etsy wall art listing?

Start with a clear first photo, then show the frame, the size or scale, and a second room if it helps the buyer picture the piece. Include an unedited product photo where Etsy requires one. If you sell a set, show all the pieces together.

Can I use WallMock mockups with Printify, Printful or Gelato products?

Yes. Upload the same artwork file you send to your print provider and choose the matching product, such as a framed canvas or a framed poster. Use the photos in your Etsy or Shopify listing.

Will the mockup show my art accurately?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. It keeps its own proportions and is never stretched to fit.

Can I make mockups for many listings at once?

Yes. The bulk mockup generator applies your saved room choices to multiple artwork files, so each design gets its own listing photos.

Do I need Photoshop?

No. WallMock runs in your web browser, with no PSD files or smart objects.

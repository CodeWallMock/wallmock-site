A listing plan that sells wall art

Buyers can't hold your print, so the photos do the work. A simple order most wall art shops use:

  1. Hero room. Your best room, bright and calm, art clearly the focus.
  2. Different rooms. Living room, bedroom, office or nursery, so different buyers see their home.
  3. The frame up close. Show the finish, the floating gap or the mat.
  4. Scale. The art above a sofa, bed or sideboard, so the size is obvious.
  5. Sets together. Pairs and trios on one wall, if you sell them as a set.

WallMock gives you every artwork in every room you saved, so filling these slots for a whole collection takes one run, not an afternoon in Photoshop.

Etsy's photo rule (updated 22 October 2026)

Etsy's listing image rules (updated 22 October 2026): most listings need at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background, with no editing or AI tools on that photo. Other photos may be edited if they show the item accurately. Print-on-demand items made by a production partner may use an authorised mockup instead, and digital downloads need a preview of the content. So use WallMock images for your room and detail photos, and add one real photo of the print or canvas unless you sell print on demand or downloads.

Questions

What is an Etsy mockup generator?

A tool that puts your artwork into realistic photos, such as a framed print on a living-room wall, so your Etsy listing shows how the piece looks at home. WallMock does this for framed canvas, stretched canvas, and framed prints and posters.

How many mockups should a wall art listing have?

Use most of your photo slots: a hero room photo first, then other rooms, a close view of the frame, a size or scale photo and, for sets, the whole set together. Different rooms help buyers picture the art in their own home.

Can I use WallMock mockups with Printify, Printful or Gelato products?

Yes. Upload the same artwork file you send to your print provider and choose the matching product, such as a framed canvas or a framed poster. Use the photos in your Etsy or Shopify listing.

Will the mockup show my art accurately?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. It keeps its own proportions and is never stretched to fit.

Can I make mockups for many listings at once?

Yes. Save your favourite rooms once and add a whole collection; each artwork is made in every chosen room.

Do I need Photoshop?

No. WallMock runs in your web browser, with no PSD files or smart objects.

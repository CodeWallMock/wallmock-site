Why printable sellers show the art framed on a wall

A digital download buyer gets a file, then prints it and puts it in a frame. The listing has to show that finished result, because the buyer can't picture it from a flat file.

  1. Show it framed. A thin black, white or natural oak frame, with or without a white mat, is how most buyers will display a printable.
  2. Show it in several rooms. Three to five rooms help different buyers see it in their home: living room, bedroom, kitchen, office or nursery.
  3. Show what they actually get. Etsy asks digital download listings to include a preview of the content, so add one image of the artwork itself and label the frame and room as not included.

A whole collection of listing photos at once

Printable shops often list dozens of designs. Set up the look once and reuse it for every new artwork.

  1. Add rooms to Favourites. Heart the rooms that suit your shop once; they stay saved for every collection.
  2. Save the frame look. Pick the framed print, frame colour and mat once.
  3. Add the collection. Drop in every printable. Each artwork is made in every chosen room, in one run.

The count is designs × rooms: 30 printables in 5 rooms is 150 images, within Lite's 200 a month; 30 in 8 rooms is 240, which fits Pro, with no monthly image count under fair use. The bulk mockup generator page walks through a full run.

Questions

Can I use WallMock mockups in Etsy digital download listings?

Yes. Clean WallMock downloads can be used in Etsy and Shopify listings, ads and social posts with no extra licence fee, even after you cancel. Etsy asks digital download listings to include a preview of the content, so add one image of the artwork itself and say that the frame and room are not included.

Is the frame included in the digital download?

No. The mockup shows one way a buyer could display the print. Say 'digital download, no physical item shipped' in the first lines of your description and on one listing image.

Can I make mockups for 100 listings a day?

On a paid plan, yes, with two numbers to check. 100 listings with 5 photos each is 500 images. Lite includes 200 clean 4K images a month, so that volume needs Pro or the WallMock Studio plan, which have no monthly image count. Fair use: paid plans can open any room in the library. Up to 300 images a day can be made on WallMock's server; images made in your browser, on your device, do not count toward that limit. Speed depends on your device, files and batch size, so try a representative file before a big run.

Does WallMock change my artwork?

No. WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Lettering, signatures, colours and proportions stay as they are in your file.

Which ratios and sizes can I show?

Each file keeps its own shape, from square to 3:1 panoramas and 1:3 tall pieces, and is never stretched. Upload one file per ratio you sell, such as 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 11:14 and ISO A, and each one goes into rooms that suit its shape.

Can I show a set of 2, 3 or 4 printables together?

Yes, on a paid plan. Compatible framed products show pairs, trios and sets of four on one wall with matching frames and even gaps. Free downloads are for single artworks.

Do I need Photoshop?

No. WallMock runs in your web browser, with no PSD files or smart objects. Try it with no sign-up and no card: up to three artworks in 8 sample rooms, as watermarked previews.

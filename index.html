Metal

A metal print is your art printed on a thin aluminum panel: no frame, small rounded corners and a thin bare-metal edge, hung on a hidden block about ½ in (12.7 mm) off the wall so it casts a soft, tight shadow. The surface is gloss white with a gentle sheen that follows the room light (the default), high gloss with a clear window reflection, or matte for a low-glare, canvas-like finish. Or choose paper in a slim aluminum frame: aluminum framed prints in black, gold or silver, with or without a mat.

  • Metal print: your art on a frameless aluminum panel, just off the wall.

Questions

What is WallMock?

WallMock is a bulk wall art mockup generator for print-on-demand, framed art and printable shops. Add several artworks at once, choose rooms and frame styles, and download 4K listing photos grouped by artwork.

Who is it for?

WallMock is built for Etsy and Shopify wall art sellers who regularly add new designs. It works with the artwork files you send to print providers such as Printify, Printful or Gelato. Choose the canvas, paper, frame or metal finish that matches what you sell.

Is my artwork changed or redrawn?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Every artwork keeps its own proportions; it is never stretched to fit a room.

Can I make mockups for a whole collection at once?

Yes. Upload your artworks together, save rooms in Favourites and choose a product and frame finish for each. WallMock fits each artwork to the room at its original proportions and makes one photo for each compatible artwork, room and look. Download photos by artwork or as one ZIP.

Can I make a video for my listing?

Pro includes short listing videos made from your finished mockups. On supported browsers, the Frame reveal video starts at the frame corner, moves along the edge and pulls back to show the whole room.

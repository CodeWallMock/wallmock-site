Questions

What is WallMock?

WallMock is a wall art mockup generator for print-on-demand sellers on Etsy and Shopify: your exact art in styled rooms as framed or stretched canvas, or framed prints. Upload your artwork, pick rooms and frames, and download listing-ready images for one design or a whole collection. It is not WallMockup (wallmockup.net), a different company.

Who is it for?

Sellers on Etsy and Shopify who sell framed canvas, stretched canvas, framed fine-art prints or posters, including print-on-demand shops using Printify, Printful or Gelato, and anyone listing many designs.

Is my artwork changed or redrawn?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Every artwork keeps its own proportions; it is never stretched to fit a room.

Can I make mockups for a whole collection at once?

Yes. Save your favourite rooms and frame looks once, add many artworks, and WallMock makes every artwork in every chosen room. Sets of 2, 3 or 4 are supported.

What frames and products can I show?

Framed canvas (floating frame), Stretched canvas, Framed print, with or without a mat, Metal-framed print (aluminium), with or without a mat. Sets of 2, 3 or 4 pieces too.

Do I need Photoshop or PSD files?

No. WallMock runs in your web browser. There are no PSD files or smart objects to edit. You still get a clean 4096 px image with the floating-frame gap, natural frame shine and a soft wall shadow, the quality you'd expect from a hand-built Photoshop mockup; zoom into the wall art mockup examples and check the corners yourself.

How much does it cost?

Free watermarked previews in every room. Clean images come with a plan: Lite $5 a month (200 images), Pro $12 (5,000) or Studio $24 (10,000). Prices in USD; the app shows local prices in EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR and BRL.

Can I use it today?

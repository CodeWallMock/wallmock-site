Questions

What is WallMock?

WallMock is a wall art mockup generator for print-on-demand sellers on Etsy and Shopify: your exact art in styled rooms as framed or stretched canvas, or framed prints. Upload your artwork, pick rooms and frames, and download listing-ready images for one design or a whole collection. It is not WallMockup (wallmockup.net), a different company.

Who is it for?

Sellers on Etsy and Shopify who sell framed canvas, stretched canvas, framed fine-art prints or posters, including print-on-demand shops using Printify, Printful or Gelato, and anyone listing many designs.

Is my artwork changed or redrawn?

WallMock places your file as it is: it is never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Every artwork keeps its own proportions; it is never stretched to fit a room.

Can I make mockups for a whole collection at once?

Yes. Save your favourite rooms and frame looks once, add many artworks, and WallMock makes every artwork in every chosen room. Sets of 2, 3 or 4 are supported.

What frames and products can I show?

Framed canvas (floating frame), Stretched canvas, Framed print, with or without a mat, Metal-framed print (aluminium), with or without a mat. Sets of 2, 3 or 4 pieces too. Frames come in black, white, natural oak, walnut, espresso, gold and silver. Design your own frame: pick wood grain, metallic or painted, then any colour with the colour picker or one of 21 one-click colours (5 woods, 6 metals and 10 paints). Custom frames are made in rooms that are not limited to reviewed looks; other rooms keep their reviewed frames. Colours are close approximations, not a supplier's finish. Framed prints come in slim, classic or bold frames, with no mat or a mat in white, warm white, cream, warm grey or black.

Why choose WallMock over other mockup tools?

Photoshop-quality framed canvas and print mockups of your exact art, made for a whole collection at once. Floating frames show the gap and the frame casts a soft wall shadow; your file is never redrawn; you set up favourite rooms once and every artwork goes into every chosen room; and you can try it with no sign-up.

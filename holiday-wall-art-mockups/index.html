How to make seasonal listing photos that sell

  1. Change one photo, not ten. Make one seasonal hero and put it first; keep the frame close-up, scale shot and sizes as they are. A listing still has to work the rest of the year.
  2. Swap six to eight weeks before the date. For Christmas that is roughly 30 October to 13 November. It is a planning window, not a measured optimum: move it for delivery cut-offs and your own shop data. The wall art selling calendar has the dates by country.
  3. Swap back as soon as the date passes. A Christmas tree in February makes a listing look forgotten.
  4. Keep the product accurate. The season goes in the room, never on the product. Show the frame, finish and mat you actually ship, and keep props beside the art, not in front of it.
  5. Make the cue big enough for a thumbnail. A tree, tartan, a lit fire or a vase of tulips reads at search-result size; small ornaments disappear.
  6. Mind Etsy's first-photo rules. Etsy says the first photo should be landscape or square and at least 635 px in width and height (Etsy, checked 2026-10-29). Treat that as a floor; keep listing photos at least 2,000 px on the shortest side.

Not as a set yet. Holiday and seasonal rooms are planned, with no launch date. Today a few rooms carry a seasonal cue, such as the Winter farmhouse living room with a small Christmas tree and the Diwali living room with a marigold garland.

When will holiday rooms be available?

Which seasons are planned?

Christmas and the winter holidays, autumn and Thanksgiving, Halloween, Valentine's Day, spring and Easter, and Mother's Day.

What is the seasonal refresh?

A planned feature that swaps the rooms in a listing set for the season in one go, and back again afterwards. It is not available yet.

When should I switch to Christmas listing photos?

About six to eight weeks before the date, roughly 30 October to 13 November for Christmas, and switch back as soon as it has passed. Change the first photo only, and adjust for delivery cut-offs and your own shop data.

Will a seasonal room change how my artwork looks?

No. WallMock places your file as it is: never redrawn, repainted or upscaled by AI. Only the room around it changes.
